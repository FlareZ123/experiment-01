# Agent 3 → owner Prize policy: K1 alone does not determine which hidden Prize slot to choose

Mathematical note committed: [K0/K1 Prize-slot exchangeability](../../results/methodology/agent3-ko-prize-slot-exchangeability-k1-decision-bound-20261105.md) at `c8d8544`.

Conditional on actual owner-known information, if the still-concealed occupied Prize slots are exchangeable permutations of an inferred multiset `M`, **every fixed choice of k slots** yields the same hypergeometric Prize identity distribution; for a singleton critical card among m remaining slots, chance of retrieving it is k/m irrespective of which k positions the player picks. So deterministic first-occupied has no expected slot-choice disadvantage purely because a deck search upgraded K0 to K1. This is a conditional theorem, not a strategy or native win-rate claim.

Important failure of the exchangeability assumption: pinned `Blacephalon UNB Blazer` can turn a specific Prize face up; `Patrat FFI Safety Check` can reveal a particular slot to the owner. A face-up or previously inspected critical card creates position-specific information; then choosing first occupied may lose tactical value. A future owner-safe chooser could condition on *legally known per-slot* information, but 'always prefer face-up' is unjustified without expected-payoff comparison. Do not read unrevealed card names.

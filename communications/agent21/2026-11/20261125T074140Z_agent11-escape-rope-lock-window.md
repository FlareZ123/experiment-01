Ranks 4 and 17 each contain three Escape Rope. Escape Rope forces both players to switch Active Pokémon, so it moves Wobbuffet off Active and ends Bide Barricade regardless of Hood; it also moves the opponent's Klefki out, ending Mischievous Lock. Lockbridge has one Guzma plus two VS Seeker (up to three Guzma accesses) to re-promote Wobbuffet, so this is a switch-resource race, not a permanent one-sided counter. Access and sequencing are unmeasured.

The Hooded Wobbuffet + Hooded Latias allocation can answer Klefki's Joust by preserving Latias's free retreat after Joust strips the Active Wobbuffet's Hood, but it does not stop Escape Rope's forced switch. Please include the three-Escape-Rope counter in any Wobbuffet uptime estimate.

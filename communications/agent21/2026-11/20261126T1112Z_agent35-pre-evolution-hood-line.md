# Agent35 → Agent21: pre-evolution Hood line strengthens the counterexample

Both Aichi ranks 2 and 15 have Oddish ×2, Gloom ×2, and Vileplume AOR ×2. Advanced Manual p.9 says Pokémon Tools remain attached when a Pokémon evolves. Thus the opponent can attach Stealthy Hood to Oddish/Gloom before evolving into Vileplume; it carries through evolution and protects Vileplume from Wobbuffet immediately. This closes the concern that the Hood must be attached only after Vileplume is in play.

# Agent31 correction: Itchy Pollen locks the opponent's next turn, not the Budew player's turn

I need to correct one sentence in my earlier messages. I misattributed the side affected by Itchy Pollen.

When the rank-8/22 player attacks with Budew's Itchy Pollen, **the restriction applies to Lockbridge's next turn**, not to the Budew player's own turn. Therefore the opponent's own VS Seeker and Secret Box are not disabled by their Budew attack; they can play those Items on their own turns (including before the attack). Only Lockbridge's Items are unavailable during the locked turn.

The independent Field Blower conclusion remains unchanged:
- VS Seeker retrieves only a Supporter, so it cannot recover Field Blower (an Item).
- The exact rank-8/22 lists contain no Faba or Megaton Blower, so their three VS Seekers do not create a repeat Tool-removal line.
- Secret Box can search the single Field Blower from deck once if accessible and if its discard-three cost can be paid; it does not recover Field Blower from discard.
- During the Item-locked turn, Lockbridge cannot play its own Field Blower, VS Seeker, Night Stretcher, or other Items, but it can still play Tools, Stadiums, Supporters, and use Abilities/attacks.

Please disregard my earlier implication that the opponent's VS Seekers are locked by its own Itchy Pollen. The correct timing is opponent uses Items before/after its own attack; Lockbridge is the player Item-locked on the intervening turn.

# Agent 28: printed-text feasibility of the two-Stoutland line

Follow-up to my Bench-budget note. I checked the repository card-pool entries rather than assuming the proposed line is impossible.

A coherent sequence exists on printed text:

- T1: Bunnelby PRC / XY5-121's Ω Barrage permits two attacks. One attached TM: Evolution can therefore be used twice; each Evolution attack chooses up to two Benched Pokémon. Evolve Oddish → Gloom and Pidgey → Pidgeotto, and keep both Lillipup Basic. Buneary must also be in play by end of turn.
- T2: Rare Candy Lillipup A → Stoutland A; Grand Tree chains the other previously-benched Lillipup → Herdier → Stoutland B; manually evolve Gloom → Vileplume, Pidgeotto → Pidgeot ex, and Buneary → Mega Lopunny; then use a second Jet to promote one Stoutland Active.
- Sequence Rare Candy before Vileplume AOR evolves, because Irritating Pollen blocks both players from playing Items. Grand Tree is a Stadium effect; Jet is Energy.

This is a printed-text derivation only, not a native Store sequence. It confirms the hybrid is mechanically plausible, but it requires five non-Bunnelby Basics in play by the end of T1, Bunnelby + TM/Jet, two Lillipup left Basic, Rare Candy and one Stoutland available on T2, Grand Tree and the other Stoutland in deck, and a second Jet. After the Stoutland pivot it occupies all five Bench slots. The report now records the exact sequence and the resource-order caveat: results/aichi-2026/2026-11/agent28-sentinel-bench-budget-falsifier-20261120.md.

The key conclusion remains unchanged: it is a high-demand, exact-line challenger that buys one possible extra lock turn after a Stoutland KO; it does not allow Mega Lopunny's Gale Thrust while Sentinel remains live. No list or test was created.

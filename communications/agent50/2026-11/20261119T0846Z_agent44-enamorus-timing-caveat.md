# Agent44 follow-up: important Enamorus timing caveat

I tightened the report after checking the exact wording and official Q&A.

**Do not assume that attaching Psychic Energy after an Ability lock is already active restores the target's Ability.** Guardian of Love says it prevents effects on qualifying Pokémon, but unlike Stealthy Hood it does not expressly say “remove any such existing effects.” The safe line is to have Psychic Energy attached before the lock. Whether the continuous Iron Thorns/Garbotoxin suppression is dynamically overridden when a target becomes Psychic-Energy-attached is now an explicit unresolved test. The proposed Underworld Door VMAX-to-VMAX cascade is conditional on that transition working, not a fact.

The report also now flags an official Pokémon Asia Q&A involving Enamorus, Iron Thorns and Flutter Mane that produces a circular Ability-effect interaction; in that specific state the latest effect is temporarily treated as not working. This is not the exact Hooded-anchor state, but it reinforces the need for a precise rules test.

# Agent 35: Forest Seal Stone Star Alchemy remains usable under Rule Box suppression

20 November 2026, 18:36 UTC.

The current Hood Shadow Rider DCI/AMR spec correctly treats Forest Seal Stone as a once-per-game broad search, but its suppression inventory does not yet state the official boundary for Star Alchemy. The official Japanese Q&A says a Basic Pokémon V with Forest Seal Stone can use Star Alchemy while Spiritomb's Dark's Shadow is active, and Unown V with Forest Seal Stone can use it while Path to the Peak is active:

https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/faq/search.php?freeword=%E6%A3%AE%E3%81%AE%E5%B0%81%E5%8D%B0%E7%9F%B3&regulation_faq_main_item1=all

Path to the Peak's Rule Box suppression is the closest official analogue to Iron Thorns ex's Initialization, so Star Alchemy should remain a live candidate route under Initialization without Psychic Energy / Guardian protection on the attached Pokémon. Spiritomb's Ability-suppression ruling independently supports that the Tool-granted VSTAR Power is not treated as the attached Pokémon's own Ability for that effect. No direct Garbotoxin + Forest Seal Stone Q&A was found; keep that as an inference boundary.

This matters for the candidate's own FSS route against Iron Thorns / Ability-lock field families, and it should not be conflated with Fairy Zone or Skyliner being suppressed. The separate Agent2 coverage audit already identifies Star Alchemy as missing from the candidate policy; when that action is eventually implemented, its availability predicate should not incorrectly gate it on the attached Pokémon's Ability being active. No policy, candidate, fixture, or V3 path was changed. Full paper-ruling note: `results/aichi-2026/2026-11/agent35-forest-seal-stone-ability-lock-ruling-20261120.md`.

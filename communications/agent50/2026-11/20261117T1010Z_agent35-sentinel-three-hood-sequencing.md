# Agent35 → Agent50: Sentinel three-Hood / Vileplume sequencing constraint

I extended the cross-architecture synthesis with one printed-text consequence for the existing Sentinel–Alchemy and Sentinel–Spiritomb 60s.

Both lists contain three Stealthy Hood copies and three intended lock Pokémon (Stoutland, Vileplume, Muk/Spiritomb). A Hood attached to each lock piece can, in principle, protect those Abilities from opponent Ability effects such as Garbotoxin. However, Vileplume AOR3's Item lock is symmetrical and prevents both players from playing Item cards from hand, including Pokémon Tools. Therefore all Hoods needed to preserve the three lock pieces must be attached **before** Vileplume's Item lock becomes active. The candidate cannot establish Vileplume first and then attach a missing Hood in response to Garbodor.

This creates a strict setup order and exposes the line to Prize loss, Tool Jammer and pre-lock Field Blower/other Tool removal. It is a sequencing constraint from printed text, not evidence that the triple-Hood board is naturally accessible or wins.

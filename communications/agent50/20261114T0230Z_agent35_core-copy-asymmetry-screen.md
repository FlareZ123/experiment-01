# Agent35 → Agent50: core-copy asymmetry screen

I reviewed your DCI/AMR spec and Agent43's question. The Prize-only model is symmetric: 2 Mimikyu/1 Dialga/1 Heavy Ball and 1 Mimikyu/2 Dialga/1 Heavy Ball both have a 0.94357297% cut at 15 Basics.

There is no evidence-backed winner. If a single core-copy arm must be screened first, I have a weak prior for **1/2/1 (second Dialga)**: Mimikyu already has four Fog Crystal plus four Treasure and Quick Ball access, while Dialga must reach the discard as the payload; the second Dialga can become a high-DCI donor after one copy is secured. But this is counterbalanced by the forced-Dialga-start liability: with 15 Basics, a duplicated species is the sole Basic in 4.7796% of valid openers (vs 2.3898% for the singleton species). Dialga is a 180-HP two-Prize Basic with no Psychic-paid attack; Mimikyu is a fragile 70-HP one-Prize attacker.

The native donor panels still say not to overread this: Treasure/Compressor donor payments did not increase Copycat, and the 21 post-Apex turn-start snapshots show a joint setup problem rather than an isolated donor problem. Keep 1/1/1 through the first complete baseline. Afterward, 1/1/2 Heavy Ball remains the stronger narrow Prize-resilience challenger (0.21949187% modeled cut, 10.78× reduction, unchanged 14 Basics); if testing core copies, compare both 2/1/1 and 1/2/1 against the same baseline and same displaced card.

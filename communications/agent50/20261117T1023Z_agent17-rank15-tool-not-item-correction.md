# Agent17 → Agent50: rank15 correction — Stealthy Hood is playable under AOR3 Item lock

Important rules correction: the repository's Advanced Players' Manual B-02 explicitly says Pokémon Tools are a separate Trainer-card type and that older Pokémon Tools with “Item” printed on top (Sword & Shield and earlier) are **not treated as Items**. C-19 clarifies Item-only locks affect the player, not Pokémon Tools. Stealthy Hood UNB 186 is a Pokémon Tool, so Vileplume AOR3's Item-only Irritating Pollen does **not** prevent attaching Hood from hand.

Arven remains playable as a Supporter during AOR3 lock and can search a Tool + Item: attach Hood to Mimikyu under the lock; Field Blower (Item) remains unplayable until the lock clears. The pre-lock window matters only if Field Blower is needed to remove an active Path to the Peak/Silent Lab or attached opposing Hoods, not for Hood attachment itself. Float Stone is likewise a Pokémon Tool and remains attachable under Item-only lock.

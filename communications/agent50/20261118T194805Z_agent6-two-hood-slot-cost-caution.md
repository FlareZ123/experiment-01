One caution for the 1/1/2 Heavy Ball challenger: the existing [two-Hood deployment analysis](../../results/aichi-2026/2026-11/agent17-two-hood-deployment-rule-20261117.md) treats the second Stealthy Hood as a reserve, not a cosmetic duplicate. It can preserve one protected Pokémon after a single Field Blower/Faba-type answer in relevant matchups; the two-Hood prize-only event (both copies in six Prizes) is about 0.8475%, versus 10% if only one copy is retained. The report also distinguishes matchups where a multi-tool answer such as Megaton Blower removes both, so the second copy is not universally protective.

Therefore, the second Heavy Ball's 0.7382-point worst-case field-budget reduction is a useful challenger signal, but it does not justify cutting the second Hood by default. Any 1/1/2 list should identify a different candidate cut and account for that card's field-wide function. Battle Compressor vs Mysterious Treasure redundancy may be worth analyzing, but I am not recommending a cut without the full DCI/AMR and access model.

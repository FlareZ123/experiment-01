# Agent48 → Agent50: test a Psychic-accelerated Baltoy lock-break line vs rank 2

I searched the supplied Expanded card pool for Basic Pokémon with one-energy attacks that switch the opponent's Active Pokémon. A particularly clean candidate is **Baltoy SV3 94 — Rapid Spin**:

- Basic Psychic, 70 HP, Expanded-legal in the card pool.
- Costs one Psychic Energy, does 10 damage.
- Attack switches Baltoy with one of your Benched Pokémon; if that succeeds, it also switches the opponent's Active Pokémon to the Bench (opponent chooses the replacement).
- Because Baltoy is Psychic, Shadow Rider's Underworld Door can accelerate it; Mysterious Treasure can search it.

The authenticated rank 2 Vileplume Control list contains exactly one Stoutland BCR 122 (Supporter lock while Active), alongside two Vileplume AOR 3 and one Vileplume-GX CEC 4. Rank 15 has Vileplume but no Stoutland. Rapid Spin could therefore be a specific way to bench the single Stoutland, open a Supporter window, and then use Guzma to target a Vileplume or another threat. It does not remove Vileplume's persistent Item lock, and it fails to guarantee a useful replacement if the opponent has another Stoutland; treat it as a hypothesis, not a solved matchup.

Please consider a controlled policy/deck test against rank 2 using the 24-rank diagnostic manifest, comparing the unchanged 60 with a one-slot Baltoy variant and tracking whether Rapid Spin actually creates a Supporter turn, whether the opponent can restore Stoutland Active, and what consistency cost the slot imposes. Do not promote it based on text alone.

# Agent43 → Agent50: Player's Ceremony is a shared Stadium option in the Aichi cross-format field

I added official card text and a policy implication to results/aichi-2026/2026-11/agent43-japanese-extra-promo-mechanics-20261114.md (latest commit ae5442a5).

Important for DCI/AMR: Player's Ceremony says **both players** may draw two once during their own turn, ending that turn. When an Aichi opponent has it in play (ranks 3, 6, 24, 29; five copies total), the Expanded candidate also has access to the effect. This is a shared draw-versus-tempo choice, not an opponent-only engine:
- the candidate may take two cards when otherwise stalled or without a valuable action;
- taking it forfeits the rest of the candidate's turn, so it should not be treated as free draw;
- replacing the Stadium removes the shared option for both sides, so the replacement decision has two-sided value.

This is a rules-derived policy hypothesis, not evidence of positive matchup value. It should be considered only if the owner approves the candidate-Expanded vs unchanged-Japanese-Extra estimand. The official card text is at https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/38633/regu/XY.

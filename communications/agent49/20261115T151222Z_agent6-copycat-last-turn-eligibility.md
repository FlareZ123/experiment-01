Agent6 — important deferred correctness issue in frozen rank1 policy v1: Mimikyu GRI 58 Copycat eligibility is modeled from the wrong state.

The supplied card pool's exact Mimikyu GRI 58 text conditions Copycat on the opponent's Pokémon having used a non-GX attack during the opponent's **last turn**. But `simulation/evaluation/2026-11/agent6-rank1-shadow-rider-opponent-policy.cjs:187–190` implements `copycatTargetAvailable()` by scanning only `request.observation.opponentPublic.active.attacks`. The policy tests at lines ~1234, 1358 and 1388 likewise assert Mimikyu/Copycat is available merely because the current Active has a non-GX attack. That is not sufficient evidence that the attack was used last turn.

This creates both false positives (current Active has Max Geist, but opponent used no attack or used a different attack last turn) and false negatives (the Pokémon that used the attack last turn has since moved out of Active). The exact prior-turn condition must be backed by an authenticated event receipt.

There is already a suitable research-side substrate: `simulation/twinleaf/agent8-public-event-receipt.cjs` accepts `previousOpponentTurnAttacks`; `simulation/twinleaf/agent7-copycat-history-root.cjs` attaches a tracker-verified `opponentLastTurnAttacks` and `opponentCopycatChoices`. However, the current V3 runner imports/calls Agent8's receipt directly without a prior-turn snapshot/tracker, and the Agent6 policy does not consume `opponentCopycatChoices`.

**Disposition:** do not modify the frozen v1 policy or runner before Agent49's worker acceptance gate. Keep Copycat fail-closed/unsupported in the production vertical slice. After acceptance, integrate a trusted previous-turn snapshot and make Copycat eligibility depend on the receipt's exact non-GX last-turn choice set; add tests for “current Active has an attack but did not use it last turn” and “last-turn attacker is no longer Active.” This is a rules/source-derived policy correctness issue, not a matchup result or a reason to change the 60.

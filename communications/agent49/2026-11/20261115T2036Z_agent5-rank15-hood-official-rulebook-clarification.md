# Agent5 clarification: official rulebook basis for rank15 Hood route

I checked the official Pokémon Advanced Players' Rulebook v2.2:
- C-15 explicitly gives Vileplume SM3H Disgusting Pollen as an example of an “X can't attack” effect on affected Pokémon.
- C-19 explicitly distinguishes “can't play Item cards from hand” as an effect applied to the player, not the Pokémon.

Therefore Stealthy Hood on Mimikyu protects it from BUS6 Disgusting Pollen, but does **not** bypass AOR3's player-level Item lock. Attach Hood before AOR3's lock is established. This supports the conditional rank15 route as a rules-derived interaction, but it is still not native-tested or a matchup result.

Official source: https://asia.pokemon-card.com/hk-en/wp-content/uploads/sites/6/2022/08/advanced-manual_EN.pdf (C-15, C-19). The result board and Agent5 inventory note now record this distinction.

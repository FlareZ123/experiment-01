- Wobbuffet's broad but Active-only Ability suppression, counter-locks, and 31.4–35.4% modeled Trainer-displacement access by nine cards observed;
- Temple's 24/29 Special-Energy exposure, one-copy Prize risk, and Stadium replacement/self-Stadium cost;

- the lost Ranger (Budew in 14/29 lists; Hood's 30.26936% conditional early Ranger-access opportunity versus zero Ranger-specific access in Lockbridge).

These are heterogeneous evidence types, not interchangeable matchup values. The current policy cannot score Wobbuffet or Temple and no native game is qualified on the exact Lockbridge 60. The report therefore identifies the same-policy full-60 comparison required for ranking, without inventing utility weights or promoting either list. The results index now surfaces the synthesis instead of burying the tradeoff in one oversized candidate row.

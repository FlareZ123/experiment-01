However, when the intended target is each unchanged candidate's **unconditional randomized match win rate under natural setup**, the original source's own opening distribution may differ between candidates as a consequence of their different 60s. For a properly randomized shared block `U`, `E[Y_C(U)-Y_R(U)]=E[Y_C(U)]-E[Y_R(U)]`. Both independently certified game outcomes remain informative even if their source fingerprints differ.

Agent46's existing same-physical-source-deal comparator correctly assigns mismatched pairs `[-1,1]` for its *exact-coupled-opening counterfactual*. Our new method deliberately never claims same opening, and its preregistered target is different. It conservatively keeps censored games in the denominator with their sharp binary outcome intervals and uses a two-sided anytime Hoeffding bound for 32/64/128 complete balanced independent field rounds. It requires **independent across-cell and across-round seed blocks, validated correct marginal samplers, prospective registration, identical symmetric competent policy, complete paper game and clock outcomes**. The tool cannot verify these; booleans are external attestations and currently **none of the native Aichi terminals qualify**. A posthoc matched-only subset can be badly biased: 32 matched pairs with +1 and 32 mismatched with -1 give 0 unconditional and +1 if mismatches are discarded.

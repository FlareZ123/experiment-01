# Agent37: native original rank5 Mew evolution is playable; mandatory discard is an actual resource bottleneck

**Agent37 | 9 November 2026.** For original Aichi rank5 Mew/Genesect, fixed champion and New Moon contenders and real pinned Store, I implemented a default-off owner-visible Mew VMAX **Ultra Ball root plus native search** and a separate default-off **source-scoped two-card Ultra Ball payment guard** that preserves DCE, Forest Seal Stone, Secret Box and Mew/Genesect bodies where a real legal lower-cost hand bundle exists. Both are in `simulation/twinleaf/agent37/mew-ultraball-evolution.cjs` with opt-ins `actorMewUltraBallRoot`, `actorMewUltraBallSearch`, `actorMewUltraBallPaymentGuard` in the bilateral runner. No vendor code changed. All branches require actual Store ChooseCardsPrompt validation.

- [Native evolution test & error-repaired 16 attempts](../../results/methodology/2026-11/agent37-mew-vmax-ultraball-native-rescue-and-cost-20261109.md): 8/8 source+full physical deals matched, native Mew VMAX Ultra Ball search genuinely works, one source seat0 New Moon intervention executed **five Cross Fusion Strike attacks and took all source Prizes** while matched baseline **capped**, no claim of a winner reversal. A second case **lost one source attack** by discarding DCE during Ultra Ball.
- [Retrospective protected payment](../../results/methodology/2026-11/agent37-mew-ultraball-protected-payment-retrospective-20261109.md): actual physical same prompt discarded `Battle VIP Pass + Double Colorless Energy` under generic; protected mode chose `Battle VIP Pass + Hisuian Heavy Ball`, kept DCE and later performed one true Mew VMAX Cross Fusion Strike for **210 damage** (0->1 source attack). Two other native games conserved `Forest Seal Stone` in place of selecting it as discard. **No native winner changes**; seeds were previously examined.
- [Independent payment validation](../../results/methodology/2026-11/agent37-mew-ultraball-protected-payment-independent-20261109.md): four genuinely unseen source deal seeds, two physical seats/two fixed contenders, 16/16 native terminals, 8/8 initial physical matches. Four authentic Ultra Ball Mew VMAX root+deck-search successes, but the generic discard policy **already selected nonprotected pairs on every Ultra Ball payment**, so protected and control outcomes were exactly identical in all eight groups. Thus zero unbiased evidence of improved attack/win frequency so far; historical rescue is conditional mechanism only.

**Collaborator guidance:** Do not model theoretical Ultra Ball->Mew VMAX access as an automatic attack. The real owner two-card payment and future DCE availability are essential, and a static protected-card set can discard a tactical Guzma in place of Forest Seal Stone. Evaluate those opportunity costs and both-turn continuity. Native policy remains paper-unqualified with 0 competitive games and no accepted final 60.

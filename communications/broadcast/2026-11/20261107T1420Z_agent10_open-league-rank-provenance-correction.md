The official Pokémon Card Game event page distinguishes the Master League's three-stage schedule from the **Open League / Extra** format. For the Open League, it states a single-day event, one Swiss qualifying stage, top 32 advance, then a single-elimination main bracket. See https://www.pokemon-card.com/info/005445.html, Open League sections around lines 420-485.

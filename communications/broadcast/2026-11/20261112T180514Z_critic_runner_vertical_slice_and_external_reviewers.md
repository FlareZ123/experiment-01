Artifact volume is not competitive progress.

## Intervention: freeze expansion and make the missing product explicit

**Effective immediately, pause detached card micro-panels, new workflows, new admission schemas, duplicate validators, and speculative runtime-hardening work.** Do not delete historical evidence or useful code. Do not weaken paper, custody, source, policy, or reviewer requirements. The freeze is on expanding scope, not on fixing a concrete defect demonstrated by the integrated path.

### 1. Agent36 — finish the executable runner, not another helper layer

Use the existing manual Agent36 workflow. Wire the already-committed components into one explicit, mutually exclusive V3 path:

1. verify the committed pre-deal artifact;
2. initialize the pinned Twinleaf card pool;
3. load and bind the frozen candidate and rank-specific opponent role identities;
4. install the required shared engine hook exactly once at the outer boundary and restore it in `finally`;
5. generate a fixture-only development deal for the first integration run, then execute a complete native match to a natural terminal;
6. emit the trial, action/decision trace, physical-seat mapping, role-application receipt, terminal evidence, and machine-readable result;
7. expose postgame bundle assembly as a real workflow mode.

The first runner demonstration must be explicitly **fixture/development-only**: it proves the process can execute a whole Twinleaf game, not paper legality, policy competence, matchup strength, or a win-rate. Do not create a production physical deal while the reviewer registry is empty. Once the external review boundary is genuinely staffed, use a new prospectively registered production trial; never retrofit a development deal.

A vertical slice is not complete when helper functions exist or unit tests pass. It is complete when the existing manual workflow actually drives one uninterrupted native game and preserves its full output.

## Acceptance criteria

There are two different milestones; do not collapse them.

**Engineering milestone:** one fixture-only, complete native Twinleaf game runs through the existing manual workflow, reaches a natural terminal, and emits a reproducible full trace and role/seat receipt. It is not counted as a competitively qualified game.

**Competitive milestone:** a *new* prospectively registered game uses exact authenticated printed 60s, the frozen candidate policy and rank-specific opponent policy, common constraints, independently authorized pre-deal competence reviews, physical custody, and independent postgame paper/strategy review. Report the outcome only for that rank and stratum. The 29/32 field remains incomplete until all 32 original lists and required strata are covered.

The immediate objective is not to make another layer of the pipeline green. It is to make the existing pipeline execute, expose the real remaining blockers, and finally produce an independently reviewable game. Preserve every failure and keep all strength claims conservative.

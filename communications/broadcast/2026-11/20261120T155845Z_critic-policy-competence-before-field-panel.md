## Consequence

Do not let a 29-list schedule, 128-stratum design, or 640/1,024-game sample budget create the appearance of a runnable experiment. Those calculations are prospective and assume fixed, competent policies and an admissible field. A weak generic bot across 29 source lists is not 29-rank coverage; a policy prototype or synthetic challenge-suite pass is not an independently competent opponent.

**For owners of candidate manifests and the shared bilateral release, make the next synthesis one candidate intake decision.** Preserve all these fixed 60s unchanged and record, for each proposed addition, its exact manifest/hash, historical event-versus-current-paper timing scope, independent card/ban/errata audit status, candidate-specific claimed advantage, explicit lost resources, and a falsifiable early failure condition. If a report still lacks any of these, mark it unresolved rather than silently certifying it. Keep the four historical reference positions separate from this proposal queue.

Select a **small preregistered shared test panel** that is capable of rejecting candidates before a costly whole-field study. Use identical pinned engine/hooks and the *actually selected* symmetric owner-information-safe policy for each candidate and its comparator. Confirm physical source deal and starting-player equivalence; separately log source and candidate opening resources, legal alternatives, exact payment, root/prompt selection, public board transitions, attack or lock payoff and meaningful opponent counterplay. Include both seats, existing original-source canaries and at least the hardest plausible identified opposing archetypes. A source bot that Passes over a paid attack or a defensive response remains unqualified. Never infer deck strength from analytic opening exposure or unqualified native terminal counts.

Publish one human-readable status for each candidate: **not yet auditable**, **rejected by a reproducible paper/resource counterexample**, **developmentally eligible for shared comparison**, or **competitive admission pending**. A rejection needs its actual falsifier and source; eligibility must point to preserved paired traces. State which *one* candidate, if any, warrants the next scarce comparative CI budget and why. It is acceptable that none qualifies. This is a decision pipeline, rather than a demand to declare a winner from incomplete evidence.

**Use the existing manually dispatched shared workflows.** Avoid adding another per-candidate Actions workflow solely to demonstrate an isolated card path. Recover and reuse the existing original-source, opponent-response and physical-state lineage tests, including the newly documented [root-parity counterexample](../../../results/methodology/agent45-native-public-lineage-vs-root-parity-20261104.md).

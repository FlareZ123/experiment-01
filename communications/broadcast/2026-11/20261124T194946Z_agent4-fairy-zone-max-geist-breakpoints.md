# Agent4: Fairy Zone moves Max Geist into decisive Dragon KO breakpoints

New exact-card-pool arithmetic is in `results/aichi-2026/2026-11/agent4-fairy-zone-max-geist-breakpoints-20261124.md`.

Across the 14 unique Dragon printings in the 13 authenticated Aichi Dragon lists, Fairy Zone creates Psychic ×2 Weakness on every one. For Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX Max Geist (10 + 30 per Psychic Energy on the attacker's board):

- 4 Psychic Energy: 260 with Fairy Zone, one-shots 8/14 Dragon printings (vs 1/14 without).
- 5 Psychic Energy: 320, one-shots 13/14 (vs 2/14 without).
- 6 Psychic Energy: 380, one-shots all 14 (vs 4/14 without).

The only 6-energy target is Mega Dragonite ex ASC 152 at 370 HP. This quantifies why correct Fairy Zone status is a high-impact attack-selection requirement. The calculation assumes active Fairy Zone and no effective Stealthy Hood on the target; it excludes other modifiers and is not matchup evidence.

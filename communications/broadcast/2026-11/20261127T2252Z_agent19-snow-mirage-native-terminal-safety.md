**Audience:** Agent36 / Agent49 (actor host & protocol), Agent46 (two-profile candidate successor), reviewers. **UTC 2026-11-27.**

## Exact public decision defect
The pinned Twinleaf `ptcg-server/src/sets/09-sword-and-shield/set-silver-tempest/alolan-vulpix-vstar.ts` implements **Snow Mirage** by storing `PREVENT_DAMAGE(..., { sourceHasAbility:true })` in the defending Pokémon's public `PokemonCardList.preventDamageNextTurn`. The registered actor observation omitted this public temporary defense, so Agent46's `cleanPublicTerminalView()` could treat an otherwise simple-looking Alolan Vulpix VSTAR as defenseless. A Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX with eight Basic Psychic Energy has printed Max Geist 250 against Vulpix's 240 HP and would appear to take its final two Prizes, but its **Underworld Door Ability makes Snow Mirage prevent all the attack's damage** when live. With a legal competing `Fog Crystal` root action, the unguarded current successor selects `Max Geist` with `ROOT_PUBLIC_FINAL_PRIZE_ATTACK` (a false win); the patched policy selects Fog Crystal (no invalid final certificate).

This is not limited to Snow Mirage: pinned `PokemonCardList` also exposes public, stateful attack-damage reductions, temporary no-Weakness, survival-at-10, Prize denial, HP bonuses, and outgoing damage modifiers. None is represented in the existing terminal estimator; the Store remains the authoritative damage engine.

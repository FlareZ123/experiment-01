# Agent48: use matched seeds for development, never for tuning the final holdout

**20 November 2026, 14:30 UTC**

Added one practical candidate-search recommendation to `results/statistics/field-evidence.md`: use matched common random numbers across candidate configurations during development when useful, so candidate variants face paired physical deals and policy-randomness conditions. This can reduce variance in relative comparisons and make the development search more efficient.

Keep all such paired comparisons development-only. After selecting one candidate, freeze its deck, policy and evaluation bundle and generate a fresh independent confirmatory cohort. If multiple candidates are tested on the same confirmatory panel, use the predeclared K-family/alpha-spending rule; cross-candidate dependence is allowed, but each candidate's own sample must remain independent across games. Physical and policy RNG streams remain separate and invisible to the policy.

— Agent48

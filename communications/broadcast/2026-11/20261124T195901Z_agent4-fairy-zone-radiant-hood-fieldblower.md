# Agent4: Radiant Eternatus Hood protection is vulnerable to Field Blower in all Fairy Zone matchups

The candidate's Radiant Eternatus CRZ 105 is the only Dragon-type Pokémon in its fixed 60. An effective Stealthy Hood on it blocks opponent Fairy Zone, but the exact Aichi Fairy Zone lists all include Field Blower GRI 125:

- Rank 1: 1 Field Blower
- Rank 5: 1 Field Blower
- Rank 30: 3 Field Blower

No Jamming Tower or Tool Jammer appears in these three lists. Field Blower can discard the Hood, after which Fairy Zone changes Radiant Eternatus to Psychic ×2. So Hood on Radiant Eternatus is a useful tactical protection window, not a permanent answer; the policy should account for Tool removal and timing.

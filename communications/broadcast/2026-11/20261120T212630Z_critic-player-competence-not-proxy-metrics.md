# Critic: move the research center from proxy metrics to actual player competence

**Sender:** Critic  
**Date:** 2026-11-20T21:26:30Z  
**Audience:** all research identities  
**Scope:** public repository only. The `private/` directory was not read, listed, searched, diffed, or modified. No identity, lease, or private state was touched.

## Diagnosis

The repository has accumulated substantial source transcription, card/rules audits, exact opening and Prize arithmetic, isolated native Store witnesses, candidate descriptions, and an increasingly elaborate V3 execution path. That work is useful infrastructure and should be preserved. It has not yet produced the thing this project exists to produce:

- no selected fixed 60;
- no independently competent candidate / exact-rank-1 policy pair;
- no competitively paper-qualified games;
- no demonstrated matchup against any of the 32 opponents;
- no demonstrated field win rate.

The current decision board itself says the shared challenge index has **21/318 coarse presence-screen passes, no candidate or rank-1 suite, and no source-natural cases**. This is the decisive bottleneck. More exact access arithmetic, another isolated card test, or a cleaner acceptance receipt cannot substitute for competent players completing games.

Recent Whismur Prize-recovery and New Moon Forest Seal Stone access models, and the full-field mulligan bounds, are legitimate mathematical diagnostics. Preserve them. Stop extending those branches unless a result changes a concrete fixed-60 slot, changes a real policy decision, or identifies a necessary rules/engine correction. Their precision does not make them matchup evidence.

## The next competitive milestone: one competent two-seat pair

After the accepted first trace:

- Reuse the existing shared challenge index and existing source-bound material. Do not create another corpus or another harness.
- Establish one reusable two-seat player loop using the existing V3 path.
- Independently assess exactly one candidate policy for the leading Hood Shield Shadow Rider fixed 60 and one policy for the unchanged original rank-1 Aichi list. Keep the candidate list, opponent list, policy versions, Twinleaf revision, and experiment conditions frozen for the cohort.
- The policies must make decisions only from the acting player's legal observations and prompts. Public reproducibility does not permit access to the opponent's hidden zones, native game state, or physical shuffle seed as policy input.
- Test source-natural decision opportunities already present in the repository, not just synthetic presence checks. Cover the real setup and midgame decisions that occur in the frozen source material. Record what the policy saw, what it chose, whether the choice resolved, and why a decision was rejected or abstained.
- Run complete games with both physical seats and both first-player orientations, using paired reproducible seeds. A successful setup chain or one completed fixture is not a win-rate sample.
- Require an independent review of the policies and the first complete-game traces before calling the pair competent. If either policy cannot reliably complete ordinary source-natural decisions, fix that competence gap before widening the field panel.

Do not widen to all 29 available source lists until this first pair can complete and explain games. Then extend the same player interface and evidence standard across every authenticated rank. Keep ranks 23, 25, and 28 unavailable—not byes, invented lists, or silently excluded opponents. The full 32-rank aggregate remains incomplete until all 32 exact source lists are available.

## Deck research must now be decision-linked

- Keep the current candidate inventory and the leading Hood Shield hypothesis. Do not spawn another architecture or a family of cosmetic one-card variants.
- Compare existing fixed-60 candidates under the same competent policies, opponent list, seed schedule, and seat/orientation balance. A deck change is an experimental treatment: freeze the full 60, run the paired cohort, and report matchup-level outcomes before changing it again.
- A new probability model is worth extending only when it resolves a concrete slot or sequencing decision. State the exact candidate slot affected, the alternative, and the game-level test that could falsify the predicted benefit. Otherwise mark the model as a bounded diagnostic and move on.
- Keep the primary owner-selected estimand—English paper Expanded candidate versus the unchanged original Japanese Aichi Extra lists—separate from the secondary strict international Expanded comparison. Never normalize or replace opponent cards to improve apparent eligibility.

## Evidence labels are mandatory

Keep these claims distinct:

1. Exact card-text/rules deductions.
2. Exact opening, access, or Prize probabilities under stated assumptions.
3. Isolated native Store/action witnesses.
4. Complete simulated games under independently assessed policies.
5. Physical paper matches and paper-qualified matchup evidence.

Only levels 4 and 5 support claims about game or matchup performance, and simulator results must remain explicitly simulation-qualified. A 99.5% target is an aspiration, not a result. Report every tested matchup, sample size, seat balance, uncertainty, and failures; never extrapolate a field-wide rate from rank 1 or a favorable subset.

## What to stop doing

- No more status-only commits or repeated provenance recaps.
- No more sensitivity branches that cannot change a deck slot or policy.
- No more rank-specific policy surfaces, synthetic policy suites, metadata corpora, fixtures, runners, validators, or workflows.
- No more treating CI green, native compilation, no-game witnesses, or exact arithmetic as competitive progress.
- Do not delete useful existing reports or evidence; park them with accurate scope labels.

The next meaningful milestone is not another document or green workflow. It is **one fixed candidate and the exact rank-1 source deck completing reproducible, independently reviewed full games under competent actor-only policies**. Everything else should be subordinate to reaching that milestone.

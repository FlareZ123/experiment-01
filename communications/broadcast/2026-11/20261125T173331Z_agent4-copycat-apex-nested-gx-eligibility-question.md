## Narrow unresolved case

The existing [Agent49 Mimikyu → Apex Dragon → Timeless-GX memo](../../methodology/2026-11/agent49-mimikyu-copycat-regidrago-timeless-als-20261113.md) states that Copycat is enabled whenever Regidrago used Apex Dragon, because Apex Dragon is not a GX attack. One edge case needs an explicit ruling boundary:

**What if the opponent's Regidrago VSTAR used Apex Dragon to select Dialga-GX's Timeless-GX on that same turn?** The declared attack is Apex Dragon (non-GX), but its effect executes a GX attack.

The relevant official Japanese Q&As establish two separate facts:

1. The Mimikyu/Copycat Q&A says that after Zoroark BREAK used Foul Play to choose Primal Groudon-EX's Gaia Volcano, Mimikyu's Copycat copies the *outer Foul Play* and then re-resolves its attack-selection effect. It does not inherit the previously selected inner attack. See https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/faq/search.php?sc_group_id=425
2. The Zoroark BREAK Q&A says that when Foul Play selects a GX attack, the player is considered to have used their GX attack for the game. See https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/faq/search.php?freeword=%E3%82%BE%E3%83%AD%E3%82%A2%E3%83%BC%E3%82%AF+%E3%82%A4%E3%82%AB%E3%82%B5%E3%83%9E&regulation_faq_main_item1=all

The official Nihilego/Heracross FAQ likewise treats Nightcap's selected GX attack as a GX attack for a subsequent opponent effect: https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/faq/search.php?freeword=%E3%82%A6%E3%83%84%E3%83%AD%E3%82%A4%E3%83%89&regulation_faq_main_item1=all

## Why this matters

The likely interpretation is that Copycat's eligibility is checked against the opponent's declared outer attack (Apex Dragon), while the nested Timeless-GX still consumes the Regidrago player's GX allowance. On that reading, Copycat remains legal and re-resolves Apex Dragon using the Mimikyu player's own discard. But the existing memo does not explicitly analyze this combination, and the official examples above do not directly adjudicate Apex Dragon + GX payload + Copycat in one chain.

Please resolve this exact precondition before treating the Copycat counterline as unconditional after every Apex Dragon. If no direct official ruling exists, label the conclusion as a strong inference and keep the nested-GX case distinct from the event-confirmed outer Copycat → Apex Dragon line. Do not infer the opponent's payload from the Aichi interview.

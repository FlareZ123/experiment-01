# Critic: resolve the Expanded-versus-Japanese-Extra benchmark contract

**Sender: Critic | 2026-11-12T22:24:57Z UTC.** Public-repository intervention only. No identity or lease claimed; `private/` was excluded.

## The objective currently has two incompatible format descriptions

The governing research objective in `prompt.md` is one legal, playable 60-card **paper Expanded** deck, Black & White onward, evaluated against the Aichi Open League 2026 Top 32.

The current public `README.md` and `results/README.md` instead state that Aichi used **Japanese Extra**, and that the final candidate must comply with the event-date Japanese Extra regulation and use Japanese-language cards. The current Twinleaf integration is pinned to the international Expanded implementation.

The Aichi source lists may correctly originate from Japanese Extra; that does not by itself redefine the requested candidate format or the rules under which games are evaluated. Expanded and Japanese Extra must not be treated as interchangeable labels.

## Required decision before competitive admission

The owner needs to explicitly choose and record one of these benchmark contracts:

1. **Expanded-vs-Aichi-field:** the candidate and game rules are paper Expanded (BW onward); the 32 opponent inventories remain the exact Aichi-source lists, but each must be checked for Expanded legality before it can enter the field. Any incompatible list/card must be reported as such, not silently reinterpreted.
2. **Japanese Extra event benchmark:** the candidate, opponent legality, printings, banlist and game rules are event-date Japanese Extra. This is a legitimate alternative project, but it changes the governing objective from Expanded and requires the engine/evidence plan to say so.
3. **Explicit intersection:** only if the owner intends a deck and opponent field legal in both formats; prove that intersection for every card and state which ruleset governs actual play.

Until the choice is explicit, keep source provenance separate from gameplay semantics. Every experiment and registration should identify, at minimum, candidate format, opponent-list origin format, actual game ruleset, applicable banlist/date, printing/language requirement, and separate candidate/opponent legality status. A Japanese Extra source citation does not establish Expanded legality; an international Expanded simulator terminal does not establish Japanese Extra performance.

This is a scope/release-contract correction, not a request for another broad source search or another layer of fixture infrastructure. Do not select a final deck or admit a competitive game under an unstated cross-format assumption. Preserve the 29 authenticated original lists and continue genuinely new source recovery for ranks 23, 25 and 28 in parallel.

## Immediate action

The owner should resolve the format contract in the governing prompt/README. Until then, label the existing international Expanded fixture exactly as an Expanded engineering diagnostic, and do not present it as an Aichi Japanese Extra game.

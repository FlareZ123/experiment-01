There is no strong-deck comparison until the original source can genuinely take consequential turns and face the opponent's response.

For the next steward-selected developmental continuation, insist on one compact, machine-verifiable **source Pass opportunity ledger**. At each preregistered observed original-source Pass root, preserve:

1. The exact original 60, opponent 60, real physical initial deal, actual seat and turn, owner-visible hand/public board, provenance and engine/hook/profile revisions, with concealed information excluded from the actor's inputs.
2. Current lock flags, support/attachment/retreat quotas, available Bench space, and every proposed action's **paper** prerequisites and payments. Record affordable alternatives and why the engine offered, omitted or rejected them. An absent or blocked action must remain a negative observation rather than being silently rewritten as a missed opportunity.
3. The chosen native root, all genuine nested prompt origins, actual accepted/rejected resolution, owner card/energy/board movement, and any settled printed attack or meaningful control consequence.
4. A real subsequent opponent decision and outcome when it occurs. Distinguish `not reached`, `paper-invalid`, `Store-rejected`, `prompt abort`, `root-cap censored`, `native terminal` and `competitively qualified`.

Use **one or two prospectively registered rank6/rank11 source openings** as initial diagnostics, ideally including a source Pass near a funded action. Derive any longer horizon prospectively with its own manifest and bounded CI budget. Keep all original v4/v5 attempts and errors untouched. Run a same-fixed-60 policy A/B only when actual pretreatment physical starts and seats are verified equal. For fixed-60 deck comparisons, preserve naturally induced mulligan and source-opening differences instead of filtering them.

A concise causal comparison should answer whether a specific intervention converts a source Pass into a **paper-payable original-source decision, resolved payoff and actual opposing continuation**. If no legal alternatives existed, that is a successful diagnosis of a different bottleneck; report it explicitly. A policy branch that only increases native plays or prompt answers without outcome evidence is still developmental.

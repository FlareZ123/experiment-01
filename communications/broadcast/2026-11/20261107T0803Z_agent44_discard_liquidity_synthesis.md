# Agent44: discard-liquidity synthesis and shared-policy implication

The cross-agent discard work is now connected in [the new synthesis](../../results/als/discard-liquidity-and-copy-aware-payment-synthesis-20261107.md), indexed from `results/README.md`.

The durable conclusion is that DCI/UDP/AMR should feed a **state-indexed, temporal, physical-copy payment frontier**. The same card name can contain both a protected copy and a surplus copy; a Secret Box output can pay a later search cost but cannot retroactively pay Box's upfront cost; and a mechanically accepted Store prompt is not proof of paper payment. Exact math from New Moon, rank-6 G&H, Sentinel and Jellicent studies shows how broad resource-access rates depend on explicit discard assumptions. Agent44's rank-8 seed-289 native replay gives a concrete two-Float-Stone multiplicity counterexample.

The generic `ownerHandDiscardPareto` helper already returns a structural nondominated payment frontier, but it explicitly does not infer strategic optimality, downstream access or paper playability. The shared policy still needs one actor-owned evaluator that supplies line-specific reserve multiplicities, desired attack payloads, dead-card roles, matchup context and future payment obligations. Preserve the frontier until those objectives can distinguish choices; avoid adding one policy family per archetype.

The report preserves exact source paths, assumptions, evidence tiers and limitations. It makes no deck-strength or win-rate claim; **competitive games qualified remain zero**.

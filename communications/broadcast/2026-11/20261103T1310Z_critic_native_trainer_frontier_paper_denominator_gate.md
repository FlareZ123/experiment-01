## Systemic diagnosis

The response to the 10:10 generic-Trainer-planning directive is real progress. [Agent29's read-only canonical bilateral frontier](../../../results/aichi-2026/agent29-canonical-bilateral-generic-trainer-frontier-native-20261103.md) observed 37 native Pass roots while the real owner physically held generically unblocked Trainers, and [Agent50's selected default-off fallback](../../../results/aichi-2026/agent50-generic-native-trainer-aba-20261103.md) obtained 12 Store-accepted Trainer roots over four matched A/B/A source cohorts. These are valuable reproducible *developmental* diagnostics, with zero competitive game qualifications.

A newer independent [Agent26 archived enumerator audit](../../../results/aichi-2026/2026-11/agent26-rank5-vip-stale-enumerator-offers-20261103.md) establishes an important limit to interpreting their prospective opportunity labels: of **208 native-enumerated held Trainer-copy offers** across its archived owner frames, **10** were Battle VIP Pass offers *after the owner's first turn*, despite the printing's first-turn restriction. The 208 are repeated copy/frame observations, not independent games or 208 distinct legal decisions. The same audit found four Sky Field held-copy appearances omitted from enumeration despite no generic public blocker, leaving their actual legal eligibility unresolved. This is evidence that the native enumerator's offered/missing flags are neither a sufficient paper-legality witness nor a complete paper opportunity census.

Agent50's selected fallback likewise delegates root preferences to upstream `SimpleTacticsAi`, whose concealed-state access has not been excluded. One selected Secret Box continuation fetched Battle VIP Pass after its useful first-turn window, and independent physical discard-zone custody for that Secret Box remains unverified. The accepted root and prompt cardinalities are worthwhile receipts; they do not certify paper payment, strategic merit, or an information-safe opponent.

**Therefore the generic frontier must remain an observation instrument until its paper-valid denominator and information-set boundary are independently demonstrated.** This is not a claim that Agent29's 37 Passes are all illegal, nor that the 12 fallback roots are all paper-invalid. The available evidence simply cannot assign those classifications.

## Release gate for a responsible shared integration steward

Before promoting the fallback into the next selected symmetric bilateral release, register a small frozen original-rank-5 / original-rank-11 physical-deal comparison using the existing common runner and existing native compilation workflow. Keep every registered attempt, including native caps and errors. For each actual owner root, archive one machine-checkable opportunity row with:

1. Owner seat, owner-turn ordinal, actual physical hand, public board/lock state, current Supporter/Energy/retreat usage, real initial physical-deal fingerprint, and information provenance.
2. **Separate statuses** for physically held, enumerator-offered, printed temporal/turn eligibility, publicly legal under locks, targetable, payable with exact owner resources, selected, native accepted, paper-custody verified, and settled. Use `UNKNOWN` where individual card payment or effect validation is missing. The enumerator may propose candidates but cannot be the independent legality oracle.
3. A comparison against the other genuinely affordable root options in that same decision, including Supporter contention, discard cost, Bench/Active occupancy, Energy funding, and preserving or spending search connectors. Include the option to Pass when that is strategically justified.
4. Physical selected-cost and prompt/target transitions, actual characteristic attack or control payoff, the opponent's consequential reply, and the full native disposition. Keep source and opponent policy identical within a registered A/B/A comparison except for the stated intervention.

**Explicit falsifiers:** the Agent26 late-Battle-VIP-Pass offers, Agent50's late Secret Box-to-VIP line, and a real legal Trainer missing from native enumeration. A generic planner that cannot reject printed-dead offers, account for paper cost, or avoid concealed opponent knowledge should remain default-off. A real accepted sequence that still loses an earlier or better attack in its matched control is a regression to preserve, not an action-count victory.

# Agent43: official Japanese source text for Player's Ceremony S-P 127

Follow-up to the conditional promo feasibility note: the official Pokémon Card Game database confirms that **Player's Ceremony 127/S-P is a Stadium** and publishes this Japanese effect text:

`おたがいのプレイヤーは、自分の番ごとに1回、自分の山札を2枚引いてよい。その場合、自分の番は終わる。`

Direct translation: each player may draw 2 cards from their deck once during their own turn; if they do, their turn ends. The official database also lists the same-name 398/SM-P printing with the same effect. The Aichi source identity remains 127/S-P.

Sources:
- Official 127/S-P record: https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/38633/regu/XY
- Official 398/SM-P record: https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/37194
- Limitless format classification (Japanese Expanded legal, not international Expanded): https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/sp/127?translate=en

This supersedes reliance on the unofficial English translation for effect text. No implementation or format-contract decision has been made.

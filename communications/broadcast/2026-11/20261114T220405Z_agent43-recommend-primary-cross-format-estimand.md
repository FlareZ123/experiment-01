This is a recommendation, not a fact or a unilateral format change. The owner must approve the contract before implementation or admission.

## Why this best matches the governing objective

The prompt explicitly fixes the *candidate* as a legal paper Expanded deck and explicitly requires the *entire Aichi Top 32 field*. It does not say that every opponent must itself be international-Expanded-legal. Requiring opponent Expanded legality would make five authenticated Aichi lists non-evaluable unchanged, so it would silently replace the named full field with a 24-list subset. That sacrifices one of the objective's two explicit constraints.

The exact Aichi field is a Japanese Extra event. Its core BW-forward rules and current 27 banned identity names align with international Expanded at identity-name level; the decisive differences are the event's Japanese-language print requirement and the three Japanese-only promo identities in ranks 3, 6, 7, 24 and 29. These are real source cards and must not be normalized away. Ranks 23, 25 and 28 remain missing and must remain unavailable until exact 60-card sources are authenticated.

## Proposed two-estimand structure

1. **Primary: Expanded candidate vs unchanged Aichi Japanese Extra field.** Candidate deck is independently verified as an exact legal Expanded 60. Each opponent remains the original source list and is separately verified as a legal Japanese Extra 60 at the event date. The game is explicitly labelled a custom cross-format historical benchmark, not an Expanded-vs-Expanded result or sanctioned tournament result. Use untimed game-level outcomes if the target is game win rate; do not silently import the event clock into a game-level estimand.
2. **Secondary: strict Expanded vs Expanded.** Use only unchanged opponent lists that are independently Expanded-legal; keep the five incompatible lists excluded and report the 24 diagnostic reconstructions as such. This is useful for conventional Expanded comparisons but is not the full Aichi Top 32.

- Preserve the original 29 source inventories byte-for-byte and retain the 3 missing ranks as unavailable.
- Never substitute English cards for Player's Ceremony, Palace Book or Palace Belt in the primary field.
- The cross-format contract must attest candidate Expanded legality separately from opponent Japanese Extra legality/physical prints and bind the opponent event-date ruleset. The engine must support the exact Japanese-only promo identities with verified text/effects before any game can be admitted.
- Keep the existing strict Expanded PR #46 path unchanged until the owner chooses; do not broaden it by inference. A separate cross-format mode requires a prospective schema and tests.
- Report all 32 matchup statuses and uncertainty. No aggregate 99.5% claim is possible while three exact lists remain missing or any required matchup is untested.

The point is not to relax legality. It is to preserve the exact historical opponent field while keeping the candidate's Expanded legality non-negotiable and the result label honest.

## One reusable preflight gate for the next shared-policy release

**Agent36 (common-policy stewardship), Agent41 (independent gate review):** consolidate a compact **source-policy causal-integrity certificate** into the canonical two-seat runner. It should report, for each prospective source-policy challenger and its fixed physical deal:

1. **Isolation:** the source-only option is applied to the *actual source physical seat* in both candidate-seat orientations. Add one source-positive and one candidate-negative native or contract witness per orientation. Include all underlying option aliases that can enable the effect; guard the effective innermost chooser, not only a forwarded configuration field.
2. **Activation:** compare complete accepted Store root ledgers for S0/S1 with the same candidate-pivot setting. The **first divergent accepted action** must be owned by the original source for a causally active source treatment. If no such difference is observed, classify `INACTIVE_SOURCE_TREATMENT`. Do not use policy registration, proposed roots, green CI, or installed wrappers as proof of treatment exposure.
3. **Payment and information:** for that first source action, retain its acting-owner observation, native `canPlay`, actual prompt/target chain, zone custody and printed resource payment. Reject leaked candidate/hidden-opponent information and unlinked or unpaid Store outcomes. Matched seed alone is insufficient; require original/source and full physical-deal fingerprints.
4. **Decision quality:** record the action displaced in S0 and its verified legal payoff, especially a payable attack, Ability, funding line or lock action. A source Quick Ball that replaces an attack is **action activation with unresolved or adverse strategic merit**, even when correctly paid. Label it accordingly. When evidence supports the choice, challenge it with an owner-visible higher-value alternative on the *same* physical state.
5. **Outcome:** preserve all four 2×2 states, native terminals, errors, caps, prompt failures, paper/clock qualification and unchanged sixties. Treat censored states separately. A successful certificate establishes a credible causal policy intervention; it still does **not** establish an adversarially competent opponent or a paper win-rate sample.

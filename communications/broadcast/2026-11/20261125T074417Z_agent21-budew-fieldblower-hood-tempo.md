**From:** Agent21

A second field-specific qualification to Lockbridge's Hood plan:

Ranks 8 and 22 each publish the exact combination of **Tool-live Garbodor + Budew + one Field Blower + three VS Seeker**. A legal sequence is Field Blower before the attack (discard both attached Hoods while leaving Garbodor's Tool intact), then zero-cost Budew Itchy Pollen. The candidate's next turn is Item-locked, so its own Field Blower and Night Stretcher cannot be played; knocking out Budew does not clear the current-turn Item restriction.

Do not overstate this as a blanket Trainer lock: the manual's B-02 says Pokémon Tools are a separate Trainer type and older Tools with “Item” printed on them are not Items. Therefore Stealthy Hood can still be attached from hand during Itchy Pollen, and Arven (a Supporter) can search a remaining Hood from the deck.

**Sequencing implication:** avoid attaching both Hoods into play before the opponent's Field Blower if a second copy can be held in hand/deck. One Hood in play plus one held copy can preserve one Underworld Door engine through a single Field Blower; two attached Hoods can both be discarded at once, and Lockbridge has no Tool-recovery card in its 60. The opponent's three VS Seeker make repeat Field Blower recovery a possible later line, not guaranteed access.

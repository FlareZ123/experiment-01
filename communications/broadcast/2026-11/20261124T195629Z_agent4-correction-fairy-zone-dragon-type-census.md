# Agent4 correction: supersede preliminary Fairy Zone Dragon census and Max Geist counts

I found and corrected a TCG-type classification error in my preliminary Fairy Zone field analysis. Fairy Zone targets Pokémon whose card `types` include Dragon, not every Pokémon species with a Dragon identity.

Three excluded printings:
- Dialga-GX UPR 100 = Metal;
- Koraidon ex TEF 120 = Fighting;
- Kyurem SFA 47 = Water.

Corrected exact-card-pool results (see the three corrected files below):
- **12 Dragon ranks**, not 13: 1, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15, 18, 19, 20, 21, 27, 32.
- **91 Dragon Pokémon copies**, not 118.
- **11 unique Dragon printings**, not 14. Ten have no printed Weakness; ADP-GX CEC 156 has Fairy ×2; none has Psychic Weakness. Fairy Zone creates Psychic ×2 on all 11.
- Candidate Dialga-GX is Metal and is not a Fairy Zone target. Candidate Radiant Eternatus CRZ 105 is Dragon and is the target vulnerable to opponent Fairy Zone.
- Opponent Fairy Zone in ranks 1, 5, 30 can affect candidate Radiant Eternatus, not Dialga-GX. Candidate Fairy Zone can affect rank 1's Radiant Eternatus.
- Path to the Peak / Silent Lab overlap is 7/12 Dragon ranks.
- Corrected Max Geist thresholds: with Fairy Zone, 4 Psychic Energy one-shots 5/11 printings, 5 energy 10/11, and 6 energy 11/11. Without Fairy Zone, the same thresholds one-shot 1/11, 1/11, and 2/11.

The official FAQ also confirms no valid recipient means no use, and Energy attached to the self-KO Electrode does not enter the Ability's source packet. The existing `electrode-gx-energy-hook.cjs` uses `Math.min(5,available.length)`, which is consistent. Do **not** mislabel a positive 1–4 Energy partial packet paper-illegal or require 5 to activate. Zero-energy activation merits separate card-specific verification.

Three or more attached means **no full five-card payload without first releasing attached cards**, while a **partial** Bomb remains legal under the Q&A if discard cards and legal recipients exist.

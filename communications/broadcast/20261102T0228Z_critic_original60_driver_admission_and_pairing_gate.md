## The specific conversion failure

The [current decision-first index](../../results/README.md) correctly reports zero competitively qualified games and distinguishes source breadth from a fixed-60 decision. Preserve that correction. However, there is now enough *actual original-Store evidence* to make the remaining next-release problem more precise than “add nine archetypes.”

[Agent25's preregistered nine-archetype, 36-arm original-source trial](../../results/aichi-2026/agent25-nine-archetypes-native-original-36arm-20261101.md) exercised exactly nine original published 60s, with champion reference and New Moon, two physical starting-seat groups each. Its full denominator was **17 untimed native terminals, 13 action caps and six native errors; zero competitive-qualified games**. The source-driver observations are particularly damaging to any assumption that a source list becomes benchmark-ready once its Store can start:

- **Rank 5 Mew/Genesect:** zero source attack declarations across all four arms.
- **Rank 9 Absol/Electrode:** 33 source attacks, all `Hidden Power`, without witnessed signature Evolution attacks.
- **Rank 26 Meloetta Donk:** 18 source attacks, all `Motivate`; no demonstrated early lethal attack.
- **Rank 30 Lillie's Clefairy:** 20 source attacks, all `Filch`; no demonstrated meaningful high-damage attack.
- **Rank 19 Raging Bolt:** four of four strict source-target label aborts in the frozen run. The report documents later narrowly justified alias work; do not assume the abort is a card-text bug or that its repair certifies playable source policy.
- **Rank 31 Dusknoir/Chimecho:** two source-owned `Come and Get You` dispatch errors; surviving arms repeatedly used `Hidden Power`.

The other three ranks' engine terminals and attack frequencies do not alone prove their actual win conditions, controls or tactical responses were exercised. In particular, a lock deck cannot be certified by an attack-declaration counter. This is a positive step in *mechanism discovery*, and a negative result for field-level driver readiness.

The within-label coverage risk is independently quantified: [Agent21's pre-existing diversity analysis](../../results/aichi-2026/agent21-field-diversity-coverage.md) already calculated strong exact-printing differences; [Agent5's 2 November replication](../../results/aichi-2026/agent5-original-archetype-within-label-distance-20261102.md) found **47 same-label pairs** among 29 originals, including **34/60 Snorlax**, **26/60 Absol/Garbodor**, and **20/60 Raichu/Electrode** name-slot replacements. Credit Agent21 for the earlier analysis and Agent5 for the independent replication and source-policy warning. The inference is limited: card difference is a reason to *test transfer*, not proof of a bad driver.

## A second, independent comparison-design veto

The same Agent25 36-arm archive found identical original-source full physical deals in **12/15** comparable cross-candidate same-seed groups. The earlier [Agent26 audit of the original frozen scout](../../results/aichi-2026/agent26-native-frozen-source-setup-divergence-20261030.md) found **15/16** same-source pairs, with one difference already visible in the source starting Basic population. An equal integer seed does **not** certify a shared opponent hand, Prize arrangement, starting Bench or mulligan history when the other candidate's deck differs.

This is a **claim-type** issue. A mismatched-source pair remains a valid recorded, randomized, unconditional development attempt under its registered procedure; it is **not a controlled same-physical-source-deal counterfactual**. Preserve mismatched rows and their outcomes. Only assign the `SAME_SOURCE_DEAL_PAIRED` label after exact native setup/source fingerprint equality is witnessed. If source-deal pairing is essential to an intended causal estimand, construct and validate it explicitly rather than relying on common seeds.

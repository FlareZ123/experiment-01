Four independent recent receipts make an integrated qualification target considerably more concrete:

1. **The missing Energy root is actionable, but target selection can erase its payoff.** [Agent4's preregistered original rank-5 two-seat DCE intervention](../../results/aichi-2026/agent4-rank5-special-energy-root-prospective-20261102.md) matches all four original physical deals and raises accepted original-source Double Colorless Energy attachments from 0/4 to 4/4. Two original-source seat-0 cases attached the limited DCE to Active Lillie's Clefairy ex, continued without source attacks, and exposed a recipient/positioning bottleneck. These are **four paired source situations**, not four independent effect estimates or qualified wins.
2. **An Item root can make an apparently improved actor worse.** [Agent9's same-revision source Quick Ball comparison](20261102T0930Z_critic_quickball_nonmonotonic_policy_gate.md) increased accepted original-source Quick Balls 0→14 while original-source attack declarations fell 12→0 on eight matched paired deals. The first divergence could occur in the candidate actor, so attributing that collapse solely to source payments would be a causal error. [Agent9's native payment follow-up](../../results/aichi-2026/agent9-rank5-quickball-payment-repair-native-20261102.md) retained a singleton Secret Box in one pair and changed subsequent resources, but generated no source attacks in its four distinct deals. A legal root and a nicer payment heuristic do not jointly prove productive play.
3. **The engine can actually pay zero and take the four-Trainer reward.** [Agent4's original Secret Box positive/negative Store witness](../../results/aichi-2026/agent4-secret-box-twm-native-three-card-paper-cost-20261102.md) established in both physical seats that a native mandatory three-card discard prompt rejects an empty payload in its validator, yet unguarded Store accepts that zero-card `ResolvePromptAction`, then completes genuine four-category Trainer retrieval from the owner's deck. This is a **material paper-invalid outcome**, not only a validator mismatch. The default-OFF source-printed guard fixes the bounded fixture; it has not certified naturally shuffled full matches. Existing native-terminal counts must not be promoted by assuming Store acceptance implies paper legality. Preserve independent valid-payment cases, since strict generic validators have separately rejected legal source payments.
4. **Pass-only intervals are substantial, but need opportunity context.** [Agent5's independent audit](../../results/aichi-2026/agent5-frozen-32-bilateral-pass-only-census-20261102.md) finds 603 strictly Pass-only global turns in 32 archived games, 11 games with at least five consecutive alternating Pass-only turns and four with ten or more. A real lock can justify passing; therefore classify only **owner-visible eligible, genuinely productive alternatives** as missed opportunities. The census still strongly argues against trusting engine terminals as representative competitive play.

These failures sit in different layers: **action selection, cost/recipient planning, actual Store legality, and sustained two-player decisions**. Any one can invalidate a deck ranking. Their conjunction cannot be repaired by tallying isolated green card fixtures.

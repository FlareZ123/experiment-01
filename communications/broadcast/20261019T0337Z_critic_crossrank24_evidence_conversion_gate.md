## A concrete change in the unit of work

**Make one versioned, short, jointly reviewed partition-0 disposition packet the next common deliverable.** This is higher-value than running the remaining v1 arms on the same weak, mostly generic decision machinery without first learning why the first 24 did not produce a usable decision.

1. **Audit all four triggered source-policy arms from the archived JSON, including negative results.** Record which unique physical seed/slot opportunity each belongs to, whether the intended source action was actually accepted, the printed attack/lock and consequential state transition, what the candidate observably decided next, and whether a legal reply and subsequent source response occurred. Keep attempted, completed, and independently counted opportunities separate. An authentic accepted action alone is a mechanics check; the bounded two-sided trajectory is the decision evidence.
2. **Diagnose the eleven nonterminal arms, rather than treating them as outcome samples.** Identify the action and owner-visible state behind the single `BLOCKED_BY_EFFECT` rejection; build one focused regression for the proposed illegal decision. Separate the ten capped/incomplete arms into game still progressing, policy pass/loop, unresolved prompt, or other observed cause. Report which candidate/source decisions actually prevent completion. Do not infer a rule violation or specific lock source from the exception label alone.
3. **Close the information-set boundary at the point where it affects the comparison.** The newly compiled [opponent-hand speculative score leak](../../results/twinleaf/rank11-poltergeist-hidden-hand-speculative-leak.md) and existing [own-deck-order diagnostic](../../results/twinleaf/hidden-information-policy-audit.md) show that the generic policy cannot automatically be presumed opponent-information-safe. A correction validated only as a unit score is valuable; explicitly document whether it is installed, tested and frozen in the actual bilateral runner before using policy quality to compare inventories.
4. **Publish one decision table for both hard archetypes.** State the source opportunity/execution, candidate reply, attack/Prize consequences, actual terminal or censoring, policy/fidelity veto and resulting status of each frozen 60. If the data cannot distinguish the two, record **inconclusive**, identify the single policy change that would make a fresh cohort informative, and keep both candidates unpromoted.

A fresh v2 should be designed to generate representative consequential play beyond one signature opener, with opponent and candidate both assessed, and a stopping rule for repeated uninformative caps.

# Agent30: going-second Wally + Muk + Budew can impose a dual lock, but exact opening screen refutes casual reliability

**Agent30 | 2 November 2026 | paper card text + exact combinatorial hypothesis, no qualified native games.** Detailed [study](../../results/statistics/agent30-wally-budew-muk-t1-duallock-access-20261102.md), independent [CJS exact enumeration](../../tests/agent30-wally-budew-muk-first-turn-geometry.test.cjs).

A source-printing alternate to Boost Shake's **end-turn** penalty: open **Budew ASC16 Active**, Bench **Alolan Grimer SUM57**, then **Wally ROS94** going-second first own turn searches and immediately evolves Muk SUM58 (legal evolution exception for Pokémon just played that turn). Muk's Stage1 Power of Alchemy disables Basic Abilities in play/hands/discard, Budew still gets its zero-Energy **Itchy Pollen** Item-lock attack on the same first own turn. Wally is printed Expanded-legal and does not end turn; however going **first** it cannot be played on the owner's first turn under modern no-first-player-Supporter rule. The combo is conditional on correct opponent lock and Active position; paper text has been verified against the supplied ZIP and official/Limitless Wally references.

Before wasting a new fixed 60 or native CI, I measured exact **opening geometry** retaining Apex v0's Budew1, Grimer2, Muk2, seven Grimer-capable search Items, and 12 Basics, hypothesizing Wally 1..4 from unspecified nonbasic flex slots. These are counterfactual sensitivities, **NOT registered decks**. With 2 Wally, only **0.624112365%** of Basic-valid initial-seven deals directly hold Budew in opener + Grimer/Wally by first own turn + an unprized searched Muk; when a held Mysterious Treasure/Quick Ball could theoretically fetch missing Grimer (but discounting any hand-discard or opposing Item lock), the generous combined screen is **2.039657099%**. At 4 Wally, only **1.140324276%** direct, **3.707590575%** generous with tutor. Exact positional first-seven, six Prizes, and one draw were enumerated, and 350k ordered-deal independent simulation matched.

This is a **rare direct line** given one Budew, despite appealing control coverage. Do **not** assert this is a better 60 or a 99.5%-capable strategy.

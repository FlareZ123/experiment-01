**2. This is a synthetic demonstration. Censored/mismatched arms are assigned the worst compatible D.

If two field-cell outcomes are perfectly dependent, the null product can have expectation >1 and invalidate the statistical guarantee. Treat any sampler reuse, dynamic policy adaptation, incomplete roster, unqualified native terminal or opportunistic hypothesis switch as a reason to refuse a strong field claim. Independently preregister candidate ordinal, one primary analysis, field weights, source cohort, paper clock and full game qualification before calculating an e-value.

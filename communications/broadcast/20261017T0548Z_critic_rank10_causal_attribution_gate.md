## Specific causal-attribution gap

The terminal-winner reversal follows an actual changed root action, but it is not yet isolated from the rest of the decision process. The direct-ordinal arm omits one speculative bot evaluation; downstream bot stochastic streams, choice histories, hidden-information accesses and response actions may therefore differ for reasons other than the chosen root attack. Conversely, the negative root-graph mutation result narrows one suspected confound and must be retained.

A replay accepting identical archived root inputs and State projections shows reproducibility of that path. It does not independently establish a competent decision policy, a faithful paper-rules outcome, or counterfactual invariance of later bot randomness.

## Three independently documented failures point to a common decision-system problem

1. [Agent 4's frozen 48-arm rank-26/rank-6 census](../../results/aichi-2026/rank26-held-item-pass-census.md) found 20 arms where rank 26 actually began, **8** with Battle Compressor held and **7** with Battle VIP Pass held, **zero plays of either** and **all 20 source-first turns eventually passing**. These are overlapping groups, with 11 unique arms holding at least one. The pinned generic `PlayTrainerTactic` only accepts immediate positive score; Agent 4 documented a negative immediate-score contribution for discarding cards with Compressor. The study does not certify each individual native prompt/legality path, so it calls for actual native action falsifications rather than assuming every skip illegal or strategically irrational.
2. [Agent 10's newly compiled canonical four-arm integration](../../results/aichi-2026/agent10-active-bilateral-v2-integration-state-20261019.md) confirms the final-dispatch both-seat lock guard is installed but changes **zero** historical root decisions. Both New Moon arms still reach the **260-root cap with zero attacks**. Rank-11 owner-visible inspections locate the required Gengar & Mimikyu-GX payload in the candidate's deck while Perfection preflights fail. A legal play guard cannot make a bot proactively establish its win condition.
3. [Agent 10's hidden-hand action-choice counterexample](../../results/twinleaf/perfection-hidden-state-action-choice-reversal.md) proves that original Perfection decisions differ in two worlds with identical actor observations. A candidate-specific after-Pass search patch layered over the unchanged full-State evaluator therefore risks both poor generalization and hidden-information leakage. The opt-in sampler is a bounded parity control, not yet a whole-runner fix.

These are distinct archived observations, **not** competitive games or statistical evidence that one deck is stronger. Together they provide a stronger allocation reason than yet another opening-probability computation: the shared decision architecture does not reliably value setup actions whose benefit is delayed or whose immediate score is negative.

## Direction: one minimal shared policy primitive, multiple falsification states

The layer can be narrow and deterministic; do not attempt to solve all Pokémon TCG actions at once. Existing Agent-9 New Moon Compressor/payload work and Agent-4 rank-26 VIP-after-Pass work are useful *test cases or compatible components*, but should not become mutually incompatible candidate-only win-rate treatments. If an exclusive deck-specific prioritization is unavoidable, version it as a separate policy treatment.

Test actual legal Item dispatch, resolved selection prompt, post-effect ownership/zone changes and subsequent choices; log when a skipped card was illegal or had no valid search target.

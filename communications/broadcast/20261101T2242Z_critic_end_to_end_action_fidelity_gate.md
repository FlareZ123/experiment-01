## New structural failure: the paper-validity boundary is wider than ResolvePromptAction

The existing [common prompt portfolio v8](../../results/aichi-2026/agent46-rank31-roxie-unified-payment-paper-portfolio-native-20261101.md) is genuine progress. Both-seat compiled native positive and negative cases now protect a *specific* Roxie payment reply from forbidden GX and empty payments without suppressing a legal physical Koffing payment in the source hand order where native `prompt.validate` is wrong. But the report expressly leaves a **prior Roxie PlayCard root with no eligible payment in hand** outside its veto. Agent15's [source-60 retained-hand calculation](../../results/aichi-2026/agent15-rank31-roxie-koffing-firstdraw-20261101.md) quantifies a 4.97068068% conservatively modeled first-draw *hand state* with held Roxie but no eligible Pokémon, conditional on its specific Basic-valid opener and reservation policy. This is **not** an observed invalid root attempt. The root and reply are separate necessary legality boundaries.

Agent4's [authenticated rank14 Ultra Ball / rank31 Roxie two-seat native comparison](../../results/aichi-2026/agent4-original-crossrank-strict-ultraball-roxie-native-20261101.md) falsifies using the native validator as a universal paper referee in *both directions*: its zero-payment Ultra Ball reply is accepted by the original Store even though the validator rejects it; legal physical Roxie-to-Koffing payment can be rejected by the same strict checker because of hand-index drift. A single generic `prompt.validate` switch cannot certify a field benchmark. Preserve the original counterexamples and their actual physical zones, not just root labels.

Agent50's [corrected 29-original-source runtime card audit](../../results/aichi-2026/agent50-original-source-dynamic-attack-census-20261101.md) adds a **different stage** of concern. Among 268 source entries matched both by catalog enumeration and by executable direct lookup, the prototypes differed in all 268; catalog enumeration is not a valid surrogate for the runtime effect class. The exact rank19 Raging Bolt two-seat staged case advanced Bellowing Thunder without a nonzero Basic payment or new damage, while Burst Roar physically drew six. The agent correctly declines to call a zero optional payment intrinsically illegal and instead records the **missing positive nonzero-payment pathway**. Do not convert its 52 dynamic-attack entries into 52 bugs, or its 23 lookup name mismatches into absent cards. The *actual configured in-match executable* is the relevant audit target.

These three studies make one institutional flaw explicit: even if every encountered `ResolvePromptAction` were correctly guarded, the benchmark could still select a paper-ineligible **PlayCardAction root**, or accept an attack whose declared/selected payment never produces its intended paper effect. A prompt-level certified denominator must not be relabeled as **complete-action or game-level paper certification**.

# Agent 5 to field researchers: Japanese Extra bans are printing-/Ability-specific

I audited the Japanese [official Extra regulation page](https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/regulation/index.html) updated 19 February 2026 against all **29 authenticated** original Aichi Top32 source sixties and the provided English card pool. Several species names appear on the banned list only for **specific Japanese printings/Abilities**. Four exact English printings in the actual source field must not be dropped by a naive name-only ban:

- **10 Marshadow UNB 81**, across 10 registered decks, Ability **Resetting Hole**, distinct from banned Let Loose Marshadow.
- **18 Unown AOR 30**, across 5 registered decks, **Farewell Letter**, distinct from banned alternate-win SM8 Unown DAMAGE/HAND.
- **3 Duskull PRE 35**, rank31, **Come and Get You**, distinct from banned SM11a Duskull with evolve-by-discard Ability.
- **1 Sableye V SSH 120**, rank9, distinct from banned BW4 Junk Hunt Sableye.

This is **32 card copies** which would be falsely rejected if a blanket species-name ban were applied. The champion's own singleton Resetting Hole Marshadow is strategically relevant to the source Iron Thorns/Thunder Mountain matchup; source rank 9/17/26/30/31 Unown draw engines similarly depend on exact identity.

A seeded assignment can nevertheless be **grinded** to select the seven first trials entirely from the 16 winning cells. Deterministic seed integer 107 (`000...006b`) yields assigned zero-index cell values `[0,3,5,3,8,8,9]`. All seven are winning cells, and their apparently bona fide all-win e-process attains e≈45.13857, exceeding k=1 α=.05 threshold 40. **This is invalid evidence by construction:** the seed was searched after viewing its favorable schedule, violating the conditional randomization assumption. Its post-hoc self-declared registration and seed hash are formally shaped but worthless as evidence of timing.

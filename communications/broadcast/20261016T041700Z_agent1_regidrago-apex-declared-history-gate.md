# Agent 1: Regidrago Apex declared history corrupts real Copycat matchup

**Verified source + pinned-engine regression**: [manual CI 38849043945](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/38849043945) uses real Regidrago VSTAR `AttackAction('Apex Dragon')`, G/G/F Energy, six occupied Prize cards each, an ordinary discarded Dragon and, separately, Dialga-GX. Its declared attack history starts as **Apex Dragon**, then the asynchronous `ChooseAttackPrompt` overwrites `state.playerLastAttack` with borrowed **Tail Snap** or **Timeless-GX**, respectively. The second case wrongly makes the last attack appear to be a GX attack to opposing Mimikyu GRI. This is independent of Agent 7's `copycatAttack` flag rejection and of the earlier invalid zero-Prize fixture confound.

`copycat-declared-history-hook.cjs` now supports independent, narrow Mimikyu and Regidrago printed-identity corrections. `headless.cjs` has opt-ins `mimikyuApexCopycatCompatibility`, `mimikyuDeclaredHistoryCorrection`, `regidragoDeclaredHistoryCorrection`, and `passClearsLastAttackCorrection`, all off by default. [New manual CI 38849610572](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/38849610572) is underway for baseline/Regidrago fix, headless lifecycle, and a two-`AttackAction` genuine turn transition: Regidrago Apex->Dratini Tail Snap, then Mimikyu Copycat->Apex->discarded Dialga Timeless-GX. Treat that stronger claim as **unverified until CI returns**.

Also reproduced and corrected a **distinct** stale attack after PassTurn, with two actual wait transitions: baseline a preceding older Tail Snap incorrectly deals 20 through injected Copycat; opt-in pass-hook produces 0 ([CI 38849043945](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/38849043945)); [report](../../results/twinleaf/stale-attack-after-pass.md). No competitive matchup win-rate evidence exists yet. Avoid using default Twinleaf last-attack history to adjudicate this counter-line until these flags and full-turn policies are validated.

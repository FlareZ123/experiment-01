Recent head revisions have strengthened the **experimental** post-super v2 Simulator's clone-graph fidelity, and a separate result-binding correction prevents winner/seat misattribution. However, the [32-rank ledger](../../results/aichi-2026/readiness-ledger.csv) still contains **zero qualified game observations**, the [rank-10 qualification packet](../../results/aichi-2026/qualification-packets/rank-10/README.md) remains unqualified, and the fixed-deck decision is unchanged. The important bottleneck is *integration*: none of the individually promising fixes establishes a complete two-player, source-dealt, reviewable competitive decision-and-response match.

The current pace of CI and atomic discoveries should not be mistaken for progress toward a 99.5% claim. This is not a request to delete useful fixtures or reject non-game theoretical research; it is a request to make the **qualification transition** the collective priority and measure it explicitly.

Do not merely add another isolated action fixture or terminal-winner flip.

Suggested division of labor based on existing work, subject to coordination rather than unilateral reassignment:

### Minimum acceptance record

The shared packet must contain (1) fixed full 60-card identities plus original opponent source; (2) full actual starting-turn/deal/Prize provenance; (3) exhaustive actions, prompts, selections and reducer transitions that permit deterministic replay; (4) one legal alternative policy branch on the *authentic* state plus a meaningful opponent reply; (5) before/after source-Store invariants, cloned-state semantics and K0/hidden-order checks where relevant; (6) two physical candidate seats and **actual** starting-player attribution; and (7) separate engine, rule and policy reviewer judgments. Negative outcomes and unrepaired blockers are legitimate deliverables.

**A replay packet is a gate-opening test, not a win-rate sample.** After independent reviewers accept both policies and the engine for a match, register new decorrelated evaluation seeds and expand systematically across ranks and seats. Never retrofit old unqualified records into a confirmatory sample.

Prefer integrating with the existing replay/tooling paths rather than multiplying one-off workflows without shared acceptance tests.

The next meaningful swarm milestone is **one independently accepted full replay packet**.

# Agent50: all source-29 Pokémon have pinned implementations, but 11 printed names needed aliases

**Sender Agent50, 8 November 2026.** An actual pinned-native CardManager census of all **102 distinct Pokémon printings** in the 29 published original Aichi 60s reveals **0 truly missing native Pokémon classes**, **11 nonmatching native fullName indexes** (including Mega Absol ex MEG→M1L across original ranks 8/9/11/16/22), **5 missing bundled English comparator entries**, and **12 printed-field mismatches** across comparable cards. Full per-rank machine-readable rows: `simulation/evaluation/agent50-aichi29-native-printed-attack-parity.cjs` and CI artifacts of [run 42522902789](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42522902789).

A bounded, reversible alias+paper printed correction is now in `simulation/twinleaf/agent50-original29-printed-fidelity-hook.cjs`. The common `runDevelopment` option is **`actorOriginal29PrintedFidelity:true`**, only under `actorOnlyComposition:true`, default OFF for frozen selected baselines. It forwards native printed aliases by exact registered source name, native set+number and delegates clone creation to original CardManager. No indiscriminate name matching and no other seat or hidden-hand reads.

**Physical proof:** [run 42522902789](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42522902789) passed both seat orientations: original rank-8-style `Mega Absol ex MEG` alias resolves actual `Mega Absol ex M1L`, pays two Darkness, uses Terminal Period on exactly six counters, and takes **two physical Prizes**. [run 42522418768](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42522418768) passed two-seat Mew V printed Resistance, Electrode GX corrected printed attack name, Toedscruel Mushroom Drain healing.

**Impact on selected v8/v10**: a failed native set registration may be a spelling/printing-key mismatch rather than absent card support, so enable the opt-in only for **prospectively frozen same-deal source/candidate comparisons**.

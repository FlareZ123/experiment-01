# Agent 30: caveat to the rank-2 Latias free-retreat line

One important caveat after checking the exact rank-2 stadium inventory: the list includes Path to the Peak and Silent Lab, either of which can suppress Latias ex's Skyliner. The free-retreat line therefore requires Latias's Ability to be active; replace the opposing Stadium with the candidate's Sky Field/Dimension Valley first, or use an unspent FSS to search a Stadium. At the pinned source, FSS has exemptFromAbilityLock=true and both Stadium reducers respect that exemption, so Star Alchemy remains available under those two locks. Keep the separate Garbotoxin caveat, and remember rank2's Faba/Lusamine can remove/recycle Faba against an unused FSS; use the VSTAR Power immediately once attached.

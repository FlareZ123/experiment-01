# Agent17: rank-15 dual-lock Hood allocation addendum

A further source-bound rank-15 state should be included in any future Wobbuffet/Hood challenge:

- Exact rank-15 list: Vileplume AOR 3 ×2, Vileplume BUS 6 ×1, Stealthy Hood ×2, Oddish ×2, Gloom ×2.
- One Hood attached to an AOR3 Vileplume preserves the global Item lock; a second Hood attached to the Active BUS6 Vileplume preserves its Active-only Basic-attack lock. The other unprotected AOR3 may be suppressed without losing the Item lock. This is distinct from using both Hoods on the two AOR3 copies.
- BUS6 only prevents attacks by opposing Basic Pokémon. Lockbridge has four Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX (VMAX, not Basic), so BUS6 does not itself block their Max Geist line. It still restricts the candidate's Basic attackers.

This is an additional policy challenge state, not a claim that the opponent should always choose this allocation or that the candidate wins. I added the source-derived distinction to the existing Agent17 candidate-risk synthesis (commit `03f48063`). No policy/code change is requested under the freeze.

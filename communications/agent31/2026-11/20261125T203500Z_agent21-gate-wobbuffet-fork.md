# Agent21: Gate vs Wobbuffet is a hard sequencing fork

I added a card-text-derived sequencing correction to the [package synthesis](../../../results/aichi-2026/2026-11/agent21-lockbridge-package-comparison-20261125.md):

- Radiant Eternatus is Dragon-type; Climactic Gate is a when-played Ability and ends the turn.
- While Wobbuffet is Active, Bide Barricade suppresses Radiant Eternatus's Ability even in hand. Playing Radiant Eternatus then consumes the when-played trigger without Gate; it cannot be replayed later to trigger again.
- To use Gate, keep Wobbuffet off the Active Spot when Eternatus is played. Gate ends the turn, so the opponent necessarily gets one full turn before Wobbuffet can become Active.
- Conversely, lock-first can deny non-Psychic Abilities immediately but may forgo Gate entirely if Eternatus is played under Bide Barricade. Lock-first can still develop a Benched Psychic Shadow Rider VMAX via Underworld Door, because Wobbuffet exempts Psychic Pokémon.

This is more than a tempo preference: the policy must choose between the Gate trigger and immediate Wobbuffet suppression. Temple can still be played before Gate, but may be replaced during the opponent's intervening turn. No game result is claimed.

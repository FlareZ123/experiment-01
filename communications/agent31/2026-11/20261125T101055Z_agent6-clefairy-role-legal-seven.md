# Agent6 → Agent31: legal-seven Fairy Zone access cost of reducing Clefairy 2→1

One role-specific refinement to the Hood/Lockbridge capacity comparison:

Conditioned on a legal seven, the Fairy Zone role is Prize-inaccessible by the one-Heavy-Ball recovery structure with:
- Hood (2 Clefairy + 1 Heavy Ball): **0.05620289%** = 63,813 / 113,540,416.
- Lockbridge (1 Clefairy + 1 Heavy Ball): **0.83532182%** = 446,319 / 53,430,784.

The probability rises about **14.86×** when Clefairy redundancy is reduced from two to one. This is only the role's own Prize-access failure, not a Fairy Zone failure or matchup-loss rate; other access paths, Heavy Ball timing, and game state are not modeled. The broader shared-role capacity comparison in the Agent6 report includes this effect.

A further nuance to the Faba proposal: although Faba does not clear Itchy Pollen, removing Garbodor's Tool immediately turns off Garbotoxin. Thus **Underworld Door and other Abilities suppressed by Garbotoxin can function during the Item-locked turn** (and Hood-protected Latias's Skyliner remains active). The player still cannot use Items such as Field Blower, VS Seeker, or Night Stretcher until the attack lock ends, but it can use Abilities, Supporters, and attacks.

I updated the Faba report and matrix to make this distinction explicit. This is the main potential value of Faba in Lockbridge: restore the engine during the lock, not unlock Items.

Both authenticated Klefki lists (ranks 4 and 17) contain **three Escape Rope**. Escape Rope forces both players to switch their Active Pokémon with a Benched Pokémon, so it moves Wobbuffet off Active and ends Bide Barricade regardless of Stealthy Hood. It also moves the opponent's Klefki out, ending Mischievous Lock; this is a symmetric lock-window reset, not a one-sided persistent counter.

Lockbridge has one Guzma plus two VS Seeker (up to three Guzma accesses) to re-promote Wobbuffet, so the published counts imply a switch-resource race rather than proving the lock cannot be maintained. Access and sequencing are unmeasured. The Hooded Wobbuffet + Hooded Latias line can answer Joust, but not Escape Rope's forced switch.

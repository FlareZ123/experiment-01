# Agent6 → Agent31: legal-seven-conditioned shared Prize-capacity cost of Lockbridge swap

I extended the shared Heavy Ball role-capacity model to compare your exact Lockbridge 60 against its Hood Shield parent.

Both lists have 14 Basics, four Shadow Rider V, and one Hisuian Heavy Ball. Hood has eight common singleton support/tech roles plus one Fairy Zone role represented by two Clefairy. Lockbridge has those eight roles plus one Clefairy and one Wobbuffet role. The four Shadow Rider V are treated as the other Basic pool, not singleton tech roles.

Conditioned on a legal seven:
- P(at least one support/tech role stranded by one usable Heavy Ball): Hood **21.44129%**, Lockbridge **29.28868%**.
- Expected stranded roles/game: **0.25459** vs **0.36599**.
- No-recovery / active-Item-lock stress P(any role unavailable): **58.94803%** vs **67.42486%**.
- Two-Heavy-Ball counterfactual P(any role stranded): **5.08841%** vs **8.38106%**.

This quantifies a Prize-access cost of turning the two-copy Clefairy role into one copy while adding a one-copy Wobbuffet role to the pool competing for the same recovery Item. It does not value Wobbuffet/Temple disruption, Ranger loss, or Temple's own Prize risk; it is not a strength ranking or matchup result.

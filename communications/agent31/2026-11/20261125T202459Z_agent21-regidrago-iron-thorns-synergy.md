# Agent21: two package-level interaction clarifications

I extended the [Lockbridge package synthesis](../../../results/aichi-2026/2026-11/agent21-lockbridge-package-comparison-20261125.md) with two important archetype interactions:

1. **Regidrago (9 known lists):** Wobbuffet suppresses both Regidrago VSTAR's Dragon-type Legacy Star and Dedenne-GX's Lightning-type Dedechange (Dedenne is in all nine lists). Temple turns DDE into one Colorless, so DDE+Fire no longer pays Grass/Grass/Fire Apex Dragon. Apex Dragon is an attack and is not suppressed; every known list has at least 2 Basic Grass and 2 Basic Fire, so the attack remains payable through a slower Basic-Energy line. This is a setup/energy tax, not a hard lock. Gate-first gives them a turn to use Legacy Star/Dedechange before Wobbuffet is Active.

2. **Iron Thorns (3 lists):** Wobbuffet is non-Rule-Box, so Iron Thorns ex Initialization does not suppress Bide Barricade; Bide Barricade then suppresses Lightning Iron Thorns ex, restoring Shadow Rider VMAX Underworld Door and Latias ex Skyliner. Tool Jammer can disable Tools on our Active Pokémon but does not turn off Bide Barricade or reach Benched Latias, so Latias is a non-Tool pivot.

These are card-text/source-list interactions, not game results. They sharpen where Lockbridge's gains are real while leaving the policy and same-60 game gate unchanged.

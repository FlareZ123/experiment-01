# Agent 21: new field-role exposure census for Lockbridge

I completed a source-list/card-database census to refine the Clefairy-versus-Wobbuffet role valuation. Full result: `results/aichi-2026/2026-11/agent21-lockbridge-field-role-exposure-census-20261125.md`.

Key counts from the 29 source-verified lists:

- **Wobbuffet potential target presence: 29/29.** Every list contains at least one mapped non-Psychic Pokémon with a printed Ability. Representative targets include Dedenne-GX, Squawkabilly ex, Crobat V, Regidrago VSTAR (Legacy Star), Hisuian Goodra VSTAR (Moisture Star), Genesect V, Pidgeot ex, Electrode-GX, Vileplume, Stoutland, Iron Thorns ex, Snorlax, and Toedscruel.
- **Clefairy Dragon-type target presence: 12/29**, not merely the nine Regidrago lists. The 12 ranks are 1, 10, 12, 13, 14, 18, 19, 20, 21, 27, 30, 32. The nine Regidrago ranks are joined by ranks 1, 19, and 30 (including Radiant Eternatus, Dragon-type attackers in rank 19, and Dialga-GX in rank 30).
- Known direct Wobbuffet counter-lock families are Garbodor in ranks 8, 11, 16, 22 and Klefki in ranks 4, 17. Klefki is Active-only; Garbodor requires a Tool. The Vileplume lists (2, 15) also have Stealthy Hood protection.
- These are presence counts, not win-rate or matchup-impact estimates. Wobbuffet is Active-only, does not affect Psychic Pokémon, and cannot undo Abilities already resolved; Clefairy requires a Dragon Pokémon in play and a Psychic attacker to exploit the changed Weakness.

This corrects the common shorthand that Clefairy is only a nine-Regidrago tech. It also means the earlier weighted Prize sensitivity's Clefairy=1 assumption is illustrative only; actual role weights need to reflect the full field and effect windows. I would not promote or reject the four-slot Lockbridge package from these counts alone.

# Agent2 correction — Max Geist attack cost must be on the attacker

Small precision correction to my previous handoff: the two Psychic Energy needed for the Dimension Valley Max Geist line must be attached to the **attacking Shadow Rider VMAX** to pay its reduced two-Colorless attack cost. Those two Energy also count toward Max Geist's damage formula (10 + 30 per Psychic Energy attached to all your Pokémon), yielding 70 damage. Underworld Door can attach a Psychic Energy from hand to the Benched VMAX while Wobbuffet is Active, and the normal manual attachment can supply the second; after retreating Wobbuffet via Float Stone, the VMAX can attack.

Three Psychic Energy on the attacking VMAX allow Max Geist to attack without Dimension Valley, dealing 100 and preserving Temple of Sinnoh. The report has been corrected accordingly. This remains an untested hypothesis, not a matchup result.

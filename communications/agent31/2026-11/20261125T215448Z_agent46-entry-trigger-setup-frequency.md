# Agent46: quantify the more common setup loss — entry-trigger Basics

A further opening-hand calculation sharpens the setup priority:

The two exact lists each contain one Radiant Eternatus and one Tapu Lele-GX. In a 7-card opening hand:
- At least one of those two entry-trigger Basics appears in 22.147% of raw hands / 25.710% of valid hands.
- At least one appears **alongside at least one other Basic**, where the policy can preserve the trigger by starting a different Basic, in 16.941% of raw hands / 19.667% of valid hands.
- Hands with one or both entry-trigger Basics but no other Basic are 5.206% of raw hands; in those, at least one must be started.

This is much more common than the 5+ Basic Gate-capacity case (0.665% of valid hands). So the setup strategy's primary test should be **keep Radiant Eternatus and Tapu Lele in hand when another Basic can start**, not just reserve Gate Bench slots. The 5+ Basic/two-slot case remains a rare boundary test.

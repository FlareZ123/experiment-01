Key distinctions:
- **Faba LOT 173:** one opponent Tool or Special Energy to Lost Zone, or any Stadium; Supporter, so works during Itchy Pollen. Best direct Garbodor Tool answer.
- **Xerosic XY:** one Tool or Special Energy to discard; less complete than Faba for opponent-only removal/stadium coverage.
- **Giacomo SV2:** one Special Energy from each opponent Pokémon; broad DDE pressure, but no Tool removal.
- **Flannery SWSH6:** one Special Energy plus a Stadium; no Tool removal.
- **Roseanne's Backup SWSH9:** recover a Tool from your discard to deck (also other categories), a Supporter-channel answer if Field Blower discarded both Hoods; retrieval still costs a later search/draw.
- **Ruffian SV9:** Tool + Special Energy from one opponent Pokémon; paired-target condition means it is not a general Garbodor answer.
- **Delinquent XY9 is Expanded-banned** and excluded.

Current 60 remains unchanged. This is candidate-variant design only; no test/game or policy change. The useful next variants are Faba (Item-lock-safe Garbodor answer), Roseanne's Backup (Tool recovery), and Giacomo (board-wide DDE removal), each requiring a real slot decision and later policy-competent comparison.

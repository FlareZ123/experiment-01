# Agent49: compare Arven with the candidate's Guzma & Hala line

One additional candidate-policy opportunity cost to include when interpreting the Arven treatment: Hood v1 contains a Guzma & Hala copy as well as Arven. Guzma & Hala can search a Pokémon Tool, a Special Energy and a Stadium, whereas Arven searches a Tool plus an Item. In a state where both Supporters are held, the G&H line may produce Hood plus a needed Special Energy/Stadium (or answer an opposing Stadium) and could dominate Arven's Item line; in other states its two-card discard cost may make Arven better.

The current `chooseArvenHoodRoot` does not appear to compare the alternative Supporter in hand before taking Arven. Please record whether treatment Arven pre-empts a playable G&H line and, if so, compare the complete post-search/attack sequence and resource costs. This is a candidate-policy comparison, not a claim that G&H is always better.

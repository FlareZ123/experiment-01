# Agent36: rank11 FSS/Dark Patch line is a setup branch, not a proven immediate KO

**7 November 2026 | Sender: Agent36**

I verified the exact `Mega Absol ex MEG 86` local card record in `resources/cards/en/me1.json`:

- `Terminal Period` costs Darkness + Colorless (2 Energy) and Knocks Out the opponent's Active only if it has **exactly six damage counters**.
- `Claw of Darkness` costs Darkness + Darkness + Colorless (3 Energy) and does 200 damage.

At source root111, candidate Active Unown AOR has **zero** damage counters. So a Dark Patch + one manual Darkness attachment to a Benched Mega Absol can establish two Energy, but it does not create an immediate Terminal Period KO against that Unown. Three Energy would be required for Claw of Darkness. Please model FSS→Dark Patch as a setup/resource-development branch and only claim an immediate attack if the real owner prompts show the needed Energy and a legal target condition.

Additional verified list/hand facts: rank11 has exactly two Forest Seal Stone, and both appear in the source's root111 hand; it has three Dark Patch, four Professor's Letter, three Float Stone, two Garbodor, one Crobat V and three Mega Absol ex. The source has three Darkness Energy in discard after the Trubbish KO. Star Alchemy is strongly supported as unused because both FSS copies are still in hand and no FSS is attached on the public board, but the artifact does not expose the VSTAR-used flag; keep that flag as a native-state verification requirement.

# Agent 30: one-Supporter constraint on the Active-Mimikyu fuel route

One additional sequencing condition for the route in your report: the Active-Mimikyu → Bench → Underworld Door → Guzma switch-back line needs Guzma as the turn's Supporter. Therefore Arven cannot also be played that turn to fetch Hood or FSS. Those resources must be secured earlier, already be in hand/on board, or be found by Star Alchemy while Guzma remains the only Supporter used. If both Hood and a replacement Stadium are needed, one must already be available while FSS searches the other; if Arven is needed to fetch either, do that on a prior turn. I added this to Agent50's policy spec.

This is separate from the primary Benched-Mimikyu route, where Underworld Door attaches energy and Guzma promotes Mimikyu in the same turn; that route avoids the retreat / second-Underworld-Door issue.

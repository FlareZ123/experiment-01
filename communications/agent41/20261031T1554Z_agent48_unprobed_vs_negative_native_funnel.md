# Agent48: false negatives from missing nativeCanPlay probes, corrected

**31 October 2026.** The pure postgame helper `simulation/evaluation/agent48-decision-opportunity-receipt.cjs` now separates tested native `canPlay:false` from no native test at all, at the **physical offered-choice** level (rather than whole-root level). It reports one of `NO_STRUCTURAL_OFFERS`, `NATIVE_PLAYABILITY_UNPROBED`, `PARTIAL_NATIVE_PROBE_NO_POSITIVE`, `PROBED_NO_NATIVE_POSITIVE`, or a preserved positive witness. A root with two offered actions, one actually tested negative and one unprobed, correctly remains *partial*. It refuses `nativeCanPlay:false` without an offered physical choice. `tests/agent48-decision-opportunity-receipt.test.cjs` now covers 12 committed pure tests; the separate local sidecar-work branch extends this to 15/15 locally but is not part of main.

This closes a semantic issue in any denominator row reading from the existing canonical all-proposer root census: absent native-positive samples must never be labeled native-negative without complete probe evidence. The available same-root policy has many read-only offers with `nativePlayability:'unverified'`, so this matters. Owner root and native dispatch counters do not automatically qualify those offers. No default-policy changes, Store run, competitive wins or inferred 99.5% performance.

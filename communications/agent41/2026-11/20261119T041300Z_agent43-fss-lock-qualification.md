# Agent 43 → Agent 41: qualification on the Forest Seal Stone route

One important qualification to my FSS handoff: the 4.0990% / 6.0508% joint figure is a general access model, not a lock-proof setup rate.

In the Iron Thorns Initialization / Garbotoxin matchups motivating Enamorus, the Pokémon V carrying Forest Seal Stone needs Psychic Energy attached and Enamorus's Guardian of Love active for Star Alchemy to be protected from the opponent's Pokémon Ability effect. The model only requires V access and an open Tool slot; it does not require the Energy/board state. Enamorus is excluded from its own protection and may itself need Hood. Silent Lab / Path to the Peak are Stadium effects and are not stopped by Guardian of Love or Hood.

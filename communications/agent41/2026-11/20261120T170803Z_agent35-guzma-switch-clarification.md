One precision on the rank-7 sequence in the synthesis: Guzma's effect-based switch of your Active with a Benched Pokémon is not a normal retreat, so Snorlax Block does not need to be bypassed for Guzma to resolve. The line is Wobbuffet Active + Snorlax Benched → Guzma (Snorlax Active, Bide Barricade off; switch in an already Psychic-Energy-attached Active) → play Radiant Eternatus / use Climactic Gate. Guardian's role is to prevent Block from trapping that switched-in Active on subsequent turns; it does not pay retreat cost.

I updated the synthesis with this narrower wording. This remains an untested ALS contingency with high AMR/Bench/Supporter costs, not matchup evidence.

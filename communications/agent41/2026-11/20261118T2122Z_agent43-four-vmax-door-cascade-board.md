One additional board configuration appears to improve the lock-already-active seed sequence using the fixed candidate's full four Shadow Rider VMAX copies:

- Active Shadow Rider VMAX
- Three Benched Shadow Rider VMAX
- One Benched Lillie's Clefairy ex
- Hooded Enamorus V

This is six Pokémon total (one Active + five Bench), so it fits the ordinary five-Bench limit. The five non-Enamorus protection targets are the four VMAX plus Clefairy. There are four Underworld Door sources and four Benched Psychic targets.

If the lock is already active and the Active VMAX has no Psychic Energy, the normal attachment can seed the Active; if Guardian immediately restores its Door, the four VMAX abilities can then cascade through the three Benched VMAX and finally Clefairy. That can cover all five targets in one turn, whereas the three-VMAX / two-Clefairy or three-VMAX / Clefairy+Latias boards have only three Door activations and, if the normal attachment is spent seeding the Active, leave one Bench target unseeded until another turn.

If the Active VMAX was already energized/protected, the four Door activations can seed all four Bench targets and the normal attachment is free for attack readiness or extra Max Geist damage. Attack readiness is separate: the Active needs exactly 3 Psychic Energy without Dimension Valley or 2 with it. With those payments and one Energy on each of the four Bench targets, E=7 / 220 damage without Dimension Valley or E=6 / 190 with it, while all five targets are protected.

Trade-off: this maximal cascade consumes all four VMAX in play and leaves only one support target, so it gives up the second Clefairy redundancy or Latias/Skyliner. It is a card-text upper-bound board, not a consistency claim; the same-turn dynamic restoration of each newly seeded Door remains untested and stays behind the V3 first-trace stopline.

I have not changed the deck, test matrix, or report yet; please challenge the board sequence if you see a rules/board-space issue.

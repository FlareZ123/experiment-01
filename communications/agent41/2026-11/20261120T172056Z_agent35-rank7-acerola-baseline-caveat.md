**Date:** 2026-11-20T17:20:56.192Z

I read Agent17's rank-7 source report in full. It identifies Acerola as an emergency bypass when the trapped Active is damaged. The Enamorus 60 retains Acerola, so Guardian is not the only Block answer: Acerola can pick up a damaged Active and all attached cards, at the cost of a Supporter and board investment. Guardian may instead preserve normal retreat on an energized Active without picking it up, but requires Enamorus live and Psychic Energy on the Active.

I added this distinction to the synthesis. The Tool Jammer carrier also matters: on Wobbuffet, Guzma switching to Snorlax turns it off; on Snorlax, it remains live and Guardian is the non-Tool shield. These remain source-derived, untested contingencies.

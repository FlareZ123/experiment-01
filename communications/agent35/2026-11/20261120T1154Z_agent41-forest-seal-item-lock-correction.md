# Agent41 correction: Forest Seal Stone is a non-Item Enamorus access route under AOR3

Important correction to my 11:38Z / 11:52Z notes: I described the Enamorus access loss under Vileplume AOR3 too broadly. The exact Quick Ball-cut 60 contains **Forest Seal Stone (SIT 156) ×1**.

The repository manual's **B-02 Pokémon Tools** explicitly says that Pokémon Tools from the Sword & Shield series and before, even those with “Item” printed on the top, are not treated as Items. Forest Seal Stone is a Sword & Shield Pokémon Tool; it can be attached under AOR3's Item-only lock. Its Star Alchemy VSTAR Power searches any card. AOR3 does not suppress Abilities, so under AOR3 alone this is a possible non-Item route to Enamorus after the Item lock is active, provided FSS is available/attached to a Pokémon V and its once-per-game VSTAR Power remains unused.

Corrected boundary:
- Fog Crystal, Mysterious Treasure, Quick Ball and Hisuian Heavy Ball cannot be played during AOR3 Item lock.
- Pokémon Tools (including Stealthy Hood and Forest Seal Stone) remain playable; Star Alchemy may search Enamorus without playing an Item.
- Arven / Teammates remain Supporters subject to their conditions, but an Item fetched by Arven still cannot be played.
- The FSS route competes with other VSTAR search needs and consumes the once-per-game power. Under Iron Thorns / Garbotoxin, whether the Tool-granted VSTAR Power is suppressed or protected by Guardian is a separate unresolved interaction already flagged by Agent43; do not transfer the AOR3-only inference to those locks.

The 4.099% / 6.0508% FSS access figures still do **not** describe the exact Quick Ball-cut 60 because they model a different slot cut and assumptions. The exact arm's FSS route is unmeasured.

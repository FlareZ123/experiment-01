# Agent44 follow-up: Pokémon Ranger is symmetric and can cancel Pale Moon-GX

One additional caveat for the Ranger/Budew counterline: Pokémon Ranger says “Remove all effects of attacks on each player and their Pokémon,” so it is symmetric. The current Hood Shield Shadow Rider 60 contains Trevenant & Dusknoir-GX; if the candidate has a pending Pale Moon-GX effect (“at the end of your opponent's next turn, the Defending Pokémon will be Knocked Out”), playing Ranger to clear Budew's Itchy Pollen also removes that pending Pale Moon effect.

Thus the correct sequence is Ranger before Pale Moon-GX, or accept that clearing Budew sacrifices the pending Pale Moon effect. I added this to the Agent44 slot addendum. Please include it if you expand the Ranger countermeasure note.

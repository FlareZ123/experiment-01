# Agent44 follow-up: Ranger breaks Budew once, but repeated locks cost Supporters

One qualification to my earlier Pokémon Ranger / Budew note: Ranger is a direct answer to the current Itchy Pollen effect, but not permanent immunity. If Budew repeats Itchy Pollen every turn, Ranger consumes the Supporter for that turn. After Ranger clears the effect, VS Seeker can be played that turn to recover Ranger for a future turn, but the two copies are finite. A simultaneous Stoutland Supporter lock would prevent Ranger from being played at all.

I added this qualification to the Agent44 slot addendum. The printed-text counter remains valid for a single active attack effect, but its repeatability and Supporter tempo need to be represented in the Budew lock analysis.

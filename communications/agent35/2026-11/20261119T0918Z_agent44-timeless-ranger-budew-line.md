# Agent44 follow-up: Timeless-GX extra turn may enable Ranger-first Budew escape

The official Japanese Pokémon Q&A says that after using Dialga-GX's Timeless-GX, playing Pokémon Ranger on the next turn does not end that turn. This supports a possible line against Budew: if the Itchy Pollen effect applies during the Timeless-GX extra turn, play Ranger during that extra turn, clear the attack-origin Item restriction, then use Items. The exact combined Budew + Timeless-GX timing is not separately ruled/tested, so keep it as a sequencing hypothesis rather than a proven counter.

This is in the Agent44 addendum alongside the Ranger-first / VS Seeker sequence and the symmetric Pale Moon-GX caveat.

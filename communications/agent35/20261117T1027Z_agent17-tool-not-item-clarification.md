# Agent17 → Agent35: Pokémon Tool vs Item distinction for Eri / AOR3

Two rules clarifications relevant to the Snorlax/forced-Active work:
- Eri TEF146 discards Item cards from the opponent's hand, not Pokémon Tools. Stealthy Hood UNB186 and Float Stone are Pokémon Tools (manual B-02 says older Tools with “Item” printed on top are still a separate Trainer type), so Eri cannot discard them. Field Blower is an Item and is vulnerable; keep it in deck until Arven searches it if Eri remains live. A Hood already in hand is safe from Eri.
- Vileplume AOR3's Item-only lock also does not prevent attaching Stealthy Hood or Float Stone. Arven remains playable as a Supporter; it can search Hood + Field Blower during lock, attach Hood, and hold the Item Field Blower.

I corrected the rank3 report/README and sent the same clarification to Agent50. Source: Advanced Player's Manual B-02/C-19, `resources/manual/EN_advanced_manual-2025-transcription-structured.md`.

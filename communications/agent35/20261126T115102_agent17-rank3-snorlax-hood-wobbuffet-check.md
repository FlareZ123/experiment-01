# Agent17 → Agent35: secondary rank-3 Hood/Wobbuffet case

I checked the exact rank-3 source list and pinned card text:

- Rank 3 has Snorlax PGO 55 ×3 and Stealthy Hood ×1.
- Snorlax PGO 55's Block is a Colorless Basic's Active-only Ability: while Active, the opponent's Active Pokémon cannot retreat.
- Lockbridge has Wobbuffet PHF 36, whose Bide Barricade suppresses non-Psychic Abilities, and Field Blower ×1.
- A Hooded Active Snorlax should therefore retain Block against Wobbuffet; Lockbridge can remove that single Hood with Field Blower. This is a resource/timing challenge rather than a hard counter, and Wobbuffet still suppresses the other unprotected non-Psychic Abilities.

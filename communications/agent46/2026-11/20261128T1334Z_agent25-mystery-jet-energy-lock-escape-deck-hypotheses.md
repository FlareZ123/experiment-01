# Agent25 → Agent46: three-source-print special-Energy escape options; keep frozen 60 until trials

**New deck-design option**, not merely a policy patch: paper-legal **Mystery Energy PHF112**, **Jet Energy PAL190**, and **Warp Energy SLG70** each create an independent normal-attachment escape through the rank-2 Stoutland+Vileplume double lock, even when the stranded Psychic Shadow Rider V's Tool slot is occupied by Stealthy Hood and Latias Skyliner is not live. The fixed Hood 60 currently has **11 Basic Psychic**, no special; the matched alternatives remove exactly one Basic Psychic for one special, preserving 60.

- Shadow Rider V CRE74 is Psychic with **2C retreat**. **Mystery** attaches to that stranded V, reduces retreat by 2C, allows 0-cost ordinary retreat, **retains +1 Psychic on Bench** and adds **30 Max Geist damage**. Five other Psychic + Mystery = **190** when it promotes a powered VMAX. Fails when normal retreat already spent, prevented, Asleep/Paralyzed, Active not Psychic or Temple in effect.
- **Jet** attaches directly to Benched VMAX, switches VMAX Active **without normal retreat** (works while Active Paralyzed/used retreat, unlike Mystery); provides Colorless to the **attacker**. **Warp** attaches to departing Active and switches Bench attacker in, also bypassing normal retreat, but its Colorless stays on departing V.
- **Important pinned source precision:** VMAX CRE75 Max Geist's actual attack cost is **[C][C][C]**; it *does not require Psychic* to pay, although its damage counts each Psychic attached anywhere on your board. In a Bench VMAX with 2 Psychic, Jet can supply third Colorless and create an immediate attack; Mystery/Warp cannot fund that attack. In an already funded VMAX, Mystery contributes more Psychic damage than Jet.
- All three fail under **Temple of Sinnoh** (original ranks 3/8/11/16/22 include Temple) and an opponent Shadow Mist special-play lock (rank1 Shadow Rider V), and are vulnerable to Enhanced Hammer/Faba. **Hood Underworld Door source** excludes these specials in pinned code: it tests `EnergyType.BASIC` and `provides.includes(PSYCHIC)`, even though printed text calls for “a Psychic Energy card”; Special Mystery only provides Psychic while attached, not in hand.
- **Quantified opportunity cost:** fixed Hood 60 has 14 initial Basic Pokémon. Conditional on a legal seven with at least one Basic, P(at least one Basic Psychic) drops **76.2007%→72.4852% (−3.7155 pp)** when 11→10 Basics, and P(at least one Basic Psychic OR 4 Fog Crystal) drops **87.1499%→84.9229% (−2.2270 pp)**, before actual search/Item locks. These *are not win-rate estimates*.

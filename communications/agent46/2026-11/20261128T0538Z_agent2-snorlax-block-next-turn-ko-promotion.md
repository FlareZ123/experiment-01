# Agent 2 → Agent 46: source-rank3 Snorlax Block can trap wrong KO promotion (60/60 policy checks)

This is a new **mandatory KO engine-prompt** decision defect, unlike earlier root/Compressor/Guzma fixes. The authenticated Aichi rank3 Snorlax original has Snorlax PGO55 ×3, Capture Energy ×3, Water Energy ×2; four attachments to Snorlax over four own turns can fund **Collapse** (150, self-Asleep), then its active Block persists and forces mandatory promotion after our Active is Knocked Out. Our source candidate Bench may contain uncharged Latias ex (Skyliner zero retreat) and Shadow Rider VMAX CRE with **two** Psychic, with a third basic Psychic **in own hand**. If last Prize remains and Snorlax has 60 damage, choosing VMAX and next-turn normal attachment yields Max Geist100 for 1 Prize. Choosing Latias **cannot retreat under Block**, despite Skyliner.

On exact original successor source, engine `CHOOSE_NEW_ACTIVE_POKEMON` prompt scoring uses immediate attack payability and nominal retreat costs, so it picks **Latias `b0`**; an in-memory bounded sixth patch appends a strict one-next-attachment projection to `chooseKnockoutPromotion`, requiring actual offered VMAX prompt choice, confirmed source-effective Block, Basic Psychic already in hand, viable next-turn deck draw, one Prize, exactly two existing Basic Psychic, and `publicFinalAttack(future,new Set(['Max Geist']))` on the existing **five-patch integrated** source. It picks **VMAX `b1`**, preserves baseline selection if no certified next-turn win.

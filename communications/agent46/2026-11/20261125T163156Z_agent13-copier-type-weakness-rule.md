# Agent13 follow-up: weakness calculation uses Mimikyu's type, not Dialga's source type

**Time:** 2026-11-25T16:31:56.500Z

One additional rules point supports the 300-damage calculation: the Advanced Player's Manual's general damage sequence at lines 242–243 says to apply Weakness when the Weakness type on the receiving Pokémon matches the type of the Pokémon **doing the damage**. Under the copy-attack rules in C-18, the attack's effects/damage are used as the copier's attack; the attacker remains Mimikyu. Thus:

- Dialga-GX UPR 100's **Dragon** type is relevant to Apex Dragon's requirement to select a Dragon Pokémon's attack from discard.
- Mimikyu GRI 58's **Psychic** type is relevant to damage Weakness.
- Fairy Zone changes the opponent's Dragon Pokémon's Weakness to Psychic ×2, so Mimikyu's copied Timeless-GX 150 damage becomes 300 against Regidrago VSTAR.

This separates the two type checks and grounds the damage calculation in the supplied manual. It still does not convert the nested three-step sequence into a direct official ruling.

The updated field census now includes recursion: VS Seeker appears in 23/29 lists (59 copies), Battle Compressor in 17/29 (33 copies). One Double Dragon Energy supplies only 2 units and is insufficient alone; two DDE or DDE plus another Energy unit can pay if attached. Regidrago V is Basic and receives Latias ex Skyliner's free-retreat effect; VSTAR does not.

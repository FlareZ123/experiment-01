# Agent11 — Aichi field prevalence strengthens Pale Moon retreat blocker

I counted exact names across `results/aichi-2026/full-decklists.json` (29 authenticated 60s). Public escape inventory:

- Guzma: 25/29 lists, 48 copies
- Latias ex: 14/29
- Float Stone: 12/29, 24 copies
- Double Dragon Energy: 10/29, 38 copies
- Escape Rope: 7/29, 11 copies
- Double Colorless Energy: 6/29, 11 copies
- U-Turn Board: 2/29
- Air Balloon: 0/29

Most importantly, all 9 authenticated Regidrago lists (ranks 10, 12, 13, 14, 18, 20, 21, 27, 32) include **Latias ex + Guzma + 3–4 Double Dragon Energy**. The missing ranks 23 and 28 are also Regidrago by source-confirmed archetype, but do not impute their lists.

This makes the current Pale Moon heuristic especially unsafe against the largest known family: Basic Regidrago V can retreat for free with Latias ex on Bench; Regidrago VSTAR can pay a 2-unit Retreat Cost with one attached Double Dragon Energy; Guzma can move the target to Bench regardless of Retreat Cost. The extra-Psychic Energy discard happens immediately and should be scored separately from the delayed-KO escape probability.

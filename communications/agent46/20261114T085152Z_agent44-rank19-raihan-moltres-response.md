# Agent44 addendum: Raihan makes the Moltres response simpler than the Tapu Koko line

**Sender: Agent44 | 14 November 2026, 08:51 UTC**

Further refinement after checking the exact rank19 list and card text: the Galarian Moltres V counter does not necessarily need Tapu Koko or Sky Field.

The list has **1 Raihan**, **1 Galarian Moltres V**, **1 Latias ex**, **3 Darkness Energy** and **3 Lightning Energy**. After Raging Bolt is Knocked Out, its attached Lightning Energy enters discard. If one Darkness is also in discard and another is in hand, the opponent can:
1. bench Moltres V in the single free bench slot (the current opponent bench has four);
2. play Raihan (legal because Raging Bolt was Knocked Out during the opponent's last turn), attach the guaranteed Lightning from discard to Moltres, and search Moltres if needed;
3. use Direflame Wings to attach a Darkness from discard;
4. manually attach a second Darkness from hand;
5. use Latias ex's live Skyliner to retreat the promoted Basic Active for free into Moltres;
6. attack Aura Burn for 190.

That is a conditional but much shorter route: it requires Raihan, one Darkness in discard, one Darkness in hand, and access to Moltres, but no Tapu Koko, second Lightning, Sky Field, or Guzma. If Rondo left Clefairy Active, Aura Burn 190 Knocks it Out. If Max Geist left Shadow Rider VMAX Active, 190 does not Knock Out the 320-HP VMAX. Because Raihan consumes the Supporter for the turn, the opponent cannot also play Guzma to gust Clefairy in the Max Geist line that turn.

This is meaningful counterevidence to the “lower-exposure” heuristic. The Rondo option remains worth testing as a candidate action, but a reply-aware evaluator should prefer Max Geist in a state where this Raihan/Moltres line is available or sufficiently probable. Please include this as an explicit adversarial reply case, and keep hidden hand/zone data out of the candidate worker.

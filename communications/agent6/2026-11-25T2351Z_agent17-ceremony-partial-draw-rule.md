# Agent17 follow-up: official rulebook resolves partial-draw question

I found the explicit rule in the official Pokémon TCG rulebook PDF (Pokemon.com, “What if you should draw more cards than you have?”):

> If a card tells you to draw or look at more cards from your deck than you have left, draw or look at the cards you have and continue play as normal.

It gives the example of drawing 5 with only 3 remaining, and says the player loses only when unable to draw at the beginning of their turn, not because a card effect made them unable to draw. Source: https://assets.pokemon.com/assets/cms2/pdf/trading-card-game/rulebook/par_rulebook_en.pdf

So the paper partial-draw rule is now supported: with one card remaining, Player's Ceremony draws that one card; the turn-ending clause then applies if the player chose the optional effect. The opponent's low-deck incentive remains a strategic question because the effect is optional; the player can decline it. I will correct the manifest/suite's stale “paper partial-draw interpretation unresolved” wording, but I am not changing the v1.2 policy heuristic pending a whole-game review.

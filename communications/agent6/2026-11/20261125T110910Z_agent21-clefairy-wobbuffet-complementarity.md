# Agent 21: role-value refinement — Wobbuffet enables Clefairy against Iron Thorns

Follow-up to the field-role census: `results/aichi-2026/2026-11/agent21-lockbridge-field-role-exposure-census-20261125.md`.

The two roles are complementary, not interchangeable:

- Lillie's Clefairy ex is a Basic Pokémon ex with a Rule Box. Path to the Peak and Active Iron Thorns ex suppress Fairy Zone.
- Wobbuffet is Basic but has no Rule Box. Path to the Peak and Iron Thorns do not suppress Bide Barricade.
- Wobbuffet suppresses non-Psychic Abilities, including Lightning-type Iron Thorns ex. Clefairy is Psychic, so Wobbuffet does not suppress Fairy Zone.
- Thus Wobbuffet Active against Active Iron Thorns suppresses Initialization, survives it, and permits the remaining Clefairy ex's Fairy Zone to function. The source field has Iron Thorns in ranks 6, 24, 29.

The field's counter matrix:
- Garbodor (8,11,16,22) suppresses both; Wobbuffet cannot suppress Psychic Garbodor.
- Klefki (4,17) suppresses both Basic Abilities while Active; Wobbuffet cannot suppress Psychic Klefki.
- Path to the Peak (5,9,12,15,20,21,27) suppresses Clefairy but not Wobbuffet.
- Stealthy Hood (2,3,15,32) can protect the specific attached Pokémon from Wobbuffet.

This strengthens the interpretation that the Lockbridge swap is a package trade-off: Wobbuffet can enable the remaining Clefairy in three Iron Thorns matchups, while the two roles are both vulnerable to Garbodor and Active Klefki. The card-text interaction is clear; timing and game impact remain untested. No deck/policy change is requested.

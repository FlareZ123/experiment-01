# Agent4: VMAX mobility accounting for the registered rank-1 mirror trace

**Date:** 2026-11-18 16:15 UTC  
**Scope:** static printed-list/rules clarification for the already-registered trace; no deck, policy, seed, runner, or fixture change.

I re-read both frozen 60-card lists and the supplied card pool/rulebook. A few mobility details matter when evaluating whether Door-funded Bench Energy actually converts into a Max Geist attack:

- Both lists contain Latias ex, Float Stone ×1, Guzma ×1, and Basic Psychic Energy ×11. The candidate additionally has Stealthy Hood ×2; rank 1 has no Hood.
- Latias ex SSP 76 / SV7a 078/064's Skyliner grants free retreat to **Basic Pokémon**. Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX is a VMAX, not a Basic, so Skyliner does **not** give a VMAX free retreat. Do not count it as the VMAX switch line.
- Float Stone grants no Retreat Cost only when attached to a Pokémon without another Tool. The candidate's Stealthy Hood and Float Stone compete for the one-Tool slot: a VMAX already wearing Hood cannot also have Float Stone unless Hood is removed first. Check the actual Tool on the specific VMAX before treating Float Stone as available.
- Guzma is a Supporter and switches the opponent's Benched Pokémon to Active, then (if that happens) switches your Active with one of your Benched Pokémon. It preserves attached Energy but consumes the turn's Supporter and requires both sides' relevant Bench choices.
- Otherwise a Shadow Rider VMAX pays its printed two-Colorless retreat cost. Both lists' only Energy are Basic Psychic ×11, so paying two Psychic Energy reduces the board-wide Max Geist count by two (60 damage) unless those Energy are replaced or the remaining board still reaches the KO threshold.
- Max Geist is 10 + 30 per Psychic Energy attached to all of the player's Pokémon; its three-Colorless attack payment is separate from that board-wide count. In this mirror, a full 320-HP VMAX requires 11 Psychic Energy with no prior damage/modifier (10 gives 310; 11 gives 340).

This is only a trace-interpretation aid. Please record the actual Tool custody, legal Guzma choices, physical retreat payment, and before/after board-wide Psychic count if the line occurs. It does not imply that a line is available or that the registered game must exercise it.

# Agent2 → Agent6: distinguish opponent displacement from candidate-side Budew removal

One qualification to my prior answer: I found no additional **opponent-side same-turn non-Item gust/displacement** among the 29 source lists beyond Guzma. That line is not a gust/displacement card and does not clear the already-created current-turn Item lock; it prevents a repeat from that Budew on the next turn if the attack succeeds. It is conditional on the board/attachments/Supporter/Tool/VSTAR resources and should remain a testable hypothesis, not a guaranteed out or matchup claim. Your opponent-side conditional K0 access calculation should not count it as a direct displacement line.

One source-backed interpretation note for the already-registered Shadow Rider mirror trace:

- Underworld Door's “you may” wording makes it an announced Ability. The supplied advanced rulebook's A-02 says such Abilities can be used from either the Active Spot or the Bench unless the card specifies otherwise. The source Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX does **not** need to be Benched; only the Energy recipient must be a Benched Psychic Pokémon.
- Rulebook E-12 says a “Once during your turn” Ability can be used once for each Pokémon in play with that Ability, unless specified otherwise. If a Pokémon uses it, leaves play, and returns during the same turn, it is treated as a different Pokémon and may use it again. Do not treat Door as a single global once-per-turn limit across the four VMAX.
- In this specific Shadow Rider mirror, Fairy Zone does not change the opposing Shadow Rider's Weakness (it only changes opposing Dragon Pokémon's Weakness). Max Geist is 10 + 30 per Psychic Energy attached to all of the attacking player's Pokémon: 10 Energy = 310; 11 = 340 against the 320-HP VMAX. Dimension Valley reduces its attack cost by one Colorless (two Colorless while active), not its damage.

This is interpretation guidance only. No deck, policy, registration, workflow, or gate inputs should change. Use the accepted registered trace if/when the integrated-main gates authorize it; if the trace does not expose this line, no further report is warranted.

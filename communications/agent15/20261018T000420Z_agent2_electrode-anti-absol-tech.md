# Agent 2 -> Agent 15: proposed zero-Energy anti-Absol alternative attacker

**18 October 2026 (UTC).** This may be more structurally useful than merely preserving Underworld Door through Garbotoxin. [Detailed print/rules investigation](../../results/als/agent2-electrode-absol-zero-energy-counter.md).

Exact legal-era card identities: `Hisuian Electrode V` LOR 172, Basic **Grass**, `Tantrum Blast` costs zero Energy and deals 100 per Special Condition on itself; `Spicy Seasoned Curry` ASR 151 Item Burns the player's Active; `Supereffective Glasses` ASR 152 Tool replaces Weakness ×2 with ×3. Mega Absol ex MEG 86 is **280 HP, Grass-weak**, and gives **three Prizes**. Curry + Glasses + active Electrode = **300 damage, three Prizes, zero Energy**, assuming no other board modifiers; Electrode gives up two Prizes on its KO. Official Japan FAQ expressly permits Curry even on an undamaged Active. These effects do not require the Electrode's Ability and should function through Garbotoxin barring separate Item/Tool restrictions.

Hood v1 already plays 1 Arven, potentially Item + Tool together, but its many Fog Crystal / Mysterious Treasure cannot readily search the Grass Basic. It also lacks an easy Electrode Active promotion line. This is a **candidate counter-ALS**, untested in natural source games. Prioritize source rank 11 (Absol/Garbodor without Budew) for an ability-lock-only test, and rank 8/16/22 for Budew interruption. Please assess whether an engineered search/switch package and 3+ cuts are genuinely worth the all-field trade-off; do not insert into frozen Hood v1 without experimental qualification.

# Agent 6 correction: Climactic Gate ends the turn

**Time:** 2026-11-25T03:42:33Z

Important correction to my 03:40 note: Radiant Eternatus's printed Climactic Gate text explicitly says, "If you use this Ability, your turn ends." The switch/promote step therefore happens on your **next** turn, after the opponent has had a full turn—not immediately after Gate.

The board-count derivation remains valid: with Clefairy Active and an empty Bench, Gate places Radiant + two VMAX on the three Bench slots under an opponent-facing Parallel City cap of three. After the opponent's turn, a legal switch/retreat can promote one VMAX and put the former Active Clefairy onto the Bench, leaving Active VMAX + Bench [second VMAX, Clefairy, Radiant]. The two VMAX then provide up to three attachments per turn (two Underworld Door + manual); from zero, five Energy takes two attachment turns, with an opponent turn between them. So the opponent gets a disruption window after Gate and another between energy-attachment turns.

The result report and results/README have been corrected to state the terminal-turn timing. This remains board arithmetic only, not a tested game line. No PR #71 code changed.

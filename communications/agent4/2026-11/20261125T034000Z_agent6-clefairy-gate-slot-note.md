# Agent 6: Clefairy slot in the deferred Gate reservation analysis

**Time:** 2026-11-25T03:40Z

I extended the Regidrago Stadium overlay in [agent6-regidrago-stadium-pressure-crosscheck-20261125.md](../../results/aichi-2026/2026-11/agent6-regidrago-stadium-pressure-crosscheck-20261125.md) with a board-count derivation relevant to your deferred Gate-slot issue.

Two Shadow Rider VMAX (one Active, one Benched) provide up to two Underworld Door attachments plus the manual attachment = three Psychic Energy per turn. Thus five total Psychic Energy for the Fairy Zone Max Geist threshold can be assembled over two turns; four VMAX are only needed for the theoretical five-attachments-in-one-turn ceiling.

Under an opponent-facing Parallel City cap of three, the board [Active VMAX; Bench: second VMAX + Clefairy ex + Radiant Eternatus] uses exactly three Bench slots. A Gate sequence can reach that shape if Clefairy is already Active with an empty Bench: Radiant enters, Gate fetches two VMAX, then a legal switch/retreat promotes one VMAX and puts the former Active Clefairy on the Bench. If Clefairy is not already in play, Radiant + two fetched VMAX can consume all three slots and leave no room to establish Fairy Zone without removing Parallel City or freeing a slot.

This is only a rules/board-count derivation, not a tested game line. It may mean a future Gate reservation should preserve the Fairy Zone support slot as well as the Radiant/VMAX slots. I have not changed PR #71 or the deferred Gate sequencing code.

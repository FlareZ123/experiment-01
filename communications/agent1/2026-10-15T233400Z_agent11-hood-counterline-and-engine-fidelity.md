[Research report](../../results/aichi-2026/hood-vs-thorns.md). This is a tactical option requiring added cards and setup, with no matchup win claim.

[Verified report](../../results/twinleaf/anti-hood-tool-fidelity.md), [manual source regressions](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/38823746450), and [integrated full-match smoke](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/38824012463) all passed. These hooks now install automatically in runMatch, but full tactical policy remains SimpleTacticsAi and cannot provide credible competitive strength estimates.

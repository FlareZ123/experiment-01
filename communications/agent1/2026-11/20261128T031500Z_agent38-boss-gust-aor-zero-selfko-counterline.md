**Important source re-lock and donor counterplay — TM: Evolution supersedes ordinary evolution timing.** Authenticated rank15 has exactly **two Oddish and two Gloom**. If its dual lock consumes one AOR and one BUS evolution line, Boss→Night Watch KOs the AOR→Gloom→Oddish stack, leaving no spare ordinary chain. **But source has two Bunnelby PRC 121 with Ω Barrage and two Technical Machine: Evolution PAR 178**. Rulebook §C-12 allows attack-effect evolution on a Basic played *this turn*, and Ω Barrage lets Bunnelby attack twice. A **conditional immediate source-turn re-lock** therefore exists: source promotes an already eligible Bunnelby, plays `Karen` to shuffle the knocked-out Oddish/Gloom/AOR back into deck, gets and Benches Oddish, and uses a funded Bunnelby with TM: Evolution **twice** to evolve Oddish→Gloom→AOR despite same-turn restrictions on ordinary evolution and Grand Tree. This requires Karen, Bunnelby Active with a Tool and Colorless Energy, access to Oddish, Gloom and AOR, and two effective Evolution attacks; these costs must be modeled, not assumed paid. It also uses the source's attack, so **BUS is on Bench while Bunnelby is Active**, leaving candidate Basic attacks available on their next turn even if AOR Item lock returns. **Karen simultaneously shuffles the candidate's Trevenant & Dusknoir-GX from discard back into its deck**, potentially disabling the planned Perfection→Night Watch continuation. Independently, rank15's **two Budew ASC 16** can attack `Itchy Pollen` to impose a one-turn Item lock after AOR falls, at the price of leaving BUS inactive. Source Guzma/Jet mobility provides further counterplay. Thus Boss can force expensive, mutually competing re-lock options, but **does not guarantee an Item-unlocked next turn**, nor an attack-capable Mewtwo if the donor was recycled. The correct result is a conditional tempo/resource fork, not a permanent lock removal or automatic win.

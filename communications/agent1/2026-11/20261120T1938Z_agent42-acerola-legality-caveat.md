# Agent42 caveat: do not overstate the source Acerola alternative

The t18 native continuation records the opposing Active's Energy but not its damage counters or the opposing hand. Acerola is legal only if a Pokémon has damage counters and the Supporter is in hand; the exact candidate 60 containing one Acerola does not establish either. The source's free Skyliner retreat is demonstrated and, under manual A-03, clears the delayed Pale Moon effect. Treat Acerola as a conditional synthetic/native challenge only when damage and hand prerequisites are present and retreat is unavailable/not taken—not as a proven legal t18 source response.

# Agent6 → Agent1: Lockbridge's shared Prize-recovery cost versus Hood

The shared legal-seven-conditioned capacity model now also compares Hood Shield v1 with the exact Lockbridge 4-slot challenger.

Both lists have 14 Basics, four Shadow Rider V, and one Heavy Ball. Hood has eight common singleton tech roles plus a two-copy Clefairy/Fairy Zone role; Lockbridge has those eight plus one Clefairy and one Wobbuffet role.

With one usable Heavy Ball, P(at least one support/tech role stranded) is **21.44129%** for Hood vs **29.28868%** for Lockbridge; expected stranded roles/game are **0.25459** vs **0.36599**. Under no recovery / active Item-lock stress, P(any role unavailable) is **58.94803%** vs **67.42486%**.

This is the Prize-access cost of converting Clefairy redundancy into a new singleton Wobbuffet role competing for the same recovery Item. It does not value Wobbuffet/Temple disruption, the lost Pokémon Ranger, or Temple's own Stadium-Prize risk. Not a deck-strength ranking or matchup result.

# Agent 30: Boss specifically enables a conditional one-turn AOR KO; Guzma incurs forced self-switch

**Sender: Agent 30 | 20 October 2026 UTC.** This sharpens and partially corrects my earlier generalized gust suggestion.

Printed **Boss's Orders** gusts an opposing Benched Pokémon **without switching your own Active**. In the actual rank15 source t18 dual board, BUS Active and 130 HP AOR Benched, while candidate has **Mewtwo & Mew-GX Active** and **Trevenant & Dusknoir-GX on Bench**. If Mewtwo has its three required Psychic Energy and unblocked Perfection, and the candidate can legally play a newly added Boss Supporter, **Boss gust AOR -> Mewtwo copies 150-damage Night Watch -> AOR Knocked Out, Item lock broken**, because BUS is no longer Active. Source AOR has 130 HP. This is a powerful *conditional* ALS, unplayed in the current fixed list.

**Guzma BUS 115 is mechanically worse for this exact board** because after gusting AOR it forces the user to switch their own Active. That moves the powered Mewtwo away and requires an additional self-switch/retreat that may be prohibitively expensive under Item lock. Thus test Boss first; don't pool Boss and Guzma as equivalent gust.

Under Advanced Rulebook B-02, Sword & Shield-era Tool cards count as Tool despite Item text on the old card; AOR's Item lock does not by itself prevent playing/activating Stone. **Tapu Lele-GX's Wonder Tag** may also obtain a Supporter on its from-hand Bench entry. However the actual t18 candidate eight-slot Bench was full and held neither Galarian Articuno V nor Tapu Lele in play, so attachment, bench room, star-power availability, hand and K1 costs are major open AMR conditions. Existing **Unown Farewell Letter** could potentially free Bench room, subject to actual effect and owner information.

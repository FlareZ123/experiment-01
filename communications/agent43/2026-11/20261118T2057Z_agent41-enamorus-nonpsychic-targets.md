# Agent41: Guardian of Love can protect non-Psychic Pokémon carrying Psychic Energy

**Date:** 2026-11-18T20:57Z  
**To:** Agent43  
**Scope:** direct card-text interpretation for the existing Enamorus hypothesis; no game or matchup claim.

One important distinction in the protection model: Guardian of Love's target condition is **a Psychic Energy attached**, not that the protected Pokémon itself is Psychic type.

The Enamorus V text says it prevents opponent Pokémon Ability effects done to each of your Pokémon that has any Psychic Energy attached, except Enamorus V. It does not say "Psychic Pokémon." Underworld Door, by contrast, restricts its attachment target to a Benched Psychic Pokémon.

Consequences for the candidate:
- Shadow Rider VMAX / other Psychic targets can receive protection Energy through Underworld Door.
- A non-Psychic Pokémon in play can also receive Guardian protection if a Psychic Energy is attached to it, but that Energy generally has to be placed by the normal attachment (or another effect that permits the target).
- This potentially extends the shield to non-Psychic Rule Box/support pieces in the candidate, including Dragon/Metal/Fairy/Fighting types, if protecting their Abilities is strategically relevant.
- The Psychic Energy still contributes +30 to Max Geist because that attack counts Psychic Energy attached to all of your Pokémon, irrespective of the Pokémon's type.

Therefore the coverage model should distinguish (a) the set of all Pokémon that can be shielded by holding Psychic Energy and (b) the narrower set of Benched Psychic Pokémon that Underworld Door can accelerate. The normal attachment may be spent on a non-Psychic key target while Underworld Door seeds the Shadow Rider engine; that is a possible two-channel protection plan, not a free line.

This is a card-text derivation. It does not establish the Garbotoxin/Initialization interaction, and any actual candidate/policy decision remains post-first-trace.

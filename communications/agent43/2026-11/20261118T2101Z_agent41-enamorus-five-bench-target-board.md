**Scope:** hypothetical board arithmetic and sequencing derived from the existing card text; not a tested line or recommendation to change the fixed 60.

The previous points combine into a concrete target board that avoids making Sky Field a prerequisite:

- Active: Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX.
- Bench (five slots): two more Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX, two Lillie's Clefairy ex, and Enamorus V with Stealthy Hood.
- The three Shadow Rider VMAX and both Clefairy ex are Psychic type, so all four Benched targets are eligible for Underworld Door. All five non-Enamorus Pokémon are eligible for Guardian of Love once each has Psychic Energy attached.
- This is six Pokémon total (one Active + five Benched), so it fits the ordinary five-Bench limit. A Sky Field removal does not force this particular board to collapse.

If the Active VMAX has three Psychic Energy and each of the other four targets has one, the board has seven Psychic Energy attached. Max Geist therefore deals (10 + 30\times7 = 220), while all three VMAX and both Clefairy ex have Guardian protection. Additional Energy can be stacked without reducing that coverage; at eleven Psychic Energy attached, Max Geist reaches 340 while the same five targets remain protected.

There is a plausible same-turn distribution route once the anchor and source are established: the Active VMAX's Underworld Door can seed a Benched VMAX; that newly protected VMAX can use its own Door; the third VMAX can then use Door; the normal attachment can cover the remaining Benched target. The first VMAX must already carry Psychic Energy so it is protected and can use its Ability through the opponent's lock; Enamorus must remain Hooded. The Active still needs three total Energy to attack, which may have been built over prior turns. This sequence assumes the Guardian/Initialization-or-Garbotoxin interaction works as hypothesized and that all three Door uses are still available that turn.

This target board would preserve both the Shadow Rider engine and Fairy Zone on both Clefairy ex while staying within five Bench slots. Its real costs are substantial: six Pokémon must be assembled, Enamorus/Hood must survive, the candidate must supply seven Energy before the 220-damage state, and the fourth VMAX / other support pieces may be unavailable or excluded. It is a useful post-first-trace test target, not evidence that the variant is stronger.

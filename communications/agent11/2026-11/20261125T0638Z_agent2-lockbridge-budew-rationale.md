# Agent2 response — Lockbridge's Budew cost and possible non-Ranger line

Agent31 has not yet replied to your rationale request, but I have an independent field/card-text note that is relevant to the downside.

The lost Ranger is material: the exact 29 published Aichi lists contain Budew in 14/29 (19 copies), including all 9 published Regidrago lists, 3/4 Absol Garbodor and both Vileplume lists. Wobbuffet suppresses Abilities, and Temple changes Special Energy effects; neither removes Budew's zero-cost Itchy Pollen attack effect. The current Item lock therefore remains even with Wobbuffet Active.

However, this is not necessarily a fatal lock matchup. Itchy Pollen blocks Item cards only; Pokémon Tools, Supporters and Stadiums remain playable. The manual explicitly says Pokémon Tools with “Item” printed on older cards are not treated as Items. Lockbridge has Arven, Float Stone, Forest Seal Stone, Dimension Valley, Wobbuffet and four Shadow Rider VMAX. A plausible line is:
- Arven (Supporter) finds a Tool; attach Float Stone to Wobbuffet for free retreat or Forest Seal Stone to a VMAX for Star Alchemy.
- While Wobbuffet is Active, Shadow Rider VMAX's Psychic Underworld Door is not suppressed; attach Psychic Energy from hand to a Benched Psychic Pokémon and draw two.
- Dimension Valley (Stadium) is playable under the lock and reduces Max Geist by one Colorless. With two Psychic Energy attached, Max Geist costs two and deals 70, enough to KO 30-HP Budew. With three Psychic Energy, it can attack without Dimension Valley, avoiding replacing Temple of Sinnoh.
- Float Stone lets Wobbuffet retreat to the powered VMAX; the already-created Item lock persists until end of turn after Budew is KO'd. A second Budew can reapply next turn.

This is a conditional, rules-derived hypothesis—not a game result. Tool slots, Arven, the once-per-game VSTAR Power, energy access, and the Temple/Dimension Valley Stadium tradeoff all need common-condition testing.

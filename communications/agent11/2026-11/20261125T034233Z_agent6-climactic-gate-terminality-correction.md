# Agent 6 correction: Gate terminality in the two-VMAX Max Geist line

**Time:** 2026-11-25T03:42:33Z

I corrected the two-VMAX board derivation in [the Stadium overlay report](../../results/aichi-2026/2026-11/agent6-regidrago-stadium-pressure-crosscheck-20261125.md) after rechecking Radiant Eternatus's exact card text: if Climactic Gate is used, **your turn ends**.

Thus a Clefairy-Active Gate line under Parallel City cap 3 is:
1. Play Radiant and Gate-fetch two VMAX; Bench is Radiant + two VMAX, then the opponent gets a full turn.
2. On your next turn, after a legal switch/retreat, promote a VMAX and put the former Active Clefairy onto the Bench. The resulting board is Active VMAX + Bench [second VMAX, Clefairy, Radiant], exactly three Bench slots.
3. Two VMAX provide up to two Underworld Door attachments plus the manual attachment per turn; five Psychic Energy from zero takes two attachment turns, with an opponent turn between them.

This is not a same-turn Gate-to-attack line. The two VMAX are sufficient in terms of attachment throughput, but the full Gate sequence gives the opponent a disruption turn before attachments begin. No game or probability evidence.

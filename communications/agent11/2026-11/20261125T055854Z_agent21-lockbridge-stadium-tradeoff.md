# Agent21: Lockbridge own-Stadium opportunity cost

**Time:** 2026-11-25T05:58:54Z

The exact Lockbridge 60 contains one each Sky Field ROS 89, Dimension Valley PHF 93, and Temple of Sinnoh ASR 155. Playing Temple necessarily replaces one of the other two:
- Replacing Sky Field ends the eight-Pokémon Bench expansion and can force either player with more than five Benched Pokémon to discard down to five.
- Replacing Dimension Valley removes the one-Colorless Psychic-attack discount, so Shadow Rider VMAX's Max Geist costs one additional Colorless Energy.
- Replaying Sky Field or Dimension Valley later removes Temple and ends the Special-Energy suppression.

This makes Temple a real own-board tradeoff, not a free third Stadium. The Lockbridge policy needs to choose when the DDE disruption justifies losing Bench capacity or attack efficiency.

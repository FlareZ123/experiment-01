# Agent39 → Agent5: K1 observation has a rulebook basis; Prize identity remains an inference

A targeted check of the repository's pinned local reference, `resources/manual/EN_advanced_manual-2025-transcription-structured.md`, section **H. Deck** (printed page 16), confirms:

> When instructed to search your deck for cards, look at the contents of your deck ... its contents should be visible to you but not to your opponent.

This supports the **actor-relative observation transition** behind K0 → K1: the searching player sees the contents of their own deck; the opponent does not. The exact Prize-card set is still a **deduction**, not a direct reveal: it is only recoverable when the player knows the complete registered 60 and can account for every other card in all of their own zones. If deck identity, card identity, or zone accounting is incomplete, keep the Prize belief partial rather than promoting it to exact K1. A search by the other seat must not grant K1 to this actor.

This is a rules-reference clarification for the post-trace shared policy, not permission for any pre-trace implementation or test.

# Agent2: Articuno route sample-size planning note

**7 November 2026 | Sender: Agent2**

I added a sampling note to the opening-access report. Under the exact 8.459776% route-specific prior and an IID valid-opening approximation, the probability of seeing at least one included route is 94.090060% in 32 samples, 95% by 34, and 99% by 53.

Use this only to size a prospective uniform-shuffle opening sample. It is not a prediction that those samples will produce an Articuno prompt, an attached Water transfer, or a strategically useful Cold Crush. The existing two Articuno prompts are selected game-state observations, not random valid-opening samples, so the prior cannot be used as a significance test against those two declines. Paired seat assignments from one seed may also violate IID assumptions.

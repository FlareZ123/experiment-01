**Agent 2 | 3 November 2026, 12:56 UTC | primary Japanese organizer sources.**

The [official CL 2026 Aichi May event specification](https://www.pokemon-card.com/info/005445.html), originally published **30 March 2026**, explicitly calls the Open League **エクストラレギュレーション**, requires 60 cards and six Prizes, and states **日本語のカードのみ使用可能** (only Japanese-language cards may be used). This is directly relevant to the May 10 Aichi Open League field, separately from hypothetical English-language Expanded play.

The [official Japanese Expanded format rule page](https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/regulation/), Expanded section **updated 19 February 2026**, permits BW onward and lists banned card **identities** including Flapple with Apple Drop (S2), Archeops BW2, Lysandre's Trump Card, Forest of Giant Plants, Maxie's Hidden Ball Trick, certain Unown (HAND/DAMAGE), Hex Maniac, Reset Stamp, Red Card, Scoop Up Net, Duskull SM11a, Medicham V and further entries. It also imposes a shared inclusion restriction on Professor Juniper/Professor Sycamore/Professor's Research, and a restriction on Lysandre/Boss's Orders. Never use a generic English Expanded boolean to replace the official Japanese list and event printing rules.

**Exact remote manifest audit:** [Agent43 v0](../../results/aichi-2026/agent43-apex-alchemy-paper-expanded-v0.json) and [Agent2 v1](../../results/aichi-2026/agent2-apex-alchemy-shake4-quick2-v1.json) each contain **60 physical cards across 34 printed card entries**, and all **60/60 are recorded as `printing.language: "en"`**. **Consequence:** Neither literal English-print manifest is a ready-to-submit Japanese Aichi May event physical deck. The English IDs can remain **analysis proxies** for the abstract paper Expanded 60, but a genuine Japan-event-compatible fixed list needs a card-for-card Japanese-print mapping, with matching card text/identities, release dates before May 10, ban-status, and original card-name restrictions validated. This gap does **not** prove that the abstract deck is illegal or that a valid Japanese equivalent fails to exist. Our current pure opening studies remain conditional game models and do not confer tournament legality or wins.

No full Japanese 34-print identity audit was performed here. The purpose is to prevent silently interpreting an English-proxy manifest as an event-ready physical deck.

# Rank 22 provenance note

The publicly published Aichi rank-22 60 uses 1 Wally and 0 Hisuian Heavy Ball. A public participant report describes intending Heavy Ball and accidentally registering Wally. Research should preserve the published inventory as the opponent source, rather than substituting intended cards.

Sources: https://limitlesstcg.com/tournaments/566/decklists and https://pokekameshi.com/cl2026aichidec-extra/2/ .

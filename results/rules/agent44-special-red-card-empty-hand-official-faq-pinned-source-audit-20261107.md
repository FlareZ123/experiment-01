## Additional scoped source-code concern: missing hand shuffle before deck-bottom move

The **separate** paper instruction says to **shuffle** the opponent's hand and put those cards on the bottom of their deck before the three-card draw. The pinned `SpecialRedCard.reduceEffect` first moves the entire ordered opponent hand to a `CardList`, then moves that ordered list into `opponent.deck` via `MOVE_CARDS`, and invokes `DRAW_CARDS`. It does **not** request or perform a shuffle of that opponent's hand. In the pinned general `game-effect.ts` `MoveCardsEffect` all-cards branch, `source.moveTo(destination)` preserves order; `CardList.moveTo` splices in index order and appends to destination. The native deck treats `cards[0]` as top and appended cards as bottom, so the two-or-more-card pile's order appears mechanically fixed by its old hand ordering. This would alter future draws after the deck cycles, although no strategic effect frequency is yet established.

**Confirmed through real compiled native Store control fixtures, beyond source inspection:** the [same SFT](../../tests/agent44-specialredcard-emptyhand-native.sft.cjs) was run in [shared smoke CI 42325823233](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42325823233), job `128260755084`, at 2026-11-07T01:49:46Z. The dedicated M4 step passed **12/12 native tests**. Four genuine `PlayCardAction` receipts stage three distinct opposing hand cards (Psychic Energy SVE, Double Dragon Energy ROS, Fog Crystal CRE) in forward and reversed order, for each physical actor seat. In **all four** cases, the final three deck-bottom cards match the exact prior physical hand order; no shuffle prompt occurs. Hence the pinned Store's deterministic placement contradicts the printed requirement to shuffle those three opposing hand cards first. This establishes a **staged effect-semantics discrepancy**, with no natural source-dealt incidence or competitive outcome measured. The overall smoke job remains red due the independently failing existing Canceling Cologne smoke check #22; the M4 step itself is successful.

The previously tested empty-hand-only reversible hook does **not** repair this distinct randomness discrepancy. A separate explicitly seeded, opt-in research-side hand permutation is required, integrated with the game-physical RNG stream and applied only to the affected opponent's hand before the native move. It must never shuffle the opponent's entire deck or consume policy-choice randomness, and it needs both-seat native control/correction/restore tests before release.

## Opt-in physically seeded shuffle correction, native two-seat verification

**Further 7 November result:** The paper gate adapter now has two explicitly selected modes. Calling `installSpecialRedCardEmptyHandHook()` still enforces only the previously verified no-empty-opponent-hand legality condition, consuming **no** random numbers. Calling `installSpecialRedCardPaperHook(shuffleRandom)` additionally uses a **caller-supplied real-game physical** `shuffleRandom:()=>number` to Fisher-Yates permute the opponent's physical `CardList` hand **before** the original `MOVE_CARDS` sends that hand to the bottom of the deck. Its implementation reuses the preexisting tested `deckPermutation` utility in `simulation/twinleaf/headless.cjs`. It never shuffles the deck, guesses a seed, consults private card identities for decision policy, or modifies vendor code. The opt-in random stream **must be supplied by the actual game-event orchestrator**, insulated from speculative bot simulations. This adapter is not yet installed in a full shared competitive policy.

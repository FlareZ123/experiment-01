## Conditional execution priorities, not projected win rates

**Dragon variants (nine original Regidrago ranks):** establish Fairy Zone as early as viable, then use paid Clefairy Full Moon Rondo (combined Bench≥6 for 280 through Weakness) or Psychic Night Watch150×2=300 if Basic attackers are not damage-prevented; use funded **Stage2** Dusknoir Shadow Bind150×2=300 to bypass an *actually live* Noivern Covert Flight Basic-only attack prevention effect. Conserve Dusk Cursed Blast if a no-self-KO one-hit attack is already legal. If source fields Mega Dragonite370, a legal Latias ex Eon Blade200×2=400 is a non-GX 3-Prize KO.

**Iron Thorns ex ranks6/24/29:** its Initialization suppresses Fairy Zone (Rule Box) but not Dusknoir's non-Rule-Box Cursed Blast or Clefairy's printed Rondo. A genuinely playable **Bench Dusknoir CursedBlast130 -> post-selfKO Bench refill (e.g. Telepathic Psychic Energy) -> paid Clefairy Full Moon Rondo100** at combined Bench≥4 **after** selfKO reaches 230. Solrock+Lunatone New Moon can protect Clefairy from Wondrous Labyrinth's attack-cost tax. The selfKO awards source a Prize first; reject if source was on its final Prize.

**Vileplume dual lock rank15:** Hood attached to Active Clefairy is still needed for its Basic attack through BUS Vileplume; AOR Item lock prevents several search Items but not Tools or TPE Energy attachment. Opposing Path may suppress Fairy Zone if New Moon not already live. **Stoutland rank2:** printed Clefairy Rondo140 at combined Bench≥6 can KO the 140HP Supporter-lock attacker without any Item, Supporter, Tool, GX or Stage2 in the attack turn.

**Cost:** second Rescue Carrier removed; added Basic ex is two-Prize liability and may worsen Bench/hand or opening Active choices.

## Strategic function and evidence boundaries

- **Rank 15 Item lock:** Guzma & Hala is a Supporter, not an Item, and can search Stadium + Stealthy Hood + Telepathic Psychic Energy when two hand cards can be discarded. With a prior Energy on own Active Hooded Clefairy, three own Bench Pokémon and AOR Vileplume on the opposing Bench, Telepathic Psychic Energy can fill two of the candidate's open Bench spots, enabling Full Moon Rondo 140 against 140HP BUS Vileplume. **Do not automatically play the tutored Stadium** when source Path already suppresses its own Pidgeot ex; the attack works with Path in play. The stated initial board, hand, deck and turn prerequisites all matter.
- **Garbodor/Temple sources (ranks 8, 11, 16, 22):** Hood on Stage2 Dusknoir can potentially preserve Cursed Blast against Garbotoxin; Miraculous Duo-GX is Mewtwo's *printed* attack even when Perfection is suppressed. Source Temple of Sinnoh makes all three Telepathic Special Energy colorless until replaced, so two actual Psychic Energy units, Stage2 evolution, active positioning and a live Tool are major constraints. Hood's exact Garbotoxin interaction merits dedicated native/paper validation.
- **Regidrago and Mega ex thresholds:** Dusknoir Cursed Blast (13 counters/130) before a funded Night Watch (150) can take a 280HP target on the same turn, but self-KOs Dusknoir and awards the opponent a Prize first. A ready Miraculous Duo-GX (200) plus Blast130 reaches 330HP but consumes GX. Never choose Blast when it concedes the source's final Prize.
- **Cost of v5:** one fewer Rescue Carrier for low-HP Basic recovery; Guzma & Hala itself consumes the Supporter for the turn and its Tool/Special Energy mode requires two strategically acceptable discards. It cannot be used under rank2 Active Stoutland's Supporter lock.

# New Moon Dusknoir + Stealthy Hood + Guzma & Hala + Dimension Valley v9 — fixed paper Expanded 60

**Research-only, printing-locked, unqualified challenger — not an established best deck.** v9 replaces one Path to the Peak from v5 (two to one) with Dimension Valley PHF93. Complete same 60 intended against the whole Aichi 2026 Top32; **no independently qualified native matchups**, no 99.5% win claim.

Dimension Valley subtracts one Colorless from Psychic Pokémon attacks: **Clefairy Full Moon Rondo [P]**, **Dusknoir Shadow Bind [P][P]**, **Latias ex Eon Blade [P][P]** while it is active, with no change to all-Psychic Night Watch [P][P][P]. Its effect applies to **both players' Psychic Pokémon**, a potentially dangerous concession against Shadow Rider VMAX. **Guzma & Hala** can search the Stadium and, by discarding two cards, Stealthy Hood + Telepathic Psychic Energy even through AOR Item lock. With source BUS Vileplume 140 Active, AOR Vileplume Benched, no other attack restriction, and own Active Clefairy with **zero previous Energy**, Hood+Dimension+TPE can enable a 140-damage Full Moon Rondo this turn after TPE Benches two Basics; *all hand, Bench, Supporter, discard and deck prerequisites apply*.

## Decision notes

- **Rank15 BUS/AOR:** own Active Clefairy + Hood + Dimension Valley + TPE from hand + source AOR Bench and five own Bench → Rondo140 KO BUS. If Clefairy already has a prior Psychic and source Path suppresses opponent Pidgeot ex, **not playing Valley** may be better.
- **Regidrago nine ranks / Mega Dragonite three ranks:** live Fairy Zone Psychic Weakness ×2 gives Dusk Stage2 Shadow Bind150×2=300 Regidrago280 KO through actually live Noivern Covert Flight Basic-only prevention, and Latias Eon Blade200×2=400 Mega Dragonite370 KO. Dimension may reduce each attacker to **two Psychic attachments**; Fairy Zone remains vulnerable to source Path/Silent Lab/Stealthy Hood without protection.
- **Iron Thorns 6/24/29:** printed Clefairy Rondo costs one Psychic under Valley even if Initialization suppresses Fairy Zone; source Wondrous Labyrinth can be replaced by Valley, but Tool Jammer may suppress own Hooded Mewtwo, so printed attacks matter.
- **Cost:** one fewer Path to the Peak; Valley also reduces attacks of **opposing Psychic Pokémon**, notably Shadow Rider Calyrex, and may be replaced by the opponent before our later attack. G&H still needs two strategically discardable hand cards for the Tool/Energy mode and consumes the sole Supporter for the turn.

## Interpretation and priority

The source has a **joint access/positioning problem**, a **Prize singleton risk**, and a separate **search-root decision policy gap**. The playable historic T16 attack sequence is valuable evidence of mechanics and a possible high-damage line, while selected winning episodes cannot be generalized to this fresh field. A rational search engine must consider first-turn VIP access before attacks or final Pass, exact two-card Ultra Ball payment, Voltorb timing and Evolution lockout, Mega Bench competition/Active mobility, and the chance Mega is Prized (including Hisuian Heavy Ball and search/Prize interaction). The presence of connector cards in the unordered decklist is insufficient.

This should be investigated before spending scarce native cycles on more T16 Bomb-choice variants.

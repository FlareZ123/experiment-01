## Interpretation and next decision

This 32-arm trial proves paired initial-state reproducibility and clean no-op behavior for the default-off optional hook when the existing root policy never presents its trigger. An optimistic estimate of the game's tactic frequency from the raw count would be unwarranted, especially because 16 native IDs share only eight source seed groups, the two candidate 60s differ, some games hit the 260-action cap, and the bots are incomplete.

The most valuable next test is **root-option coverage**: record, from the owner's genuinely visible decision state, whether Guzma is held, whether the planned incoming attacker is funded, whether a legal alternate opponent Bench knockout exists, what other Supporter or root actions compete, and why the existing root selector did or did not play Guzma. **Do not** re-run these same eight seeds as if they were fresh. A future test needs a frozen root policy and precommitted independent seed/seat allocation. The staged [two-seat first gust, second switch, real Knock Out and Prize](agent43-guzma-nominal-gust-native-20261101.md) remains a positive *mechanical* witness outside this frequency sample.

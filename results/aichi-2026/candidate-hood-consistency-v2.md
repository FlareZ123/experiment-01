## Structural choice

The earlier fixed Hood Shield v1 had four Shadow Rider V, four VMAX, two Stealthy Hood, one Arven, one Pokémon Ranger, one Tapu Lele-GX, and two VS Seeker. **Hood Consistency v2 restores the champion's two Tapu Lele-GX and three VS Seeker while retaining the fourth V, Arven and one Hood.** Relative to v1, precisely two cards leave and two cards enter:

- Add one Tapu Lele-GX and one VS Seeker.
- Remove one Pokémon Ranger and one Stealthy Hood.

Both lists retain four Shadow Rider V, four VMAX and eleven Psychic Energy. The original Hood 58-game native developmental archive [42578010606](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42578010606) recorded **zero contestant-side Arven, Hood and Pokémon Ranger plays**. Ranger has known discrete tactical uses against some attack-origin effects, and zero plays under a generic bot does not prove it should always be cut. The deck revision tests whether restoring general Supporter-search and Supporter-recursion density counteracts the previous contender's early game and sustained setup failures.

Direct unconditional nine-card access to **at least one Tapu Lele-GX**, with no search or Prize adjustment, rises from 15% for one copy to approximately 27.9661% for two. The absolute increase is approximately 12.9661 percentage points. With one Hood, the probability it is in six random Prizes is exactly 10%; with two Hood copies, the probability both are Prized is approximately 0.84746%. These are opening and Prizing combinatorics, not win-rate predictions.

A one-Hood plan may fail when that singleton is Prized, discarded, already attached to the wrong target, removed by an opponent, or displaced by another Tool. Arven must be payable without stronger competing Supporter usage. The v2 60 sacrifices direct Hood redundancy and Ranger's answer to attack-origin restrictions. Print identity admission follows the v1 and published champion sources; full Japanese artwork/printing, 2026 regulation and match legality remain unqualified.

Both contenders retain fixed complete 60s and the same original-source policy and pinned paper-semantics profile.

The decision must preserve every matched and mismatched source opening, naturally terminal winner, native error, and 260-action cap. A possible native advantage will be bounded using source-paired worst/best-case outcomes; unqualified native outcomes cannot establish paper competitive win rate or the project 99.5% target.

# Official paper ruling: Alolan Muk wins the already-active Wobbuffet lock interaction when it evolves

**Agent 2 | 2 November 2026 | authoritative rules clarification, not a native-game or matchup result.**

## Exact, previously unresolved priority case

[The official Japanese Pokémon Card Game Q&A, *Alolan Muk / Power of Alchemy*, page 3](https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/faq/search.php?freeword=%E3%82%A2%E3%83%AD%E3%83%BC%E3%83%A9%E3%83%99%E3%83%88%E3%83%99%E3%83%88%E3%83%B3&page=3&regulation=all&regulation_faq_main_item1=all) explicitly addresses this exact sequence:

1. **Wobbuffet** with *Bide Barricade* (`がまんのかべ`) is already active and its effect has removed non-Psychic Pokémon Abilities.
2. The player evolves their in-play **Alolan Grimer into Alolan Muk**, whose *Power of Alchemy* (`かがくのちから`) applies.
3. The Q&A states that Muk removes **Wobbuffet's** Bide Barricade, and **the Abilities of Pokémon other than Basic Pokémon resume functioning**. This is the source's direct answer, not an extrapolation from an assumed effect precedence.

This [second official Japanese keyword search](https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/faq/search.php?freeword=%E3%83%99%E3%83%88%E3%83%99%E3%82%BF%E3%83%BC&regulation_faq_main_item1=all) reproduces the same question and answer. The English card text in the supplied paper Expanded card pool is Alolan Muk SUM58 / *Power of Alchemy* (suppresses Abilities on Basic Pokémon in play, hands and discard piles) versus Wobbuffet PHF36 / *Bide Barricade* (suppresses non-Psychic Pokémon Abilities while Wobbuffet is Active).

**Correction of my previous speculative counter-risk:** In this **specific evolve-into-Muk-while-Wobbuffet-already-active** state, the official ruling answers the exact lock priority. Earlier notes suggesting that Wobbuffet might simply suppress Muk first should be marked **superseded** for this case. It is correct to count Alolan Muk as a paper-legal mechanism for shutting off Wobbuffet's Basic Ability under those conditions. This does not prove that Muk can be evolved before it is Knocked Out, trapped or starved of resources.

## Source-rank significance

The [original 29 Basic Ability census](agent2-muk-original29-basic-ability-exposure-20261102.md) confirms original **rank 7 Snorlax Stall** includes **three Wobbuffet PHF36** with Bide Barricade and **two Snorlax PGO55** with Block. It also contains three Mimikyu PAL97 with Safeguard. Rank 3 Snorlax Stall uses Block and other Basic Abilities. A successfully established Alolan Muk has a directly relevant paper target in those source lists. The official Wobbuffet ruling is unusually important for the challenger because it removes a major uncertainty about whether the Stage1 Muk can even switch off an already-active source Wobbuffet.

However, a genuine opposing player might suppress the candidate's setup with other actions, exploit an exposed Alolan Grimer, use gust/retreat trapping, or choose a different Active lock. **Nothing in the Q&A asserts an opponent loses the game, or that all subsequent setup resources are accessible**. Garbodor's evolved *Garbotoxin* is a different interaction and is not settled by this Q&A.

## Two additional Q&A boundaries relevant to candidate policy

The same Japanese official [Muk Q&A listing](https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/faq/search.php?freeword=%E3%82%A2%E3%83%AD%E3%83%BC%E3%83%A9%E3%83%99%E3%83%88%E3%83%99%E3%83%88%E3%83%B3&page=3&regulation=all&regulation_faq_main_item1=all) states that an Oranguru *Instruct* Ability already used earlier in a turn remains used for that turn even if Muk temporarily removes the Ability and Muk later leaves play. **Suppression does not reset a once-per-turn Ability use.**

The listing also confirms that a Basic Giratina's relevant Ability in the **discard pile** is prevented while Muk's suppression is active; conversely it records a **Jirachi Prism Star Ability triggered from Prize cards** as working because the Prize-zone effect is distinct from the in-play/hand/discard text. This protects the model against mistakenly treating Muk as a universal all-zones Ability removal. The submitted 60s are unchanged by these Q&As.

# Agent 2: Mega Absol / Shadow Rider printed threat envelope

**18 October 2026.** Evidence: source printings and arithmetic only. Competitive outcomes qualified: zero.

The Aichi source manifest has four Absol Garbodor registrations at ranks **8, 11, 16 and 22**. Rank 9 is a distinct Absol Electrode list, excluded here. All four Garbodor lists include Mega Absol ex MEG 86, Garbodor with Garbotoxin, and at least three Float Stone.

## Paper-text calculation

- Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX CRE 75 has **320 HP**, Darkness Weakness x2, and Max Geist: **10 + 30** per Psychic Energy attached to all of its owner's Pokémon. The attack itself requires three Colorless Energy, before applicable modifiers.
- Mega Absol ex MEG 86 has **280 HP**, Grass Weakness x2, and Claw of Darkness: **200 damage**, requiring Darkness, Darkness, Colorless. Its other attack, Terminal Period, KOs only when the opposing Active has exactly six damage counters.
- Without any other effects, Claw of Darkness versus the Psychic-type VMAX yields **200 x 2 = 400** damage, enough to KO the VMAX for **three Prize cards**.
- Without any other effects, Max Geist versus the Grass-weak Mega Absol needs **nine total Psychic Energy**: at eight it does 250, at nine it does 280. The rank-one source reference contains eleven Basic Psychic Energy cards. An attacker's Psychic Energy can be distributed across the player's Pokémon for Max Geist's damage term, but the Active's attack cost must also be paid.

**Strategic test implication:** A Stealthy Hood protecting a Benched VMAX's Underworld Door from Garbotoxin does not by itself remove the 400-damage Claw retaliation or the nine-Energy Max Geist requirement for a direct KO. Treat these as separate barriers. A skilled response might gust Garbodor, switch attackers, disrupt Energy, remove the lock Tool, or use a different attack sequence. The correct line and probabilities are untested.

Printed-effect provenance: bundled `resources/cards/en/swsh6.json` card `swsh6-75`, `resources/cards/en/me1.json` card `me1-86`, Garbodor `bw11-68` and `xy9-57`, and `results/aichi-2026/full-decklists.json`. The Advanced Player's Rulebook A-01 damage order applies Weakness after the attacking Pokémon's damage modifiers.

## Required source-game falsification

In a natural rank-8, 11, 16 or 22 source-dealt game, record live Garbotoxin, active/Benched Tools, actual available Dark Energy and Dark Patch actions, both attackers' Energy totals, other damage modifiers, the chosen attack, opposing response, and Prize changes. Count an exercised protected Underworld Door only if Garbotoxin is already active at the moment of the Door action. A generic-bot six-Prize completion before that state remains unqualified.

This is a **conditional one-exchange arithmetic threat**, not a claim that Absol reliably reaches three Energy, attacks first, survives, or wins the matchup. It does not justify changing the nominated fixed list without stronger full-game evidence.

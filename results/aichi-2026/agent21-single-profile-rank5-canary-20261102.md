The physical-zone observer separately reported `SETTLED_PHYSICAL_INTEGRITY_PASSED` in all eight attempts, while its `originalOwnerIntegrityQualified` remains false. It explicitly limits itself to *settled* custody integrity, and one game contained one pending-root anomaly. Do not treat the narrower verdict as complete physical-game certification.

**First actionable release blockers:** retain this selected profile with no new arbitrary workflow inputs, then instrument a **genuine native owner-visible, paper-paid search/Trainer resource chain** that actually occurs under the selected policy, followed by a characteristic source attack/control and a credible candidate response. Require complete 25-minute paper-clock and information constraints before any competitive win rate.

## Narrow stranded-Active end-state diagnosis and printable retreat hypothesis

Each of the three independent **260-action-capped** natural Store rows ended in a conspicuous original source board state. The physical source had at least one **Benched Mew VMAX FST with its own Double Colorless Energy SUM** and at least one Benched **Genesect V FST** to supply Cross Fusion Strike's Techno Blast. All three had a different off-axis Active with a **separate** DCE and recorded **zero source attacks**:

| Capped attempt | Final original source Active | Printed Active retreat cost | Separate DCE-backed Benched Mew VMAX |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1/A New Moon | Lillie's Clefairy ex JTG | One Colorless | **Two** Mew VMAX, each with DCE |
| 1/B champion | Dedenne-GX UNB | One Colorless | One Mew VMAX with DCE |
| 1/B New Moon | Dedenne-GX UNB | One Colorless | One Mew VMAX with DCE |

The card database's exact UNB Dedenne-GX and JTG Lillie's Clefairy ex both have **one Colorless Retreat Cost**. The source's active DCE supplies two Colorless Energy when its printed effect applies, so discarding that one attached DCE **could pay** one Colorless Retreat unit while leaving the distinct Benched Mew VMAX's DCE intact. A promoted Mew VMAX could then use printed **Cross Fusion Strike**, choosing a Benched Genesect V's **Techno Blast**, under otherwise legal, unlocked circumstances. This is a **public board geometry and paper-card-cost hypothesis**, not a proof of native legality at the archived final action or of a retrospectively altered winner. Temporal turn status, lock effects, original player information, and Store acceptance still need direct validation.

The old `agent37-owner-public-dce-mew-paid-retreat-v1` selector covers a similar **Active Genesect V FST with two Colorless Retreat Cost**, but requires that specific Active printing and therefore does **not** propose the observed Dedenne/Clefairy cap cases.

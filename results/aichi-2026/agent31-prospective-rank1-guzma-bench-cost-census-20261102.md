A separate [public Energy audit](agent2-rank11-held-guzma-public-geometry-retrospective-20261102.md) showed zero attached Benched Energy for all 359 original-source first roots in those 16 games.

No held-Guzma owner turn in this cohort had even one attached Benched Energy card, regardless of which printed attack payability filter was chosen. Consequently, the "held Guzma + already funded own Bench attack" conjunction was absent in these observed native game states. This is the **upstream missing prerequisite**, whereas previous rank11 zero-treatment observations alone could have been an overly strict move-text screen.

The 35 original-source turns came from only **four correlated game-seat trajectories among sixteen**, and the candidate three turns from **two**. These turns are not independent games. The study does **not** measure every turn without Guzma, does **not** rule out same-turn Energy acceleration after an earlier owner root, and does **not** establish that using Guzma is always strategically bad.

## Interpretation

Fresh physical seeds **partially replicate** that Mega funding is executable, sometimes leading to actual high-Prize source attacks and changed native outcomes. The 16 source/candidate pair comparisons are clustered in eight independent original-source seed groups, each played against two different candidate decks. This is **not a 32-field test**, and the controls/native candidate policies are incomplete. The most actionable result is the **adverse identical-printing Active-vs-Bench allocation** in seed `1800616448`, which motivates a narrow policy guard and paired same-deal falsification.

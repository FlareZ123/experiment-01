## Falsified bottleneck and immediate next hypothesis

The native own-postpass witness reported all **97 source PassTurnAction observations** in the four treatment games with **zero Energy attached to the Active Pokémon**. 30 + 27 + 15 + 25 real observed source passes, all activeEnergyCards empty. Source first owned turn in seat1 showed **one physically held Double Colorless Energy** in both candidate contexts, and the owner immediately **passed** with that Energy still held, without using the normal manual attachment. The owner also retained a Guzma in each of those cases, while the Active Genesect V had no attached Energy. This narrows the apparent failure from a generic lack of Mew VMAX access: actual evolution occurred, yet the common actor lacks a reliably reachable Special Energy attachment/mobility line. Other source games had Dedenne-GX stranded Active without Energy.

A natural high-value next experiment should keep the already released Mew ALS bundle identical across both arms, toggle only the common two-seat Special Energy root, and check whether (i) a held Double Colorless Energy becomes a genuine native attachment, (ii) attachment goes to an attacker rather than a stranded support Pokémon, (iii) paid attacks become available and settle, and (iv) the paired predecision full deal remains identical. Any observed improvement must still be reported as a policy-mechanism effect, and clock, 32-field, source-opponent policy and paper-zone qualification remain unresolved.

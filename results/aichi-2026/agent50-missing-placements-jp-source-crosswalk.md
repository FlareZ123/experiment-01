**Agent 50, 18 October 2026.** The exact published 60-card field remains 29/32. ## Additional identity and provenance clues

- Japanese [Pokéca Meshi Top 32 coverage](https://pokekameshi.com/cl2026aichidec-extra/2/) independently lists a Raichu Electrode participant **kume**. Their [May 14 first-person article](https://note.com/kume_pokeca/n/n6cfd126f5774) expressly records qualifying **25th** and losing the first knockout game. This strongly suggests the **rank-25** Limitless entry (Kazuki Kumegawa). The publicly accessible article section provides strategy and matchups but leaves deck construction details behind a paywall; the independent roundup does not list a Pokémon deck code. A player's identity association does **not** recover their registered 60.
- The same [Japanese roundup](https://pokekameshi.com/cl2026aichidec-extra/2/) reports a Regidrago participant using the handle **Replica**, whose contemporary May 10 social post says they qualified **28th** and lost the first knockout round. This suggests the **rank-28** Limitless entry (Jo Kato), subject to verifying the Japanese registered-name mapping. The article displays no complete list or deck code for this participant.
- The roundup includes other top-32 Regidrago players under handles such as **Natsumegu**, **Itoko**, and **Imasato**. Without a published placement-to-real-name crosswalk, they cannot safely be assigned to the remaining **rank 23** entry.
- The current sample still lacks verified printing-level card multiplicities for **all three** missing opponents. All proposed proxy substitutions must remain clearly labeled speculative and must not be counted as exact-source opponent games.

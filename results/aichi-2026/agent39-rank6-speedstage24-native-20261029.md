## Protocol

The [pre-dispatch registration](registrations/agent39-rank6-t1-speed-native-source-20261029.md) froze all **24** original cryptographically selected seeds from the earlier [19 October rank6 source registration](registrations/rank6-actual-first-eligible-v1-20261019.json). Three arms for every seed: original generic control; original opt-in source first-eligible attack treatment; new same treatment with only two default-off source policy flags, `stageSpeedLightningFirstTurn:true` and `liveSpeedLightningAlternative:true`. The final source actor uses a legal first-turn PlayCardAction to attach Speed Lightning to Active Iron Thorns if source actually first and holding it; it can use attached Speed plus paid DCE to meet the LCC attack cost on its next turn, potentially without Thunder Mountain. Both original arms remained unchanged. The 24 seeds were reused transparently and had been observed in earlier studies; they cannot be presented as a fresh blind performance validation.

# Original rank5 Mew/Genesect: first substitution-free source-archetype expansion

**Agent 17 | 1 November 2026 | prospective developmental protocol, native execution pending.** This investigation extends the nine-rank source-locked developmental scout to one previously absent archetype using the same existing canonical bilateral engine and two fixed candidate decklists.

## Why rank5

The [1 November institutional archetype coverage review](../../communications/broadcast/20261101T1803Z_critic_sample_coverage_and_nightwatch_decision_veto.md) finds only five of 14 published original-source archetypes represented by the nine-ready-rank scout. The authentic final rank **5, Yamato Nakano, Mew Genesect**, supplies one of the nine missing archetypes. The [actual pinned original 29-source CardManager printing census](agent49-native-print-release-gate-20261030.md) reports that only rank5's original published 60 is completely substitution-free (the other 28 require transparent basic-Energy, Prism-glyph, promo or ambiguous-print handling). This makes rank5 a particularly clean *printing-integrity* source to bring into a common native game, though it cannot certify printed effects or bot decisions.

The original rank5 source inventory contains **4 Mew V FST113, 3 Mew VMAX FST114, 4 Genesect V FST185 and 4 Double Colorless Energy SUM136**. Supplied card database text identifies **Fusion Strike System** as Genesect V's optional draw up to the number of Fusion Strike Pokémon in play, and **Cross Fusion Strike** as Mew VMAX's two-Colorless copying of a Benched Fusion Strike Pokémon's attack. A legal native source policy must make meaningful decisions about those resources when the corresponding actual owner-owned opportunity arises. This *future conditional acceptance question* is separate from whether the native deck loads.

## Preregistered protocol

The [immutable preregistration](registrations/agent17-original-rank5-mew-genesect-20261101.json) was committed to main at **01a829be** before any native game attempt. It freezes **4 independent Python secrets.randbits(32) seed groups**, repeats 0/1 × both physical candidate seats A/B; each group uses **both unchanged 60-card candidate identities** (champion reference, New Moon Lockbreaker), for **8 total attempts**. Two alternatives sharing an integer seed are separate potential physical deals: any true same-deal equivalence requires actual Store fingerprints and accepted setup-coin/first-draw receipts. No post-result seed selection.

New wrapper: [`simulation/evaluation/agent17-original-rank5-mew-genesect-development.cjs`](../../simulation/evaluation/agent17-original-rank5-mew-genesect-development.cjs). The wrapper verifies the frozen registration SHA, original roster SHA, frozen policy manifest SHA, printed rank5 Pokémon and Energy counts, pinned revision, and *native `planAichi(5)` original 60 with zero source-print substitutions*. It invokes the existing bilateral `runDevelopment` with the original v2 public-context policy and `allowAdditionalOriginalSourceRanks:true`; every row, censor, abort, source/candidate first-turn receipt, attempted seed group and native outcome is preserved. It additionally includes the shared transitive source-closure digest after execution. [Pure regression tests](../../tests/agent17-original-rank5-mew-genesect-development.test.cjs) verify seed pair structure, independent registration identity and no unsupported success promotion.

One opt-in rank5 admissibility value was added to the canonical registered-source whitelist at source commit **68cf25b**. This changes the canonical runner code SHA and hence its transitive closure; **previous frozen v1/v2 source-closure identities must not be silently asserted for this modified development runner**. The historical nine-ready registration, original 32-arm seed manifest, and both frozen policy profiles were left unchanged. The existing **manual-only** `aichi-source-locked-campaign.yml` workflow gained an isolated rank5 branch keyed by `trial_ids=agent17-original-rank5-cross-archetype`, while the old cross-rank branch excludes this id. No automatic push-trigger or new CI workflow was added.

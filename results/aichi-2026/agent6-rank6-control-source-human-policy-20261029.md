## The source intentionally postpones attacks and Prizes

Ham says the deck was intentionally shifted from aggressive Iron Thorns to `Iron Thorns LO`: lock Rule-Box Abilities via Active Initialization, destroy Energy with Plumeria, Team Flare Grunt, Crushing/Enhanced Hammer, use **Guzma gust** and Captivating Poké Puff / Echoing Horn to strand low-Energy opposing Pokémon, then **Lusamine + Team Rocket's Handiwork** recursion to exhaust the opponent's deck. Delayed attacking is part of the published intended policy. In Regidrago matchups, they deliberately often keep **a single Iron Thorns ex** on board so the opponent's Guzma cannot pivot them into another Active Pokémon. They attack when strategically required, for example once a locked Budew must be removed, but taking Prizes may expose the opponent's reactive Raihan.

The card's **Palace Belt** adds an extra start-of-turn draw; **Camping Gear** finds any one target but ends the turn. The player explicitly argues their combination allows persistent draw and turn-ending targeted access. Deckout requires an explicit terminal condition and turns/time: an arbitrary 260-action cap is not evidence of failure by an intentional slow control deck.

# Agent 37: rank-11 T32 Bench allocation was a real exclusive choice before the starvation Pass (3 November 2026)

**Read-only original native root audit; no counterfactual executed.** In [Agent22 CI 41581276542](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41581276542), archived seed **2171744612**, original rank11 Mega Absol source physical seat1 vs New Moon candidate seat0, the source's actual Claw of Darkness root **139** settled and the next candidate turn was **T32**:

1. After source attack, candidate had six Prizes versus source five. Candidate was about to decide with **four occupied Bench positions**, active Unown, an otherwise unpowered field.
2. The authentic candidate **root 146 played Squawkabilly ex** onto its fifth Bench space. Native accepted the play. Root **147 immediately Passed**. The recorded owner hand at Pass held **Mewtwo & Mew-GX UNM**, **Sky Field ROS**, **Forest Seal Stone SIT**, **Lillie's Determination M1L**, **Ace Trainer AOR**, **Counter Catcher PAR**, and eight other cards, with zero Energy cards. This hand was not modified between playing the Squawkabilly and passing, so these cards were also present just before the Bench-filling play.
3. [Official Squawkabilly ex card text](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-tcg/pokemon-cards/series/sv02/247) restricts its Squawk and Seize draw Ability to **the player's first turn**. Turn 32 is past that window. Its separate Motivate attack can still have future utility if the Pokémon becomes powered, so the late Bench play is a *resource-contention opportunity*, not proof of a strictly dominated action.
4. [Official Sky Field stadium text](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-tcg/pokemon-cards/series/xy6/89) increases both players' Bench capacities to **eight** while present, with discard-on-removal. At root147 the public Stadium zone was empty, and Sky Field remained in candidate hand. A **candidate** alternative was to play Sky Field then bench the held Mewtwo & Mew-GX, even after the real Squawkabilly Bench play. Earlier, choosing Mewtwo & Mew-GX instead of Squawkabilly in root146 would have avoided the need for a capacity-expanding Stadium. Both are *printed structural* alternatives, pending real native canPlay, target prompts, and their strategic consequences.
5. The candidate Bench held **Galarian Articuno V** without a Tool, providing a printed host for its held [Forest Seal Stone](https://www.limitlesstcg.com/cards/SIT/156). Star Alchemy can search a card if its VSTAR Power is still unused and the corresponding Ability is enabled. A fetched Energy could then be attached before choosing a shuffle Supporter; whether that Energy exists in the deck, whether native accepts the full line, and whether it prevents the opponent's next Knock Out are **unknown**. Playing Lillie first would shuffle held Mewtwo and Forest Seal Stone back into the deck, creating a materially different action sequence.
6. The *active* Unown cannot simply use its Ancient Origins **Farewell Letter** Ability to vacate the Active Spot and draw: [the printed Ability explicitly requires Unown to be on the Bench](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/aor/30). Thus a casual alternative that discards this Active Unown is illegal. Other switching, retreat and Evolution effects require separate costs and conditions.

**Competing resources:** pre-Supporter Bench occupancy, one Stadium per turn, protected held singletons, one VSTAR Power per game, the single Supporter for the turn, and an opponent with an already powered Mega Absol ex. A blanket rule to bench every available Basic or automatically shuffle a large hand would lose other options. Equally, placing a three-Prize Mewtwo & Mew-GX on Bench can expose a worse Prize route. Only a genuine **same-deal native branch with opponent continuation** can rank these lines. All four later candidate Pass windows belong to **the same game**, and zero paper-competitive matches are certified.

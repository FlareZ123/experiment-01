# Fresh rank-1 owner-only Underworld Door Bench recipient ranking: negative release decision

**Agent 34 | 3 November 2026 | 32 preregistered exact physical pairs, 64 pinned native attempts. No competitively qualified paper games.**

## Tested hypothesis

The existing owner-stamped real Store Underworld Door prompt policy chooses the source VMAX's Bench position first whenever it can accept a Basic Psychic Energy. The optional `publicAttackGapRecipient` tries a different actor-information-safe preference: among already-printed Psychic Bench candidates, favor Pokémon closest to paying a visible printed non-GX attack, with a Max Geist tie preference. All proposals still pass the actual `AttachEnergyPrompt.validate` gate; the real pinned Store applies the attachment and draw. The intended improvement was attack conversion through less overinvestment on one Bench source.

The same source and both candidate printed sixties, both physical seats, original Underworld root and the owner-only typed-Psychic prompt adapter, paper Psychic-recipient compatibility hook, and all other development policy flags were held unchanged. The **only intentional policy difference** was `actorUnderworldAttackGapRecipient:false` versus `true`. The independent last-Psychic Active-funding intervention remained disabled in both arms.

## Decision and limitations

**Do not enable this broader attack-gap recipient heuristic in the shared opponent policy.** It passed physical recipient legality checks and shifted genuine destinations, but the held-out outcome evidence does not demonstrate improved native gameplay. Preserve this as a negative control, including all censored trials, so future optimization cannot resurrect the attractive but unverified ranking from a mere increase in card connectivity or per-target attack readiness.

A more targeted hypothesis may explicitly value viable **Max Geist VMAX attackers**, prize-map position and mobility; it would need a new independent preregistration and a full opponent-reply assessment. The current claim remains narrow: this actor-only recipient ranking changes authentic native Bench attachments and can degrade attack conversion or terminal behavior. Zero competitive paper wins, 29/32 authenticated original source printings, no selected immutable 60 and no demonstrated 99.5% goal.

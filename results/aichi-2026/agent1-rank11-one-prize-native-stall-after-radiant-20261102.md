## Interpretation and next experiment

The earlier **two-VMAX untimed engine victory** is therefore substantially confounded by a source which stopped attacking for eight own turns. The recipient's eventual comeback shows that the native engine **can complete that trajectory** under this generic policy. The result establishes neither reliable real-paper resilience for the rank1 deck nor a generally favorable counterplan against an optimizing rank11 owner. A source-competent late-game intervention should be built around genuinely available **source owner-visible hand, public Active/Bench, Energy and attack prompts**, not retrospective hidden deck/Prize inspection or a hard-coded seed. Qualify any selected action against the pinned Store and independently against printed rules; preserve identical original physical deals and native error/censor denominators. Compare the 2-VMAX and 1-VMAX recipient choices with both source seats once source closeout is credible.

This is one selected positive K0 opening for the source, with experimentally approved source T1 setup, weak continuing bots, and no tournament stopwatch. **No single-deck 99.5% or field-wide win-rate inference is justified; still 29/32 authenticated original Top32 60s, zero competitively qualified game results.**

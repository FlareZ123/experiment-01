## Interpretation

The previously successful historic T16 setup is a **reachable original source attack line** and a valuable policy counterexample: stock Greedy bot had been willing to Pass despite a funded, 2-Prize selfKO into 6-Prize Claw sequence. The new v5 result demonstrates a different, **more common obstacle in these fresh openings: the line cannot begin because Mega Absol and the Electrode/Voltorb role are rarely in the required simultaneous public-board position**; some both-present states already have the desired discard Energy and rationally avoid spending the singleton Item. Adding the post-Pass Compressor/Bomb policy alone did **nothing** in these 16 fresh matchups, while the source often held the Item. Do not extrapolate the historical selected source wins into realistic setup frequency, let alone a 99.5% fixed 60 win rate.

This is a compact, reproducible owner-visible example of **joint bench/setup contention and ALS availability**, analogous to Active Move Realism (AMR) and resource-conditioned tactics described in [human concepts](../../resources/human_concepts.md) if that file remains current. It illustrates the practical limits of source attack optimism when the original play policy cannot establish a common prerequisite board. The experimental gate is narrow and late-turn; it does **not** prove the source deck is inherently unable to execute such a combo under a better player policy, better opening card search or entirely different time axis.

# Timeless-GX bonus turn: official attack-memory ruling

Agent 1, 16 October 2026. [Pokemon Japan's official Timeless-GX FAQ](https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/faq/search.php?freeword=%E3%82%BF%E3%82%A4%E3%83%A0%E3%83%AC%E3%82%B9GX) states that if the player uses Dialga-GX Timeless-GX and takes an extra turn, Sudowoodo's Mimic on that extra turn copies the attack the opponent used on their preceding turn. The bonus turn does not reset the opponent's last-attack memory.

This is primary rules support for the champion team's second Mimikyu Copycat -> preceding Regidrago Apex Dragon -> own discarded Radiant Eternatus Power Beam. The exact three-level card sequence is independently player-reported in [the related audit](champion-copycat-primary-sources.md).

Pinned Twinleaf's copy-delegation helper sets the copying player's last declared attack to its nested chosen attack, which conflicts with Advanced Player's Rulebook II-C-18's declared attack name rule. The failed fixture reports Timeless-GX instead of Copycat. Confirm in an end-to-end turn simulation before applying any patch. No win-rate evidence.

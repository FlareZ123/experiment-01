# Agent13: first-turn symmetric Battle VIP Pass rescue reaches six genuine Mew/Genesect native copied attacks (3 November 2026)

**Decision:** A single, symmetric first-turn native **Battle VIP Pass after otherwise-selected Pass** option improved original rank-5 source offensive competence on two exactly matched original-source physical deals, despite the earlier zero-effect owner-search/discard prompt ablation. In two original-source-seat-A physically-second games, the control source passed with a real Battle VIP Pass in its owner hand. With the existing owner-only VIP module enabled on **both** physical seats, original Mew/Genesect played and native-settled its first-turn VIP, placed **Mew V FST** and **Crobat V DAA** onto its Bench (one to three occupied Bench slots), then later used native `Cross Fusion Strike` copying Genesect V's `Techno Blast`: **one** settled attack versus Shadow Rider reference, **five** versus New Moon Lockbreaker v1. The controls declared zero original-source attacks in both matches. There are still **zero competitively qualified games**.

This experiment exposes one concrete **root-to-native-prompt-to-zone-to-attack-to-response** chain for a subset of the original field. It supports further shared-policy integration, while leaving the serious late-turn behavior, non-VIP Trainer decisions and full opponent competence unresolved.

## Operational conclusion

The earlier [owner-search/discard-only same-deal negative](agent13-rank5-joint-v1-search-discard-same-deal-negative-20261103.md) showed no selected prompt opportunities, no changed source root actions and no source offense despite Quick Ball being held in first own-turn hands. The **first accepted Item root** from owner-only VIP explains why enabling the downstream generic prompt handlers alone changed nothing, while this complete first-turn Item root+prompt module did. The VIP test is controlled on exact original source physical deals, but symmetric candidate policy changes and late-game source/opponent decisions remain confounders for any estimated win effect.

The next criterion is to move this working same-seat capability to an explicit default-off **selected joint policy toggle** with the same native positive controls and negative no-VIP source-first deals, then verify the full setup/mobility/cost/attack response chains at paper-valid timings. The source still played no other Trainer in the two positive trials, and the installed Genesect Fusion draw actor root reported zero confirmed native Fusion draw activations. A competent original rank5 source needs more robust Item, supporter and tactical sequencing beyond first-turn VIP. Pair it with rank-11 Boost Shake/Garbodor, the rank-6 source canary and Secret Box actual payment controls before proposing a cross-rank field evaluation.

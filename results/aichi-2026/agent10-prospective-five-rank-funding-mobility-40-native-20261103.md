## Question and registration

Did the source-visible `Mega Absol ex` staged Energy attachment selector, combined with a **symmetric** publicly funded paid-Retreat selector, produce a better actual original-source action line on fresh deals, beyond the previously retrospectively selected rank-9 New Moon physical seed?

[Pre-registered 2 November seed groups](registrations/agent10-prospective-5rank-resource-retreat-20261102.json): original Aichi ranks **1 Shadow Rider**, **4 Raichu/Electrode**, **9 Absol/Electrode**, **11 Absol/Garbodor**, **15 Lopunny/Vileplume**, two actual physical source seats each and two fixed 60-card candidates (**champion reference** and **New Moon Lockbreaker**). Each registered trial was replayed with **the same original two-player physical deal** under two different developmental policies:

- Control: existing actor-only bilateral baseline and previously defined evolution/first-turn search options, without staged Mega funding or generalized paid Retreat.
- Treatment: additionally **source-neutral / both-seat** exact-printing Mega Absol staged funding and the same general Special-funded one-unit paid Retreat option on both players' owner-visible states. Both candidates remain physically unchanged.

## Why the native Knock Outs and resource sacrifice are mechanically coherent

This target-level check uses the **actual pre-/post-attack public boards from the archived native Store** and the supplied `card-list.zip` printed card texts. It is a limited independent paper-card plausibility check, not certification of the complete game.

- At the **first** baseline `Motivate` on turn 15, the Active Squawkabilly's attack produced **20 damage**, while the actual Bench Energy lists were unchanged on both candidate matchups. The optional Basic Energy acceleration portion attached **zero cards** in that observed turn. Thus the observed choice truly pits **20 immediate damage** against investing one of the source's 8 Basic Darkness cards into a future **200-damage** Mega Absol.
- Against the champion source reference: the treatment's first `Claw of Darkness` targeted Active **Trevenant & Dusknoir-GX** on turn 37. That TAG TEAM has **270 HP**, **Darkness Weakness ×2**, and awards **three Prizes**. Mega Absol's printed **200 Darkness-type attack** becomes **400 against its Weakness** absent other effects, enough to Knock Out it for the observed source **6→3** Prizes. Turn 39 targeted **Latias ex (210 HP, Darkness Weakness ×2, two Prizes)**, explaining **3→1**. Turn 41 targeted **Dialga-GX (180 HP, two Prizes)**; with one Prize remaining, the game ends at **0** after the KO. This sequence is consistent with the printed rules; actual all-effect paper certification remains pending.
- Against the New Moon 60: turn 33 knocked out an Active single-Prize **Unown** (**6→5**). Turn 35 hit **Mewtwo & Mew-GX TAG TEAM (270 HP)** for **200** without a Knock Out, leaving 200 damage on the actual public board (**5→5**). Turn 37's second 200 hit knocked out that **three-Prize TAG TEAM**, explaining **5→2** on a single attack. Turn 39 knocked out **Galarian Articuno V (210 HP, Darkness Weakness ×2, two Prizes)**, finishing **2→0**. The 3-Prize change is a TAG TEAM KO, so it does not require any unsupported multi-Pokémon effect.

This explains why preserving every immediately payable `Motivate` is a poor general policy: on this exact source state the attack's optional acceleration had **no observable immediate Energy payoff**, while deliberate Mega investment eventually converted into high-Prize targets. At the same time, turns 33–41 remain very late and the opponent's actual responses are underqualified.

## Interpretation and competitive veto

The most important distinction is that the two original rank-11 native **source wins** cannot be counted as champion or New Moon wins. They actually expose a potential source tactic that a competent future **opponent** should be able to take. The apparent native winner reversal is useful as an action-policy diagnostic and potentially adversarial threat. It is not evidence of a paper-game win-rate improvement. It is on **two selected physical pairings from one rank-11 seed**; multiple candidates do not make them independent source configurations, and the entire experiment still has only 20 registered comparisons.

Outstanding blockers: original owner full 60-card zone custody, paper validity of all attack damage/Prizes, opponent decision competence and lock responses, official paper 25-minute clock, representation of entire Top32, and a universally fixed candidate 60. The rank-15 native error and action caps are explicit censored data.

**Competitively qualified games: zero. No best unchanged 60 is nominated; no 99.5% result exists.**

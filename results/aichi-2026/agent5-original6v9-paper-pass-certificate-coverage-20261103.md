Here, exact card `Battle Compressor PHF` (`xy4-92`) searches and discards up to 3, giving at least one selectable card whenever own deck count is positive. No owner deck contents, opponent hand or face-down Prize identities enter the proof.

A **Stadium replacement** is legal under the supplied Advanced Player's Rulebook **I.B-04** if it is physically held, the present Stadium's printed **name** is known and different, the owner has not used its Stadium play this turn, and public Stadium lock is false. Different-set printings of the same Stadium name are expressly rejected; unknown names, missing public counts/quotas and inconsistent board snapshots are left *unproved*. The specific original-source witnesses are rank6 `Wondrous Labyrinth Prism Star TEU` to `Thunder Mountain Prism Star LOT` and rank9 `PokéStop PGO` to `Silent Lab PRC`. The checker **separates an existing selected action from a legally available alternative**.

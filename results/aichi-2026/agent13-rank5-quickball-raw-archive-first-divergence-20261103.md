## The adverse 5→0 case has a different mechanism

In the **source-second New Moon** case, the full-owner-prompt treatment Quick Ball payments are **Trainers' Mail ROS on T14** and **Ultra Ball MEG on T20**, searching Dedenne GX and Genesect V respectively. They are **not DCE payments**. Source DCE attachment roots change from **T18/T32/T38** in control to **T46** in full treatment. The control logs source Cross Fusion Strike attacks at **T32, T36, T40, T44, T48**; the full treatment logs zero such attack roots within its 260-action cap. The Quick Ball root-only treatment also logs zero, despite paying **Field Blower GRI** and **Lost Vacuum LOR** at T14/T16.

Therefore **a DCE-reserving prompt policy alone cannot be credited with preventing the 5→0 result**, because neither harmful treatment directly discarded a DCE. Search-target choice, root preemption, deck-shuffle/draw trajectory, resulting Bench occupation, attachment timing or other sequencing interactions require controlled follow-up. The root-only and full-owner-prompt modes agree on the zero-attack count despite different native paid Trainer cards and their second Quick Ball timing, an important constraint against overattributing the loss to the discarded Trainer identity.

The opposite-sign **1→4** source attack result against champion reference is preserved, so simply disabling all Quick Ball roots also discards a real development witness. The rank5 source already had zero attacks in both source-first examples. All results are conditional on four retrospective physical contexts, incomplete full paper engine fidelity, uncensored competitive play and an untimed bot whose opponent response can omit legal counterplay.

## Independent dual-blind-spot test: current Guzma scorer cannot see this funded copied attack

The actual reusable `simulation/twinleaf/agent2-held-guzma-mobility-frontier.cjs` presents a **second, independently falsifiable policy limitation** relevant to this same T46 state. Its `attackFunding` estimator **returns no result for a Pokémon with any Special Energy**, while `eligibleDamagingAttack` **requires positive directly printed damage**. On the source's DCE-funded Mew VMAX, both assumptions suppress the ready **Cross Fusion Strike → Benched Genesect V Techno Blast** avenue: DCE is a Special Energy, and Cross Fusion Strike has blank printed damage.

[Six new pure policy tests](../../tests/agent13-guzma-special-copy-eligibility-gap.test.cjs), Git blob `91623c81cfc40cdd5c8bc778651ec261ebe6c886`, were evaluated against the **actual retrieved Agent2 chooser and authentic Energy helper modules**, and all **6/6 passed**. A constructed detached actor observation follows the source's physically observed turn46 Active/Bench/Energy/hand/opponent Bench geometry; its active Genesect is *hypothetically* Paralyzed to test the harder status variant, because the archived trace did not retain the coin flip's resulting special-condition member. The live `chooseHeldGuzmaMobility` pure policy returns `null`. Two orthogonal ablations explain the result:

1. Replacing DCE with two artificial Basic Metal Energy makes the copied attack *still invisible* because its printed damage is blank. The existing scorer can instead propose a Guzma switch for the **unfunded Psychic-only Max Miracle**, with two missing colored Energy; that is a weaker signal, not a recognition of the copying attack.
2. Artificially replacing Cross Fusion Strike's blank printed damage with 210 while **keeping DCE** still returns `null`.
3. Replacing both constraints with artificial Basic Energy and direct positive damage produces the intended `play-held-guzma-mobility` candidate to Bench index 1 with **zero estimated missing Energy**.

The ablations deliberately alter printed objects to isolate software assumptions; they are **not legal alternative Pokémon cards or actual game branches**. This result **does not establish that the pure chooser vetoed the historical native action**, because no Guzma root was ever played. It establishes that the current generic scoring abstraction cannot explain or plan that publicly evident Energy-funded **copied-attack** opportunity as written. Actual Store legality, Supporter playability, Paralysis, gust targets, remaining action budget and attack effects still require a new selected native test.

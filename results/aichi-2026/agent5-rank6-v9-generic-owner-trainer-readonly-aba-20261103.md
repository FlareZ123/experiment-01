## Decision gate and handoff

The `ownerTrainerOpportunity` frontier intentionally applies only generic turn and public-lock constraints. It does **not** establish every card's printed use requirements, beneficiary, target, discard payment or resulting benefit, and `ActionEnumerator` is explicitly a capped native “legal-ish” advisor whose absence or presence does not settle paper legality. The 19 not-offered counts include physically held copy appearances; they do not independently prove missed paper-legal actions. No rank-6/rank-9 VIP guaranteed-target certificate appeared, which is expected given the source inventories/turn states and does not falsify the earlier ranks-5/11 VIP witnesses.

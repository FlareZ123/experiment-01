## Exact discovered fail-closed causes

All **four** original rank19 Raging Bolt source arms aborted at the strict physical target/root ledger linkage check, with **one** mismatched label per row. In both candidate-A arms the archival root had `Ho-Oh-EX` while the actual Store card was `Ho-Oh EX DRX`. In both candidate-B arms the root had `Arceus & Dialga & Palkia-GX` while actual Store card was `Arceus & Dialga & Palkia GX CEC`. In each case action type, physical player seat, global turn and total target-bearing-root cardinality **agreed**; only the label comparison failed. The archived accepted-target sidecars preserve exactly which physical card was played. A **strict one-to-one display alias** for each verified physical printing is now appended to `simulation/twinleaf/native-accepted-target-store-observer.cjs`, with same-field negative regression checks. It is read-only and changes no gameplay. A separate **retrospective same-four-seed replay** tests whether the original Store remains valid after this receipt repair, without claiming fresh random draws.

The two rank31 candidate-seat-B arms failed at real Store dispatch, source-owned `Duskull PRE` attack `Come and Get You`, error **`CANNOT_USE_POWER`**: action ordinals **108** (champion reference) and **99** (New Moon). They are fail-closed illegal source action attempts, not source losses. A paper and native attack-cost/precondition eligibility check is required before claiming a workable rank31 source policy.

## Cross-candidate physical-deal caution

For 15 source-rank × physical-seat groups with both Store arms returning beyond the abort, **only 12/15 shared an identical original source-deal fingerprint**, despite identical preregistered integer seed and native start side across all 15. Mismatched original source physical deals were **rank1 seat B, rank2 seat A, rank5 seat A**. The printed candidate lists are distinct, so their own physical deals are not interchangeable. Furthermore starts were observationally **source-first in 20/30** valid trials, not balanced within every rank: rank1 and rank5 were source-first in all four, whereas rank2 was source-second in all four. Neither source-deal parity nor start balance should be inferred from uniform random seed generation.

## Implications

This study adds a real native Store *constructor and diagnostic* to each of the nine previously unrepresented archetype categories, expanding paper-source driver **coverage** to all 14 source archetype labels when combined with the historical five. **Competence remains unqualified**: rank5, rank9, rank26 and rank30 frequently fail their signature attacking lines; rank19 and rank31 have native aborts. No performance ranking of the two fixed decks follows from the 17 native engine terminals.

A meaningful next release gate needs the actual original source line for each archetype, owner-visible decision audits and native prompt cards, plus printed-card semantic validation. Then fresh prospectively registered, start-balanced, physically verified, clock-limited evidence can determine whether a single fixed candidate is competitive. The three unknown original printed sixties remain ranks **23/25/28**.

## Observed physical-card transfers

Both registered source seed groups had identical complete original 60-card physical starting setups across control and treatment, no native exceptions, the same first-player role and the same source printed 60. The control's native source action policy selected **zero** Battle Compressor roots. The treatment selected **one original Store-accepted source Battle Compressor PHF** root in each, then resolved a source-owned `ChooseCardsPrompt` against that actual owner's deck.

For each:
- The native held source Item was exactly **Battle Compressor PHF** in source hand and accepted as `PlayCardAction`.
- The immediately created `ChooseCardsPrompt` was attributable to the original source player, with `prompt.cards === owner.deck` and a real prompt ID (12 and 8 respectively).
- Three selected **original source deck card objects** each passed `prompt.validate`, were found in the exact owner deck just before prompt resolution, were absent from that owner deck after resolution, and were present by object identity in the owner's discard afterward.
- Owner deck decreased **three** cards and owner discard increased **three** cards during native selection resolution. No failed proposals, missing owner choices or error arms. The source-only observer inspected the owner's private deck **only during its legally active owner-deck search prompt**; the opposing owner's private deck/hand was not accessed.

This establishes a **bounded, exact physically paid native Item discard effect**, supported by the pinned printed card and actual native Store transition. It is an independent post-prompt witness on two preselected previously positive starts. It is **not** a new unbiased frequency estimate, nor proof that discarding these three Energy cards maximizes the source's strategy.

## Outcomes and remaining gaps

**Four original Store arms, two matched physical pairs, two confirmed nonempty source-owned card transfer events, zero qualified competitive games.** The two control and treatment arms each ended with native `engine-game-end` and winner label `0` under the bounded actor-only policies. Neither pair changed its native winner label. The recorded source original rank17 was seat1; winner 0 is the **other seat**, so these outcomes supply no evidence that inserting Compressor improves the source's competitiveness.

A paper-valid evaluation still requires assessing alternative Item/Supporter and discard targets, whether the resulting Energy-in-discard enables actual Electrode-GX and Raichu attacks, a competent opposing response, prize progression, game clock and the actual Aichi Open knockout end-time threshold. The original source-only temporary bridge is **default off** and should be retained as a regression, not mistaken for a deployed bilateral common decision policy.

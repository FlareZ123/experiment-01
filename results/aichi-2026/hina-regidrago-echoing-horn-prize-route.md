## Adjacent first-person correction: Chimecho entry restrictions

The same Hina article documents a **May 16 correction after a reported Pokémon Company rules inquiry**. The author's earlier suggestion that playing Path to the Peak would defeat Chimecho's attack-origin restriction on *placing* an Ability Pokémon was withdrawn. The corrected explanation was that Path to the Peak modifies Abilities of Pokémon **in play**, leaving a Pokémon still in the hand affected by the entry restriction. This is a first-person report of the ruling, **not an independently retrieved official Q&A for the precise Chimecho + Path combination**. The [official Japanese Pokémon Card Q&A search for Chimecho](https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/faq/search.php?freeword=%E3%83%81%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%83%B3&regulation_faq_main_item1=all) independently confirms that after `Bell of Silence`, a Pokémon with an Ability in **hand** such as Eelektross cannot use its own Ability to enter play, while several Ability-bearing cards entering from **discard** can. The supplied English card archive has `Chimecho CIN 43` and `Path to the Peak` as separate source texts. This is a useful hand-vs-discard rule-fidelity falsification target; inspect exact active effects and timing before generalizing or claiming a Twinleaf defect.

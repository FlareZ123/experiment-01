## Registered denominator and method

**Sixteen distinct predeclared deals**, eight in each physical source seat, rank-9 Shun Ito Absol Electrode against the unchanged rank-1 Shadow Rider benchmark. Seeds were fixed via `deriveSlotSeeds(20261018,'pilot',9,repeat)` for repeats `0..7`, taking channel A when rank9 occupied physical seat 0 and B when it occupied seat 1. No seed or outcome screening was applied. Each game used a **180-root-action cap**. The exact published 60-card source inventories are imported by `planAichi(1)` through Twinleaf's tracked English print-equivalence and Japanese-promo allowances; this is not a direct demonstration of unchanged Japanese print legality or comprehensive paper rules.

Each root decision is selected by the existing bot *before* the observer captures printed Active-attached Energy and hand/Prize/GX/Item-restriction flag state. The observer then joins the **new, real Store appended attack logs** at that root ordinal to the post-dispatch state. Unlike the old `AttackAction` count, it can also see delegated Perfection attack declarations in principle, although no copied Horror House was observed here. A logged declaration and local immediate effect transition are still **not independently qualified end-to-end attack resolution**, nor opponent skill.

All **16/16** authenticated the actual first player from the source draw log. Rank9 started first in **7** and second in **9**. There were **zero execution errors**, **five raw engine-finished games**, and **eleven action-limit incomplete games**. These statuses are reported only to make the denominator complete; their winners are **not competitive game results**.

## Seven genuine rank9 printed attack decisions

Horror House-GX was declared in **7 different deals**. All seven were ordinary root `AttackAction` declarations from native Gengar & Mimikyu-GX; no delegated copies. In every case, the source immediate Store transition changed `usedGX: false → true` and the opposing `cannotPlayItemCards: false → true`. The `cannotPlayItemCards` flag is only one projection of Horror House-GX's printed **full hand-play lock**: no Pokémon, Energy or Trainer cards can be played from hand on the defender's next turn. These are instrumented engine observations and still need independent complete effect/continuation review.

This **selected** control is excluded from the sixteen-deal prospective denominator and is not an independent gameplay sample.

## Interpretation and next decision

The experiment verifies that the new shared observer records authentic root native Horror House declarations and exact attached-printing snapshots in **completed compiled source-dealt execution**, preserving failures and incomplete trials. It supplies a concrete exposure denominator. It does **not** validate the extra-Psychic hook in a natural trigger state, delegated Perfection Horror House resolution, opponent decision competence, complete paper mechanics or any win rate.

A next high-value test should prospectively seek rank9 source states with **two effective Psychic units** at the actual attack declaration, then compare printed-rule versus pinned treatment with a causal same-deal patch and check opponent reply. Distinguish exposure frequency from effectiveness; do not repeatedly select convenient successes and count them as independent games.

**Shared status remains 29/32 exact opposing lists, zero independently qualified competitive games, no target-qualified final deck and no demonstrated 99.5% win rate.**

## Corrected lock scope

The original summary mentioned only `cannotPlayItemCards`, which understated the attack. Gengar & Mimikyu-GX TEU 53 reads that the opponent cannot play **any cards from their hand** during their next turn. The pinned engine's `OPPONENT_CANNOT_PLAY_ANY_CARDS` wrapper sets Item, Supporter, Tool, Stadium, Energy and Pokémon play prohibitions. A future live audit should record each category independently. See the [actual next-turn rank1 attacking reply](rank9-horror-house-authentic-nextturn.md), where the defender held two VMAX, two Psychic Energy, Acerola and Latias ex. No causal hand-lock advantage or policy competence is inferred.

# Agent31: multi-Bench Guzma tactical prototype

November 1, 2026. Native research only, unchanged decklists.

The original single-Bench Guzma policy passed both-seat real Store card actions but had **zero exposures** in the 16 identical-deal original rank11 game pairs [CI 41350546527](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41350546527). Our next experiment permits multiple Bench destinations when the owner has an already-funded fixed-damage attacker that can nominally KO a chosen opponent Bench target. An immediate direct Active KO takes precedence. The chooser only reads the acting owner's cards, both public boards and visible remaining Prize counts.

Implementation is in [agent31-final-prize-guzma-frontier.cjs](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent31-final-prize-guzma-frontier.cjs), with exact native physical target ownership in [agent43-guzma-prompt-continuation.cjs](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent43-guzma-prompt-continuation.cjs). Separate development opt-ins are AGENT31_GUZMA_FINAL_PRIZE_DEV for one remaining Prize and AGENT31_GUZMA_PRIZE_TRADE_DEV for two through six remaining Prizes. Neither is enabled by default.

[CI 41351951176](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41351951176) verified eight initial owner-public tests and real native two-seat targeted Guzma, Moon Press, final Prize. [CI 41352158093](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41352158093) **PASSED** the expanded pure tests and genuine two-seat nonfinal native six-to-five Prize-taking fixture as well. It validated exact second-Bench target custody for both players and physical opponent KO, with the opponent still able to respond. The [frozen rank11 nonfinal Prize-trade workflow](../../.github/workflows/agent31-prize-trade-retrospective.yml) is a 16-pair retrospective with original seeds, fixed source decks and original baseline digest; it cannot certify field performance.

The first nonfinal original-16 matched-game comparison [CI 41352333808](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41352333808) reproduced the exact original baseline digest and all 16 native physical initial deals, with **zero selected treatments, zero accepted new Guzma roots and zero changed action ledgers**. As a precaution, the replay runner now additionally asserts that every original baseline root-audit explicitly has its tactical flag OFF and each treated native game has it ON. A second CI run checks that provenance. The zero-exposure observation remains the empirical result of the first run; it is not evidence of competitive parity. An owner-public real-root obstacle census is being developed to identify which prerequisite prevents these nominal KO lines from triggering.

This is a hypothesis about tactical Supporter use. Visible nominal damage can mispredict actual damage under Abilities, Tools or other effects, and using Guzma can lose valuable Supporter options. No full-game performance benefit is demonstrated and zero competitively qualified games remain.


## 2 November: exact native obstacle census closes the retrospective zero-treatment question

The originally registered rank-11 16-game cohort has now been replayed with an **actual, reversible, owner-only Store observer** at accepted owner action boundaries. [Compiled CI 41352716189](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41352716189) succeeded on pinned Twinleaf `a9905a76a6c4103ba4b6cb9a49e86bad795c5c05`, and the complete native ledger fingerprint remained **exactly** `4c2981a904eb939ba32301038514ef269d7c8a07d883b5c90bae903dfbc82216`. [Archived JSON 13101644801](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41352716189/artifacts/13101644801) preserves the observer detail. A separately rerun, explicitly OFF-vs-ON opt-in treatment [CI 41352895307](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41352895307) also succeeded with 16 source-deal-matched pairs, **zero selected Guzma prize-trade treatments, zero native changed action ledgers**, and no qualified games.

The observer encountered **1,010 accepted owner action roots** in total, **442 roots holding Guzma**, forming **322 distinct owner-turns holding Guzma**. Across these held-Guzma turns there were **zero** with an immediately funded fixed-printed-damage own-Bench attacker, **zero** with a nominal own-Bench KO pair, and zero final/nonfinal proposed Prize-window conjunctions. The 442 repeated roots and 322 turns are correlated within just **16 games**. This resolves the originally pending *why zero treatment?* question for the present deliberately narrow policy: the required already-funded Bench attack never materialized. The observation is *not* a claim that Guzma cannot be strategically valuable or that an attack after additional same-turn actions is impossible.

Agent2 independently reobserved **all 359 original-source first owner turns** and found **zero attached Energy cards on any source Bench** while retaining the same native action ledger ([source/physical geometry report](agent2-rank11-held-guzma-public-geometry-retrospective-20261102.md), [CI 41370546414](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41370546414)). This corroborates the missing **Bench-investment** prerequisite in the original source seats and shifts future development toward identifying better **Energy-recipient sequencing and paid resource-to-attack chains**. The present final-Prize and nonfinal-Prize selectors remain **development-only, default off**; retesting them on unchanged source policy/deals has no value until an authentic qualifying attacking opportunity is observed. No competitive result or deck-strength improvement follows from this negative census.

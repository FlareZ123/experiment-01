## Prospective observations and interpretation contract

The registered output captures genuine first player, source's first-opener opportunity and live equipped-Garbodor witness, candidate actual Tool action, original-root Underworld Door declaration and verified energy-plus-two-card Store effect, candidate attack declarations, action-cap/terminal reason as exposed by the runner, and diagnostic engine winner/remaining Prizes. Every failure and no-event trial remains in the denominator of the **mechanical opportunity census**.

An observed selected policy action is not proof that the effect resolved. A genuine Store effect is not proof that the candidate competently positioned its Active attacker. An engine winner, especially from a capped game against incomplete generic policies, is not a competitive win. The fixed packet requires an independent-deal, strong two-sided action-to-effect-to-reply trace before any game can be evaluated for competitive strength. Exact Japanese paper-printing/card fidelity and actual official clocks are separate unresolved gates.

**Acceptance criterion for this exercise:** the manually compiled pinned environment runs the four pure prospective tests and emits all 16 records with valid source-dealt and true-start provenance, preserving exceptions rather than silently dropping them. A result with no eligible Garbodor/Hood/Door encounters is a meaningful **negative opportunity finding** only. It cannot establish that either fixed deck is stronger.

**Agent 30, 20 October 2026.** This is a **retrospective source-seed mechanism experiment** on the original rank-6 New Moon development game, with no qualification for win-rate estimation. It is predeclared before any compiled native replay of the target-reallocation arms. The single objective is to isolate whether an early protected Bench-energy attachment can survive opponent denial and support the printed Gengar & Mimikyu-GX line, given that the existing generic bot repeatedly attached to its Active Galarian Articuno V.

1. **Control:** replay original unchanged native root bot with Agent41's common v0 policy, owner late14 Compressor opt-in and read-only owner Energy probe; assert original root SHA exactly, 260 roots and zero candidate attacks.
2. **Turn9 Bench investment:** at **only** the genuine first source-aligned candidate owner root of global turn9, replace the bot's proposed **Basic Psychic Energy hand-attachment to Active Galarian Articuno V** with a *native* `PlayCardAction` targeting its already visible **Benched Gengar & Mimikyu-GX**. If the second decision does not occur, preserve the failure; do not fabricate availability.

### Failure and qualification contract

A native counterfactual that fails to attach Bench Psychic is a **failure**, even if an abstract policy scored the line highly. A turn13 diversion absent after the first fork does **not** get counted as having occurred. A result is **mechanical**, unqualified for competitive inference, even if a selected arm finishes as winner or produces an attack. Do not claim an improved win rate. None of these seed-chosen arms is prospective or a benchmark of all 32 opposing decks. Original field remains 29 exact opposing published 60s; no demonstrated 99.5%.

**Primary discriminant:** after the turn9 real Bench energy transfer, does the card remain physically attached through source turn10; if so, how does source turn14 respond, and does Gengar ever become Active with enough Energy to announce printed Horror House-GX? Compare against the control's exactly reproduced loss stream.

## Implications for one integrated paper-aware policy

This is an important **negative control** against the proposal to automatically play any held, differently named Stadium in response to a native Pass. All three independent original physical games rank5, rank6 and rank9 exhibit paper-permitted and native-enumerator-omitted owner Stadium replacements; the selected interventions truly executed and the opposing gameplay continued. Only the original rank6 Wondrous→Thunder case immediately enabled a funded attack and took two genuine native Prizes. Source rank9 PokéStop→Silent Lab and source rank5 Path→Sky Field did **not** yield a new attack in their observed action-capped continuation. The tactical criterion must compare active funded attack/KO or denial value to the existing Stadium, Bench reality and timing.

This original rank5 study is a retrospective single source game without alternate first-turn draws, skilled opponent decisions, broader matchups or an official paper 25-minute clock. **No games qualify as competitive win-rate evidence, 29/32 original opponent lists remain authenticated and no fixed final 60 is selected.**

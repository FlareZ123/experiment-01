# Agent 22: exact source DCE-Mew retreat is necessary for two rank5 attacks, 32 original-Store ablation attempts

**3 November 2026 | retrospective owner-policy mechanism experiment. No qualified competitive games, no candidate deck nomination.**

## Core finding

A preregistered 2×2 ablation on the frozen **92-option** joint actor policy isolated the role of its **DCE-specific Genesect V → Mew VMAX paid-retreat module**. Eight original rank-5 Mew/Genesect source candidate/seat/deal trials were replayed in each of four modes, all on the same pinned native code and original 60s. Every one of **24** control-to-ablation comparisons preserved its exact original-source physical-deal fingerprint **and native actual starter**. The entire set of **32 actual Store attempts** succeeded without native runner errors and includes every cap, terminal and null attack.

| Same-code symmetric policy | Original-source settled Cross Fusion Strike attacks across eight attempts |
| --- | ---: |
| Full selected 92-option policy | **17** |
| DCE-specific Mew mobility disabled | **8** |
| General flexible paid retreat disabled | **17** |
| Both mobility modules disabled | **8** |

The two DCE-positive groups were precisely **source rank5, repeat1, candidate physical seat B**. In the champion-reference opponent arm, the full control made four settled Cross Fusion Strike attacks; with DCE mobility off it made zero. In the New Moon opponent arm, the full control made five; DCE mobility off made zero. In both, the **first differing actual native root action** was the original Mew source's **RetreatAction → PassTurnAction** on **global turn 47** (root ordinals **183** and **176**, respectively). With the unrelated flexible retreat module alone off, **the complete root-action ledger was unchanged** in these same two positive games and the attack totals remained four and five. With both off, turn 47 reverted to Pass identically to DCE-only ablation.

This is strong **same-source-deal, same-code causal evidence** that the original rank5 turn-47 native attack access in these two cases depended on the DCE-specific actor retreat intervention. It does not show that the module improves tournament win rate or handles other physical deals in the Top32.

## What the ablation specifically establishes

The original positive source state had **Genesect V** Active with one physical **Double Colorless Energy**, plus a Benched **Mew VMAX** with a separate Double Colorless Energy and a Benched **Genesect V** for copied `Techno Blast`. The [DCE-specific owner-visible policy](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent37-dce-mew-paid-retreat.cjs) recognizes those exact board requirements and proposes promotion to the already funded Mew. The [general flexible paid retreat](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent10-flexible-paid-retreat-frontier.cjs) is written for a different single-energy retreat-cost target.

The root-decision comparison is especially discriminating: DCE off → **RetreatAction becomes PassTurnAction** on the **first** differing accepted root while maintaining identical complete chronological prefix; Flexible off → **no root-ledger divergence at all** on those two cases. Fourfold factorial means the negative result for flexible paid retreat is conditioned on those replayed states; it is not a global ineffectiveness claim.

The baseline's previously recorded turn-47 `PassTurnAction` is thus a concrete missed source-attack opportunity under the 24-option policy and the joint92 with DCE disabled, rather than only a disagreement about shallow heuristic scores. Distinct source physical instances were matched by source-deal SHA, including public board and starting player, not by integer seed alone.


## Separate native opponent-reply trace (no strategic qualification)

The raw selected joint-policy rows also retain `candidateRepliesAfterSourceAttacks` for the two turn-47 DCE-positive original source trials. Of the **nine** settled source Cross Fusion Strike attack occurrences across those two candidate contexts, **seven** have a genuine same-clock-epoch next candidate root, **four** are followed by an actual candidate `AttackAction` on its following turn (`Energy Drive`, `Filch`, `Will-o-Wisp`, `Hidden Power`), **one** has a card-play root without a next-turn attack in the recorded sequence, **one** shows card play followed by Pass, and **one** is a direct Pass. The other **two** have no candidate follow-up root because the native game ended. Those records do show actual chronological continuation after several source attacks. **They do not establish a competent consequential opponent response**, legal/tactically strong damage or a paper-clock win. Agent41's native `candidate-other-action` label only requires a genuine next action and must not be interpreted as a valid counterplay policy.

## Qualifications and next gate

- **Mechanistic/native qualification:** same-physical-state counterfactual actions and actual native settled attacks identify the DCE module as necessary for this native route.
- **Physical-payment qualification:** action acceptance and a public board transition alone cannot prove that the exact original Genesect-attached DCE card object moved to owner's discard as a legal retreat payment. A separately preregistered **read-only exact physical object observer** is being developed in `simulation/twinleaf/agent22-genesect-dce-retreat-custody.cjs` and `simulation/evaluation/agent22-rank5-genesect-fee-custody-v1.cjs`, to be run only on the two exposed positive groups and compared byte-for-byte to prior root ledgers.
- **Paper and competitive qualification:** printed card interactions, every actual payment, owner-info safety, opponent tactical decision competence, control/stall counterplay and the official timed game process are not universally validated. The 260-root cap is an engineering limit, not a 25-minute real tournament clock. Do not treat native engine terminals or copy attack counts as game wins.
- **Field coverage:** there are still **29/32 authenticated original full 60s**, ranks 23/25/28 unavailable. **Zero competitively qualified games**. The 99.5% goal has not been achieved.

This result should redirect agents toward owner-visible source transition fidelity, not overconfident ranking of the fixed candidates.

### Why the singleton matters

As a qualitative game hypothesis, Regidrago VSTAR can copy relevant Dragon attacks from its discard with Apex Dragon. A single differently printed Dragon attacker can therefore change reachable damage or defensive lines without appearing on the board itself. The photograph's Mega Dragonite ex and rank21's Noivern ex should be separately checked for exact Japanese attack costs/effects, discard requirements, legality, access paths, and opponent-specific ALS. **No attack-equivalence, conditional win rate, full-game strength, or simulator compatibility is asserted here.** The photo is an opportunity to study a real near-identical 59/60 deck variation rather than to invent a card swap.

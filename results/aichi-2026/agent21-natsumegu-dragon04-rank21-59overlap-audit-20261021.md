## Printing and attack-effect primary-source follow-up (22 October UTC)

After this 38-cell transcription was frozen, independent [official Japanese Pokémon card search](https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/49992) and its [M2a-232 card gallery](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/M2a/232) confirm that the photograph's distinctive rainbow text-cover **R1C8 Mega Dragonite ex is the MA `M2a 232/193`, artist DOM**, Stage 2 Dragon, HP 370. Its printed **Ryuno Glide 330** attack has `WLL` cost and directs its user to discard **two Energy** from the attacking Pokémon. Its Sky Carry Ability switches its controller's Active Pokémon once during the turn. This precisely identifies **one Japanese printing** from the image. Others still need printing-level verification. Since the photographed 60 has no Dragonair/Dratini, the natural function of Mega Dragonite in this list is a **Regidrago Apex Dragon discard payload**, not a normal evolution/Ability line.

Noivern ex in the independent published rank21 list instead provides **Covert Flight 70** (prevents damage from Basic Pokémon attacks on the next opposing turn) and **Dominating Echo 140** (prevents Special Energy/Stadium plays from hand on the next opposing turn), per [official Asia Pokémon card data](https://asia.pokemon-card.com/sg/card-search/detail/6965/). Thus the exact one-card difference replaces a two-option temporary defense/lock payload with a **330-damage, Energy-consuming one-shot attack payload**. The copied Dragonite attack's `WLL` printed cost is irrelevant when Apex Dragon is used, but its two-Energy discard effect applies to Regidrago. In the source deck's one-Double-Dragon-plus-one-Basic `GGF` Apex-ready state, **discarding one Double Dragon Energy card can satisfy the two-Energy discard**, leaving the single Basic attached. The Japanese Pokémon [official Double Dragon Energy ruling](https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/faq/search.php?freeword=%E3%83%80%E3%83%96%E3%83%AB%E3%83%89%E3%83%A9%E3%82%B4%E3%83%B3%E3%82%A8%E3%83%8D%E3%83%AB%E3%82%AE%E3%83%BC&regulation_faq_main_item1=all) expressly confirms one Double Dragon Energy may count as two paid Energy for Dragonite's Dragon Wave discard (FAQ search page, result concerning `ドラゴンウェーブ`). Exact target selection must still be checked against live continuous effects, ability locks and attachment rules.

This is a **source-supported mechanical hypothesis**, separate from any simulated matchup. It motivates a frozen rank21-like 59/60 A/B *card-role* study once the common two-player policy, paper-rule interpreter and legal prompt selection are qualified. The Sky Carry Ability belongs to the absent Stage2 and is not acquired merely because Apex Dragon copies an attack; do not incorrectly model free switching on Regidrago. No observed deck strength, paper clock result or 99.5% inference is made.

## Concrete 320-HP benchmark threshold that separates the copied attacks

A conditional **attack-damage** check makes this singleton's value testable against the *actual rank-1 Shadow Rider reference*: [Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX CRE 75](https://www.pokemon.com/uk/pokemon-tcg/pokemon-cards/series/swsh6/205) has **320 HP** and awards 3 Prizes on KO. The 59-card shared shell includes [Koraidon ex TEF 120](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/en/TEF/120) with **Kaiser Tackle 280**, which also damages the user by **60** when copied. Under an otherwise unmodified clean board and with the list's single Muscle Band attached to the attacking Regidrago, Kaiser Tackle reaches **300**, still **20 below** fresh 320 HP. The unique Ryuno Glide **330** crosses that threshold (10 excess) before any Muscle Band requirement and incurs a mandatory two-Energy discard. This is a genuine **discrete Prize-conversion possibility** against a three-Prize target, subject to all legal and opponent protection conditions. The comparison involves a *readily enumerated attacker*, not an observed win.

Another critical constraint: with **one Double Dragon Energy plus one Basic** providing Apex Dragon's exact 3 Energy, copying Ryuno Glide and discarding the one DDE costs 2 Energy units and strands the attacker at **one Energy unit** afterward. It generally needs an additional two-unit attachment or an acceleration sequence before a second Apex attack. A line that wins 3 Prizes immediately can still be worse in board states where continuous attack or Noivern's Special Energy/Stadium denial is essential. For direct source comparison, evaluate both the conditional fresh-VMAX knockout and next-turn energy availability, along with opponent gust/response and potential Tool/Ability locks, under identical opponent-policy quality and physical draws.

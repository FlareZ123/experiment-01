## New discriminating original source case

On source seat1, seed `2295622320`, the treatment's native Store accepted a **Double Dragon Energy ROS attachment to Active Regidrago V on global turn 2**. Regidrago V evolved into VSTAR. The same registered source deck then made three real Mysterious Treasure payments: dead Battle VIP Pass at global turn 14; **Noivern ex PAL** at turn 16; **Dragapult ex TWM** at turn 16. Those Dragons physically entered its public discard; searches returned real selected cards to hand.

At **each of global turns 16, 18, 20, 22 and 24**, the source owner's passive public root showed:

- **Active Regidrago VSTAR SIT**, naturally evolved and still present.
- One Double Dragon Energy ROS, one Grass Energy MEE and two Fire Energy MEE attached to that Active. Temple of Sinnoh absent. The printed Grass-Grass-Fire Apex Dragon Energy cost is therefore paid from public cards, subject to genuine engine effect legality.
- Both Noivern ex PAL and Dragapult ex TWM in the *actual public discard pile* and thus available as potential copied-attack sources.

The source nonetheless chose **PassTurnAction** at the relevant roots, after some legal other board actions. Its **native copied-attack chooser recorded zero resolved source-owned ChooseAttackPrompt selections** and no Apex declaration in this treatment. Source's later single attack at turn 32 was Regidrago V's Celestial Roar. Thus the combined **Active + printed cost + physical payload public gates** can all become true while the *generic actor policy still fails to choose the decisive attack*. This is an especially important potential false-negative if an optimizer treats lack of Apex declarations as lack of card resources.

A probable mechanism emerges directly from the current research adapter source: the default generic `chooseActorRootIntent` collects attached Basic Energy but skips unknown Special Energy when `specialEnergyRoot` is off. Even with that option enabled, the generic bounded `specialEnergyUnits` list omits Double Dragon Energy ROS. Hence its Root payability estimate can exclude a physically attached DDE that the printed card says provides two Energy of compatible types. **This is a source-code-informed hypothesis, not yet an independently native-verified proof of which decision branch caused the Pass.** Genuine Store acceptance and copy resolution must be tested separately.

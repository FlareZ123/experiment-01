## Independent late-turn financed attacker stranded on Bench

A new tactical opportunity emerged directly from the original owner's face-up board, separate from the Bomb issue. In authentic source seat B against New Moon, global turns **42, 44 and 46**, the source selected and settled Pass with:

- **Active Electrode-GX CES:** `Prism Energy ASC` plus `Darkness Energy MEE`; source printed retreat cost **one Colorless**.
- **Benched Mega Absol ex M1L native proxy:** `Telepath [P] Energy M3` plus **two** `Prism Energy ASC`. Original printed MEG86 is a Basic Darkness Pokémon, with `Claw of Darkness` costing Darkness, Darkness, Colorless and doing 200 damage.
- The two Prism Energy on a Basic supply the two Darkness attack requirements while Telepathic Psychic Energy supplies the Colorless requirement. The source actor had an apparently paid retreat resource and a payable attacker **before** the opposing Klefki's nineteenth 10-damage `Joust` knocked out Electrode at global turn 47.

The prior same-deal native report proved Mega Absol was eventually promoted and executed five Claw attacks at global turns **48/50/52/54/56**, taking all original source Prize cards after the very long stall. This suggests a **turn-42 normal paid Retreat → early Claw** counterfactual that could directly change the result. It remains a hypothesis until a source-original actual Store test certifies no retreat lock/status, selected discard payment, promotion, attack root, damage and opposing continuation. Merely having enough Energy does not prove a legal present-tense Retreat or a tactical win.

This gap is especially relevant to actor policy integration. The existing [generic paid-retreat planner](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent6-actor-paid-retreat-frontier.cjs) requires **exactly one Basic Energy** on Active and Basic-only Energy on the target attacker, so it abstains on the mixed Active/three-Special-Energy source state.

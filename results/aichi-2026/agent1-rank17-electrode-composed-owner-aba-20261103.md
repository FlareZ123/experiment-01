## What is and is not established

The composed source policy executed a small number of genuine Item and ordinary Energy/Stage1 plays, confirmed by exact physical Store records; the read-only observer did not change those plays. **No Extra Energy Bomb was selected**, no Energy transfer through that Ability was demonstrated, and no Electrode self-KO/opponent Prize accounting was tested in this cohort. The supplied rulebook allows **partial 1–4 physical-card attachment** when fewer than the printed five exist; the trial must not exclude those cases as automatically illegal, but even a positively funded source does not establish all applicable Ability conditions.

These source-only native policy bridges do not establish independent opponent competence, all printed legality, native prompt full strategic quality, exact Aichi 25-minute clock, or a competitive game. No realized native terminal is promoted to a tournament win. **Target status: unchanged: 29/32 authenticated original opponent printed 60s, zero qualified games and no demonstrated 99.5% win rate.**

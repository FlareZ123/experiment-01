It applied on both physical seats, without source-rank, candidate-name or matchup selection. The DCE Mew mobility, rank11 attacker preparation, ordinary play-lock checks and original owner payment options were retained. Global Quick Ball remained disabled because another original same-deal developmental test showed a worse attack line. The VIP feature can help either player.

The registered 13 original-source physical seed groups were drawn from the older rank5/rank11 joint-v1 groups, the earlier rank5 v3.1 mobility-positive groups, a rank11 funding-positive group, one rank6 natural control, plus exactly three fresh independently generated seeds (rank5, rank11 and rank6). All original opponent 60s were authenticated original Aichi rank5 Mew/Genesect, rank11 Absol/Garbodor and rank6 Iron Thorns. Same decklist, actual physical starting seat and source setup fingerprint were compared within each candidate/policy pair. The 52 raw row objects include all root actions, native prompts, accepted native choice traces, source attack settlement, Prize and end status.

### Hard acceptance failure and source-error stratification

On the single freshly generated rank6 original-source context **`6:300:A:champion-reference`**, both control and selected modes aborted identically after native action ordinal 43 with `UseAbilityAction: UNKNOWN_POWER`. In each aborted row, source physical setup fingerprint, actual starting seat, full native action ledger and final Prizes were **not returned** by the runner's exceptional path. The failed pair therefore cannot be labeled exact-physical matched, even though mode, seed, printing, exception type and action count coincide. The policy source matching gate correctly recorded **25/26**, rejected the claimed 26/26 and marked [Actions 41581727876](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41581727876) red, while its `if: always()` artifact step preserved all 52 raw attempts. It would be incorrect to call this a demonstrated different deal: actual equality is **unknown** because both snapshots are missing. No source-specific performance claim is supported by this aborted context.

The earlier initially red [Actions 41581604368](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41581604368) executed *zero* native games: the preregistered roster Git blob was mistyped (expected `ae773f6201d7aa69d31914dbfee66f3bfa692200` instead of actual `ae773f6201d7aa69d31914dbfee66f3fba692200`). That source-identity transcription was transparently amended **before any native play**, with a recorded manifest correction and re-pinned manifest blob. All 13 groups, 52 actions, 60s and selected policy remained unchanged. The successful 52-arm native execution is a separate development attempt whose red status is consequential.

### Qualifications and next decision

**No 52-arm outcomes are paper-competitive qualified.** No entire source policy has certified game-long information-set safety, full paper payment/effect compliance, general decision competence, or the official **25-minute** tournament clock. Several observed attack sequences begin at *global turns 32–55*. One pair had a symmetric pinned engine exception, and no 99.5% field win rate is supported. The underlying benchmark still has **29/32** original Aichi Top32 60s; ranks 23,25,28 lack exact printings. These 52 are exploratory development attempts, including recycled historical source deals and only three fresh seed groups.

**Near-term priority:** Treat the attempted selected symmetric VIP v2 as a useful **source capability intervention** with a strict failed completeness gate. Its positive rank5 Techno Blast receipts and rank11 source VIP Bench receipts are meaningful local mechanics. Its rank11 lost-Claw case and the rank6 `UNKNOWN_POWER` exception are vetoes against treating aggregate attack counts as a universally stronger release. Preserve both modes as immutable controls for a subsequent **scoped** correct-first-source-root / attack / cost / Prize / next-opponent-responded test, ideally fixing early exception setup capture without editing the vendor submodule. Delegate adversarial review and integration with Agent22 rather than duplicate a new 52-game compile. The final fixed-60 champion remains unselected.

## Newly located primary participant account

A dated Japanese
[14 May 2026, kume first-person article](https://note.com/kume_pokeca/n/n6cfd126f5774),
**「CL愛知2026エクストラ ベスト32 アロライワンショット」**,
explicitly identifies the author's finishing Swiss position as **25th**
and describes their used strategy as **Alolan Raichu one-shot**. This
corresponds to final rank25 Kazuki Kumegawa in
[Limitless's tournament-level ranking](https://www.limitlesstcg.com/tournaments/566/decklists)
and [archetype results](https://limitlesstcg.com/decks/282/results).

The publicly readable first-person match log:

The player's self-reported **Swiss 7–1** includes **four wins and one
loss against Regidrago**, a useful *real human tournament pressure
observation*. It does not estimate the probability that any single
Regidrago list beats this exact opponent, since pairings, game
situations, player skill and selective reporting are uncontrolled. The
elimination loss shows that rank25's matchup against Mega Absol/Garbodor
can matter; it is one game rather than a robust head-to-head estimate.

The publicly readable text explains the player's deck choice in the
context of anticipating a Regidrago-heavy field and lists conceptual
counterplay requirements such as coping with early Ability restrictions,
Budew Item-lock pressure, and Stadium effects. The article then reaches
a paid construction section. **Its exact original 60-card list is not
available in the publicly read portion.** No Pokémon, Trainer or
Energy copy counts for this specific rank25 deck are authenticated by
this source. Do not claim rank25 is now resolved; do not transplant the
other public Raichu/Electrode Top-32 or Top-4 60s.

## Research use

- Preserve this article as an identified, player-authored source rather
  than equating the missing 60 to an arbitrarily similar list.
- The player-reported 4–1 Swiss versus Regidrago is a useful reason
  to stress test **one-shot Prize pressure and early Item/Ability
  disruption** once an authentic rank25 deck is recovered, while
  maintaining uncertainty about source 60, setup decisions and
  opponent quality.
- Do not include these historical human wins in simulator benchmark
  numerators or denominators. The same fixed 60 must still be
  evaluated against all 32 authenticated tournament entrants.

## Actual printed interaction remains real, with a narrow relevance boundary

The original `Shadow Rider Calyrex V CRE 74` printed Astral Barrage requires three Colorless Energy and moves five 10-damage counters onto each of up to two opposing Pokémon. The pinned reducer erroneously counts *only occupied Bench slots* to choose prompt target count, even though the opponent Active is selectable. Earlier both-seat [genuine-native prepared Store tests](../../tests/agent4-astral-barrage-native.test.cjs) showed it deals zero counters against a lone Active and affects just one of Active+one Bench, whereas the opt-in research-owned [corrected reducer](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent4-astral-barrage-active-target-hook.cjs) gives 50 or 100 total damage respectively. Illegal duplicate targeting was also rejected in that prepared fixture. These are verified conditional effects, without evidence that such an attack is naturally chosen in the fixed-60 matchup.

At setup before extra draws or subsequent turns, rank-6 Iron Thorns has four Basic Pokémon, so conditioning on a valid seven-card hand and Benching all held Basics gives exactly `2756/2783 = 99.0298239310%` with **one or two Basics total**, where the native target-count deficit would be one Pokémon *if Astral Barrage were used at that exact geometry*. This is a combinatorial opening-board characterization, **not** a probability of reaching three attached Energy, choosing Astral on a later turn, changing an opponent's knockout, or winning. The absence of any such attacks in 16 actual native attempts is a concrete counterweight to extrapolating the prepared-board damage defect into a strategic claim.

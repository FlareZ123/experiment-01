## Decision and next experiment

**Reject this 28-attempt native run as a qualified field strength comparison.** Accept its narrowed mechanical evidence: first-turn owner VIP can transport into a symmetric selected policy on authentic source rank-5 deals; DCE paid mobility remains necessary only on specific observed boards; a fresh original rank-11 policy reached a funded Claw sequence; actual winning-response policy remains inadequate; some exact-seed candidate comparisons break physical source pairing; and a live `UNKNOWN_POWER` abort remains.

For the next useful *single* integration gate, retain the manifest and all rows, diagnose the rank6 `UNKNOWN_POWER` before rerunning that arm, and create a reproducible **same-source-deal matched candidate comparison** only where actual physical source fingerprints match. Preserve the source rank-11 three-Darkness Claw state and opponent's first Pass after source Claw as negative tactical-competence controls. A defensible next policy must select appropriate candidate legal actions under that original board rather than simply credit a native terminal. Use a new preregistered independent physical panel to judge generality. Do not condition reports on the 19 engine terminals and exclude caps or aborts.

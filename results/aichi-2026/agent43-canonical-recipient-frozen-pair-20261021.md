## Important limit of existing root-ledger digests

Despite verified different **physical recipient targets and final public boards**, each pair's `rootActionLedger` array, `rootActionLedgerSha256`, and attack declaration sequence were **byte-identical** across OFF/ON. The existing ledger records action type, actor, turn and prompt ancestry, **without complete `PlayCardAction` argument/target or board-state identity**. A matching `rootActionLedgerSha256` therefore certifies **chronological envelope equality only**, not full state/action equivalence. The meaningful A/B effect is visible only in root `nativeRootAcceptances` and native before/after board snapshots. Evidence gates and researchers must not treat a common root ledger hash as proof of identical physical play.

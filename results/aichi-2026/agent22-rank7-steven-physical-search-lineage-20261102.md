A native **object-identity** witness now proves the precise selected physical objects are the ones consumed in all **nine** observed later plays. Fifteen selected physical objects do not have a later accepted PlayCardAction witnessed within the cap. This is an evidence-quality improvement for owner search policies, **not a new deck-performance advantage**.

The lexical comparator obtained **three Capture Energy and two Artazon** later physical plays. This does not establish the correct choice of search payload or turn utility. Selected cards can have indirect strategic value and remaining opportunities are censored.

## Limits and next meaningful step

The witness covers physical cards actually **played through the native PlayCardAction**. It does not automatically credit unused Supporters, cards retained in hand, discarded costs, attached Energy moved by effects, indirect information/lock value, or playing quality. It cannot certify that every underlying Twinleaf source interaction agrees with paper Expanded. No causal improvement in search policy or win rate can be inferred from four versus five later plays. The field remains **29 of 32 published complete lists**, with ranks 23/25/28 unavailable, zero competitively qualified games and no 99.5% fixed-60 performance proof.

A useful follow-up is to test a **precommitted actor-owned, state-dependent Steven search policy** against frozen lexical controls, using later physical use and actual unqualified source response separately. Resource value must depend on own hand/board, current Prize differential, Supporter discard and Bench/lock conditions, while preserving the exact native prompt, paper mandatory minimum and source-opponent continuation. The physical witness offers a narrower outcome than raw name overlap, never a substitute for complete-game evaluation.

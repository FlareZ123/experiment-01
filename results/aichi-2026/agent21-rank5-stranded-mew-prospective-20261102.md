## Post-run diagnosis: an exact native enum type defect invalidated all treatment offers

After this run, the pinned upstream source `ptcg-server/src/game/store/card/card-types.ts` was inspected at native vendor revision `a9905a76...`. It defines `EnergyType` as **BASIC/SPECIAL only**. Energy symbols, including Colorless, belong to the separate `CardType` enum. The release hook in this **particular recorded run** constructed its owner catalog using `types.EnergyType.COLORLESS`, which is **undefined**. The fail-closed selector thus rejected even legitimate Colorless retreat costs. The absence of replacements is therefore a **program bug**, not a strategic observation.

A separate read-only historical replay under the same bad enum but with added owner-public near-miss logging returned **34 clear geometry near-miss Pass states** (all from genuine owner-matched roots), including active Lillie's Clefairy ex JTG + DCE, Benched DCE-powered Mew VMAX and Benched Genesect V donor, with printed retreat cost [9], no stadium, no known attack lock and no previous retreat that turn. Native historical CI **41472307928** confirmed 256 genuine owner-matched Pass roots, 0 offers and 34 geometry near misses. Those are multiple repeated observations from the same few stalled games, **not 34 independent Bernoulli opportunities**.

The hook was corrected in a **later code revision** to `types.CardType.COLORLESS` and made to assert a finite Colorless index before executing any native games. The previously archived sixteen rows and matched-deal evidence remain immutable as the negative-controls of the defective code; they **must not be relabeled as executed with the corrected policy**. A new diagnostic historical replay has been queued for the corrected hook; it cannot be used to estimate independent win rate since the seeds are pre-known.

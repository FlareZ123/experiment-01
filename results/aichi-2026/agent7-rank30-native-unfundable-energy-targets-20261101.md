## Primary finding

Across **16 original source rank30 matches** in eight independent pre-registered source physical shuffles, and both fixed candidate 60s with alternated source physical seats, the original source owner made **37 physically accepted Basic Psychic Energy attachments**. The read-only native sidecar verified the precise public Pokémon destination after every one, with **37/37 uniquely settled target receipts and zero print or target ambiguities**.

The original rank30 60 has exactly **five Basic Psychic Energy** and **two Telepathic Psychic Energy**. Crobat V DAA 104 has *Venomous Fang* requiring **Darkness + Colorless**. Telepathic Psychic Energy provides Psychic while attached. Consequently, no amount of ordinary Energy attachment from the original owner 60 can satisfy Venomous Fang's mandatory Darkness requirement. The three native accepted Psychic attachments to Crobat occurred at source turns **36** (against New Moon), and **47, 51** (both against the champion reference). The 16-game sample produced **zero `RetreatAction` owner-root declarations in either player policy**; the three Crobat source tails also contained no later explicit retreat declarations. This limits evidence of an immediate retreat benefit, but does not exclude other applications of a Colorless Energy attachment, future cost modifiers or switch effects. Treat the 3/37 (8.1081%) as a descriptive **per-attachment direct printed attack-funding deficit**, not a rate of harmful actions and certainly not a win-rate.

## Interpretation

This finding validates a concrete connection between a pre-existing theoretical resource concern (see [Agent30 original direct attack Energy census](agent30-public-inert-leading-attacks-20261101.md) and [Agent14 exact original partial-effect census](agent14-source-printed-bench-energy-move-census-20261031.md)) and **actual original-source Store-accepted owner decision traces**. Future actor policy designs can test a conditional, owner-only `registeredSixtyAttackEnergyCeiling` flag to challenge attachments to a Pokémon with no funded printed attack, but only after considering publicly available mobility, Ability use, Energy recovery and supported alternative recipients. A live forced-energy-target intervention needs matching original source physical deals, full action and physical target sidecars, native replies and an adverse-control sample. Static impossibility alone is insufficient reason to refuse a retreat-enabling or setup-enabling attachment.

The 16 games provide eight independent source shuffles with two correlated candidate opponents per shuffle. All outcomes include source native engine wins, losses and 260-action censors, and none has official paper-clock, optimal opponent play or comprehensive paper-prompt qualification. **Competitive-qualified games: 0. Published original opposing sixties: 29/32. The 99.5% fixed-deck target remains unverified.**

An effect-driven evolution may occur without a PlayCardAction for the evolved Pokémon; this census neither certifies nor refutes full native board effects.

This independent audit uses **successful dispatched root actions**, not requested/considered cards, hand availability, actual Energy removal, lasting trap, net deck mill, legal time, or counterfactual tactics. The documented source failure applies to the exact preregistered cohort under the frozen default policy and must not be projected to human performance. No 99.5% evidence.

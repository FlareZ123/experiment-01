## Mechanical conditions to preserve in tests

- **Budew: next-turn Item lock:** Attack with Budew (Itchy Pollen); requires the turn and Active Spot. English card IDs: me2pt5-16.
- **Vileplume: continuous Item lock:** Vileplume AOR Irritating Pollen Ability must remain enabled in play; locks both players. English card IDs: xy7-3.
- **Garbodor: conditional Ability lock:** Garbotoxin requires a Pokémon Tool attached and an enabled Ability. English card IDs: bw11-68, xy9-57.
- **Iron Thorns ex: Active Rule Box Ability lock:** Initialization requires Iron Thorns ex in Active; Future Pokémon are exempt. English card IDs: sv6-77.
- **Stoutland: Active Supporter lock:** Sentinel requires Stoutland in Active Spot and an enabled Ability. English card IDs: bw7-122.
- **Vileplume: Active Basic Pokémon attack lock:** Disgusting Pollen requires Vileplume BUS in Active Spot and an enabled Ability. English card IDs: sm3-6.
- **Snorlax: Active retreat lock:** Block prevents normal retreat while Snorlax is Active; switching can still work. English card IDs: pgo-55.

## Evaluation consequences

- Budew appears in every one of the nine **published** Regidrago lists; the two unpublished Regidrago lists remain unknown. Test Item access after a successful Itchy Pollen separately from merely drawing Budew.
- Item lock and Ability suppression can interact. For example, Garbotoxin can suppress Vileplume's Ability. Treat simultaneous lock markers as constrained board states, without adding their effects independently.
- Stress testing should include pre-lock, locked, lock-broken, and counter-gust states. Explicitly measure costs of ACE SPEC access, Supporter and Bench contention, Prize cards, and first-player assignment.
- Coverage is incomplete by design. This sheet selects seven clearly identifiable effects. Other control effects, attacks, Tools, Stadiums, and matchup-specific counters may be strategically decisive.

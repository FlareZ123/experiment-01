# Forest Seal Stone exception

Star Alchemy is printed on the Tool and remains usable under Path to the Peak. It is marked `exemptFromAbilityLock: true` in the pinned Twinleaf source. This corrects the broad wording in the champion exposure report.

The TPCi Rules Team ruling (10 November 2022) was quoted in [the official Pokémon forum](https://community.pokemon.com/en-us/discussion/4212/path-to-the-peak-and-forest-seal-stone). The Pokémon V can use the Tool's Ability without possessing that Ability itself. Item lock does not block attaching Pokémon Tools, though Tool interference and the one-VSTAR-Power-per-game rule still apply.

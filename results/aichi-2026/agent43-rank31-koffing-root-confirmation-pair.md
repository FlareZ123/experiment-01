# Rank 31: source-dealt Roxie/Koffing root confirmation and public damage

**Agent 43, 18 October 2026.** Evidence class: bounded authentic-source game-state counterfactual and an actual Store-dispatched decision, using the pinned Twinleaf engine. **No completed game, qualified matchup, or win-rate estimate.**

## Question

Does the pinned generic AI reject the actual optional **Koffing CEC 76** Blow-Away Bomb prompt after **Roxie CEC 205** discards that Koffing from the Aichi rank-31 source 60, and can a strictly scoped decision correction resolve the prompt without altering unrelated confirmations?

The printed Ability places one damage counter on *each* of the opponent's Pokémon when its owner chooses to use it. The [official printing](https://www.pokemon.com/uk/pokemon-tcg/pokemon-cards/series/sm12/76) says the counters are placed after Roxie's effect. The pinned engine's [generic confirmation resolver](https://github.com/twinleafgg/twinleafgg/blob/a9905a76a6c4103ba4b6cb9a49e86bad795c5c05/ptcg-server/src/simple-bot/prompt-resolver/confirm-prompt-resolver.ts) returns false for all ConfirmPrompts. This is a general policy limitation. The test below establishes one particular authentic encounter with it.

## Limits and next critical path

This trial concerns **opponent policy competence** against the unchanged rank-1 benchmark reference. It does not optimize or nominate a new final 60. Opponent and candidate decisions elsewhere still use a weak generic bot. The seed was selected after exposure was known; the observed physical rank-31 starting seat and unfinished 240-action path cannot estimate either start-stratified matchup win rate or full paper-rule fidelity. Opponent replies were not independently adjudicated for strategic competence. Both arms ended at the same Prize totals, which does not establish tactical equivalence.

The next useful test is a **prospectively chosen**, genuine rank31-versus-fixed-candidate source game in both actual starting orders, retaining nonexposures in the denominator, with attacks and opponent replies verified and the exact printing/lock gates checked. A Koffing-only correction can serve as an adversarial opponent-policy module once its scope is independently reviewed.

Current shared benchmark remains **29/32 exact source decklists, zero independently qualified competitive games, no demonstrated 99.5% win rate**.

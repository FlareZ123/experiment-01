**Five previously unblocked native Powers are newly blocked** across the four candidate-conditioned native opponent-root observations in **two independent source-deal groups**. Group 13 New Moon had Path to the Peak in both baseline and treatment; the pre-existing Perfection block is **excluded** from the five new blocks. In all other three arms no Stadium was in play. Thus the study demonstrates more than attaching a Tool: a real source-constructed Garbodor board co-occurs with genuine differential native Power suppression, and the candidate's next authentic Store root was reached.

No actual opponent Ability-use attempt, paper-qualified opponent reply, legal attack payoff, time-control outcome or game win can be inferred from a native predicate alone. No statistical matchup win rate is claimed.

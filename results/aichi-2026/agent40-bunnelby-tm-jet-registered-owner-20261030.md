Bunnelby's Omega Barrage permits two attacks. The actual printed `Technical Machine: Evolution` attack costs one Colorless, has **no attack damage**, and evolves up to two Benched Pokémon per attack by searching the owner's deck. Bench attaching `Jet Energy` supplies that Colorless and switches its new Benched recipient into Active. This allows a first-going-second **Tool equip → Jet promotion → Evolution → Omega Barrage Evolution** lock-building line when setup cards, valid targets, turn and prompts permit. The preceding Agent40 Jet rescue scorer only selected positive printed-damage attacks, so it systematically missed this discrete zero-damage TM line. The native Tool attack is separate from Bunnelby's printed Burrow and Rototiller attacks.

## Independent paper-rule confirmation

The Pokémon Company's [official 2025 Expanded-format feature](https://www.pokemon.com/uk/features/a-deep-dive-into-the-2025-pokemon-tcg-expanded-format) independently describes this exact archetype-specific line: Bunnelby with Ω Barrage uses TM: Evolution twice to evolve Oddish/Pidgey through Gloom/Pidgeotto into **Vileplume and Pidgeot ex**, with Guzma & Hala retrieving a Stadium, TM and Special Energy and Jet specifically used when Bunnelby requires Active promotion. This is evidence for **paper** gameplay, independent of our pinned Twinleaf implementation.

The [Pokémon Company's rulebook, Appendix M: Ancient Traits](https://assets.pokemon.com/assets/cms2/pdf/trading-card-game/rulebook/swsh7_rulebook_en.pdf) says that Ancient Traits are a separate class of card text from attacks and Abilities and therefore are not automatically suppressed by effects that prevent those from being used. Thus, merely Ability-locking a Bunnelby does **not** inherently disable its Omega Barrage trait. Treat more specific attack-prevention effects and Tool lock according to their exact text. This historical rulebook corroborates the mechanic rather than certifying every 2026 erratum; the supplied advanced rulebook and current text remain the primary in-repository source.

## Lock geometry and limits of a Stage2 Bench

The supplied complete card archive confirms the exact printed 2026 source card text:

- **Vileplume AOR 3, Irritating Pollen**: prevents **both players** from playing Item cards from hand, with no Active-position requirement. A Stage2 AOR evolved onto the Bench becomes a potential immediate Item-lock source on the same owner turn, subject to other Ability-suppression effects.
- **Vileplume BUS 6, Disgusting Pollen**: prevents the **opponent's Basic Pokémon** from attacking **only while BUS Vileplume occupies the Active Spot**. A rank15 double Evolution that ends with Bunnelby still Active and BUS on the Bench has **not** yet applied this Basic attack lock.
- **Bunnelby PRC 121 Ω Barrage**: two attacks in the turn are permitted, but the first Evolution attack is followed by the native second-attack decision and **the turn ends after completion**. There is no intervening ordinary retreat, Tool attachment or Stadium action. This means getting BUS into Active after the double-TM attack requires an additional card effect already resolving or a later turn and cannot be assumed by graph reachability alone.

Thus, even a genuinely settled first-turn double Evolution is **not an immediate complete Item+Basic attack lock** if BUS remains Benched. The native rank2 fixture establishes AOR Vileplume and Pidgeot ex on Bench, not a two-lock BUS/AOR board; an analogous rank15 dual-Vileplume setup still needs to solve Active position and counter-lock (e.g., opponent Ability lock) separately. This is a consequential ALS sequencing distinction; do not count a successful Stage2 search as a lock that requires a different board position.

## Exact *direct opening component conjunction*, not an ALS win-rate

The independent [source-derived BigInt enumerator](../../simulation/evaluation/agent40-bunnelby-direct-opening-count.cjs) examines an **unordered eight-card subset**: seven opening cards plus one natural turn draw, with the required combination **at least one Bunnelby, at least one of the six listed evolvable Basics, and either Guzma & Hala/Tag Call or both held TM and Jet**. The 60 physical cards are partitioned into disjoint categories; overlap between the Supporter path and held TM+Jet is counted only once. All **2,558,620,845** eight-card combinations enter the denominator. The opponent's mulligans/bonus draws are excluded.

| Original rank | Exact favorable subsets | Unconditional fraction | Conditional on seven-card Basic-valid opening |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **2** | 228,532,145 | **8.93185%** | **10.36888%** |
| **15** | 210,475,593 | **8.22613%** | **9.54962%** |

The conditional column divides by the exact **86.14093%** chance of at least one Basic in opening seven, since the conjunction necessarily already contains two Basics within the eight cards and therefore a valid seven-card opener. **These fractions are neither full ALS probabilities nor bounds on them.** Other paths can find Bunnelby/targets through Artazon, search Items and other card actions without this conjunction. Even hands with this conjunction may fail due to two-card discard constraints, singleton protection, Prize configuration, lack of Pokémon Bench placement, or actual game response. The calculation is a reproducible measurement of one narrow direct-card-access channel; it does not replace native matchup trials.

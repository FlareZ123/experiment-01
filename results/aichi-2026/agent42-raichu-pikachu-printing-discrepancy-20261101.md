# Agent 42: printing identity matters in the two original Raichu lists

1 November 2026. Source: `results/aichi-2026/full-decklists.json` plus bundled English card pool (`resources/cards/en/`). This is a printed card-definition fact, not a matchup test.

Rank4 and rank17 are both Raichu/Electrode Combo and share 40 copies by card name. They differ by 20 named slots, but **21 exact-printing slots**. The extra discrepancy is **Pikachu**: rank4 uses **Lost Origin 52 (LOR 52)** and rank17 uses **XY 42 (XY 42)**, one copy each. The paper card database identifies LOR Pikachu as HP60 with **Pika Dash** Ability (if any Energy is attached, no Retreat Cost), and attack **Whimsy Tackle** (three Energy). XY Pikachu HP60 has no Ability and has **Nuzzle** (one Colorless, coin-flip Paralysis) and **Quick Attack** (two Colorless). Both are the same printed name for Alolan Raichu evolution purposes. The difference can affect Active opening/retreat and attack options in some states, subject to applicable locks.

Across all nine published Regidrago original 60s, the 6-21 slot name-distance range also equals the exact-printing distance range for every one of 36 pairs. This has been independently calculated from the registry, not from match simulations.

A missing-rank25 deck remains unverified, and no assumption about its Pikachu printing is justified. The rank4/rank17 name metric is a lower bound on printed interaction variation. No competitive-qualified games, no claimed 99.5% winner.

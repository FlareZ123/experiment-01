From the provided card pool and the [advanced player rulebook C-18](../../resources/advanced-players-rulebook.md), Mew VMAX's **Cross Fusion Strike** costs two Colorless; it can use a Benched Fusion Strike Genesect V's **Techno Blast** (210 damage) without paying Genesect's printed Metal/Metal/Colorless cost. This line still requires correctly evolved Mew VMAX, a legal active attacker, the DCE attached without applicable suppressing/restrictive effects, a Benched Genesect, and genuine legal native prompt/effect settlement. Merely holding any subset of these cards does not prove that an attack was executable.

## Additional pure owner-prompt discovery: donor order can suppress damage

Independent inspection of the shared [actor copied-attack chooser](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent13-actor-only-choose-attack.cjs) found that `chooseActorCopiedAttack` currently chooses the **first eligible source attack**, except for narrow opt-in Gengar cases. Its default score does not distinguish Mew V's non-damaging `Energy Mix` from Genesect V's 210-damage `Techno Blast`. In the actual card pool, both are Fusion Strike Pokémon and may appear on the Bench at the same time. As a result, **reordering the same two legal displayed donors changes which attack the owner-only prompt policy chooses**.

A committed [three-case pure regression](../../tests/agent48-mew-original-copy-option-order.test.cjs) explicitly constructs the same owner-visible two-donor menu with Mew V first versus Genesect V first and demonstrates the policy's different selections. It separately verifies that the existing owner evolution frontier selects a previous-turn Mew V as an eligible Mew VMAX evolution, while it rejects a just-played Mew and an earlier global turn. All three tests passed in a mocked CommonJS V8 harness. They have **not** been validated with a compiled Store. This is evidence of a general source-player *decision-ranking weakness*, not proof that the exact original rank-5 physical games encountered that menu.

This strengthens the release requirement: an owner Mew evolution and DCE attachment are necessary components of some powerful attack lines, while a native `ChooseAttackPrompt` must also select, resolve and verify the actual donor. A source-driver comparison that counts only evolution roots or nonzero attack announcements will still miss this strategic defect.

### Scope, reproducibility and decision

This is a genuine **one-flag matched mechanical policy test** with a large difference in native result: two previously capped rank11 games now terminate through source-side Prize completion when given the same physical deals, and rank6's prior failure remains recorded. It shows why historical bot-vs-bot results using a deficient source policy must not be treated as deck strength: source bot incompetence alone can censor games, and correcting it can completely change apparent matchup outcomes.

**No field game is qualified as competitive.** Both rank11 matches are original, retrospective, candidate-first historical seeds, not source-first stress or prospectively balanced samples. The source-owned early Garbodor Tool-lock plan did not complete, generic/native prompt choices still have hidden-information and actor-competence limitations, paper Cologne quota was not enabled, clock/rules fidelity remains partial, original source rank6 champion prematurely transitions to sudden death, and three of 32 published opponent 60s are still missing. Fixed 99.5% win probability remains unsupported.

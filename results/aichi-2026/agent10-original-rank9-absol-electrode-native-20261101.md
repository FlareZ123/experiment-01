This source **does attack repeatedly** under the generic bot, unlike rank 5 Mew/Genesect's four surviving zero-source-attack games. Yet the exact printed attacks used by the original rank 9 opponent are:

The source's **2 Electrode-GX**, **1 Mega Absol ex**, and **2 Toedscruel** are integral potential development paths in its registered 60. **None of the three surviving source attack traces contains an Electrode-GX, Mega Absol ex, or Toedscruel attack**, and the accepted source PlayCard sidecars show no natural evolution into those lines. This absence is an *observed coverage failure*, not proof that an evolution or attacker was legally held and playable at a particular owner root. In the A champion arm, the source did successfully accept a **Plumeria BUS** PlayCard root at turn 44, showing partial source Trainer execution, but this alone does not establish credible source strategy.

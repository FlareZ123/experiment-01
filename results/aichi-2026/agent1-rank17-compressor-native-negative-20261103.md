I [registered that limitation before replay](registrations/agent1-rank17-compressor-trace-correction-20261103.json), enabled native read-only root logging and made missing ledgers fail closed. The corrected actual full ledger supplies the valid zero-play observation.

A second reporting bug required handling the native ledger's bare card name `Battle Compressor` rather than assuming a set suffix. The revised `sourceCompressorRoots` now recognizes both forms and its pure test includes the bare form. Independently scanning the full corrected archive confirms no actual Compressor root.

The source's 33 Pokémon plays and zero Trainers are a serious **opponent decision-policy shortcoming**. The present experiment does **not** prove any on-turn owner hand actually held a Compressor while the prerequisite Basic was visible. It therefore cannot distinguish inaccessible original opportunities from an unselected legal Item, policy interception, or other hidden sequencing. No evidence suggests the static Pikachu XY change is harmful; no game-level benefit was observed on these matched deals.

## Critical source policy gap and falsification

A player's first genuine search through their own deck reveals the available card identities. The source-rank6 Iron Thorns owner can therefore learn on the first turn that its singleton Thunder Mountain is missing from the deck after a real Tag Call search. The existing separate first-turn owner-only Tag Call adapter and third-turn broad source policy lost that knowledge between the two actions. On an earlier published same-seed branch, the source had instead searched with Tag Call on turn 3, discovered missing Thunder then, selected the available Speed Lightning alternative and genuinely attacked. The suspected issue was **how long the owner kept legally learned K1 information**, independently of any candidate list change.

Before implementing the new bridge we committed [the original three-deal, two-policy registration](registrations/agent6-cross-turn-owner-k1-tagcall-native-20261101.json) at `41a6c6a`. Only the explicit retention of the previous authenticated owner-deck search changes between arms. The three known positive Tag deals were selected from prior work **retrospectively**, so the contrasts are mechanical fixtures and not population game trials.

The later [pure owner K1 gate](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent6-owner-k1-turn-bridge.cjs) rejects wrong owner, seat, earlier prompt, unchanged-state mismatches and unavailable paid resources. ## Interpretation, limitations and integration request

This demonstrates a genuine **cross-turn information-architecture defect** in a composition of otherwise legal owner-only policies. If an early accepted owner search reveals a singleton required for an archetype line has become inaccessible, a later search planner should retain that *own* K1 observation, subject to publicly knowable intervening changes. A state-resetting policy that forgets K1 can spend a Supporter on a known-empty search and forfeit a legal attack, even when the required fallback is in its originally observed deck.

The K1 predicate is deliberately narrow, source-family-specific and opt-in. It currently requires unchanged own Prize count and exact T1→T3 turn, preattached DCE, held G&H, a previous owner-deck Speed witness, two disposable hand cards and available normal Supporter and Energy permissions. It does not yet implement a general history update for later draws, deck returns, Prize exchanges or multiple searches, nor claim those events would be automatically safe to ignore. A principled shared stateful owner-knowledge model is more general than hard-coding a rank6 fallback. The new rule is a developmental cross-turn positive control and remains outside Agent41's qualified same-root chooser. The prior seed itself was selected knowing Tag Call resources and is not a prospective test of prevalence, game win rate or superiority of one fixed 60.

# Agent 11: read-only-backed resource-tutor × known-dead attack joint-policy counterfactual, original Aichi ranks 5 and 11

**3 November 2026. Result: two genuine same-deal developmental native outcome conversions, zero competitively qualified games.** This is a **retrospective interaction study** using previously examined seeds, not a new prospective performance sample. A source win in pinned Twinleaf against a rank-5 actor that never attacks is not evidence of competitiveness against the human Aichi field.

## Objective and treatment contrast

The immutable original rank-5 Mew/Genesect and original rank-11 Absol/Garbodor 60s were loaded as paper-source printings without card swaps. The same canonical actor root and all existing shared options applied to both real physical seats. We enabled the **existing, default-off, public-state known-dead first-attack choice** (`AGENT7_KNOWN_DEAD_ATTACK_ROOT_DEV=true`) **symmetrically in both arms and both seats**. Within that corrected-attack common policy, the controlled difference was Agent11's **owner-hand/public-only after-Pass resource tutor** (held `Battle VIP Pass FST` / native `Professors Letter BKT`), with live owner deck-search prompts.

The question is whether paid resource access has any **downstream effect after the shared known-dead attack-selector failure is repaired**. Previously, Agent11's baseline without the known-dead option recorded 16 accepted Items but all 81 original rank-11 attacks were Terminal Period against zero-damage targets, all futile. The independent, genuinely prospectively seeded [Agent50 study](agent50-preregistered-rank11-symmetric-known-dead-20261101.md) had already demonstrated a native material effect of the known-dead choice without this combined tutor factor. Our distinct research contribution is the controlled **interaction** in one additional original-rank opponent pair, with full public effect transitions and exact original physical replays.

Native source terminal after **79 total Store actions**, against control's 90-action cap and zero source Prizes.

**Rank-5 seat 1, seed `11312108`, rank-11 source seat 0.** After identical initial physical setup and seven identical native root actions, control source passed. Treatment source played first-turn VIP to Bench `Latias ex SSP` and `Oranguru V ASR`; real Professor's Letter fetched two Darkness Energy; attached one to Active Mega Absol. In both cases, actual 2-Prize attack transitions occurred only after the printed-energy threshold was met. The source's earlier useless Terminal Period choices before the third Energy remain a future setup-planning concern. The native opponent policy recorded **zero attacks across all 32 combined-policy attempts**. It can play some resources, but its failure to attack prevents interpreting the source wins as meaningful competitive victory.

## Causal interpretation and release veto

The **resource tutor effect conditional on the known-dead attack choice** is evidenced by two same-deal **Cap→S** paired reversals and two separate improved capped Prize positions, while the corrected known-dead option was held fixed between each control and treatment. They are bounded native findings and are sensitive to the simulated opponent's inability to produce a single attack.

The original tutor-only run without known-dead attack choice had **zero native terminals**, four native errors, and 81 completely futile Terminal Period selections. Combining the two default-off components produced both actual native paid 200-damage Claw outcomes and tutor-to-KO turn sequences. This is **consistent with complementarity**, but is **not a statistically powered or fully controlled 2×2 causal interaction estimate**, because the source branch diverges and seed selection is retrospective. Do not treat the error reductions alone as proof of a paper fix.

Remaining blockers before promoting this to a competitive deck evaluation include: (1) independently paper-valid costs and full Trainer/attack/prompt effects; (2) a hidden-information-safe generic rank-5 Mew/Genesect attacking and recovery policy with nontrivial reply after Absol acts; (3) source-side Supporter competition, lock and Bench opportunity accounting; (4) prospective fresh native input seeds and official Aichi paper clock; (5) full 32 original 60s (ranks 23/25/28 missing) and a selected, invariant candidate 60. Keep both finite action caps and all non-result cases in the denominator.

**Project status after this study: 29/32 authenticated original opponent sixties, zero competitive-qualified games, no fixed 60 selected and no demonstrated 99.5% win rate.**

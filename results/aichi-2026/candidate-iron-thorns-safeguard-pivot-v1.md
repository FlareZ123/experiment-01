**Total: 5 Pokémon + 45 Trainers + 10 Energy = 60 cards.**

Derived from the published original Aichi rank6 Iron Thorns ex list: retain 4 Iron Thorns ex, remove 2 Camping Gear BST122 and 1 Captivating Poké Puff STS99, and add 1 Mimikyu PAL97, 1 Jet Energy PAL190, and 1 Basic Psychic Energy MEE13. Preserve all remaining original printed counts. No Temple of Sinnoh is included in v1.

**Intended game plan:** put Iron Thorns ex Active to activate Initialization and pressure opponents dependent on Rule Box Pokémon Abilities. Benched Mimikyu, a non-Rule-Box Pokémon with Safeguard, can be built with Basic Psychic and a Colorless Energy to attack with Ghost Eye. Jet Energy can attach to a prepared Benched Mimikyu and move it Active, which can improve the opponent Iron Thorns mirror despite leaving Initialization. The one Mimikyu and one Basic Psychic are each Prize vulnerable. The owner policy is a deliberate developmental intervention and does not represent full tournament-grade play.

**Actual comparative result:** [48-game printed Safeguard pilot](../methodology/2026-11/agent12-safeguard-pivot-48-native-20261109.md) reported 11W1L11cap1error native against original rank6 control 7W7L9cap1error, with broad source-paired uncertainty. [Fresh prospective 360-root 48-game comparison](../methodology/2026-11/agent12-temple-owner-fresh-360-root-actual-20261109.md) against otherwise identical Temple v2 produced Safeguard **16W7L1engine-error** versus Temple **11W12L1engine-error**, 13 both-terminal source-matched pairs, sharp Temple minus v1 native difference **[-4,-2]**. This favors v1 in the examined native population and is not a verified paper match win rate.

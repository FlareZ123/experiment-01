# Rank-13 original Regidrago: prospective public flat copied-attack study stopped by missing joint setup

**Agent 8 | 3 November 2026 | Prospective originally registered native development experiment | Zero qualified games**

## Frozen method and execution

A new, information-set-limited, default-off `preferFasterNominalCopy` policy evaluates **only the attack names, flat printed damage and native-blocked choices actually offered in the acting owner's copied-attack menu**, plus the opponent's public Active HP and counters. It selects an alternative when it reduces nominal attacks to Knock Out and raises flat printed damage by at least 60. All other choices preserve the original first-offered policy. It uses no opponent hand identities, owner deck order, or hidden Prize identities. This is **only a heuristic**: printed base damage ignores many card effects, attack text, Weakness/Resistance, protection and future turns. The live native Store remains responsible for legality and settlements.

The pre-execution [eight independent registered physical seed groups](registrations/agent8-rank13-public-flat-copy-prospective-native-20261103.json) covered both source seats (four each). Both unchanged printed 60s were the exact rank-13 Hiromu Sasaki Regidrago and rank-1 Yasunori Kato Shadow Rider from `full-decklists.json`. Both arms used the same natural evolution, actual native owner Mysterious Treasure/Double Dragon payment and Apex choice roots, paper Celestial Roar/minimum attachment correction, and 160-action cap. Only the opt-in copied-attack chooser differed. Common owner-only policies and the opponent's generic decision policy remain **competitively unqualified**. The true Aichi Open 25-minute or 35-minute event clock was not simulated.

The existing [manual workflow](../../.github/workflows/agent8-rank13-phantom-copy-native-20261103.yml) now runs a precompile [registration contract](../../simulation/evaluation/agent8-rank13-public-copy-preflight.cjs) and the [native paired runner](../../simulation/evaluation/agent8-rank13-public-copy-prospective-native.cjs). **Exact observed results:** 16/16 native attempts returned and 8/8 source-plus-opponent full physical starting deals matched between policy arms. All eight pairs had identical complete recorded decision-trace SHA-256 and source copied-attack selections (zero in either arm). Zero native errors and zero verified competitive games. Across the eight control deals, exactly **three had an Active VSTAR at least once**, **five had an eligible Dragon in the visible discard at least once**, and **two had enough nominal Energy for Apex at least once**. No owner-root observation in any game satisfied all three simultaneously. Individual marginal setup metrics therefore failed to predict actual copy-decision exposure.

## Discriminating negative and follow-up

The source-seat-1 **seed 2686109268** is the strongest development target: source Regidrago VSTAR was Active by global turn 16, with Double Dragon Energy and Fire Energy attached, and the owner actually held **Dragapult ex TWM**, **Quick Ball FST** and **Battle Compressor PHF**, while its visible discard contained no copyable Dragon. A later public root showed two Double Dragon, Fire, and two Grass Energy attached but still no public copyable donor. There were 18 owner decision snapshots with a held Battle Compressor, Regidrago in play and no payload, all from this same deal. The real root policies produced zero copied selections. This is a **public/owner-known tactical discard opportunity**, with K0/K1 deck-search and discard-target availability still uncertain; an unplayed proposed Quick Ball or Compressor line is not a native paid chain.

Other source deals show the reverse: **1481213574** and **2381994580** had Active VSTAR and public Dragons but no nominally payable Active Energy. That suggests testing discard/attachment/Active positioning as a *coupled* line rather than upgrading copied-attack choice in isolation.

The previously observed rank13 seed `2295622320` did give eight native Apex/Covert copies and a 6-Prize terminal after choosing Phantom Dive in a separate retrospective experiment. **That selected prior deal is methodologically separate from this independent sample**, and supplies no missing positive result here.

## Decision

Retain the new public-flat copied-attack policy as a reversible, development-only candidate and a tested behavioral hypothesis. It was never exercised in these fresh games, so **this study neither supports nor refutes its conditional tactical value and supplies no measured win improvement**. Next decision-changing experiment should first produce an authentic, owner-payable donor-to-discard chain on a naturally exposed funded Active Regidrago, with correct source payment/search and competing connector opportunity accounted for. Reuse archived data and existing hooks before another compiled trial; preregister any new independent seeds before gameplay.

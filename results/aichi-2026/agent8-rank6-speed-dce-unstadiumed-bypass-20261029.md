# Agent 8: rank6 source-first Volt Cyclone without Thunder Mountain

**29 October 2026. Printed-card/rules deduction plus exact finite-deck calculation. No new pinned Store execution or competitive win claim.**

The original rank6 Kazuma Kashi Iron Thorns 60 has **4 Iron Thorns ex Basics, 4 Speed Lightning Energy, 1 Double Colorless Energy, 1 Thunder Mountain, 2 Guzma & Hala and 2 Tag Call**. Printed Iron Thorns ex TWM77 uses **LCC** for Volt Cyclone. Speed Lightning Energy RCL173 provides Lightning; DCE provides two Colorless. Thus one Speed and one DCE attached to the same Active Iron Thorns across the first and second own turns pay LCC **without playing Thunder Mountain**. The first player cannot attack on its initial turn, but can manually attach Energy. The printed Speed effect draws two on Lightning attachment. Sources: [Iron Thorns](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/en/TWM/77), [Speed Lightning Energy](https://www.limitlesstcg.com/cards/RCL/173), and the provided advanced player's rulebook.

The canonical [rank6 scripted opener](../../simulation/twinleaf/rank6-broad-thorns-policy.cjs) plans DCE with Thunder Mountain, optionally using G&H/Tag Call. Its `planOpeningHand` does not recognize a pre-attached Speed Lightning Energy as satisfying the Lightning part without the Stadium. The generic fallback might still play the valid two-attachment route, so this is **a scripted-policy coverage gap**, not evidence that an actual native opponent missed an available attack.

For a conservative, opponent-unobstructed source-first structural subset, take seven cards with at least one Basic, six randomized Prizes, one natural draw on source turn one, and a second normal draw on its next turn. Ignore bonus draws, Trainer searches and opponent disruption. Require **at least one Speed and the sole DCE in those first nine cards**, with no Thunder Mountain, Guzma & Hala or Tag Call among the nine. The source can attach whichever of Speed/DCE is first available on its initial turn and attach the other on its second turn.

Exact enumeration of `(Basic,DCE,Speed,Thunder,G&H,Tag,other)=(4,1,4,1,2,2,46)` over first-nine physical-card compositions with two distinguished draws, respecting a Basic in the *initial seven*: **13,344,865,308 favorable / 425,221,917,120 valid = 3.13832960%**. Reproducer: [BigInt exhaustive census](../../scripts/agent8-rank6-speed-dce-without-stadium.cjs). An independent Python run of 250,000 shuffled physical decks saw **3,124 favorable among 99,318 Basic-valid openers** (3.14545%), consistent with the exact fraction.

This 3.1383% is an **unobstructed original-source structural access subset**, distinct from the [32.0807% rank6 going-second single-attachment resource model](agent8-rank6-second-start-attack-access-20261029.md). It is not the probability of attack execution or a win. Source-first play can add cards with Speed's own draw, and opponent actions may remove Energy or change the turn. A future policy test should compare whether a real, owner-visible Speed+DCE line was preserved, actually attached and accepted by the original Store, especially when Thunder is unavailable. Retain non-opportunities and actual starting seats; zero competitively qualified games.

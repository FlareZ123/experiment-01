## Paper requirement and known engine omission

The supplied [Advanced Player's Rulebook v3.4, E. Win and Loss](../../resources/manual/EN_advanced_manual-2025-transcription-structured.md) requires a fresh six-Prize tiebreaker, won by the first player to have **fewer Prize cards remaining** than the opponent. The [official CL Aichi 2026 Open League procedures](https://www.pokemon-card.com/info/005445.html) separately specify event-specific clocks, playoffs and post-clock adjudication. This test targets a **native replay already initiated by the engine after a simultaneous tie**. It is not a model of the Open League Swiss 25-minute cutoff or the Top32 post-clock overtime.

The pinned native `checkWinner` implementation treats a first strict lead `[5,6]` or `[6,5]` as ongoing while both players have a live Active Pokémon and neither has taken all six Prizes. The internal `isSuddenDeath` flag is insufficient to correct that mismatch. Agent3 previously confirmed it in isolated native reducer fixtures; we reuse that provenance and do not present the discrepancy as newly discovered.

## New read-only paper-replay adjudicator

[Native replay first-Prize witness](../../simulation/evaluation/agent24-native-replay-first-lead-witness.cjs) evaluates a caller-supplied original native Store state **without modifying any player or winner**. It requires an authenticated replay epoch input, an accepted and settled original root, the native `isSuddenDeath` marker, live two-seat Active boards and exactly six one-card-or-empty Prize slots per player. It compares the public remaining-Prize counts and emits `PAPER_REPLAY_FIRST_PRIZE_LEAD` plus seat **0 or 1** as the paper winner when a strict lead exists.

**Fail-closed behavior** is explicit: without a verified replay marker, on a normal game, with unresolved prompts, without a full six-slot replay structure, or during a non-player-turn phase, the sentinel emits an unverified status and **no winner**. Equal counts are reported as `NO_STRICT_PRIZE_LEAD`. Every result carries `paperGameOutcomeQualified:false` because the observer does not verify the original tie, the legal action that took the first Prize, all intervening card-text semantics, the 25-minute clock or event-stage adjudication. [Seven pure tests](../../tests/agent24-native-replay-first-lead-witness.test.cjs) cover seat symmetry, equal counts, normal-game rejection, false epoch, unsettled root, invalid board and read-only mutation safety.

The immediate reusable product is the **read-only, owner-policy-invisible paper stop witness**. The canonical bilateral runner can call it only after retaining the native reset ordinal, authentic fresh six-Prize source deal and each paper-valid root/prompt/payment/effect.

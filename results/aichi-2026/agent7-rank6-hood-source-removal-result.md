## Strategic and methodological conclusion

This establishes a **source-opponent counteraction** that directly challenges the static paper-text inference “Hood on Bench safely bypasses Initialization.” The conditionally available protected Ability is mechanically real, and a source-ranked Iron Thorns 60 can also remove the Tool in a real dealt game. An optimizer needs to account for the source's Tool removal and the opportunity cost of spending ACE SPEC or Supporter, plus whether its own setup/attack line keeps pace.

Four selected responses on **16 inspected** true-deal seeds measure the frozen bot's mechanical response opportunities. They are **not an estimate of a human opponent's counterplay frequency**. The policy's selective Faba prompt is generic and may choose something else. Important remaining qualification gaps include realistic source and candidate tactical decisions, precise attacked-target damage/effects, independently replayable serializable reducers, engine mutation/card equivalence fidelity, clock and all 32 exact opponent lists.

The cross-archetype record now contains authentic rank-11 Garbotoxin source-state protection, rank-6 active Iron Thorns protection, and rank-6 authentic printed Tool removal. No demonstrated win rate, target-eligible final list, or competitively qualified game exists. Current exact Aichi source field remains **29/32**, missing ranks 23, 25 and 28.

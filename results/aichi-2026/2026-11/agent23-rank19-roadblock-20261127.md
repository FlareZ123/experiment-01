# Rank19 Roadblock: proactive Bench denial

2026-11-27. Agent23. Development branch agent23/rank19-moltres-shadow-rider-20261127, commit 51ebd4b166f438ba03a664aab0c44f75a368d87e.

Existing source v2 only prioritizes playing Sudowoodo when the opponent already has 5 or more Benched Pokémon. Roadblock's exact GRI66 text caps them at four, even in conjunction with Sky Field (smaller cap applies). Waiting lets the candidate develop a fifth position and possibly use an extra Underworld Door first. The new source rule proactively plays an exact visible held Sudowoodo from an offered actor Bench root at four opponent Bench occupants, or three with live Sky Field. It requires an established source main attacker and at most three occupied source Bench positions so at least one ordinary-cap slot stays open. It does not presume active Wobbuffet's Bide Barricade is suppressed without public evidence. Direct terminal attack and lock-clearing precede Roadblock. Other fallback rules are unchanged; this is an additive root priority, not a claim the old v2 policy cannot choose Sudowoodo elsewhere.

13/13 constructed V8 helper cases passed. Both v3/v4 development policy call the helper. Tests cover cap boundary, Sky Field, occupied slot reserve, existing Sudowoodo, live/suppressed/unknown Wobbuffet, absence of main attacker, no offered root, incorrect held card and wrong target seat. File tests/agent23-rank19-roadblock.test.cjs.

Potential real value against both fixed Shadow Rider builds: one fewer prospective Bench position reduces room for VMAX, Clefairy and utility support, and for Full Moon Rondo reduces damage by 20 per removed Bench Pokémon (40 to Dragon with live Fairy Zone). This is not a predicted match win, and one of the source's own Bench slots is spent. When already five opponent Bench occupants, they may discard their least useful Pokémon, which can deny source gust prizes; this is why preemptive cap is a distinct action. No shared worker, candidate deck, native game, CI, or owner gate changed.

| Source printing | Source copies | Original ranks | Public card category | Trusted third-party source listing |

Limitless explicitly marks **Japanese Expanded: legal** and **international Expanded: not legal** for these three printed identities and says none have been released internationally. The original Aichi event is Japanese Expanded. The translations are unofficial, so this signal is **external evidence rather than an official Japanese-language rules determination**. The source data's `language:'en'` identifies Limitless's translated display row and does not transform the underlying Japanese physical print into an English release. **Palace Book's `NAN25` is an electronic catalogue discriminator for a physically unnumbered SM-P promotional card**, independently established by [Agent42's official Japanese card-source crosswalk](../../methodology/agent42-palace-book-unnumbered-physical-promo-key-20261101.md). Its official Japanese card page ID is `34382`. Neither `NAN25` nor the source file's `number` field may be described as a printed physical collector number.

The card effects themselves are **not** interchangeable. Palace Book's translated text draws three and ends the turn; Player's Ceremony is a Stadium whose optional draw-two ends the turn; Palace Belt modifies the owner's start-of-turn draw when attached to the Active Pokémon. These are materially different from a generic Item/Tool/Stadium placeholder. **Existing project tests already cover important staged native mechanics:** [the Palace Book and Palace Belt genuine pinned Store tests](../../../tests/palace_promos_store.test.cjs) exercise Item draw/end-turn, Vileplume Item lock, Active-only Tool draw and Jamming Tower suppression; [Agent49's Player's Ceremony test](../../../tests/agent49-native-players-ceremony-store.test.cjs) uses a **research-owned registration hook** and resolves an optional draw-two/end-turn in both seats. This work reuses their evidence rather than claiming to implement those effects anew. Stage tests do **not** establish native full-deck registration, all printed timing subtleties, legal owner target selection, opponent strategic competence, or qualified natural source matches. The updated source metadata records the existing staged-test location and keeps `nativeFullGameQualified:false`.

## Decision consequence and explicit limitations

The correct paper target format for reproducing the Aichi field is **Japan Expanded as of 10 May 2026**. An optimizer that silently checks international Expanded legality may reject these three original opponents' printed cards. An optimizer that silently accepts translated Japanese records without source provenance may falsely certify a proposed fixed deck or allow unimplemented actions. Retain separate ruleset, type classification, translated effect text, exact printed identity, release chronology, historical banlist and native engine implementation flags. Public card-category coverage is now complete for the 29 *source manifests*, but game action coverage and legality remain materially incomplete.

The highest-value next step is a real source-card implementation coverage census that joins each of the 1,740 original cards to pinned native class/effect support and a genuine, symmetric opponent decision policy. Prove 60/60 original deck registration for each of all 29 and ultimately 32 field entries, exercise paper timing of Japanese-only cards and the prize/draw state, and compare wins only after **all** original opponents are qualified. Do not report 100% card-category metadata as 100% simulation fidelity.

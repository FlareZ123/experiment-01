Both existing candidates have one Guzma and two VS Seeker, exactly three possible direct gust Supporter uses assuming no resource denial.

Rank 5's only Energy are four Double Colorless. Its direct colored attacks are unpayable. Mew VMAX's Cross Fusion Strike can copy Genesect V's Techno Blast (210), Mew V's Psychic Leap (70) or Energy Mix; no listed Fusion Strike attack exceeds 210. A single Muscle Band raises this to at most 230 against Psychic Shadow Rider VMAX (Darkness Weakness, no applicable weakness). No Fusion Strike Energy, Power Tablet or other damage-increasing card appears. No multi-target damage in these payable attacks. Two attacks are required to KO a healthy 320-HP VMAX.

## Lockbridge-specific attack-denial window

Lockbridge's single Temple of Sinnoh adds an exact structural complement to Shadow Rider V's Shadow Mist. Temple makes attached DCE provide just one Colorless and lose its other effects; Shadow Mist (P, 10) prevents the opponent playing Special Energy and Stadium cards from hand on its following turn. If each opposing Pokemon has at most one attached DCE, no rank-5 attack is payable while Temple remains and Shadow Mist restriction holds. The cheapest relevant attack is Mew VMAX Cross Fusion Strike for CC; direct Energy Mix needs P. Thus no attack-mediated acceleration or Psychic Leap escape is payable either.

A concrete opening if resources permit: going second, select Wobbuffet as the sole initial Active, hold other Basics in hand until own turn. Bide Barricade denies non-Psychic Genesect V Fusion Strike System, Crobat V Dark Asset and Dedenne-GX Dedechange during opposing first turn. Rank 5 has Escape Rope, but with no Bench it cannot displace the sole Wobbuffet; first player cannot attack or ordinary Guzma. Opponent can still use Items, bench, attach one DCE, and Psychic-host Forest Seal Stone. On own first turn bench Shadow Rider V and Latias ex, retreat Wobbuffet free with Psychic Skyliner, attach one Psychic to V, play Temple, Shadow Mist. Opponent's existing DCE cannot fund Cross Fusion Strike under Temple and cannot add another DCE or replace Stadium from hand while locked. This is a tempo line competing with immediate Radiant Gate, not a guaranteed opening, and releasing Wobbuffet re-enables Genesect draw.

**Exact live breaks:** rank 5 has Field Blower ×1 and Lost Vacuum ×1 (remove Temple without playing a Stadium); Pokémon Ranger ×1 clears Shadow Mist, then allows Stadium or attachment. VS Seeker ×2 recovers Ranger. The opponent can proactively recover Ranger with VS Seeker after use, denying Girafarig an exposed discard target. Temple is only one copy in Lockbridge, so spending it into held Stadium removal is costly; no candidate stadium recursion. Do not classify attack lock as a permanent win merely because it exists for one turn.

**Permanent resource-denial target:** if Girafarig can Lost Zone at least three of the four opposing DCE, then Temple alone prevents every opposing attack, even without Shadow Mist. Special Charge recovers discarded Special Energy but never Lost Zoned ones. To make this lock durable, all opposing Stadium replacement/removal must also be unavailable (2 Sky Field, 2 Path, Field Blower, Lost Vacuum). This is a stronger but narrow late-state condition. Do not waste turns chasing it when the gust race already wins; a competent opponent should preserve a Temple answer and avoid exposing DCE to Get Lost.

Opponent policy must preserve and use Item/Ranger counters and hand disruption when valuable; do not engineer its exhaustion to force a favorable trace.

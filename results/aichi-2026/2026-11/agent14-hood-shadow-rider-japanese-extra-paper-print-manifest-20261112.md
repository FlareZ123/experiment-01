## Legality checks

- **60 cards:** 18 Pokémon + 31 Trainers + 11 Basic Psychic Energy = 60.
- **Copy limits:** no non-Energy card name exceeds four copies.
- **ACE SPEC:** exactly one, Unfair Stamp. Its official card page states the one-ACE-SPEC-per-deck rule.
- **Banned list:** no candidate card is banned under the 19 February 2026 Extra list.
- **Marshadow false-positive resolved:** the candidate's Marshadow is the Resetting Hole card, represented by SM12a 064/173. The banned entry is specifically Marshadow SM3+ (and equivalent printings) with the Let Loose / やぶれかぶれ Ability. Do not treat every Marshadow printing as banned.
- **Other name traps:** Forest Seal Stone (森の封印石) is not the banned Forest of Giant Plants (巨大植物の森); Unfair Stamp (アンフェアスタンプ) is not the banned Reset Stamp (リセットスタンプ).
- **Japanese language:** every selected card is a Japanese printing; the English proxies are not to be placed in the paper deck.

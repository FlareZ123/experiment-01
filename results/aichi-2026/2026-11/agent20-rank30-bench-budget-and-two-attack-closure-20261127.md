## The actionable change

Against the authenticated rank-30 Clefairy list, neither Shadow Rider candidate should fill Sky Field simply because it can. For an undamaged 320-HP VMAX, seven of our Benched Pokémon enables an opposing eight-Bench Clefairy to deal 320; six of our Benched Pokémon caps it at 300. A second independent hazard is Tapu Lele-GX Energy Drive: rank 30 has only seven Energy cards (5 basic Psychic + 2 Telepathic Psychic), so distributing our Energy among attack-ready VMAX rather than stacking it also prevents a Lele one-hit KO. With nine Energy on our Active VMAX and seven on Lele, Energy Drive reaches 320; at eight on ours it caps at 300. This is a worst-case resource bound, not a forecast that the opponent can naturally concentrate all seven.

A policy should (1) evaluate the *projected opponent reply* including Sky Field, not just the currently visible Bench; (2) prefer six or fewer own Benched Pokémon while a healthy VMAX is the relevant three-Prize target; (3) keep each of two VMAX independently funded, preferably 3+4 Psychic rather than 7+0; (4) avoid offering vulnerable three-Prize TAG TEAM targets; (5) convert to a two-Prize gust race once seven total attached Psychic makes Max Geist 220. Do not bench or attach extra cards solely to maximize immediate engine or draw scores. This is a conditional priority: an extra Bench occupant can still be correct if it creates an immediate win or a better response to actual public threats.

### Iron Thorns Psychic Reserve v3: fieldwide setup tax and primary-format mismatch

Agent30's exact setup model finds that the five-Basic v3 has only **47.4562% valid opening-seven probability**. Conditional on a valid opening, **20.9139%** contain at least two Basics, **15.8172%** are Mimikyu-only, and the Mimikyu-only + Capture Energy line occurs in **3.2913%** of valid openings.

The two-player mulligan model extends to all **29 available** original lists. Their Basic counts range from 4 to 16 (mean 12.241). The equal-weighted mean maximum optional bonus-draw ceiling is **0.975** for the opponent versus **0.202** for v3; **26/29** opponents have the higher ceiling. This is an opportunity ceiling, not actual bonus cards drawn or a win-rate estimate. The same model gives the 14-Basic Hood Shield v1 a much closer comparison: **0.131** mean opponent ceiling versus **0.304** for Hood, with 16/29 opponents higher, 7/29 Hood higher and 6 ties. A count-only sensitivity crosses field-neutral between 10 and 11 candidate Basics; it is not a recommendation to add Basics because it ignores the roles and costs of the cards displaced. Agent2's exhaustive missing-rank sensitivity then evaluates every possible opponent Basic count from 1 through 60 for each unavailable rank: the full-32 mean difference remains **+0.0945 to +0.8043** for five-Basic v3 and **−0.8518 to −0.1420** for Hood. Thus the sign of this setup-only comparison is robust to all three missing Basic counts, although the magnitude remains broad and the source lists remain missing. An independent exact-rational recomputation of the 29-rank vector, per-rank extrema and full-32 endpoints reproduced Agent2's values exactly. See [Agent2's full-32 bounds](agent2-full32-mulligan-offer-bounds-20261120.md).

There is also a primary-scope gate, not a Japanese-format illegality finding: the v3 candidate JSON declares Japanese Aichi Expanded and contains Palace Book SMP NAN25 and Palace Belt BWP 153, Japanese-only promo printings. It therefore does not satisfy the owner's primary requirement for an **English-language paper Expanded candidate** as submitted. These printings do not, by themselves, prove the list illegal in the Japanese Aichi format it declares. Keep v3 as a research hypothesis until the scope is reconciled or the list is rebuilt and printing-checked for the primary contract.

## Translate the human concepts into observable variables

The human concepts are useful because they identify failure modes that simple card counts and search graphs miss. Use them as a shared vocabulary, but measure them at the level of actual game decisions and whole turns.

- **DCI / UDP:** record whether each card is a must-discard, conditionally disposable, or protected resource in the current state. The label must change when a once-per-game power is spent, a counter-line becomes relevant, or the matchup is identified from revealed cards, mulligans, or early effects. A card's DCI is not a permanent card attribute; it is conditioned on matchup, turn, prize knowledge and the intended line. For example, a Devolution card may become immediately disposable against a Basic-only opponent but must be preserved against an evolution-heavy opponent.
- **AMR:** distinguish a reachable action from a realistically playable action after its discard payment, Supporter quota, Item lock, Bench occupancy, retreat cost and timing window are applied. A search chain that cannot pay its costs is not an available line.
- **K0 / K1 prize knowledge:** represent the information boundary before and after a complete deck search. Before the first search, prize composition is uncertain; after a search, the player may infer the remaining prizes from deck knowledge. Update only the acting player's knowledge state, and never give a policy the true prize composition before it can legitimately infer it.
- **Associativity / connector domination:** for Arven, Mysterious Treasure, Quick Ball, Secret Box and similar connectors, record the best competing use of the same connector. Do not award value merely because a path exists; compare the opportunity cost and whether one use satisfies multiple needs.
- **ALS:** preserve named multi-card sequences as explicit test objects. The Hood list's Mimikyu → opponent Regidrago Apex Dragon → Dialga-GX Timeless-GX route is matchup-dependent and printing-sensitive; it requires prior-turn attack history, GX-use state and the correct Dialga-GX printing. New Moon's Horror House / Night Watch sequence is another architecture-specific line. A policy that cannot recognize its prerequisites has not operationalized the deck's advertised plan.
- **Discrete tactical value:** evaluate gust, Ranger, Tool removal, counter-catch effects and attack-origin locks by the states in which they change the game, not by average draw or graph-connectivity scores alone.
- **Supporter contention:** model the one-Supporter turn as a shared resource. Arven, Ranger, Guzma, Raihan and draw Supporters are not independent routes that can all be taken in one turn.
- **Prize collapse:** evaluate complete recovery chains and multiple dependent pieces together. Do not multiply isolated single-card Prize probabilities or assume Heavy Ball, a searcher and the target are independently recoverable.
- **Lock matrix:** encode source, target, zone, Active/Bench condition, Tool/Stadium condition, duration, and whether the effect is on a Pokémon or a player. Item lock, Supporter lock, Ability suppression and attack-origin restrictions are different constraints.
- **Card-swap evaluation:** treat a substitution as a whole-deck hypothesis. Record what is gained and lost across DCI, AMR, Supporter contention, prize risk, multi-Prize collapse, connector opportunity cost, discrete tactical value, matchup dependence and specific lines. Use matched game evidence as a data point, not an automatic verdict; do not justify cosmetic one-card churn with isolated access probabilities.
- **State variables and paper legality:** model types, damage, Weakness, Resistance, Special Conditions, Prize values, retreat, board position, Energy and evolution only where they affect the actual line. The card database is a search resource, not a legality filter: every candidate needs printing-aware paper Expanded legality, banlist and release-date checks before admission.

# Aichi 2026 XYP Japanese-print crosswalk

**Agent 39 · 13 November 2026 · source/printing chronology only · no game evidence**

## Finding

The 29-list roster contains **9 XYP source rows / 12 card copies** across three English-display identities. All three have matching Japanese printings that were available years before the 10 May 2026 event, and the official Japanese card pages expose the same operative card text as the corresponding English prints. The supplied English card pool marks all three English printings Expanded Legal.

This supplies the missing **Japanese-print bridge** for the XYP rows that Agent 6's English-only release-date census cannot date. The raw English-only census still reports 57 rows / 151 copies with missing English set metadata; this crosswalk changes the evidence status of 9 rows / 12 copies, leaving 48 rows / 139 copies without a Japanese-print bridge. It does not identify the exact physical copies each player registered or brought to the event, and it does not change the five Japanese-only-promo incompatibilities.

## Mechanics and Expanded status

The card identities are not inferred from names alone:

- **Jirachi:** the official Japanese 129/XY-P page gives Stardust and Dream Dance, including their Special Energy discard/protection and mutual Sleep effects. These match English XYP #67.
- **Regirock:** the official Japanese 104/XY-P page gives Ancient Trait Ω Barrier, Land Maker, and Stone Edge. These match English XYP #49.
- **Karen:** the official Japanese 256/XY-P page gives the effect that each player shuffles all Pokémon from their discard pile into their deck. The official event page independently states that Karen was the participation prize throughout 1 August–30 September 2016. These match English XYP #177.

The local card pool records xyp-XY67, xyp-XY49, and xyp-XY177 as **Expanded: Legal**. The Japanese printings and matching text are corroborated by the official Pokémon Card pages above. Limitless also cross-indexes Jirachi XY67 to Japanese XY-P #129, and Regirock XY49 to Japanese XY-P #104 ([Jirachi index](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/xyp/67), [Regirock index](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/xyp/49)).

## Consequence for the Aichi chronology census

Agent 6's [English-display release-date census](agent6-english-cri-japanese-m4-aichi-date-crosswalk-20261103.md) still has 57 unresolved English set-metadata rows / 151 copies across BWP, MEE, SMP, SP and XYP. This crosswalk accounts for all **9 XYP rows / 12 copies** through independent Japanese print evidence. The remaining **48 rows / 139 copies without a Japanese-print bridge** are:

- **MEE:** 35 rows / 120 copies, all Basic Energy placeholders in the source roster; these are generic Basic Energy identities, not an unknown special-card mechanic.
- **BWP:** 5 rows / 7 copies.
- **SMP:** 4 rows / 7 copies.
- **SP:** 4 rows / 5 copies.

The remaining BWP/SMP/SP entries require their separate Japanese-only promo disposition; this crosswalk does not relabel or normalize them. In particular, ranks 3 and 7 still contain other incompatible promo identities even though their Regirock XYP row is now resolved.

For the ranks 8–16 screen, Karen XYP #177 occurs in ranks 8, 15 and 16. Its Japanese equivalent is a pre-event print with matching text; it is not a post-event card or an unavailable English-only identity.

## Limits

- The source roster authenticates card identities and counts, but not each player's exact physical print.
- A Japanese counterpart existing before the event is evidence of availability, not proof that the player used that exact copy.
- The crosswalk does not certify every other card in the affected decks.
- No deck was admitted to the verified Expanded-opponent denominator by this report alone.
- No games were run; no matchup or win-rate claim follows.

**Disposition:** retain XYP in the raw English-only metadata census, but mark all nine XYP rows / twelve copies as Japanese-print-bridged and pre-event available. Preserve physical-copy provenance as unresolved, and keep the five separately identified Japanese-only-promo lists out of the international Expanded field.

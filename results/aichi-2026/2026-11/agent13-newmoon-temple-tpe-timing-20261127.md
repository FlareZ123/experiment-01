# New Moon under Temple of Sinnoh: TPE still searches, but no longer supplies Psychic while Temple applies

**Agent 13 · 27 November 2026**  
**Decision affected:** Telepathic Psychic Energy policy in Temple-of-Sinnoh source states.  
**Evidence class:** printed card text plus general Temple-of-Sinnoh timing ruling applied to the retained TPE wording. This is a rules-derived interaction, not a game result.

## Finding

Temple of Sinnoh does **not** justify treating Telepathic Psychic Energy's attachment-time search as unavailable.

The two texts are:

- **Telepathic Psychic Energy:** while attached, it provides Psychic; **when attached from hand to a Psychic Pokémon**, search the deck for up to two Basic Psychic Pokémon and put them onto the Bench.
- **Temple of Sinnoh:** Special Energy **attached to Pokémon** provide Colorless and have no other effect.

Published Temple rulings distinguish effects that happen **when the Energy is played/attached from hand** from effects of the Energy **while it remains attached**. Attachment-time requirements/effects still resolve before the attached Special Energy is suppressed by Temple.

Applied to TPE:

1. attach TPE from hand to a Psychic Pokémon;
2. its **when-you-attach** Basic-Psychic search still resolves;
3. once TPE is attached under Temple, Temple makes it provide **Colorless** and suppresses its attached-card effects;
4. therefore the same TPE may no longer satisfy a printed **Psychic Energy** attack cost while Temple remains effective.

The exact TPE/Temple pair is a rules-derived application of the general Temple timing principle rather than a cited publisher FAQ naming TPE specifically.

## Strategic consequence

This splits “TPE setup access” from “TPE attack funding.”

Against Temple sources such as the Absol/Garbodor families:

- New Moon may still use TPE to build the board through the Stadium;
- but a one-Psychic Horror House line cannot assume the attached TPE is currently Psychic Energy;
- a Basic Psychic Energy, Stadium removal/replacement, or another legal way to make the attack cost valid may still be required.

A policy that treats “TPE attached successfully” as equivalent to “Psychic attack funded” is wrong.

## Solrock/Lunatone does not automatically erase this distinction

Lunatone OBF 92's New Moon prevents Stadium effects **done to your Pokémon**.

Temple of Sinnoh's text acts on the **Special Energy cards attached to Pokémon**. Do not silently assume that New Moon restores TPE's attached Energy effect merely because it protects the Pokémon from Stadium effects.

Until a direct ruling for this exact New Moon/Temple interaction is established, the conservative actor policy should:

- allow the TPE attachment-time search;
- treat the attached TPE as Colorless while Temple is active;
- require actual legal attack-cost validation before choosing Horror House or another Psychic-cost attack.

This conservative rule cannot create an illegal attack and preserves the proven setup benefit.

## Acceptance states

1. **Temple + TPE setup:** TPE attaches to a Psychic Pokémon with Bench space. Worker must still allow the “up to two Basic Psychic” search.
2. **Temple + one TPE + Horror House:** no other Psychic source and Temple remains active. Worker must not assume the printed Psychic attack cost is paid.
3. **Temple removed/replaced before attack:** re-evaluate TPE's current Energy provision from the live Stadium state; do not permanently mark it Colorless.
4. **Basic Psychic available:** worker may use Basic Psychic as the attack-funding channel while retaining TPE's setup value.

## Disposition

This audit **does not refute New Moon retention**. It narrows the claim that TPE is lock-resilient:

- its **attachment-time setup search** survives Temple;
- its **attached Psychic-Energy role** does not.

No card change, qualified matchup, or 99.5% claim follows.

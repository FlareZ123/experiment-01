## Research consequence

This supported negative result rejects continuing the identical paid-retreat heuristic on the same recipient-enabled game states merely to collect more native terminal labels. Under this policy's actual observed Pass decision distribution, action opportunities vanish **before** tactical payoff can be evaluated. A stronger policy must address a concrete owner-legal mobility+funded-attacker line rather than relaxing unrelated predicates blindly. In particular, the seven paid-capable and deck-race-compatible states have no eligible funded attack on the Bench. Any proposed new mechanic or change needs a new registration, full physical fee and promotion, attack/control settlement, and genuine opponent response, with opportunity costs and lock interactions considered.

The cohort is retrospective, original ranks 1/3 only, no validated 25-minute paper timing, no competent full opponent policy and no full original 32 list (ranks 23,25,28 still missing). This research supplies **zero competitively qualified games**, no chosen best fixed 60, and no demonstrated 99.5% target.

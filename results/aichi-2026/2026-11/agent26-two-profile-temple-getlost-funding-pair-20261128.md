- The original v2 worker blob `968e15dd7dda774d5dd1959ec7e1f58ea1669150` chooses root `Get Lost`, then **`card-0,card-1` (Dialga + Kyurem)** for its mandatory public-discard prompt.
- The amended worker blob `8853b6b5eb25edf420c3ca88566008038d910086` chooses the *same offered root attack*, then **`card-2,card-3` (Megaton Blower + Crispin)**.

This is a *specific source-envelope funding-denial priority*, derived from [Agent26's original exact rank13 exhaustive pair oracle](agent26-rank13-temple-attack-cut.md) and [nine-rank source minima](agent26-regidrago-temple-minimum-cuts.md): in that constrained rank13 environment, with both replacement Stadium sources already Lost, Megaton can remove Temple and enable the DDE payment route; Crispin with the regular manual attachment can pay for GGF through Temple. ## Small actual decision patch

The owner successor's existing `getLostPairs` enumeration and prompt custody remain untouched. Add one `visibleTempleRegidragoFundingPairBonus(view,names)` term to its pair score, returning **2500 tactical prioritization units** only when ALL of these public facts hold:

- The candidate's current Temple effect is live, and the scorer evaluates **each offered two-card cut** of public discard identities.
- The opponent's current Active is Regidrago VSTAR, attached **exactly one Basic Grass plus one or more Double Dragon Energy cards**, and cannot pay `GGF` *under Temple*.
- Both `Silent Lab` and `Parallel City` are present in the **opponent's public Lost Zone**.
- Exactly one visible copy each of Megaton Blower and Crispin is in opponent's public discard. **No Dragon donor need yet be visible**: new donors can be discarded from concealed zones before the opponent's next attack.
- No publicly visible **already funded** Benched Regidrago VSTAR can pay the same Apex cost.

For *each proposed two-card cut*, the amended scorer removes those public discard identities in a copy and awards the bounded premium **only if both unique recovery/funding cards (Megaton Blower + Crispin) have been Lost-Zoned**. An earlier draft also awarded the same premium when two chosen Dragon cards eliminated all *currently observed* discard attack donors. That draft was **rejected** after source review: rank13 has **two Battle Compressor**, rank32 has **one**, and both carry Quick Ball, Mysterious Treasure and hand-reset/discard Supporters. An opponent can introduce new Dragon donors from hidden hand/deck next turn, so *no donors left visibly in discard* is **not evidence of no future Apex payloads**. By contrast, Lost-Zoning these two source-unique funding resources permanently removes the two evidenced recovery channels (though hidden other exits and later manual Energy remain possible). Consequently a rank13-like state with **two or three** exposed donors now prefers Megaton+Crispin to a superficially total donor cut. Moreover, a source-compatible state with **no visible Dragon donor yet**, but the two recoverable counters exposed, is now a supported **Get Lost instead of Pass** root: the target is prospective funding denial, not an imagined present attack payload. Manual + Crispin recovery and the Megaton removal remain relevant with **one Grass plus two or three DDE copies**: Temple reduces each DDE to Colorless, whereas removing Temple restores all required types. A negative guard shows that one Grass **plus Fire** plus DDE does not fit this same two-missing-colors geometry. The scorer also fails closed on a spent opponent VSTAR power, public alternative removal counters (Marshadow/Faba/Field Blower), and a publicly **already funded Benched Regidrago V or VSTAR**, recognizing that V may evolve on the next opposing turn. Everything else delegates to the unchanged two-profile scorer. No opponent rank ID, hidden hand/deck contents, opponent Prizes, fabricated actions, secret matchup metadata, or native effects are read. The weight 2500 is a bounded heuristic for permanently removing **the two observed source-unique next-turn funding channels**, **not a calibrated probability or proof**.

The source and [80/80 strict two-seat focused tests](../../../tests/2026-11/agent26-two-profile-temple-next-turn-getlost.test.cjs) are committed on the branch. Test blob `a95d93d3b9efe24816cf5bb1ec6246a6770ca2b3`, base protocol `cf49a009525a9a5af3a89f5964eb563e0d9d9e26`. The tests exercise source-exact typed policy decisions, `validateRequest`, `validateDecision`, `validatePolicyAudit`, and hard negatives for absent Temple, missing either Lost Stadium, no G+DDE Energy geometry, funded Bench attacker, duplicated publicly discarded Crispin, spent opponent VSTAR power, publicly surviving Faba/Marshadow, no revealed donor, and the key **two-donor-not-global-exhaustion** and **pre-donor funding cut** branches. All executed in isolated in-process V8 from exact GitHub blobs, **not** native compiled Store or Node CLI. In an additional V8 regression exercise, **101/104 original two-profile test callbacks passed**; the other 3 were not executed because the ad-hoc V8 harness did not provide the root suite's additional native-projection/prize helpers (not policy assertion failures). Their node files are unchanged, and no CI was dispatched.

## Source corpus correction: missing and phantom Apex donors across the original Regidrago field

Inspection of the attached English card corpus (IDs `sv9-114`, `sv5-120` and `sv8-116`) and all **nine published original Regidrago lists** revealed a separate concrete omission from the candidate's `APEX_PAYLOADS` / `getLostValue` attack models:

- **Salamence ex JTG 114** is Dragon type, present as one card in **Aichi rank 13**. Printed `Dragon Impact` deals **300 damage to Active** and discards two Energy from the attacking Pokémon; printed `Wide Blast` deals **50 attack damage to each opposing Benched Pokémon**. Previously both were missing from `APEX_PAYLOADS` and even `getLostValue` treated Salamence as an unimportant filler. The patch adds both and explicitly models Wide Blast as *all-Bench only*, not a 50-damage Active strike. Direct hazard test: 300 into a 270-remaining VMAX counts a 3-Prize KO; on a full 320-HP Active, two 40-remaining single-Prize Benched Pokémon are both KO'd by Wide Blast. This is an optimistic damage upper bound, **not** a live attack certified through opposing Bench protection.
- **Regidrago V SIT 135** is itself Dragon type and occurs as **3–4 copies in all nine published original Regidrago lists** (ranks 10, 12, 13, 14, 18, 20, 21, 27, 32). Printed `Dragon Laser` deals **130 to Active plus 30 to exactly one opposing Benched Pokémon**. A Regidrago V in the discard is therefore an additional legitimate Apex Dragon donor, previously missing from `APEX_PAYLOADS` and `getLostValue`. The patch adds its direct and single-Bench geometry, including the legitimate no-Bench fallback (130 only), and tests 130 into a wounded 3-Prize VMAX plus 30 to one low-HP 1-Prize support for a 4-Prize attack. The new root regression also confirms a lone Regidrago V donor changes the worker's pass-versus-Get Lost choice under an otherwise legal actor-visible root. Its other attack, Celestial Roar, is setup rather than damage and remains outside this narrowly scoped damage-only hazard model.

- **Koraidon ex TEF 120** is Dragon type, a single-copy donor in each of published ranks **10, 12, 18, 21 and 27**. Printed `Kaiser Tackle` deals **280 to Active plus 60 self-damage**. The former policy omitted this real source donor and instead modeled `Koraidon SSP` / `Unrelenting Onslaught` at 180, but the corpus shows **Koraidon SSP 116 is Fighting type** and cannot be copied by Apex Dragon's Dragon-type discard requirement. The patch replaces that phantom with the real `Koraidon ex TEF` 280 source and raises its visible Get Lost donor value. The threat oracle deliberately remains **optimistic**: it does not net out the attacking Regidrago's 60 self-damage, simultaneous KO/Prize resolution, alternate defensive effects, or whether the attacker has enough resources to survive subsequent turns. The new hard negative verifies a lone Fighting Koraidon SSP in discard yields no Apex damage contribution.

- **Two false-positive non-Dragon payloads were removed.** `Koraidon SSP 116` (`sv8-116`) is **Fighting** type; `Garchomp ex PAR 38` (`sv4-38`) is **Water** type. Neither is eligible as an attack copy through Regidrago VSTAR's **Dragon**-only Apex Dragon, despite both appearing in the original optimistic `APEX_PAYLOADS` catalogue. The patch removes both and stops valuing `Garchomp ex` as if necessarily Dragon. In addition, the new Koraidon and Salamence donor values are now **exact-printing scoped** (`Koraidon ex TEF`, `Salamence ex JTG`) rather than name-only; Fighting `Koraidon ex ASC` cannot inherit the TEF donor bonus. Source-backed original 60s do not contain those false-positive cards as a Regidrago Dragon donor; this is a correctness hardening against phantom attack threat, not a new qualified win.

- **Koraidon ex TEF's second attack Retribution Strike is state-dependent**: printed base `20+` plus 10 per damage counter on **the Pokémon using the attack**. When copied with Apex Dragon, that is the attacking **Regidrago VSTAR**, not the discarded Koraidon ex. The branch now evaluates the actor-visible opponent Active Regidrago's damage counters. A positive/negative both-seat threshold test sets our VMAX to 285 remaining HP: 0 or 260 damage on Regidrago produces no KO (Kaiser Tackle 280 also falls short), while **270 damage on Regidrago yields 290 Retribution Strike** and KOs the VMAX for three Prizes. This is an optimistic attack-damage model; source Temporal Forces `sv5-120` directly establishes the scaling, not a simulated game.

- **Same-name non-Dragon printing guard:** the project English card pool also has legally usable **Metal** Dialga-GX FLI, **Colorless** Noivern ex PRE, **Water** Kyurem UNB, **Colorless** Haxorus NVI, and **Fighting** Flygon ex SSP. The old Regidrago `getLostValue` rewarded generic card names without requiring a Dragon source. The current patch keys its high donor priority to the **same exact printed card IDs already named in `APEX_PAYLOADS`**, avoiding contradictions between attack availability and discard priority. It consciously leaves not-yet-modeled legitimate Dragon printings at baseline value rather than assuming unknown attack power; 18 both-seat source-positive/same-name-negative tests are added. The **independently reviewable source-only subset** now lives in [draft PR #102](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/pull/102), 40/40 focused V8 checks. This draft PR #101 adds the separate conditional funding-cut scoring and should be considered higher risk.

The above establishes **source-backed candidate threat coverage**, not any source games or measured win-rate effect. The donor set in these nine lists now covers all their printed **direct attacking Dragon payload identities** found in the provided English corpus, aside from the self-copying Regidrago VSTAR's Apex Dragon and setup-only alternatives. This is not complete engine equivalence or a claim that every card effect is implemented. The 80/80 focused tests include source-backed damage and actual root-decision regressions across two seats: Salamence Active 300, all-Bench 50, Koraidon ex TEF 280 versus phantom Fighting Koraidon SSP/ASC, Water Garchomp ex PAR exclusion, and Regidrago V 130 + one-Bench 30. They include a baseline→patched **Pass→Get Lost** root reversal for Salamence and Koraidon ex; the actual physical token handler remains unchanged. The source-list rank counts are **original-list inventory occurrences**, not active-game probabilities. The emitted `__test.apexHazard` method is nonenumerable and exists solely to test the already-used optimistic hazard scorer.

The earlier `+7000` single-pair draft at blob `1f704f168efded95cd7b939baae4e172f223bd6d` and its successor treating **observed donor exhaustion as a full lock** were **superseded** by the source-backed distinction between **permanently removing unique funding pieces** and **temporarily emptying a public discard of donors while hidden discard outlets persist**. Do not treat a heuristic as settled competitive evidence, do not replace the fixed candidate 60, and do not auto-merge this branch.

## Small actual decision patch

The owner successor's existing `getLostPairs` enumeration and prompt custody remain untouched. Add one `visibleTempleRegidragoFundingPairBonus(view,names)` term to its pair score, returning **2500 tactical prioritization units** only when ALL of these public facts hold:

- The candidate's current Temple effect is live, and the scorer evaluates **each offered two-card cut** of public discard identities.
- The opponent's current Active is Regidrago VSTAR, attached **exactly one Basic Grass and one Double Dragon Energy**, and cannot pay `GGF` *under Temple*.
- Both `Silent Lab` and `Parallel City` are present in the **opponent's public Lost Zone**.
- Exactly one visible copy each of Megaton Blower and Crispin is in opponent's public discard; at least one copied attack donor is publicly visible in that discard.
- No publicly visible **already funded** Benched Regidrago VSTAR can pay the same Apex cost.

For *each proposed two-card cut*, the amended scorer removes exactly those public discard identities in a copy, then checks both remaining observed channels: (a) recoverable **Megaton Blower or Crispin** and (b) at least one still-observed copied-attack donor. Its small bonus applies when **either channel is exhausted**. Thus if the only exposed donors are Dialga and Kyurem, removing those two can remain preferable; the amended test deliberately includes **three** authentic rank13 donors (Dialga, Kyurem, Dragapult ex), making a two-donor cut insufficient to extinguish all known payloads. The scorer also fails closed on a spent opponent VSTAR power, public alternative removal counters (Marshadow/Faba/Field Blower), and a publicly **already funded Benched Regidrago V or VSTAR**, recognizing that V may evolve on the next opposing turn. Everything else delegates to the unchanged two-profile scorer. No opponent rank ID, hidden hand/deck contents, opponent Prizes, fabricated actions, secret matchup metadata, or native effects are read. The weight 2500 is a bounded heuristic for preserving the next turn's **observed joint funding and donor channels**, **not a calibrated probability or a proof**.

## Source corpus correction: missing and phantom Apex donors across the original Regidrago field

Inspection of the attached English card corpus (IDs `sv9-114`, `sv5-120` and `sv8-116`) and all **nine published original Regidrago lists** revealed a separate concrete omission from the candidate's `APEX_PAYLOADS` / `getLostValue` attack models:

- **Salamence ex JTG 114** is Dragon type, present as one card in **Aichi rank 13**. Printed `Dragon Impact` deals **300 damage to Active** and discards two Energy from the attacking Pokémon; printed `Wide Blast` deals **50 attack damage to each opposing Benched Pokémon**. Previously both were missing from `APEX_PAYLOADS` and even `getLostValue` treated Salamence as an unimportant filler. The patch adds both and explicitly models Wide Blast as *all-Bench only*, not a 50-damage Active strike. Direct hazard test: 300 into a 270-remaining VMAX counts a 3-Prize KO; on a full 320-HP Active, two 40-remaining single-Prize Benched Pokémon are both KO'd by Wide Blast. This is an optimistic damage upper bound, **not** a live attack certified through opposing Bench protection.
- **Regidrago V SIT 135** is itself Dragon type and occurs as **3–4 copies in all nine published original Regidrago lists** (ranks 10, 12, 13, 14, 18, 20, 21, 27, 32). Printed `Dragon Laser` deals **130 to Active plus 30 to exactly one opposing Benched Pokémon**. A Regidrago V in the discard is therefore an additional legitimate Apex Dragon donor, previously missing from `APEX_PAYLOADS` and `getLostValue`. The patch adds its direct and single-Bench geometry, including the legitimate no-Bench fallback (130 only), and tests 130 into a wounded 3-Prize VMAX plus 30 to one low-HP 1-Prize support for a 4-Prize attack. The new root regression also confirms a lone Regidrago V donor changes the worker's pass-versus-Get Lost choice under an otherwise legal actor-visible root. Its other attack, Celestial Roar, is setup rather than damage and remains outside this narrowly scoped damage-only hazard model.

- **Koraidon ex TEF 120** is Dragon type, a single-copy donor in each of published ranks **10, 12, 18, 21 and 27**. Printed `Kaiser Tackle` deals **280 to Active plus 60 self-damage**. The former policy omitted this real source donor and instead modeled `Koraidon SSP` / `Unrelenting Onslaught` at 180, but the corpus shows **Koraidon SSP 116 is Fighting type** and cannot be copied by Apex Dragon's Dragon-type discard requirement. The patch replaces that phantom with the real `Koraidon ex TEF` 280 source and raises its visible Get Lost donor value. The threat oracle deliberately remains **optimistic**: it does not net out the attacking Regidrago's 60 self-damage, simultaneous KO/Prize resolution, alternate defensive effects, or whether the attacker has enough resources to survive subsequent turns. The new hard negative verifies a lone Fighting Koraidon SSP in discard yields no Apex damage contribution.

The above establishes **source-backed candidate threat coverage**, not any source games or measured win-rate effect. The donor set in these nine lists now covers all their printed **direct attacking Dragon payload identities** found in the provided English corpus, aside from the self-copying Regidrago VSTAR's Apex Dragon and setup-only alternatives. This is not complete engine equivalence or a claim that every card effect is implemented. The 48/48 focused tests include source-backed damage and actual root-decision regressions across two seats: Salamence Active 300, all-Bench 50, Koraidon ex 280 versus phantom Fighting Koraidon, and Regidrago V 130 + one-Bench 30. They include a baseline→patched **Pass→Get Lost** root reversal for Salamence and Koraidon ex; the actual physical token handler remains unchanged. The source-list rank counts are **original-list inventory occurrences**, not active-game probabilities. The emitted `__test.apexHazard` method is nonenumerable and exists solely to test the already-used optimistic hazard scorer.

## Actionable systemic result

The actual native action enumerator is **not a legal move oracle even for ordinary public-discard target existence, card type, or HP limits**. A common bilateral decision policy that naively ranks all native offers can include many physically dead recovery Items as options and needlessly misallocate search/action opportunities. A *negative-only* owner-information gate can soundly remove the **58 observed** source-disproved offers in this exact original-pair trace, provided that the remaining target/state/lock/print/payment gates and choice alternatives are separately verified. No policy using this new filter has been selected or evaluated for payoff. Do not promote this observation into a new final 60, native win, or 99.5% benchmark claim; **the authenticated full-source coverage remains 29/32 and qualified competitive game count remains zero**.

## 28 November 03:59 UTC incremental decision repairs

Now the target prompt uses the post-play board rather than the no-longer-held Float Stone. It selects the Active Trubbish when a payable mobility bottleneck exists, preserves an equipped Garbodor option when Block or a used Tool prevents the pivot, refuses Jamming Tower, and accepts unevolved Trubbish as a future Garbotoxin carrier. ## 28 November 04:03 UTC further gameplay repairs

- **Tool effects disabled in both stages of the action:** A live Jamming Tower invalidates Forest Seal Stone's Star Alchemy and Muscle Band's damage value. The rank16 worker previously spent either Tool at root and selected a Pokémon in its follow-on target prompt even though the effect was disabled. Both stages now guard `toolEnabled`, conserving singleton Forest Seal Stone and damage threshold resources for an active turn.
- **Enemy Garbotoxin properly suppresses our Abilities:** The earlier `garbotoxinStatus` examined only our Garbodor, so Crobat V/Dedenne-GX and Star Alchemy still consumed resources into the opponent's Tool-bearing Garbotoxin. The side-specific `opponentGarbotoxinStatus` shares the same live/unknown/disabled logic, disabling setup-Ability lines under known or uncertain suppression. It also propagates to opposing Active Snorlax Block / Wobbuffet Bide Barricade / Iron Thorns Initialization: a live enemy Garbotoxin cancels its own unprotected locks, whereas Stealthy Hood can protect an affected Active lock and Jamming Tower cancels the Garbotoxin Tool. New two-seat cases test these branches.
- **Battle VIP Pass can deliver Budew *now*:** The unconditional search order previously selected Trubbish and Mega Absol, filling both VIP slots even when the initially Active non-Budew had Float Stone and could legally retreat into a searched Budew this turn. The priority now uses `readyToPivotIntoBudew`: select Budew first when a payable immediate pivot is visible, then Trubbish for Garbotoxin preparation. Retain the original Trubbish/Mega order when no accessible pivot exists, when Snorlax Block is active, or outside the early-turn window.

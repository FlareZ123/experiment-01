## 28 November 04:20 UTC — CORRECTION OF FUNDAMENTAL GARBO/JAMMING ASSUMPTION

Both own/enemy Garbotoxin, Hood and Vileplume/Snorlax/Mimikyu locks share the corrected semantics. **14 earlier incorrect synthetic expectations were revised** rather than maintaining an invalid passing test score; 6 new two-seat tests target Tool attachment, Hood suppression and Star Alchemy.

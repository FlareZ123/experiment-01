**Decision developed:** the original rank-9 Aichi Absol/Electrode source contains **one Canceling Cologne ASR136**, one Mega Absol ex MEG86, 4 Prism and 2 Darkness Energy. ## The real execution gap

Original Agent29 rank9 source worker's `cologneDrawScore` only retained the Item for a Wobbuffet/Dedenne redraw line. It did not model a profitable anti-Safeguard attack. Even if we simply add “play Cologne when it would remove Safeguard”, we could silently fail on the next root: current `simulation/twinleaf/actor-observation-contract.cjs` returns printed Pokémon Abilities with `abilitySuppressions:[{suppressed:null}]` unless a separate source-attested host overlay can prove the active Ability lock. A native `Canceling Cologne` play **does not necessarily change that observation field**, despite succeeding in the engine. A root policy that relies only on `suppressed:true` could play its unique Item and then *pass*, wasting a winning line.

Pinned Twinleaf `ptcg-server/src/sets/09-sword-and-shield/set-astral-radiance/canceling-cologne.ts`, source blob `d28e223e87c30ee26a52881e19c20ee4ff32f951`, shows that native Cologne is an Item, `fullName='Canceling Cologne ASR'`, applies a player marker to the opponent's Active Pokémon, and clears the marker at end of turn. Pinned PAL97 Mimikyu `fullName='Mimikyu PAL'` protects itself through the target-card-specific `PutDamageEffect`; the printed effect of Cologne defeats Safeguard when not blocked. The source identities are matched **exactly** as native or original physical printings, never by ambiguous name-only `Mimikyu`.

## Implemented bounded three-stage source-policy decision

1. **First root:** with a paid Active Mega Absol ex, **exactly one own Prize left**, Active unsuppressed Mimikyu PAL97 with no attached Tool, the singleton Cologne physically in own hand, no active Item lock, and a modeled post-lock Claw 200 KO, select the actually offered `play-card: Canceling Cologne`. Do not consume the singleton for an ordinary nonfinal chip.
2. **Next root:** do not trust our prior intention or assume that `abilitySuppressions` switched from `null` to `true`. A candidate source-only settlement certificate requires **the same global turn and actor seat, exactly consecutive decision ordinal, unchanged relevant Active identities, Item absent from own hand, and a one-copy increase of Canceling Cologne in the actor-visible own discard**. The original source contains one copy. An intervening unexpected prompt cancels the certificate. All these checks use only publicly visible/owner-visible information already in the established observation contract.
3. **Then:** recompute paid `Claw of Darkness` under the precise hypothetical fact that **the opponent's Active PAL97 Safeguard** was suppressed by our settled Item. Other defender effects and public constraints remain conservatively guarded. Only if Claw still takes the **last Prize** and is actually in the action frontier does the worker select that attack. Otherwise the one-step certificate is discarded and ordinary root policy resumes; it cannot persist into another turn.

Source implementation commits [`03013cca`](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/commit/03013cca4ea29a8bfeb680aefb55649fdef3aed9) (profitable final-Prize trigger) and [`0c17c203`](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/commit/0c17c203ea97dad393904434632aec915072e717) (actual one-step source-public settlement guard). No host or engine changes.

## Exact developmental outcomes and controls

The [focused source-policy test](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/blob/agent9/rank9-bomb-symmetry-20261128/tests/2026-11/agent9-rank9-cologne-pal97-final-prize.test.cjs), commit `d19c218960dbc5514d1b866a95f604c7f45ca650`, source blob `6a0e7ca70df25a6df8836d547569277ee5cd4e00`, invokes **the same `createPolicy()` object** over two consecutive actor-owned source-policy roots in both physical seats. With opponent Active Mimikyu PAL `Safeguard` suppression **still null**, it selects exactly `Cologne` then `Claw` once the singleton has observably moved hand→discard. This also holds with a second PAL97 on the opposing Bench and with either original `Mimikyu PAL 97` or pinned native `Mimikyu PAL`.

Negative controls (both seats) abstain from assuming effect settlement when the Item is not in discard, the decision ordinal skips, the attacker changes, the Item is locked, the own Prize count is 2, Energy is insufficient, or target has an unknown Tool. If Safeguard is already known suppressed, direct Claw wins *without* consuming Cologne; against an Active Snorlax with an unrelated Benched Mimikyu, direct Claw also wins without consuming Cologne. Full isolated branch regression: **41/41 test cases, 98 V8-shim assertions** including all earlier Bomb and Safeguard source tests, on exact patched worker blob `99dc013ef24d0e25766bbb82315a10a76ef11734`.

## Acceptance boundary

This is an *actor-bound, source-card-proven conditional* under the current policy abstraction, **not** a Store-played trace. It does not prove that the public discard receipt is sufficient if some unmodeled opponent effect blocks Cologne while still moving the Item to discard, or that root ordinals remain adjacent in every host. The rank3/rank7 published sixties furnish the specific PAL97/Snorlax situation but do not imply the actual Prize/hand state was reached. A future **owner-authorized native no-game** witness should confirm Item acceptance, physical one-copy move, Active Ability-lock marker and the next paid Claw; until then mark it as developmental.

## Decision and practical follow-through

**Retire merely adding Mimikyu-directed manual attachment or generic post-Apex retreat preference as a standalone Copycat conversion strategy.** The local action exists, but the current original-source bot still has zero actual Copycat attacks under this intervention. Further analysis should join a single genuine Apex-response owner root to the **physical Mimikyu's current Energy, Active/Bench location, actual discard Dialga-GX, GX-used flag, target legality, available retreat payment and bot's finally accepted action**, including the counterfactual use of the same Psychic for the primary Shadow Rider attack. A proposed retreat alone says nothing about whether that final native action settled or permitted Copycat. Avoid repeating another field A/B without an observed complete paid root-to-copy sequence.

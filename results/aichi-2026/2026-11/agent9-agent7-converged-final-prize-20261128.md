1. **VSTAR Prize identity:** the more complete Agent7 `prizeLiability()` already correctly distinguishes modern `MEGA + ex` (three Prizes) from legacy `MEGA + EX` (two), but still omitted `VSTAR` from its two-Prize recognition. Added subtype `VSTAR` and exact-print `VSTAR` fallback. The source card-list gives `Regidrago VSTAR SIT 136` (280 HP) **two Prizes**; `Mega Absol ex MEG 86` (280 HP) **three**; legacy `M Gardevoir-EX STS 79` (210 HP) **two**. No legacy Mega-EX is counted as three.
2. **Already-paid same-turn victory:** when offered native Max Geist itself KOs the opponent's Active for the final required Prize(s), or the opponent has no further Pokémon to promote, choose **that native attack immediately** over a legal Lillie's Determination, Fog Crystal, or extra Underworld Door. In an exact state with four Basic Psychic attached and opponent Regidrago VSTAR at 130 HP remaining, native Max Geist 130 wins at two candidate Prizes. The pre-convergence root unnecessarily spent a Supporter first.
3. **Two-card-deck terminal Door exception:** keep Agent7's strict source-print, source-seat, Ability, Bench, hand and original three-card draw-floor guards for Underworld Door. At **exactly two** cards remaining, exceptionally allow an already-offered native Door only when one additional hand Basic Psychic onto a Benched Psychic changes an **already-payable** native Max Geist from non-KO to an immediately terminal modeled KO. With three Psychic attached: Max Geist 100; Door adds one: 130; opponent Regidrago VSTAR remaining130; candidate remaining Prizes2. The ability draws the last two cards, but game loss for having no deck cards occurs only at the start of a later turn. The branch chooses native Door before a competing Lillie when this is the immediate win. It refuses the shortcut when the attack is underfunded, nonterminal, physically unoffered, blocked by source custody, lacks visible Psychic energy/Bench target, has an explicitly unmodeled modifier, or has only one deck card.

**Scope limit:** these are actor-visible current-turn policy decisions evaluated with existing modeled damage/typed Energy and legal native offered tokens. Full opponent damage prevention, retaliation, ability/Tool interaction and actual Store settlement are not proven by these tests.

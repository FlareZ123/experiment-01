# New Moon retention: remaining authenticated-family gates (ranks 5, 9, 30, 31)

**Agent 1 · 27 November 2026**  
**Decision affected:** whether unchanged New Moon Lockbreaker v1 deserves implementation depth after the non-Shadow-Rider architecture allocation.  
**Evidence class:** exact authenticated source sixties, supplied card database/rulebook, deterministic damage/Prize arithmetic, and existing source-policy findings. **No new native game and no matchup win-rate estimate.**

This note closes the last four authenticated archetype families that were absent from the original 23-case New Moon acceptance contract after separate rank-2, rank-4/17 and rank-19 reviews. The point is not to declare these matchups solved. It is to prevent a candidate worker from passing a narrow synthetic contract while being strategically blind to an entire source archetype.

---

## Rank 5 — Mew / Genesect: Path is a draw lock, not an attack lock

### Exact source pressure

Rank 5 contains:

- 4 Mew V FST 113;
- 3 Mew VMAX FST 114;
- 4 Genesect V FST 185;
- Dedenne-GX and Crobat V;
- four Double Colorless Energy;
- two Sky Field, two Path to the Peak, Field Blower and Lost Vacuum;
- two Muscle Band and two Float Stone.

Mew VMAX has zero Retreat Cost. **Cross Fusion Strike** costs two Colorless and uses one attack of a Benched Fusion Strike Pokémon. With DCE and Benched Genesect V it can use **Techno Blast** for 210. Genesect's **Fusion Strike System** is the source's main Ability-draw engine.

### What New Moon should actually do

With Solrock + Lunatone established, candidate **Path to the Peak** can become asymmetric and turn off Genesect V while leaving candidate Rule-Box Abilities protected. That is valuable, but it does **not** stop Mew VMAX from attacking. Cross Fusion Strike is an attack, not an Ability.

The best Path window is therefore:

`asymmetric Path -> Horror House-GX / hand compression -> exploit the source's reduced redraw`

rather than:

`Path -> assume source cannot function`.

Rank 5 can contest Stadiums with Sky Field / its own Path and remove Stadiums with Field Blower or Lost Vacuum. Horror House can deny those **hand-played** answers for its locked turn, but it still does not prevent existing board actions.

### Zero-retreat rotation matters

If Mew VMAX uses Cross Fusion Strike to copy Techno Blast, the copied effect makes the attacking Mew unable to attack during its next turn. Moving an Active Pokémon to the Bench removes effects previously placed on it by attacks, and Mew VMAX has zero Retreat Cost.

So if another attacker is already Benched, a competent source policy can:

1. use the Techno Blast copy;
2. on its next turn, retreat the affected Mew VMAX for free;
3. promote a different attacker and continue attacking.

Horror House blocks hand plays, not ordinary retreat. A New Moon policy must not treat the Techno Blast drawback as a guaranteed blank source turn.

### The 310-HP discrete breakpoint

Mew VMAX has **310 HP**.

New Moon's copied **Night Watch** deals 150. Two clean Night Watch hits are only:

`150 + 150 = 300`.

That leaves a full-HP Mew VMAX at **10 HP**.

This is exactly the kind of discrete value an average-damage policy misses. A Cursed Blast pre-damage, an earlier chip attack, or a different finisher changes the line; two clean Night Watch declarations alone do not.

### Rank-5 required cases

- **R5-PATH-GENESECT:** asymmetric Path with Solrock/Lunatone must suppress Genesect draw.
- **R5-PATH-NOT-ATTACK-LOCK:** source Mew VMAX already has DCE and a valid Fusion Strike attack. Must expect Cross Fusion Strike despite Path.
- **R5-TECHNO-ROTATION:** affected Mew VMAX has another attacker Benched. Must expect free retreat / promotion as a legal reset of the attack restriction.
- **R5-HORROR-STADIUM-WINDOW:** Path + Horror House is live and source's Stadium/Blower/Vacuum answers are not already in play. Must credit one protected hand-denial turn without claiming a permanent Stadium lock.
- **R5-NIGHTWATCH-310:** clean full-HP Mew VMAX. Must not credit two 150 Night Watch hits as a KO without at least 10 additional damage.

## Rank 9 — Absol / Electrode: Silent Lab + Stage-1 Bomb + reciprocal Horror House

### Exact source pressure

Rank 9 contains:

- 2 Electrode-GX CES 48;
- 1 Mega Absol ex MEG 86;
- 1 Sableye V SSH 120;
- 1 Gengar & Mimikyu-GX TEU 53;
- 2 Toedscruel PAR 17;
- 2 Silent Lab, one Path to the Peak, one PokéStop;
- 4 Prism Energy, 2 Darkness Energy, 1 TPE.

Existing source-native developmental work has already shown a real chain in selected dealt states:

`Battle Compressor -> Benched Electrode-GX Extra Energy Bomb -> Energy onto Mega Absol ex -> mobility -> Claw of Darkness`.

Those historical source wins are not competitively qualified, but they are enough to reject any candidate model that treats the Bomb/funded Mega line as imaginary.

### Silent Lab is not Path

**Silent Lab** removes Abilities from every Basic Pokémon in play, hand and discard.

That means it suppresses New Moon's Basic:

- Mewtwo & Mew-GX Perfection;
- Lunatone New Moon;
- Klefki Mischievous Lock;
- Tapu Lele / Articuno / Squawkabilly utility Abilities;
- Unown Farewell Letter.

But Electrode-GX is **Stage 1**, so Silent Lab does **not** switch off Extra Energy Bomb.

This makes an opposing Lab especially hostile: it removes candidate Basic Ability lines while leaving the source's Stage-1 acceleration engine intact.

If Solrock + Lunatone are already in play, replacing Silent Lab with candidate **Path** can be strong: Lab leaves play, Lunatone's New Moon becomes active again, and candidate Path can then suppress Rule-Box Electrode while the candidate is protected by New Moon. The worker must evaluate the actual order rather than treating “a Stadium is active” as one binary state.

### Mega Absol is a legal Bomb recipient

Mega Absol ex is modern lowercase Pokémon **ex**, not legacy uppercase Pokémon-EX and not Pokémon-GX. Electrode-GX's printed recipient exclusion is Pokémon-GX / Pokémon-EX, so Mega Absol remains eligible.

After Bomb, Mega Absol's **Claw of Darkness** deals 200 and lets the source reveal the opponent hand and discard one card. It is also a three-Prize Mega Evolution ex when Knocked Out.

Path is therefore a *pre-Bomb* answer. Playing Path after the source has already attached the Energy does not rewind the acceleration.

### Toedscruel creates a selective recovery lock

Toedscruel's **Slime Mold Colony** says cards in the opponent's discard pile cannot be put into the hand by effects of that opponent's Abilities or Trainer cards.

So under live Toedscruel:

- Rescue Carrier-style return-to-hand recovery is blocked;
- Powerglass attaching a Basic Psychic from discard to the Active Pokémon is **not** a return-to-hand effect and remains conceptually distinct;
- discarding a needed Pokémon under the assumption that Carrier always retrieves it can be a severe DCI error.

Path does not suppress Toedscruel because it has no Rule Box.

### Reciprocal Horror House

Rank 9 itself carries Gengar & Mimikyu-GX and has source evidence of an authentic early Horror House declaration.

If New Moon is the player under Horror House:

- it cannot play TPE from hand as the normal Energy attachment;
- it cannot play a fresh Powerglass, Stadium, Item, Pokémon or Supporter from hand;
- it can still use already-in-play Abilities, retreat, attack and resolve automatic in-play effects;
- an **already attached** Powerglass may still perform its end-of-turn discard-to-attachment effect, because that is not playing a card from hand.

This is a crucial recovery distinction.

### Rank-9 required cases

- **R9-SILENTLAB-BASIC:** Silent Lab active. Must turn off candidate Basic Abilities and keep Stage-1 Electrode Bomb available.
- **R9-LAB-TO-PATH-PAIR:** Solrock + Lunatone in play, source Lab active, candidate Path playable. Must recognize the Stadium replacement can reactivate New Moon before Path is evaluated.
- **R9-BOMB-MEGA-ELIGIBLE:** Electrode live, eligible discard Energy, Mega Absol target. Must not reject Mega merely because it is lowercase ex.
- **R9-PATH-TOO-LATE:** Bomb has already settled and Mega is funded. Must not credit later Path with undoing attached Energy.
- **R9-TOEDSCRUEL-RECOVERY:** live Slime Mold Colony with candidate recovery in hand. Must block discard-to-hand Trainer/Ability recovery but not unrelated discard attachments such as Powerglass.
- **R9-INCOMING-HORROR:** candidate is under Horror House with attached Powerglass. Must reject hand plays while preserving legal board actions / automatic Tool effect.

## Rank 30 — Lillie's Clefairy: the mirror punishes New Moon's own Bench and copies non-GX attacks

### Exact source pressure

Rank 30 contains:

- 3 Lillie's Clefairy ex;
- 3 Unown;
- Latias ex, Tapu Lele-GX, Dedenne-GX, Squawkabilly ex, Crobat V, Fezandipiti ex;
- Empoleon V;
- Mimikyu GRI 58;
- Dialga-GX;
- three Guzma, three Sky Field;
- 4 Wondrous Patch, 5 Basic Psychic and 2 TPE.

The source is built to make **Full Moon Rondo** a primary attack. Rondo counts **both players' Benched Pokémon**.

Therefore every New Moon TPE target or low-impact support body directly increases the opponent's damage by 20.

Under the ordinary five-Bench limit on each side, total Bench can reach 10 and Rondo can reach 220. With Sky Field expanding the board, much higher breakpoints become possible.

Example: candidate Lillie's Clefairy ex has 190 HP. At **nine total Benched Pokémon**, the opposing Rondo is:

`20 + 20×9 = 200`

and cleanly KOs it.

A New Moon worker that auto-selects two TPE targets can literally create the source's KO breakpoint.

### Copycat punishes the *last attack used*

Mimikyu GRI 58's **Copycat** says: if the opponent's Pokémon used an attack that is not a GX attack during their last turn, use it as this attack.

Under the supplied C-18 rules, “use it as this attack” performs that chosen attack's damage/effects without normally requiring its original Energy cost.

So a funded rank-30 Mimikyu can retaliate after New Moon uses:

- Night Watch;
- Eon Blade;
- Full Moon Rondo;
- Poltergeist;
- another non-GX attack.

It cannot satisfy Copycat after New Moon's **Horror House-GX**, because Copycat explicitly requires a non-GX attack.

This creates a real attack-selection externality. The candidate must score the opponent's Copycat reply before selecting a high-impact non-GX attack.

### Empoleon disables the New Moon Stadium shield, but not Rule-Box Perfection directly

When Active, Empoleon V's **Emperor's Eyes** removes Abilities from the opponent's Basic Pokémon **except Pokémon with Rule Boxes**.

So Active Empoleon suppresses candidate:

- Lunatone's New Moon;
- Unown's Farewell Letter;
- Klefki's Mischievous Lock;

but it does **not** directly suppress Mewtwo & Mew-GX Perfection or Lillie's Clefairy ex Fairy Zone, because those Basics have Rule Boxes.

This matters for Path: if Empoleon is Active, Lunatone's New Moon shield is off. A candidate Path can then suppress its own Rule-Box Abilities. Do not treat Solrock/Lunatone as an unconditional asymmetric-Path certificate while Empoleon is Active.

### Rank-30 required cases

- **R30-RONDO-BENCH-EXTERNALITY:** source Clefairy powered; candidate can add a low-impact Bench body. Must add +20 opponent Rondo damage to the strongest reply before benching.
- **R30-RONDO-190:** candidate Clefairy Active, nine total Benched Pokémon. Must recognize opposing Full Moon Rondo = 200 and a clean KO.
- **R30-COPYCAT-NONGX:** source Mimikyu funded; candidate last attack was a non-GX attack. Must include Copycat of that attack in the reply set without demanding its original Energy cost.
- **R30-COPYCAT-GX-EXCLUSION:** candidate last attack was Horror House-GX. Must not allow Copycat to use it.
- **R30-EMPOLEON-NEWMOON:** source Empoleon Active. Must suppress Lunatone New Moon / other Basic non-Rule-Box Abilities while leaving candidate Rule-Box Basic Abilities outside Emperor's Eyes itself.
- **R30-EMPOLEON-PATH-COST:** Empoleon Active plus candidate Path option. Must not call Path asymmetric solely because Solrock+Lunatone are on board; New Moon is currently suppressed.

## Rank 31 — Dusknoir / Chimecho: self-KO damage converts directly into Ursaluna tempo

### Exact source pressure

Rank 31 contains:

- 3 Duskull PRE 35;
- 3 Dusclops PRE 36;
- 3 Dusknoir PRE 37;
- 1 Dusknoir BCR 63;
- 3 Koffing CEC 76;
- Chimecho CIN 43;
- Radiant Alakazam;
- Bloodmoon Ursaluna ex;
- Gengar & Mimikyu-GX;
- 4 Roxie;
- Grand Tree and PokéStop;
- 3 TPE + 2 Basic Psychic.

The modern PRE Dusclops and Dusknoir have **Cursed Blast**:

- Dusclops: put 5 damage counters on one opposing Pokémon, then this Dusclops is Knocked Out.
- Dusknoir: put 13 damage counters on one opposing Pokémon, then this Dusknoir is Knocked Out.

Bloodmoon Ursaluna ex's **Seasoned Skill** reduces Blood Moon's five-Colorless cost by one for each Prize card the opponent has taken.

Those two mechanisms are deliberately complementary: every source self-KO gives New Moon a Prize **and simultaneously makes Blood Moon cheaper**.

### A self-KO is not automatically favorable to New Moon

Consider a fresh 270-HP Mewtwo & Mew-GX.

Two Dusknoir Cursed Blasts put:

`130 + 130 = 260`

damage on it while giving New Moon two Prizes.

If even one Koffing was discarded by Roxie while Mewtwo was in play, Blow-Away Bomb places one more damage counter on it:

`260 + 10 = 270`.

That is an exact TAG TEAM KO by effects. The source gives New Moon two Prizes from the Dusknoir self-KOs, then takes **three Prizes** from Mewtwo's KO: net +1 Prize in the exchange, while Blood Moon has become two Energy cheaper.

Even without the Koffing point, a subsequent Blood Moon for 240 easily converts the damaged target.

This is why “opponent self-KO = good for me” is wrong.

### Wide Bench makes Roxie/Koffing stronger

Koffing's Blow-Away Bomb puts one damage counter on **each** opposing Pokémon when discarded with Roxie.

One Koffing against two candidate Pokémon creates two total counters. The same Koffing against six candidate Pokémon creates six total counters.

Then Dusknoir BCR's **Sinister Hand** can move opponent damage counters among the opponent's Pokémon as often as desired.

So New Moon's wide-board habit does two bad things here:

1. creates more total Koffing damage counters;
2. gives Sinister Hand more material to consolidate into exact KOs.

This is a different mathematical reason to keep the Bench narrow from Raichu's Electro Rain and rank-30 Rondo, but it reaches the same construction-policy conclusion.

### Chimecho does not stop TPE deployment

Chimecho's **Bell of Silence** says the opponent cannot play Pokémon that have Abilities **from their hand** during the next turn.

TPE does not play a Pokémon from hand. Its Energy-card effect searches the deck and **puts** eligible Basic Psychic Pokémon directly onto the Bench.

Therefore Bell of Silence can block hand-played Mewtwo, Lunatone, Tapu Lele and other Ability Pokémon, but it does not globally prevent TPE from establishing those Basics from the deck.

A policy that treats Bell of Silence as a universal “Ability Pokémon cannot enter play” lock is incorrect.

### Path is specifically useful against Ursaluna, not Dusknoir

Path suppresses Bloodmoon Ursaluna ex's Rule-Box Ability, removing the Seasoned Skill cost reduction and returning Blood Moon to its printed five-Colorless cost.

Path does **not** suppress the non-Rule-Box PRE Dusknoir / Dusclops Cursed Blast Abilities or BCR Dusknoir's Sinister Hand.

Thus candidate Path under this family should be scored as **Ursaluna tempo denial**, not “turn off the Dusknoir engine.”

If Solrock + Lunatone protect the candidate, this can be asymmetric. Without that shield, Path also suppresses candidate Rule-Box Abilities.

### Grand Tree / Horror House repeats the in-play Stadium exception

Rank 31 carries Grand Tree. If Grand Tree is already in play, Horror House does not disable the Stadium's existing in-play effect. Candidate should compare replacing Grand Tree first, exactly as against rank 2.

### Rank-31 required cases

- **R31-CURSE-URSALUNA-COUPLING:** source Dusknoir self-KO gives candidate a Prize. Must simultaneously lower Blood Moon's cost by one in the source reply model.
- **R31-TWO-DUSKNOIR-KOFFING-MEWTWO:** candidate Mewtwo has one Koffing counter and source can resolve two 130 Cursed Blasts. Must recognize exact 270 effect KO and the 2-Prize-given / 3-Prize-taken exchange.
- **R31-ONEPRIZE-ACTIVE:** source can self-KO one Dusknoir then attack with Ursaluna. Must compare a one-Prize Active against exposing a two-/three-Prize Rule Box rather than scoring HP alone.
- **R31-KOFFING-WIDE-BOARD:** Roxie discards Koffing with multiple candidate Pokémon in play and BCR Dusknoir live. Must count one new counter per candidate Pokémon and allow Sinister Hand to consolidate them.
- **R31-CHIMECHO-TPE:** Bell of Silence active, TPE attachment/search legally available. Must allow TPE to put Ability-bearing Basic Psychic Pokémon from deck onto Bench.
- **R31-PATH-URSALUNA:** Ursaluna Seasoned Skill is currently reducing Blood Moon. Must credit Path with removing that Rule-Box Ability while leaving non-Rule-Box Dusknoir Abilities active.
- **R31-HORROR-GRANDTREE:** Grand Tree already in play before Horror House. Must not treat hand lock as Stadium-effect lock.

## Architecture consequence

After adding the separate rank-2, rank-4/17, rank-19 and these rank-5/9/30/31 gates, **every authenticated Aichi archetype family now has at least one explicit New Moon adversarial decision family**.

That does not mean New Moon is good enough. It means there is now a finite, concrete falsification surface for the retained architecture rather than a growing collection of generic “test the matchup” statements.

The strongest cross-family pattern is not “more search”:

- **bench width is a liability** against Raichu Electro Rain, rank-30 Rondo and rank-31 Koffing/Sinister Hand;
- **native/non-Ability attackers are necessary** against Klefki, Silent Lab, Flutter Mane and other Ability suppression;
- **Horror House only blocks hand plays**, not retreat, attacks, already-in-play Stadium effects or board Abilities;
- **Path has different jobs** by family: stop Electrode, suppress Genesect draw, deny Ursaluna discount, or suppress Rule-Box support—not necessarily remove the opponent's primary lock;
- **discrete HP/Prize breakpoints matter**: 310 Mew VMAX survives two Night Watch hits by 10; 140 Stoutland dies to six-bench Rondo; Altered Creation changes every active Prize map; two Dusknoir + one Koffing counter exactly delete 270-HP Mewtwo.

The unchanged New Moon 60 remains retained for policy investment, but a policy should be considered unready until these family gates and the earlier deltas are satisfied with actor-visible state.

No final deck is selected and no 99.5% result is demonstrated.

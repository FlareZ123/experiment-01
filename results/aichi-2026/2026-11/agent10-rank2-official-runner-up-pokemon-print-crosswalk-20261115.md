**Agent10 · 15 November 2026 · source-authenticity supplement**

## Finding

The official Pokémon deck-code page for `FVkbFV-S0oL2k-VvFvkF` exposes the Japanese card name, set and collector number for all **26 Pokémon copies (17 distinct identities)** in the official 60-card runner-up list. Its normalized card-name/count inventory is the same as Limitless source rank 2, as independently reconciled in [Agent36's inventory report](agent36-runner-up-rank2-inventory-reconciliation-20261115.md).

This extends the source record from a name/count match to a **published Japanese-print crosswalk for the Pokémon section**. It does not establish the physical copies actually shuffled at the event, and it does not resolve the player-attribution discrepancy: event recaps and the player's self-report attribute the code to Otaro, while Limitless labels source rank 2 Takahiro Ando. Keep that identity join unresolved; do not create a 30th opponent or rename the Limitless row.

## What remains unresolved

- The official deck-code text extract exposes print numbers for the Pokémon section, but its Trainer and Energy rows expose only names and quantities. No Trainer/Energy physical-print assignments are inferred from the Limitless English proxy printings.
- No physical deck, card backs, or event-day copies were inspected. This is a published deck-code crosswalk, not physical-print authentication or paper admission.
- The code is attributed to runner-up Otaro by the [player's own event-day report](https://note.com/quick_garlic8250/n/n3f1b573b253a) and by the [event recap](https://pokekameshi.com/cl2026aichidec-extra/). The Limitless source-row player label remains Takahiro Ando; the inventory match does not prove these names identify the same person.
- The field remains incomplete at ranks 23, 25 and 28. This report changes neither the 29/32 source-row count nor the zero paper-qualified matchup count.

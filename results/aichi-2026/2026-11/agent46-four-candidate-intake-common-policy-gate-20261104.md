## Shared-status decision as of this audit

**All four: NOT YET AUDITABLE FOR COMPETITIVE SHARED COMPARISON.** They possess honest and testable deck hypotheses, distinct lists and mathematical/corpus preflights. None has a prospective, actually selected, symmetric owner-private policy, paper-payment verified full consequence, meaningful counterplay and complete official-clock outcomes that would qualify for competitive admission. This status does not reject any list as unplayable, nor certify it legal for the historical Japanese event. All prior tests are developmental.

**Developmental native-pilot prioritization (conditional): first Apex Alchemy v0; other three require unblocking physical-print/native mechanisms.** Apex is the only one of these four with a reported completed 24-attempt original-source-versus-frozen-candidate pilot and preserved alias-preserving native resolution. That gives an existing physical-source disposition and source decision failure to target with a comparable historical control. The candidate cannot be promoted on that basis. The choice is about *next falsifiable integration work per scarce CI budget*, not an ordering of win probabilities.

## Evidence status, limitations, and an owner-safe definition of progress

- Data: existing committed proposals, 29 authenticated original name/count sixties, and Agent50 original rank26 source pilot. None are fresh games or independent raw-trace reanalysis in this intake note.
- Paper eligibility: each proposal reports limited archive/banned-name checks; latest Japanese errata and event-era printing authority have not been independently completed. Candidate list identities cannot be silently swapped to fit the native engine.
- Native transfer: a success in a prepared, staged Store state does not prove the characteristic line is available in natural candidate starts. A successful native game termination without competent opponent action and clock is not an independent win.
- Falsifier quality: demands a **complete paid path and consequential opponent reply**, instead of increasing abstract access or one action count. Bench congestion, Item/Supporter contention, Prizing, ability locks and actual source attacker options remain live requirements.
- Decision output: **zero qualified original source paper games, 29/32 authenticated original inventories and ranks 23/25/28 missing**, so one fixed strongest 60 remains unselected and the 99.5% objective has no demonstrated evidence.

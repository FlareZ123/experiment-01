# Rank 17: consume the Stadium answers before committing Lockbridge's Temple

This is a conditional paper tactical solution for the unchanged original rank-17 Raichu/Electrode sixty and the unchanged Lockbridge candidate. It extends the earlier [Temple/Wobbuffet counterline](../agent17-raichu-temple-wobbuffet-counterline-20261102.md), not its prize-location calculation.

**Key result:** the exact rank-17 list has one PokéStop and one UNB 81 Marshadow, seven Pokémon-recovery Items, and twelve Special Energy with no Basic Energy. No card in that list returns a discarded Stadium or retrieves a card from the Lost Zone. The attached English card-pool archive was inspected for every relevant printing. Thus the minimal durable cut is PokéStop irretrievably spent (discard/Lost Zone, or current Stadium about to be replaced) plus Marshadow in the Lost Zone. Marshadow merely being discarded is insufficient; Rescue Carrier and Rescue Stretcher can repeatedly return it. PokéStop in discard need not be Lost Zoned. Both original and candidate lists have 60 cards; the candidate has one Temple of Sinnoh and one LOT 94 Girafarig.

## Executable conditional line
- With opponent PokéStop active and their Marshadow in discard, a legal Temple play sends PokéStop to discard. Before giving the opponent a turn, an already legal Get Lost removes the Marshadow and the most valuable remaining discard card. If PokéStop was previously discarded, the same line works. If Marshadow was already Lost Zoned, no additional attack is needed.
- If PokéStop is not spent or Marshadow is still on board/hand/deck/prize, do not claim this is permanent. If Temple is already discarded, this exact list cannot recover it. If Girafarig cannot deterministically attack (e.g. Confusion), cannot produce a certificate. If there is an immediate win, take the win, not the lock.
- Do not discard or replace your own live Temple afterward unless another line is better; your own Field Blower/Resetting Hole can undo it.
- The cut eliminates the printed Lightning costs of both Voltorb, Electrode-GX and Alolan Raichu attacks, and Psychic costs of both Gengar & Mimikyu-GX attacks, independent of how many Special Energy are attached or prize-deficit conditions. The remaining printed attacks are Hidden Power (10), Nuzzle (coin paralysis), Quick Attack (20/30), Motivate (20; no Basic Energy targets), Joust (10; removes Tools). Ditto has no attack; Lost Zoned Marshadow cannot attack. Electrode's Extra Energy Bomb and other abilities still exist absent separate suppression. These residual attacks, paralysis, tool removal, decking, prize race and other state constraints mean this is **not a win proof**.

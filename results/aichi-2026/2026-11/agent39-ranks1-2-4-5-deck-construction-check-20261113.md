## Method and limits

- Input: `results/aichi-2026/full-decklists.json` for ranks 1, 2, 4 and 5.
- ACE SPEC identities were derived from the supplied card pool's ACE SPEC rule text/subtype, then counted by card name across all printings.
- Prism Star identities were derived from the card pool's Prism Star subtype/name marker; source glyphs ♢ and ◇ are treated as the same display identity.
- Radiant Pokémon were counted by card identity/name.
- Four-copy counts are summed by name across distinct printings, with only Basic Energy exempted.

This is a deck-construction check, not a physical Japanese print certificate, official tournament registration proof, game result or matchup estimate. Agent48's [identity-screen report](agent48-ranks1-2-4-5-expanded-eligibility-screen-20261113.md) remains the source for the card-pool, event-date and banned-printing conclusions for these ranks.

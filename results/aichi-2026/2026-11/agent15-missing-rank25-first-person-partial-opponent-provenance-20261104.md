The latter two have accessible published source transcriptions, but independent players' lists cannot substitute for Kazuki's unpublished sixty.

The 14 May first-person [kume / `kume_pokeca` Note article](https://note.com/kume_pokeca/n/n6cfd126f5774), titled `CL愛知2026エクストラ ベスト32 アロライワンショット`, confirms that the author used an Alolan Raichu one-shot combo and reached the top-32 knockout stage. The same Japanese source describes entering the elimination stage in **25th Swiss position**, then losing the first elimination match. This correlates to the tournament rank and deck category; no player-name identity beyond the source labels is required to use the archetype evidence.

**Availability boundary:** the note's public excerpt ends at the opening of `デッキ構築解説` (deck construction explanation); the subsequent full list and three supporting images are marked paid (`¥320` promotional / `¥480` standard). That paid section is not represented in this source review. The public excerpt does **not** authenticate any complete 60, print-count quantities, card substitutions, or exact Prize routes.

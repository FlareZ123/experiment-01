## More useful next optimization frontier

The frozen source snapshot dataset shows **21 authentic owner turn-starts immediately following a previous opponent Apex** in each policy arm. Of these 21 treatment turns, Mimikyu physically appears anywhere in play at only **8**, appears with at least **two attached Energy at 4**, and has a Dialga already in owner discard at only **1**. These are turn-start counts, not success probabilities or within-turn impossibility claims; the seed209 Copycat demonstrates that discard and attack can occur during the same turn after the snapshot.

**Decision:** Keep the donor Item policy as a research-owned, optional and tested positive *conditional tactic*. Do not claim it improves overall source-game performance or change the current nominated deck. The next worthwhile intervention has to increase joint feasibility of an actual pre-Apex/Mimikyu-on-board/energy/donor sequence, and convert the Timeless-GX extra turn into Prize progress. The tested singleton donor-payment intervention is too rare, and its two prospective activations happened without actual Apex. Address physical Bench availability, Item-lock timing, first attacking-turn sequencing and competing use of the same Treasure and Psychic Energy instead of repeating a speculative scalar success proxy.

The full Aichi Top32 single fixed-60 target remains unanswered: three original opposing lists are still missing, no full-field policy-qualified data exists, and no verified 99.5% win rate is justified.

## Executable decision and competing action

For **both physical seats** in one actor-visible constructed state, the native action frontier offers the source Mega Lopunny ex PFL84's **Gale Thrust** and **Spiky Hopper**. The event receipt certifies Mega moved from Bench to Active this turn. Opposing Active Girafarig LOT94 has 90 HP, source Mega has 120 damage, candidate has a Benched Mimikyu GRI58 with one Psychic and Dimension Valley, and opponent is within three Prizes. Both legal attacks immediately KO Girafarig. Gale deals 230, Spiky 160; moving the opposing Mimikyu into Active on its own future turn may enable a 230-damage Copycat return KO on the 120-damaged Mega. Spiky's copied 160 does not KO in that same conditional response. **The worker selects Spiky over Gale** by token, not an invented attack.

**Hard negative / reversal:** if the source has one Prize remaining, an immediate legal KO ends the game, so it selects Gale rather than spend the decision on later Copycat risk. When candidate Mimikyu lacks observable funding, it selects Gale; when moved-to-Active evidence is absent it does not assert Gale's 230 damage; when the source Mega did not move, Spiky is selected because stationary Gale's 60 does not KO. Each case is deterministic and local, not a natural game frequency claim.

**Second executable root→prompt continuation:** Buddy-Buddy Poffin TEF144 is selected from offered legal Items when the source has insufficient Vileplume evolution bases and a Bench slot. Its actor-owned `ChooseCardsPrompt` searches the offered deck choices for up to two source Oddish UNB5 and returns their **exact tokens**, rejects an opponent-owned search, and handles the explicit empty-selection token where allowed. Subsequent offered Oddish Bench and Gloom-on-Oddish evolution actions are selected in both seats. The Store, not the worker, decides whether each offer is legal or changes game state.

**Source-specific lock trap:** an Active source Pidgey FLF75 can Peck Off opposing Hood, but if both Oddish are visibly in its discard and a paid opposing Girafarig is Active, the resulting one-turn non-BUS window enables a Get Lost permanent two-base cut. The worker **abstains** from initiating Peck Off in that state instead of acting on Tool removal alone; with only one visible Oddish in discard, it selects the offered Peck Off.

## New competitive defensive and tactical channels (62/62 developmental decisions)

**Guzma BUS115 — actual two-switch sequence.** The worker can play an *offered* Supporter when a Bench Mega Lopunny has visible Energy, the opponent has a viable high-Prize vulnerable Bench target, and the Supporter is unused. It selects the specific opponent-owned Bench Pokémon, then **re-evaluates the subsequent own promotion against the new board**: the chosen opponent Pokémon has already moved to Active by then, so searching for the old Bench target in the updated opponent Bench would incorrectly fail. It chooses the powered source Mega from offered actor-owned Bench choices despite this intervening state change. The constructed dual-seat test explicitly simulates the opponent Active/Bench change and marks the Supporter as already consumed. Guardrails refuse a costless/unpowered attacker, ambiguous owner/index, opponent mixed into own choice set, an unproductive one-Prize gust, and casually removing the source's Active BUS Basic-attack lock.

**Source Karen XYP177 versus Regidrago’s discard payloads.** Nine of the **29 verified original Aichi lists** are labeled Regidrago (source ranks 10, 12, 13, 14, 18, 20, 21, 27 and 32). The exact visible attack payload prints across that group include Dialga-GX UPR100 (Timeless-GX), Dragapult ex TWM130 (Phantom Dive), Kyurem SFA47, Noivern ex PAL153, Hisuian Goodra VSTAR LOR136, Koraidon ex TEF120, Mega Dragonite ex ASC152 and Salamence ex JTG114. The policy will spend Karen only when a Regidrago VSTAR is Active or visibly powered on Bench and **at least two different recognized Dragon payloads are actually in the opponent's public discard**; alternatively, a visible Timeless-GX alone justifies Karen while GX is publicly unused. Clearing these Pokémon from discard removes their immediate Apex Dragon copy access. It refuses Karen without a Regidrago threat, after the GX has been used when only Timeless remains, or under Supporter contention. It does not assume all nine Regidrago lists use every payload or that the opponent cannot re-discard later.

**Artazon PAL171 and Pidgeotto TEU123 Air Mail.** The worker uses Artazon’s offered owner Stadium action to search a non-Rule-Box Basic into an available Bench slot, prioritizing an incomplete two-Oddish/Vileplume line, then Bunnelby, Buneary and Pidgey as state warrants. ## Bunnelby late-game attrition and recursion, including non-TM Omega Barrage

The original Aichi rank15 source has two **Bunnelby PRC121** with the Ancient Trait Omega Barrage, and its actual printed attacks are **Burrow** (discard the opponent's top deck card) and **Rototiller** (shuffle a card from your discard pile into your deck). Previously, after the Stage2 lock setup the worker could abstain instead of attacking and had no plausible way to finish a deck-out control game. This development branch now selects an actually offered paid Burrow when the opponent has cards left (especially one or two), and chooses Rototiller when the source has one or fewer cards remaining in its own deck to prevent a loss on the next draw. With the source’s deck safe and BUS ready to become Active against a Basic attacker, it promotes BUS instead of consuming the turn on a less urgent Burrow; immediate self-deck-out avoidance overrides that priority.

Rototiller’s `ChooseCardsPrompt` is strictly limited to **physically visible cards from the source owner’s public discard**. It returns a single offered token for a high-impact recovery target such as Karen against a live Regidrago discard threat, Grand Tree during an incomplete Stage2 setup, Jet Energy for BUS promotion, or setup evolutions. When AOR's Item lock is live, the policy does not waste its only recursion target on an unusable Poffin over an immediately useful resource. It refuses the opponent's discard and offered aliases for cards not in its own discard pile.

Omega Barrage’s actor-owned `ConfirmPrompt` now considers **all three source-available attacks**: after a first Burrow, a second Burrow can continue milling without Technical Machine; after Rototiller, the second attack can accelerate a resource-loop line. Therefore the worker says **Yes** if any meaningful second attack can be funded, not only when TM: Evolution is attached. It says No when no Energy, compatible evolution, opponent deck cards or recoverable own discard makes the second attack useful. If an exact K1 inventory proves both Vileplume Stage2 absent yet the opponent still has deck cards, Yes may still correctly enable **Burrow**; K1 forbids only a known-dead Evolution transition, not all other attacks.

All these choices are constructed actor-only decision regressions. The 85/85 pass count does not certify a live engine attack, opponent response, completed match, correct policy under all lock interactions, or qualified win rate.


## Physical damage evaluation and remaining source Supporter/recovery channels

**Damage evaluation corrected against the V3 printed-card schema.** The earlier prototype treated *any* printed Weakness or Resistance as sufficient to make a Colorless attacker’s damage unknown, effectively refusing to recognize legal knockouts against ordinary Pokémon that have a completely irrelevant Psychic/Fighting modifier. The worker now evaluates typed `{type,value}` Weakness and Resistance in rulebook order, including Colorless ×2 and −30 cases, while not inventing results for an unrecognized matching modifier. Mega Lopunny ex’s **Spiky Hopper** is allowed to bypass defensive effects on the target when its printed text says so, even though **Gale Thrust** cannot be assumed to ignore an opposing Tool or active defensive Ability. The **Guzma → Gale Thrust** Bench-target reachability gate uses the same type-aware calculation. Local reversible examples show ordinary irrelevant weaknesses restore legal damage, Colorless Resistance can remove a conditional KO, and Colorless Weakness can restore it.

**Xtransceiver NVI96 and N FCO105.** The worker has a real offered Xtransceiver Item root (if Items and the Supporter window are available) followed only on a *successful engine coin result* by an actor-owned one-Supporter deck search. It selects Karen when a currently visible Regidrago discard threat matters, Guzma for a high-Prize gust, Guzma & Hala for an incomplete Bunnelby/Vileplume line with affordable discard payment, or N when hand/prize information calls for disruption. A known-zero K1 Supporter pool makes Xtransceiver nonproductive; K0 does not. N can rescue a nearly empty source hand while six Prizes remain, or shrink a large opposing hand when the opponent is down to two Prizes; it refuses known protected near-term singleton resources and a consumed Supporter window.

**Eldegoss V RCL19: Happy Match and Float Up.** Original rank15 carries exactly one Eldegoss V. It now Benches Eldegoss only when a Supporter in the own *public* discard is useful, the Bench can still reserve missing Vileplume lock slots, and public Path to the Peak/Iron Thorns ability suppression does not make Happy Match knowingly inert. Its owner-discard `ChooseCardsPrompt` chooses only a physically held source Supporter, for example Karen against Regidrago, not an opponent’s or imaginary discarded card. Eldegoss’s paid Float Up attack can return the vulnerable two-Prize Pokémon and its attachments into the deck; the subsequent *optional* self-shuffle confirmation is deliberately **No** if Eldegoss is the source’s only Pokémon in play, since returning its last Pokémon would immediately lose the game. With a Bench, the policy chooses Yes. This closes a critical recursion and win-condition safety hole.

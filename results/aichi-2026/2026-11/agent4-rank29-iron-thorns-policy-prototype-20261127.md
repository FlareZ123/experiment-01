## Concrete implementation

Rank 29 shares 32 distinct card names with the exact rank-6 Iron Thorns list. The new module copies the standalone rank-6 actor-visible root-and-prompt implementation as an implementation base, leaving rank 6 unmodified, then adds rank-29 identity and action logic. The rank-29-only three names replace rank-6-only Gladion/Palace Book/Echoing Horn:

| Rank-29-only card | Root decision | Actor-owned continuation |
|---|---|---|
| **Field Blower GRI 125 ×1** | Plays to remove an opponent's Stealthy Hood, other Tool, or Stadium; Hood response receives higher priority than routine Energy denial | Chooses up to two targetable opponent Tools/Stadium, prioritizing Stealthy Hood; does not target own Tools |
| **Target Whistle PHF 106 ×1** | Plays only if opponent has an open Bench slot and a *visible known Basic from the registered candidate 60s* in discard; no false access for arbitrary discard entries | Selects a known Basic in the opponent's discard, preserving source-zone information when the destination is Bench |
| **Peonia CRE 149 ×1** | Last-resort play only when the host exposes exact owner K1 membership, at least three safe hand replacements, and at least three remaining Prizes | Chooses three physical Prize-slot **indices**, then three high-DCI owner hand cards to re-Prize, then their order. Declines one/two-card variants and never reads concealed Prize identities |

Additional rank-29 changes: **Palace Belt ×2** (up from one) receives a positive Active Iron Thorns setup score when no Tool is attached; **Trainers' Mail ×2, Enhanced Hammer ×1, Tropical Beach ×2** remain the rank-29 source counts. Existing common source actions (Energy denial, Guzma, Tag Call/Guzma & Hala, Lusamine/Handiwork, Thunder Mountain, tool-lock responses, Volt Cyclone) are retained. **VS Seeker ×3 was previously a dead root card in the copied policy**; this module adds its public-discard-dependent Item root and typed actor-owned Supporter retrieval prompt, distinguishing immediate Active Energy removal from lower-priority deck milling.

**Actual native setup choices are now implemented:** choose available Iron Thorns Basics in the engine's setup order, choose second when offered pre-deal GO_FIRST (a strategic hypothesis to enable Guzma & Hala on the first own turn), accept available post-mulligan Bench placements, and take the largest legal mulligan bonus-draw choice. Setup prompts are accepted only when their typed engine source and phase agree. The going-second decision is not empirically qualified.

## Opening-line feasibility result (new, separate from policy test count)

A separate reproducible sampled sensitivity companion `simulation/evaluation/2026-11/agent4-rank29-opening-line-montecarlo.cjs` drew **150,000 accepted rank-29 source hands** using seed **20261127**, real four-Basic mulligan redraws, exact six-Prize sampling, and a normal first draw. The machine-readable `results/aichi-2026/2026-11/agent4-rank29-opening-line-mc-150k-20261127.json` records raw counts/Wilson intervals. The joint one-turn `Guzma & Hala → Thunder Mountain + Double Colorless Energy` line was reachable in **33.945%** of simulated hands under a relaxed non-reserved two-card discard payment rule (95% sampling interval **33.706–34.185%**), versus **21.929%** under a deliberately restrictive cheap-discard proxy (95% **21.721–22.139%**). Holding both required singletons in the starting/first-draw hand without any Supporter occurred **1.271%** of samples. At least one of Thunder Mountain and DCE was unavailable because it was Prized in approximately **19.3%** of cases; each individually was Prized around 10.1%. Average mulligans **1.5014**, consistent with the exact one-hand no-Basic probability **60.050%** and geometric mean redraw count **1.5031**. The underlying ideal location-event probability was already established **exactly** by Agent11 and Agent33 on 2 Nov: **33.78150571%** (the 33.945% Monte Carlo is compatible within sampling variation). This 150k sample is **not a new discovery of the opening access rate**; its narrow additional perspective is a hand-specific cheap-discard proxy yielding 21.929%, which should be compared with Agent18's prior exact rank-6 protected-card sensitivity and not treated as a validated bound. See `results/aichi-2026/agent33-rank29-firstturn-tagcall-ghala-envelope-20261102.md` for exact counting and existing successfully staged two-seat paid native Tag Call→G&H→Volt Cyclone (140 damage) witness. These probabilities do **not** model Trainers' Mail, Speed draw, Camping Gear, opposing mulligan bonus draws, opposing lock, later turns, or actual wins; they are neither a lower/upper bound on the real gameplay rate nor tournament game evidence. They make the high-impact first-turn target quantitatively concrete and explain why discard realism and connector timing matter.

In response, the policy now explicitly prioritizes **Tag Call/G&H/Thunder Mountain before Speed Lightning attachment** when it can assemble a first-turn DCE Volt Cyclone, and prioritizes the first backup Iron Thorns as an Energy handoff while limiting excess two-Prize Bench exposure. The last isolated actor-protocol test execution on the branch showed **46/46 passing**. These remain deterministic decision-path checks, not independent native game traces.

It is *not* the Volt Cyclone owner-owned transfer.

- The [provenance hook](../../../simulation/evaluation/2026-11/agent36-prompt-provenance.cjs) now identifies the **Handheld Fan Tool reducer** for this exact `AfterDamageEffect` rather than erroneously attributing it to the attacker's `effect.attack.name`. - The [projector](../../../simulation/evaluation/2026-11/agent36-prompt-projection.cjs) now accepts only the exact Tool still attached to the actor's Active, exact native prompt source/shape, physical attacking Active attached Energy, and visible opposing Bench targets. Every move is native decoded/validated; no hidden contents or fake actor-owned Energy are used. It offers **one native-validated empty selection only when no physical move is available**, respecting the printed requirement to move an Energy when possible.
- The [typed worker protocol](../../../simulation/evaluation/2026-11/agent49-policy-protocol.cjs) admits the exact `Handheld Fan TWM` zero-transfer branch for `energy-attachment`, while rejecting an empty choice for any different source or incorrect effect name. ## 28 November: Peonia only when a singleton is provably Prized

The source rank-29 list carries singleton `Double Colorless Energy`, `Thunder Mountain ♢`, and `Megaton Blower`; the first two can be decisive for establishing Iron Thorns and the third for accessible Tool/Special Energy denial. The prior worker played Peonia whenever an exact owner-K1 deck view existed, three Prize cards remained, and three unprotected replacement cards were held—even when the missing card was already accessible in the deck, discard, Lost Zone, hand or on the board. This was a genuine Supporter-contention and resource-waste risk.

The branch now computes an **actor-only, singleton-specific Prize inference** from a certified current `ownerK1.knownPrintings` deck-membership witness and the owner's visible non-Prize zones: own hand, discard, Lost Zone, resolving Supporter, own Stadium, and all own Active/Bench stacks, Energy and Tools. When a unique source singleton is absent from both the certified deck and every visible non-Prize zone, it is inferred to remain Prized. The policy then allows the **existing native-exact-three Peonia branch** only if DCE is known Prized (urgency 180 when the Active still needs DCE), Thunder Mountain is known Prized (urgency 150 when the Active needs acceleration), or Megaton Blower is known Prized and exposed opposing Tools/Special Energy make it important. It continues to require three low-cost replacement cards, at least three remaining Prizes, and an unused Supporter. No identity or face-up value of any hidden physical Prize is exposed. The chance of retrieving a single known-Prized singleton by picking three uniformly unknown cards among six is only **3/6**, so this improves action relevance without guaranteeing recovery.

New tests reject Peonia on mere K1 certification, source card present in K1 deck, source card in owner discard, Lost Zone, or attached to the Active, K1 untrusted, and non-urgent lone Megaton; they accept urgent known-Prized DCE or Thunder. **Current local result: 68/68 worker/typed-protocol V8 cases and 6/6 mock-native projection/continuation cases.** This is a deterministic policy heuristic, not sampled game evidence or a validated numerical win-rate improvement. The branch has not been merged or executed in full native matches.

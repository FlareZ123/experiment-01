# Rank 22 candidate discriminator: Lockbridge Wobbuffet preemption vs Hood Ranger recovery

Agent17 · 2026-11-27. **Decision affected:** Hood Shield vs Lockbridge fixed-60 ranking. This is exact-list/card-text state analysis, not a game or matchup result.

## Candidate delta that matters

The two current candidate 60s are almost identical at this axis:

- **Hood Shield:** Lillie's Clefairy ex ×2 + **Pokémon Ranger STS 104 ×1**; no Wobbuffet, no Temple.
- **Lockbridge:** Lillie's Clefairy ex ×1 + **Wobbuffet PHF 36 ×1 + Temple of Sinnoh ×1**; no Pokémon Ranger.
- Both carry Stealthy Hood ×2, Field Blower ×1 and otherwise share the relevant Psychic engine.

Rank22 has Vikavolt V ×2, Tapu Koko ◇, Garbotoxin, Wally, Battle Compressor ×2, Lightning ×3 and only Basic Energy. Temple of Sinnoh therefore does not directly deny rank22's Energy package; the rank22-relevant Lockbridge substitution is primarily Wobbuffet versus Ranger.

## Opposite timing advantages

### Lockbridge: preempt the acceleration

Active Wobbuffet's **Bide Barricade** removes non-Psychic Pokémon Abilities in play, hands and discard. Tapu Koko ◇ is Lightning, so a live Active Wobbuffet prevents **Dance of the Ancients**.

That directly attacks rank22's fastest source-native route to a powered Benched Vikavolt. It also suppresses rank22 Crobat V, Dedenne-GX and Squawkabilly ex while Active. Psychic Tapu Lele-GX and Psychic Garbodor retain their Abilities under the exception.

Important cost imposed on rank22: if Guzma is required to move Wobbuffet out of Active before Dance, that turn's Supporter is consumed. If rank22 still has only Trubbish rather than an evolved Garbodor, it cannot simultaneously assume Wally that same turn. Thus Wobbuffet can create a real **Supporter-contention tax** on the Koko → Vikavolt + Garbotoxin dual-lock sequence.

This is pre-lock prevention, not proof the lock never happens: Vikavolt can be powered over later turns; Wobbuffet can be gusted/KO'd; an already-evolved Garbodor can receive a Tool after Koko without Wally.

### Hood Shield: clear the attack effect after it lands

Vikavolt V's **Paralyzing Bolt** puts an effect on the opponent: during the opponent's next turn they cannot play Item cards from hand.

Pokémon Ranger says: **remove all effects of attacks on each player and their Pokémon.** If Hood Shield can actually play Ranger on the affected turn, it removes Paralyzing Bolt's Item-lock effect.

That is a direct answer Lockbridge no longer contains.

But the answer is availability-sensitive:

- VS Seeker, Fog Crystal and Quick Ball are Items and cannot be newly played while Paralyzing Bolt is active.
- Tapu Lele-GX Wonder Tag can find Ranger only if Lele can be played and its Ability is live; a simultaneous Garbotoxin lock can prevent this.
- Forest Seal Stone can search any card only if it was already attached and its Ability remains usable; Garbotoxin can suppress it.
- Therefore “one Ranger in the list” is not equivalent to “Ranger is always available after Bolt.” The strongest positive state is Ranger already in hand (or an already-live non-Item search resource not suppressed by Garbotoxin).

## Matched candidate challenge states

A future two-profile policy comparison should include at least these same public states:

1. **Pre-Bolt Koko line available.** Rank22 has discarded Lightning, Benched Koko + Vikavolt and no established Garbotoxin; candidate turn ended with Wobbuffet Active versus the Hood profile's corresponding non-Wobb Active. Measure whether Lockbridge actually prevents Dance while Hood cannot.
2. **Bolt landed, no Garbotoxin.** Candidate is Item-locked for the turn; Hood has Ranger already in hand. Hood should play Ranger when restoring Items is materially useful; Lockbridge has no equivalent card.
3. **Bolt + Garbotoxin, Ranger already in hand.** Hood can still play the Supporter Ranger to remove the attack effect, but Garbotoxin remains. Do not falsely model Ranger as removing an Ability lock.
4. **Bolt + Garbotoxin, Ranger not in hand.** Hood should not assume Item-based access to Ranger or Wonder Tag under active Garbotoxin. Lockbridge likewise cannot claim Wobbuffet preemption after the lock has already landed.
5. **Wobbuffet gets Guzma'd.** If rank22 uses Guzma to enable Koko, account for the consumed Supporter: no same-turn Wally unless Garbodor was already evolved. This is the state where Lockbridge's preventive value should appear as a contention cost rather than a binary permanent lock.
6. **Temple control.** Rank22's exact Energy are Basic Darkness/Lightning, so Lockbridge's Temple should not receive phantom Energy-denial value in this matchup.

## Decision implication

Rank22 is a good fixed-60 discriminator because the two candidates solve the same threat at different times:

- Lockbridge spends a deck slot on **preventing/raising the cost of the Koko acceleration before Paralyzing Bolt**.
- Hood Shield spends a deck slot on **removing Paralyzing Bolt after it has been used**.

Neither conditional dominates from card text alone. The next useful evidence is paired actor-visible decisions/full games with these branches represented correctly, not a generic consistency average. Until then, rank22 should be treated as an unresolved candidate tradeoff, not evidence for either final 60.

No candidate list was changed by this analysis.

## Result
The previously isolated Max Geist attachment and Guzma promotion defects are instances of one problem: greedily scoring legal root actions independently fails to evaluate a full same-turn terminal route. Compose damage, resource expenditure, target identity, one-use connectors, hidden information and mandatory choices before preferring a terminal action.

Implemented modules:
- [Attack oracle](agent1-terminal-attack-oracle-20261127.cjs): direct stable Psychic-board attacks and Astral Barrage damage-counter pair choices; correctly distinguishes Energy units from cards, cost discounts, remaining HP, damage versus counter immunity, Prize award and board-out.
- [Deck belief](agent1-actor-deck-belief-20261127.cjs): exact category-count Bayesian mixtures for unknown Prize allocations, random draw, hand shuffle into deck, Iono bottom segment, and posterior reveal at authenticated deck search.
- [Joint planner](agent1-joint-terminal-planner-20261127.cjs): exact rational finite-horizon DP over fixed own/opposing board identities, attachment, Underworld Door, Guzma, retreat, Fog, Night Stretcher, VS Seeker, Compressor, Float/Forest Seal Stone, Valley, Raihan, Teammates, Arven, Lillie, Unfair Stamp and Iono. Card/Supporter/Ability/retreat limits consumed once. It has probability-maximization, failure-spend tie objectives, universal hidden-support guarantee and deterministic witness modes. 
- [Public adapter](agent1-mirror-public-witness-20261127.cjs) and [tests](agent1-mirror-public-witness-tests-20261127.cjs): exact Hood/Lockbridge list conservation, actor-owned observations, physical Bench indices, current root token match, typed Guzma/Underworld Door/known discard and authenticated K1 search choices, sequence-scoped intent.

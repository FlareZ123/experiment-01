## Research conclusion

Repairing a proposal-only Supporter quota mutation may be necessary for native action legality, but making more real Store-accepted Supporters available does **not** establish better tactical decisions. The common bilateral policy must separately compare how each Supporter changes mobility, imminent attack setup, resources and opposing responses. This is a **regression and qualification-falsification input**, not an eligible deck win, competent opponent, 25-minute paper match, or proof of an unchanged best 60. The Aichi benchmark stays at **29/32 exact original decklists, zero qualified games, no nominated winner and no demonstrated 99.5%**.

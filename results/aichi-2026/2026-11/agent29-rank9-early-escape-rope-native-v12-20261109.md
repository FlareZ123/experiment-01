## Limits and next decisive test

These two native attacks gained from an **opponent-selected switch target**. The native opponents' use of Tapu Lele followed by Pass after Claw demonstrates policy weakness. We cannot infer that a competent opposing player would choose those same replacement Pokémon. The native Match state also lacks an independently qualified paper 25-minute clock and complete original-owner custody. Full Japanese paper Expanded legality and accurate current card effects cannot be deduced from this execution alone.

Exactly **zero** games meet the repository's competitive qualification threshold. The rank-9 source deck is a useful fixed original physical benchmark, with **no nomination** of one field-dominating final 60. The objective remains one single universally fixed 60 against all Top32, with no 99.5% demonstration.

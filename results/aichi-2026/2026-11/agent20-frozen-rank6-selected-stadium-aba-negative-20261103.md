## Interpreting the negative result

- **Native-accepted action availability does not establish tactical advantage.** For these two exact historical original physical starts, candidate Stadium was playable and chosen when offered, yet source continued attacking and the native winner stayed unchanged. The resulting game does involve other altered public Stadium effects, so equality of winner counts does not mean identical action histories throughout.
- Stadium access and Supporter access **compete at the same owner root**. Agent7 had already certified that the two identical original Pass states also had playable and fully settled `Lillie's Determination M1L` alternatives. The current treatment explicitly chooses Stadium over a possible Lillie route; it does not optimize their opportunity costs.
- Existing original source cards, both fixed candidate 60s, the frozen v1 86-option base policy, and all initial physical deals are unchanged. However, treatment alters only **candidate seat A turn4** and is selected after studying precisely these old states. Its zero native winner improvements are an illustrative developmental result, **not** a statistical estimate of wider win-rate effect or a prospective two-seat optimization.
- Actual paper-competent opponent decisions, all card-effect fidelity and cost guards, official tournament clock, and matched prospective independent source-seed samples remain unqualified. Native game-end is an engine observation, not a paper benchmark win.

The clear next test is one **precommitted general owner policy** comparing all paper-payable Stadium, Lillie, attack, setup and resource-conservation lines using owner-visible public state across both physical seats and original source ranks 5/6/11, then testing genuine selected actions and opponent reactions. A generic unconditional Stadium-after-Pass rule would not be justified by these four original games.

**Global field qualification remains 29/32 printed original 60s authenticated (missing ranks 23,25,28), zero paper-competitive qualified games, no selected fixed final 60, and no demonstrated 99.5% win rate.**

## Interpretation and release gate

This experiment rejects the narrow practical claim that the tested after-Pass Compressor hook repairs the observed two inspected Regidrago replays under the **registered corrected** engine and policy profile. It does **not** show that Battle Compressor is strategically bad, redundant, unusable, or absent from other paper-valid states. It cannot explain why an earlier development study reported a potential held Compressor at a funded VSTAR root: that report used a materially different correction/profile stack, so replay states are not interchangeable. Preserve the native correction flags, source deck hash, original seed and physical Store evidence when comparing.

The chance that five separate singleton Dragon donors are all Prized, the value of an available donor's particular attack, future Energy/Tool and Supporter contention, opposing locks and response lines all require separate work. Those conditions must not be inferred from the untriggered policy's zero card moves.

The more productive next step is **a general same-root action planner** that compares Item/Supporter options, legal payments, search and discard requirements, attack cost, alternative connector use and opponent response. Follow Critic's owner-only Trainer planning direction and avoid another retrospective source-specific post-Pass intervention without independently registered opportunities. The [source13 opening conjunction census](../agent8-rank13-compressor-vstar-opening-conjunction-20261103.md) is a mathematical access reference, never a verified attack rate.

**Fixed-60 objective:** 29/32 authentic opposing original lists, zero competitively qualified games, no selected best 60 and no established 99.5% field win rate.

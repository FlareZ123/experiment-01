# Selected bilateral v3 prospective attempt: no exercised policy difference and two hard native aborts

**Agent 4 | 3 November 2026 | independently registered development test, 48 genuine pinned Store attempts; release gate failed closed**

## Question and the decision

Can one actual symmetric, environment-bound policy composition replace an arbitrary collection of optional interventions, carry exact owner-visible paper prerequisites at every genuine Trainer decision, and improve real funded Shadow Rider mobility while preserving physically fair matched opponent starts?

**Result:** The selected v3 implementation was exercised on 48 predeclared attempts with the native Twinleaf Store. The read-only paper denominator was successfully attached to all **14 intact selected-mode actor observation records**. The funded Float Stone root was enabled in the selected mode and selected **zero** times. In the 14 intact, physically verifiable original-source/candidate contexts, the selected policy, control A and control A repeat had **identical complete native root-action ledger hashes**, action endings and root trajectories. The other two contexts were identically aborted at a real native Ability dispatch in **all three arms**; their accepted preabort roots and source-deal fingerprints matched, while the full candidate+source initial-deal fingerprints and runtime actor receipts are missing. Thus the predeclared **all-16 release gate correctly failed**. There is no demonstrated improvement, game win rate or qualified fixed 60.

The finding is stronger than a mock policy test: it falsifies any claim that merely selecting both existing interventions changes outcomes in this specific prospective 48-attempt panel.

## Release decision, next experimental priority

The selected v3 attempt is **rejected as a qualified release**. It provides authentic read-only paper-risk coverage but no realized gameplay intervention on these registered deals, and the two rank6 aborts mean two full paired contexts and runtime receipts are missing. Historical Agent31 independent held-out data found a genuine funded Float Stone Tool → Retreat → attack chain in **1 of 16** paired rank1 contexts, with unchanged opponent 60s and one changed native trajectory. That source-specific finding remains valid as a developmental counterexample; this new broader v3 sample does not contradict it.

A higher-value next experiment should:
1. Capture the exact rejected Ability and owner/public lock geometry at both new rank6 aborts; test a conservative native-valid fallback under identical preregistered seeds, retaining both original decks, physical opening, and decisions.
2. Select and register one *actually action-changing* owner-root replacement from proven public paper-affordable cards, compared to all competing uses of the same Supporter/Item resources. Agent5's independently verified original rank9 held Battle Compressor after native Pass is one feasible paper-legal root, though it still requires strategic choice, paid Store settlement and opponent follow-up.
3. Expand to all **32 authenticated exact original printed 60s** before any field claim; currently original 60s for **ranks 23,25,28** have not been independently authenticated.
4. Show actual paper rules, full opponent competence, match clock and uncertainty on complete played games before treating any native game termination as a competitive result.

**One final fixed 60 selected: no. Competitively qualified field games: zero. Demonstrated 99.5% win rate: none.**

## Three distinct prospective branches with printed prerequisites

1. **Bare two-Item trap, A:** Candidate plays Field Blower, physically targets source Bench index3's Float Stone (Tool removal), then plays Counter Catcher targeting that one-Psychic VMAX (opponent's targeted gust). Printed `Counter Catcher PAR` requires candidate **more remaining Prizes** than source; **6 > 1** holds. The source's next turn is then adversarial: it may use its held `Guzma BUS` to gust one of candidate's five Benched Pokémon and switch its own one-Psychic trapped Active to the **previously funded three-Psychic VMAX**. That path avoids ordinary retreat cost if both switch prompts can be completed. It can potentially resume `Max Geist`, subject to actual prompts, other effects, and resolution.
2. **Hand-reducing three-card trap, B:** Candidate first removes Float Stone, then gusts the one-Psychic VMAX, then plays physically held `Ace Trainer AOR`. Printed Ace Trainer's prerequisite is also **6 > 1**; it shuffles both hands into their decks, gives its user six new cards, and gives the opponent **three**. Candidate played no Supporter in real turn37 roots180–182, so the ordinary quota is unused. The source's held `Guzma` and two `VS Seeker` are moved out of immediate hand by this effect **if correctly resolved**. It may redraw search/switch outs, and the source may draw into a normal Energy attachment to pay Retreat or other mobility; exact source continuation remains to be evaluated.
3. **Timing/target controls, C:** Repeat the same physical trial with **Counter Catcher alone**, **Field Blower alone**, and reversed order of the two Items, plus a **printed-legal Ace Trainer without the two Items** as distinct exploratory controls. Printed target selection and whether the source can attack must be checked against genuine native Store prompts. Reserve a negative control in which the user has no greater remaining Prize count, to ensure the card-specific gate rejects Counter Catcher and Ace Trainer.

## Another independent escape: a single Energy attachment can pay normal retreat

A separate printed-card inspection sharpens the tactical diagnosis. Original source rank1 has **11 Psychic Energy** and **four Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX** in its authenticated 60; the VMAX printing `swsh6-75` has **retreat cost two Colorless**, and the gusted one-Psychic Active VMAX physically retains its one Psychic Energy. If the source has or draws **one attachable Energy card on its next turn**, its normal one-per-turn hand attachment can take that Active to **two Energy**, allowing it to **pay retreat two** and promote the still-funded three-Psychic Benched VMAX. The source can then potentially use Max Geist without having drawn Guzma. The original native Store must verify the retreat and attack under all current effects and prompt rules. The owner might also choose a different route.

This is **conditional** on actually obtaining the Energy and on no applicable retreat lock. `Underworld Door` itself specifies a **Benched** Psychic target; it cannot attach directly to the gusted **Active** VMAX. It may draw additional cards if the source can attach to an eligible *Benched* Pokémon, indirectly improving recovery. Therefore the Ace Trainer branch must challenge **both a switching Supporter line and a normal one-attachment, two-Energy paid-retreat line**. A temporary trap, even after hand disruption, is not automatically a turn-denying lock. The exact source hand after Ace Trainer and native source turn38 have not yet been tested in that counterfactual branch.

# New Moon fixed-60 decision: no material redesign justified after full authenticated-family adversarial pass

**Agent 1 · 27 November 2026**  
**Decision:** retain the exact 60-card New Moon Lockbreaker v1 for future policy investment. Do **not** add Pokémon Ranger or another narrow silver bullet yet.

This is a deck-allocation decision, not a win-rate claim. The full authenticated-family acceptance pass exposed real weaknesses, but it also showed that the current unusual slots are doing overlapping work across several opponent families. The obvious “fix” cards would trade broad survival for one narrow contingency.

## The strongest proposed addition: Pokémon Ranger

The clearest unresolved source effect is rank-19 ADP's **Altered Creation-GX**. Once the boosted effect resolves, New Moon has no Ranger and must play under the extra damage / extra-Prize map for the rest of the game.

A Pokémon Ranger would erase that persistent attack effect after it resolves. That is a real benefit.

It is still not enough to justify changing the 60 now, because:

1. rank 19 is one authenticated source list, while the slots Ranger would displace cover several ranks/families;
2. Ranger consumes the turn's Supporter, directly contending with Ace Trainer / Lillie's Determination;
3. the existing 60 has proactive counterplay: Horror House before an unfunded ADP, Fairy-Zone Dragon weakness, Full Moon Rondo breakpoints and 300-damage Night Watch under live Fairy Zone;
4. source rank 19 has three Sky Field and Marshadow, so a defensive plan still needs correct timing and cannot be reduced to “draw Ranger”;
5. no evidence yet says post-Altered-Creation states occur often enough, or are sufficiently unrecoverable, to dominate the wider opportunity cost.

Ranger remains the **leading reopen card** if rank-19 policy testing later shows that funded Altered Creation is both frequent and decisive.

## Why the plausible cuts are worse right now

### Cut one Canceling Cologne?

**Reject.** Two Cologne copies answer a much broader set of Active-Ability failures: Iron Thorns Initialization; opposing Klefki; rank-15 Active BUS Vileplume; rank-19 Active Flutter Mane; rank-30 Active Empoleon V; and other Active lock states where the continuation needs a one-turn Ability window.

### Cut one Field Blower?

**Reject.** Two Blower copies are central against the four authenticated Absol/Garbodor ranks, Stealthy Hood protecting lock Pokémon, and Tool/Stadium contention. Reducing the count makes already-tight multi-Garbodor states worse.

### Cut one Path to the Peak?

**Reject.** Path suppresses Electrode-GX before Bomb, Genesect V draw, rank-31 Ursaluna Seasoned Skill, rank-1 Shadow Rider Underworld Door and other Rule-Box support. It can be asymmetric behind Solrock + Lunatone.

### Cut one Sky Field?

This is the least absurd Ranger swap, but still **not justified**.

Sky Field is a neutral Stadium replacement when Path would self-lock the selected attack and Solrock/Lunatone are unavailable; it replaces Grand Tree / Silent Lab / Temple without consuming an Item; it can create the Full Moon Rondo breakpoint needed to KO rank-2 Stoutland; and it supports emergency board construction.

The review also found that Sky Field can be actively bad against Raichu, rank-30 Rondo and rank-31 Koffing/Sinister Hand. That makes it highly state-dependent, not dispensable.

If later evidence demands Ranger, **Sky Field #2 is the first slot to re-open**, because its marginal value is more matchup-dependent than Cologne/Blower/Path. That is a future testable branch, not a present change.

### Cut Ace Trainer / Lillie's Determination?

**Reject.** Ace Trainer is part of the intended Cursed-Blast Prize donation -> hand compression -> Night Watch line. Lillie's Determination is ordinary recovery/draw. Ranger would consume the same Supporter quota and reduce the density of the deck's own primary plan.

### Cut a setup Item, TPE, Powerglass or Pokémon?

**Reject absent stronger evidence.** The hard-family work made these cards more important: TPE is a non-Item deployment engine through rank-2 dual lock and Chimecho Bell of Silence; Powerglass remains a Tool through Item lock; native Gengar is the non-Perfection Horror House channel under Klefki/Silent Lab; Clefairy is the two-Energy Stoutland answer and Dragon weakness engine; Dusclops supplies exact damage/Prize manipulation; Solrock/Lunatone creates the strongest asymmetric Path states.

This is not a quantitative win-contribution table. It is the opportunity-cost map required before changing a fixed 60.

## Reopen conditions

Reconsider the exact 60 only if:

1. actor-visible rank-19 tests repeatedly reach funded Altered Creation states where the current 60 has no credible Prize-map recovery and Ranger changes the legal continuation;
2. Sky Field #2 is shown to be dominated where it is intended as a neutral Stadium out / Rondo enabler;
3. a different multi-family addition solves two or more newly identified hard families without destroying current lockbreak coverage;
4. policy cannot realistically realize native Gengar, Clefairy, Path sequencing, TPE narrowing or lock-resilient Powerglass lines.

## Recommendation

**Retain the exact New Moon Lockbreaker v1 60 unchanged.** Allocate future work to the consolidated actor-visible acceptance contract before changing cards.

Every authenticated archetype family now has an explicit failure/recovery surface, and the candidate's contested slots have identifiable cross-family jobs. The remaining issue is whether a competent policy can realize those jobs often enough—not a demonstrated need for a new 60.

No final best deck and no 99.5% performance claim is made.

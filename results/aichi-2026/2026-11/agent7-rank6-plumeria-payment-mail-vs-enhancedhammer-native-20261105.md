## Decision point and falsifiable question

The authentic Aichi Open League 2026 original rank-6 Iron Thorns ex source,
on physical seat1, original game `6:100:A:champion-reference`, turn3,
held Plumeria BUS, two Trainers' Mail ROS and Enhanced Hammer TWM
in an otherwise nine-card hand. After the opponent's actual first Shadow
Mist, the native source bot chose Pass. The independently tested
owner-only Plumeria after-Pass branch instead paid both Mail to remove
the only Basic Psychic Energy from opponent Active Shadow Rider Calyrex V.
The recipient's turn4 original native decision then became Pass rather than
the baseline next Shadow Mist.

This new test asks whether **a third available two-card payment,
one Enhanced Hammer plus one Trainers' Mail**, could obtain **the same
native real Store denial** while preserving one Mail as a potential
future Trainer-search connector. No claims of superior long-run win rate
or optimal discard policy were registered.

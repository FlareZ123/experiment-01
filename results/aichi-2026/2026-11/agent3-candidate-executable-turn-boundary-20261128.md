For initial-opening intuition only, the frozen 60 has four VMAX and one Radiant. Conditional on the particular Radiant being among the original seven, the other six hand cards and six Prize cards occupy 12 of the remaining 59 cards. Ignoring other Basic requirements, the mathematical chance that **all four VMAX** are outside the remaining deck is
`C(12,4)/C(59,4)=0.00108761` (about 0.109%); the chance that fewer than two remain in deck is about 2.381%. These are exact *card-location* calculations under the stated random distribution, **not** opening-execution or game-success probabilities. Later turns and a choice conditioned on other opening Basics change the distribution.

More directly: the 60 has **14 Basics** including Radiant and 13 others, and 4 VMAX. A valid initial seven contains at least one Basic with probability `1-C(46,7)/C(60,7)=0.8614093191`. The probability a seven-card opening has **Radiant plus another Basic** (Radiant can be Benched) is `(7/60)×[1-C(46,6)/C(59,6)]=0.0924132975` unconditionally, or **10.728% of valid openings**. Enumerating the multivariate hypergeometric opening hand and subsequent six random Prize cards, the chance this opening also has **at least two of the four VMAX in the remaining deck** is `0.0904487480` unconditionally, or **10.500% of valid openings**. This quantifies the physical frequency of a possible *opening* Climactic Gate line, not its game value or execution feasibility. It does not credit finding Radiant by a search card, or playing later-turn Radiant.

For **Wonder Tag**, the opening probability of the unique Tapu Lele-GX among seven, at least one *other* Basic, and **no Supporter in hand** is
`(7/60)×[C(50,6)-C(37,6)]/C(59,6)=0.0351260304` unconditionally or **4.078% of valid openings**, using exactly nine Supporters and the same fourteen Basics. This is card-location reach only; it does not mean the Supporter search succeeds, has no opportunity cost, or produces a winning line.

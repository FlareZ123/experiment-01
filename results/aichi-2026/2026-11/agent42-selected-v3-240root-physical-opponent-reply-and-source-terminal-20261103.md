## Limits and implications

This v2 experiment uses an **outcome-selected historical seed**. In the selected arm, a source opponent completed a printed move that is tactically meaningful and defeated the candidate in the native environment; this informs simulator source-policy strength and potential weakness of that candidate. The complete Aichi Top 32 field has neither been supplied in full nor evaluated with competitively audited human-level opposing decision policies, a paper rule fidelity certificate, or a 25-minute match clock.

The result justifies more original-source **independently seeded prospective** tests under a fixed full combined policy.

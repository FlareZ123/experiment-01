## Decisions that change games, not just accessibility graphs

1. **Immediate winning gust:** When a powered Regidrago has Goodra's 200-damage attack available, Counter Catcher takes a visible 160-HP two-Prize Benched Dedenne-GX to win rather than attacking a 320-HP Active Shadow Rider VMAX. Native `benchIndex` is used, never the unrelated token presentation `selectionIndex`. Conversely, Guzma is disfavored if its mandatory self-switch strands the only powered VSTAR; with a second powered Benched VSTAR it can be used.
2. **Attack and Energy preservation:** Rolling Iron is preferred to Ryuno Glide if both achieve the same KO, avoiding Ryuno's two-Energy discard while gaining Goodra's 80-damage protection. Ryuno still wins if the target is outside Rolling's range, including high HP with Dragon Weakness. Ineligible native-offered Rolling choices cannot suppress Ryuno. Apex copied Phantom Dive is **intentionally unsupported** because the actor-only V3 damage-allocation continuation has no supported native projection.
3. **Practical resource access:** Battle Compressor sends source-specific Dragon donors to discard without padding its optional three-card selection with Energy. Ultra Ball costs require genuinely disposable hand resources; one donor plus protected Double Dragon Energy is insufficient to pay two discards. Energy attachments use native target-seat/target-zone/target-printing and GGFire deficits; Temple of Sinnoh turns DDE Colorless, so replace it with Parallel City or Silent Lab before attacking. Stealthy Hood goes to the exact own VSTAR under Wobbuffet lock, not the first Tool-eligible support Basic.
4. **Recovery safety:** Legacy Star requires a safe deck count and a real recovery reason, then uses *up to two* choices from the native post-mill discard offer, preserving Goodra/Dragonite/Dialga/etc. as Apex donors. Super Rod similarly recovers Regidrago and Basic Energy rather than shuffling away the needed copied attacks. Cynthia & Caitlin retrieves useful Supporters while refusing optional discard of protected Double Dragon Energy.
5. **Full initial setup and source-specific engines:** Native GO_FIRST, starting Pokémon/Bench, and mulligan-draw prompt types receive bounded token decisions. Dedenne, Tapu Lele, Crobat, Squawkabilly and Oricorio are not prematurely Benched during setup when their play-from-hand Ability would be lost. Rank 14 uses Sky Field only to resolve meaningful Bench pressure, Squawkabilly only on the first turn with low hand and no threatened DDE, Oricorio only after a **public V3 prior-turn KO receipt**, Sudowoodo only against wide opposing Benches, and guards Carmine/Research full-hand discards.

Under current after-Pass behavior, that preserved Supporter was played next turn in **all five** fresh, precommitted positive cases.

Exact attribution between Energy shortage, changed hand effects, stochastic continuation and generic bot policy has not been isolated. No native engine-terminal superiority, statistically reliable win-rate difference, paper 25-minute clock compliance, human-level two-player policy, complete 32-opponent field or 99.5% final deck result is established.

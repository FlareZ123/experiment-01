## Research decision

The new combined native physical-action audit can detect a **genuine, same printed Energy / same accepted root / different Active-versus-Bench target**, under a fixed starting physical deal for both players. Three of four source-first matchup/deck combinations exhibited that direct intervention. **None changed the terminal winner or resolved the fourth capped matchup.** All four unchanged-control replays reproduced their full accepted root-target and typed Energy-prompt sidecar SHA hashes, root label digest, action count and game status.

This is **negative evidence for the proposed Basic Energy recipient intervention in these already-selected Aichi rank6/rank11 exposures**, while establishing that the current instrumentation can reliably distinguish a physical action-policy difference from an identical label string. It does **not** establish that recipient choice is globally unimportant; the owner-visible policy remains strategically incomplete and the source actions are retrospective.

## Interpretation for collective deck research

The experiment supports a precise negative conclusion: **simply rerouting an available Basic Energy from Active to Bench via the current shared actor-only frontier does not repair these four historical source-first games**. In three rows it truly changes physical behavior, yet that change alone does not overcome deficient attack conversion, board positioning, lock exposure or source game tree. The fourth row is a clear no-exposure/capped case.

Further one-slot or one-recipient policy changes should be tied to a concrete **payable attack or strategic response** and tested with actual opponent replies. The next useful matched experiment should target a known unproductive source-first board where a legal attacker can be funded and attack on time, with a deliberately changed candidate choice and **frozen, owner-information-safe source response**. Work toward realistic opponent policy and paper-KO order remains a blocker. Expanded claims require all 32 original lists, both starting sides and prospective fresh deals.

The main current validated outputs are **mechanical intervention and deterministic physical receipts**, never an estimate of deck strength. Current field: **29/32 exact opponent 60s, ranks 23/25/28 missing; no legal-paper 99.5% demonstration**.

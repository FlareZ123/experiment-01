**Agent 1, 19 October 2026. Evidence: participant first-person public narrative, no exact 60.**

Rank 25 of the 10 May 2026 Aichi Open League Top 32 is **Kazuki Kumegawa, Raichu Electrode Combo**, according to [Limitless official placement records](https://limitlesstcg.com/players/3108) and the [event results](https://limitlesstcg.com/tournaments/566). The tournament listing supplies **no downloadable rank-25 decklist**.

The competitor's public [14 May first-person retrospective](https://note.com/kume_pokeca/n/n6cfd126f5774), titled *CL愛知2026エクストラ ベスト32 アロライワンショット*, describes an Alolan Raichu one-shot concept with the following pre-event strategic reasoning:

- Regidrago was expected to comprise approximately half the field and be the main deck to beat.
- For a Regidrago opponent going second, the author valued opening Ability lock through **Klefki** to interrupt Latias ex and Tapu Lele-GX, reducing access to an early Budew Item-lock attack.
- The chosen deck was meant to retain an answer when going second into Silent Lab/Path-like constraints, Wobbuffet and ability-locking starts and a way to resist Regidrago's second-turn pressure.
- The author compared aggressive first-turn Garbodor Tool + Boost Shake locking but rejected it for this build due to slot cost and reliability.
- Self-reported eight Swiss rounds were 7 wins and one loss. Versus Regidrago, the reported rounds were 1, 2, 4, 5 and 7, with four wins and one loss. The subsequent Top 32 match was a loss to Mega Absol/Garbodor.

# Rank 22: contemporaneous registration-error provenance, Wally versus Hisuian Heavy Ball

**Agent 48; 31 October 2026.** This is a new source-traceability observation. It changes no benchmark decklist and supplies no matchup results.

## Independent contemporaneous testimony

The [May 2026 Pokéca Meshi Top32 roundup](https://pokekameshi.com/cl2026aichidec-extra/2/) identifies a Top32 player using the handle **airbird / @FcDqc22454**, with the archetype label **Mega Absol / Vikavolt**, and reproduces a dated **10 May 2026** first-person post. That player explicitly wrote:

> デッキ登録ミスってヒスイのヘビーボールがミツルになっちゃってます。

In context, this reports a deck registration error: the slot intended as **Hisuian Heavy Ball** was registered as **Wally**. [Tomopon's independently edited May-event analysis](https://note.com/tomopon_ptcg/n/n980eeda2406e) reproduces the same primary social post and phrase.

The statement is player-reported and does **not independently attest a legal name**, confirm a judge's physical deck check, or establish which physical printing was handled during every played match. Its narrower evidence is that the public list's Wally can plausibly be a *registered mistaken inclusion* rather than a planned metagame tech.

## Deck-evaluation consequence

**Keep Wally ROS 94 in rank 22's authenticated 60** when computing field evidence. A future evaluator must never silently substitute intended Hisuian Heavy Ball ASR 146 for the registered Wally slot. The former is a Supporter with evolution-oriented function; the latter is a Prize-inspecting Item that recovers a Basic Pokémon. Their Supporter contention, item-lock vulnerability, and tactical opportunities are very different. A substitution would be an explicitly labeled **counterfactual alternative opponent**, not the original published rank-22 source.

A follow-up could seek the original registration submission, the player-to-legal-name crosswalk, or judge-authorized event corrections. Until then preserve the published printings and the first-person explanation side by side.

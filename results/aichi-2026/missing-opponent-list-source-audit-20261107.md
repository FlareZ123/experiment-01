### Rank 25: Kazuki Kumegawa, Raichu Electrode Combo / Eleki Rain

- The Limitless results page names Kazuki Kumegawa at 25th.
- The Limitless Raichu Electrode Combo tournament history lists Kazuki Kumegawa at 25th for the same event.
- The Japanese event recap labels the Best32 entry as **Eleki Rain**, under “kume”, and quotes the public account `@kume_pokeca`.
- The public excerpt of https://note.com/kume_pokeca/n/n6cfd126f5774 says “予選25位抜け” (qualified 25th), describes the first-round knockout, and titles the deck Alolan Raichu One-shot. The detailed deck explanation is paid content. Do not attempt to bypass the paywall or reconstruct the 60 from snippets.
- The kume-to-Kazuki Kumegawa identity bridge is strongly corroborated. The note author's public self-introduction identifies kume as the CL Yokohama 2023 runner-up with Lost Box Charizard. The public CL Yokohama 2023 recap names `kume@シロシ団のジャッジ` as runner-up and reproduces a post from `@kume_pokeca`. Limitless independently lists Kazuki Kumegawa second at Champions League Yokohama, held on 17 September 2022, and identifies his second-place deck as Lost Box Charizard. The Japanese event was branded CL Yokohama 2023 despite its 2022 calendar date. The matching rare finish and archetype strongly corroborate the account/player mapping.
- **Disposition:** Raichu Electrode Combo / Eleki Rain archetype and player identity strongly corroborated; exact 60 unavailable. Do not substitute another Alolan Raichu list or unseen paid content.

### Rank 28: Jo Kato, Regidrago

- The Limitless results page names Jo Kato at 28th.
- The Limitless Regidrago tournament history also lists Jo Kato at 28th in the same event.
- The Japanese event recap labels a Best32 Regidrago entry under “Replica” and quotes `@nnaacc5_uec`: “CL愛知エクストラ 予選28位トナメ1没でした！” (28th in qualifying, first knockout loss).
- This is useful event and archetype corroboration. It does not establish that Replica is Jo Kato. Qualifying rank is not a safe join key for final placement: the [rank-16 qualifier/final counterexample](agent19-rank28-replica-60-photo-transcription-20261030.md) demonstrates the problem.
- No direct identity bridge from Replica/`@nnaacc5_uec` to the Limitless player record for Jo Kato has been established.
- **Disposition:** Regidrago archetype corroborated at event level; the photo transcription remains provisional and must not be counted as the official rank-28 list until the identity bridge and exact 60 are established.

## Event disambiguation warning

Search results for generic Japanese phrases such as “CL愛知エクストラ 23位” also surface the **2025** Extra event recap:

https://pokekameshi.com/cl2025aichi-extra/2/ (published 14 May 2025).

That page includes a 23rd-place Dragon Lugia and a 25th-place Alolan Raichu list from 2025. Neither is evidence for the missing 2026 Open League lists. In particular, do not substitute the 2025 25th-place Alolan Raichu/Suzu list for the 2026 “kume” / Eleki Rain lead.

Search results also return the **standard-format CL Aichi May Master League** recap:

https://pokekameshi.com/cl2026aichimay/2/

That is a different tournament from the Extra/Open League. Its 23rd-place Katsuya / Kangaskhan Box list is not evidence for the missing rank-23 Yuki Ueno list in the Open League. Do not substitute Standard results or decklists into this Japanese Extra opponent field.

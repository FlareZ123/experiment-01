# WallStall pilot matchup claims: source and limitations

Agent 5, 16 October 2026. **First-person qualitative evidence, not simulator game outcomes.**

[Hancha's public 13 May 2026 Aichi Top 8 article](https://note.com/hancha_poke/n/n8a47f9d64073) reports a 6-0 Regidrago record in that tournament. Its author's subjective normal-play matchup estimates are Regidrago 80%, Iron Thorns 80%, Mega Absol 80%, Lugia 70%, Ceruledge 70%, Mew 70%, Electro Rain 50%, Gardevoir 40%, Shadow Rider 40%, and Bunnelby 20%. The author separately gives higher rates against inexperienced opponents. These are *beliefs without reported denominators or frozen opponent lists*. The six Regidrago tournament matches are a selected, nonrandom sequence. A hypothetical one-sided 95% exact binomial lower bound for six wins out of six under an additional iid assumption is just 60.69622% (0.05^(1/6)); the actual tournament does not justify that sampling assumption. There is no statistically qualified matchup evidence here.

[A contemporary tournament roundup](https://pokekameshi.com/cl2026aichidec-extra/) links this account to Top 8 Snorlax F.H. and describes a Bunnelby loss and an Electro Rain loss. Connection to the registered rank-7 player Hiroto Fuji remains an inference. Rank 3 Snorlax is a separate 60-card list and pilot.

This is card presence, not policy or legality verification.

## Why this matters

In the preregistered eight-game original-rank6 source control panel, native Iron Thorns won five games, yet only three of those ended with all six source Prizes taken. A native terminal can instead be produced by exhausting the opposing deck or removing the last opposing Pokémon. These differences matter when judging opponent-bot competence and when designing a reproducible game-result evaluator.

The same source policy has a known genuine Store defect: its unguarded `Guzma & Hala CEC` enhanced mode accepts payment of zero or one card in isolated native fixtures. The original 16-game policy comparison did not collect every source-owned payment's actual hand/discard movement. This study resolves **only the encountered payments** in the eight original registered control games.

## Native source Guzma & Hala cost census on eight original control games

The separate passive `simulation/evaluation/agent7-rank6-control-ghala-physical-custody-replay.cjs` wraps **all eight already registered original control games** in the native selected source policy, without changing any chosen action or source card. - **Two actual enhanced payment prompts**. Both selected two *distinct physical objects*, each named `Plumeria BUS`. All four objects (two per game) left the correct owner's hand and newly entered that owner's discard pile on the same native resolution.
- **Two of two positive native physical two-card custody witnesses**; zero encountered enhanced prompts were underpaid or unverified. The two games share one source physical seed, so this is one source opening cohort observed against two fixed candidates. No Guzma & Hala roots occurred in the other six control games.

This evidence establishes the two encountered source payments were physically satisfied as printed in the actual native source games. It leaves the underlying engine underpayment vulnerability live outside those two observations. No root with no enhanced payment was upgraded to certified legal base-mode behavior, and other cards' cost, effect, turn or search legality remains unverified. Prism Star payments may move cards to Lost Zone and need a separate receipt semantics. Previous frozen seed `430745698` independently supplied two physical enhanced payments using `Crushing Hammer POR` and `Team Flare Grunt XY`, documented [here](agent7-natural-rank6-ghala-custody-to-pass-reply-20261102.md).

## Implications

Native terminal cause and source-hand payment custody are now reproducibly measurable in real original-source games without altering the compared decklists or policies. This resolves two **specific** prior observability gaps, while exposing why source native win count alone is a poor optimization target. The proposed next integrated frozen release should preserve these observers and enforce the mandatory printed payment guards on every supported card effect before permitting paper-qualified game status. More original Top32 source policy competence and the official match clock remain unverified.

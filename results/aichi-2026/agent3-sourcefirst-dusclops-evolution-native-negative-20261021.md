## Causal interpretation

Natural evolution can be **physically legal, desirable in theory, and still fail to improve actual game outcomes**. A one-card evolution was inserted at turn4 into an otherwise unchanged physical deal; subsequent native game state diverged and a previously realized three-Energy Moon Press setup was missed. It would be incorrect to claim early evolution inherently removes the required Energy, because the native card-draw/search sequence and future owner hand can change after a root insertion. The experiment establishes accepted-action first divergence and observed downstream loss of the attack line in this precise historical deal; its causal internal resource chain remains unresolved.

The original proposed turn20 Cursed Blast conversion window did **not** survive the treatment. Even with a native Dusclops evolution now established, the selected actor policy declared **no Cursed Blast Ability**. A specifically prioritized, owner-visible Ability use and prompt-target decision could be investigated separately, but its exact legality under all real locks and opponent replies requires live Store testing. Existing synthetic `simulation/twinleaf/new-moon-cursed-blast-ace-trainer.cjs` demonstrates the reducer contract only on injected state.

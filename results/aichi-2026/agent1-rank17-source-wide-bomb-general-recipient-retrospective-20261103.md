## Provenance, precise distinction

The original [16-deal 48-arm composed owner A/B/A study](agent1-rank17-electrode-composed-owner-aba-20261103.md) found **zero** co-occurring original Alolan Raichu UNM and Electrode-GX CES in actual owner roots. This remains true.

But the printed Electrode-GX `Extra Energy Bomb` can attach to **any of your Pokémon other than Pokémon-GX or Pokémon-EX**. Its text does **not** require Alolan Raichu. The original authenticated rank17 source's non-GX/-EX potential recipients include **Unown AOR, Voltorb ROS, Pikachu XY, Alolan Raichu UNM, Marshadow UNB, Klefki SVI, Ditto Prism Star LOT**, and **Squawkabilly ex PAL**. The last distinction matters: [Pokémon's official Scarlet & Violet announcement](https://press.pokemon.com/en/MEDIA-ALERT-New-Pokemon-Trading-Card-Game-Scarlet-Violet-Brings-Back-P) expressly distinguishes modern Pokémon `ex` from older BW/XY Pokémon-`EX`; the Pokémon TCG rulebook also treats their names as distinct. This source roster includes Electrode-GX and Gengar & Mimikyu-GX as its two **excluded** GX printings. Any dynamically printed lock, ability suppression or illegality still needs live Store/paper adjudication; a pure name/printing matcher cannot settle it.

The new **general source recipient** classifier was added to [the source-root necessity checker](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent1-rank17-electrode-owner-necessity.cjs) and [bounded owner-root census](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent1-rank17-electrode-census.cjs). It counts exact original source recipient printings, physical discarded source Energy **cards** and source Electrode board positions; it never inspects hidden opponent hand, any prize identities or private deck identities. `paperLegality` remains `UNVERIFIED`. The original Raichu-specific denominator and all existing fields are preserved separately.

## Why the distinction matters

1. The human-intended **Raichu win line** was not assembled in the examined native games, so a zero action count on that line is consistent with missing physical prerequisites.

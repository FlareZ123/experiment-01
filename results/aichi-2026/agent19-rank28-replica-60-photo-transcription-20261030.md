- Row 4/2's orange-haired professor, gray Minccino and Pidove, orange-roofed building and turquoise sky visually match [Pokémon's official Japanese アララギ博士 card image](https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/27619) from the Reshiram-EX Deck. The same Kanako Eo art was published as [English Dark Explorers Professor Juniper 98](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/dex/98), rather than the differing Ken Sugimori art on BLW 101. Therefore **2 Professor Juniper**, specifically the appropriate art-matching printing proxy, are justified.
- Row 5/2's gilded sky, round rooftop, pillars and staircase visually match [Japanese シンオウ神殿 UR S10P 087/067](https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/44888). Its matching English gold-art proxy is ASR 214; the effect is Temple of Sinnoh. The presence of three Double Dragon Energy beside this Stadium warrants *state-dependent* treatment: its own printed Special Energy-nullifying effect can reduce Double Dragon's functionality when in play. The photograph itself does not tell us intended sequencing.
- The grid's 3/1 and 3/6 are different items: **three VS Seeker**, **three Battle VIP Pass**. 2/8 is the separate singleton **Battle Compressor**. This distinguishes near-similar search/draw art categories and is necessary to reconstruct an honest 60.
- Kyurem 1/8 is the **130 HP Kyurem with アンチプラズマ**, matching the SFA 47 `Plasma Bane` Ability. Marshadow 2/6 has 80 HP, matching UNB 81 `Resetting Hole`, and Wobbuffet 2/7 has 110 HP, matching PHF 36 `Bide Barricade`.

## Cross-source rank semantics and full-field novelty check (30 October follow-up)

A direct provenance check finds a **concrete rank-label collision** in the **same archived gallery**: `dragon-03.png` shows `@yamatodaisuki` publicly saying **「予選16位通過の決勝トナメ1没」** while playing Regidrago. The [Limitless tournament results table](https://limitlesstcg.com/tournaments/566) labels final **16th** as **Takumi Matsumoto, Absol Garbodor**, with a published list in `full-decklists.json`. Thus the first screenshot explicitly says **preliminary rank 16** and Limitless uses an overall **final placement**; treating those rank numbers as interchangeable demonstrably creates a false identity/archetype join. This strengthens the demand for an independent player-identity registration bridge before labeling the rank-28 photograph official Jo Kato: the uploader states **qualifier 28**, while the unresolved Limitless row is **final 28**.

I additionally compared the 38 transcribed quantities, with equivalent English printed names and generic Basic Energy names, against **all 29 currently registered exact original 60s** in `full-decklists.json`. **No exact multiset match** exists. The nearest registered 60 is rank **10** (Regidrago), with **8 card-substitutions of minimum L1 distance** (sum absolute count differences divided by 2). Distinguishing entries include two pictured **Professor Juniper**, the gold **Temple of Sinnoh**, and the 3-VIP / 1-Quick Ball / 2-Ultra Ball split. This validates that the photo describes a **distinct 60-card construction** instead of merely reproducing an already published opponent, subject to visual reading errors. Neither a unique construction nor the qualifier-rank claim proves which final rank the player occupied.

## Distinct follow-up: preliminary vs final ranking is an unsafe join

The *same gallery* includes `dragon-03.png`, in which `@yamatodaisuki` describes a Regidrago **「予選16位通過の決勝トナメ1没」**: 16th in preliminary qualification, eliminated in the first knockout. Yet [Limitless final tournament ranks](https://limitlesstcg.com/tournaments/566) name **Takumi Matsumoto, Absol Garbodor** in final place 16. Thus two rankings assign different players and archetypes to the *same numeral*. This is a concrete counterexample showing why a player's Swiss qualifier rank is not, by itself, an authenticated join key for the final Top32 placement table.

The rank-28 photographed player's public self-report is specifically **「予選28位トナメ1没」**, while [the field registration](field.csv) labels final 28 **Jo Kato, Regidrago**. The earlier studies already left this identity bridge unresolved. The qualifier/final counterexample strengthens that existing caution. To promote the photographed 60, obtain original player-registration identity/official deck code, not a rank-ordinal coincidence.

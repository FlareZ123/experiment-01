### What this rules out and what it leaves unresolved

Within this selected **historical** original rank5/rank11 observed set, inability to produce the *bare number of physical hand cards* required to discard does not explain any of the 39 generically unblocked paid-cost variants, including the eight native Pass roots. This eliminates a narrow mechanism on these roots. It does not prove the cards had any legal searchable target, that card-specific play conditions were met, that a protected card could be sacrificed rationally, or that playing a Trainer would outperform the incumbent Pass. All tests used the **empty strategic-protection set**, thus none claims that all discards were desirable under game-state DCI. No actual selected Trainer payment or prospective improvement was tested by this read-only feature.

The zero observed initial-hand pairwise conflicts reflects only the small *actual physical hands and covered printing combinations at these roots*. General two-Trainer resource contention remains possible in other hands and ranks, as independently demonstrated by exhaustive pure examples. Normal Simulator errors, action caps, unsupported paper effects, incomplete source decision options and official Aichi Open League stage clock remain separate qualification obstacles.

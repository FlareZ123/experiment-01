## Methodological implication and next gate

This **third-party independent fresh-seed reversal** supports keeping Active Mega's faster headway as a meaningful *regression guard* against a globally aggressive Benched-funding tactic. It does not prove optimal allocation, and seven silent contexts imply that this tactic alone has weak exposure in the selected source panel. The two previously selected controls remain useful: seed `1800616448` identifies the harm of diverting a held Darkness from an already Active Mega, while seed `4096421492` demonstrates a setting with Active Squawkabilly where Benched Mega funding can enable later Claw. Integration should include both mechanisms in one **actually selected, symmetric two-seat resource policy**, then gate source source-line competence, complete printed effects, payment and 25-minute clock before ranking the fixed 60 candidates.

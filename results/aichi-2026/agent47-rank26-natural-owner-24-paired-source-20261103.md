## What the observation supports

The enhanced policy currently succeeds more often at selecting and **paying** a particular original source's early held Battle Compressor when the source actually goes first. Within this small fixed sample, it **did not** build the full original first-turn 2+2+1 self-KO and Crisis Punch attack. The separate [complete mechanically valid continuous firstturn native line](agent47-rank26-unownless-single-lightning-full-chain-native-20261103.md), [owner-chosen Egg-to-Egg prefix](agent47-rank26-eggd-2plus2plus1-owner-selected-native-20261103.md), and [owner-selected one-Lightning finisher](agent47-rank26-one-lightning-owner-finisher-native-20261103.md) all use synthetic or prepared resources. Their success is a conditional reachability proof; this natural cohort directly exposes the missing bridge to early actual physical resource acquisition.

The sample is small, targeted to one opponent, uses unqualified simple native policies, records action caps, and is **unsuitable for win-rate inference**. Zero native completions within a 120-action ceiling is not evidence that real Pokémon games are unwinnable; it is evidence that the experimental bots and action cap did not produce completed games in the prescribed attempts. Any next treatment should explain actual post-Compressor hand/discard, Bench, attached Energy and TM/Rope accessibility, preserve all failed attempt receipts, and avoid jumping from five paid Compressor actions to a Donk win claim.

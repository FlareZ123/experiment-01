## Causal interpretation and limitations

The pinned Store physically implemented four native free-Flare roots in two development trajectories. Observed original-Store followthrough can vary and is recorded. **Zero of the six games ended before the action cap.** There is no win-rate denominator, no demonstrated winner improvement, no clock qualification, and no evidence for 99.5%.

Although zero-additional-card discard dominates two-card discard **on one fixed opponent Active Energy removal channel when the source values the hand resources**, Plumeria may target **Benched Pokémon**; it also may benefit decks that want to discard specific cards, and a single Supporter per turn creates resource contention. The score is therefore a deliberately narrow, default-off heuristic subject to future native and mathematical study. One source seed / two correlated opponent pairings / six total native runs is not a prospectively randomized test.

**This is a genuine integration advance:** a single both-seat policy can physically select, native-dispatch, resolve and audit two very different Supporters with their incompatible payment mechanics, without patching the vendor simulator. The next gate is prospectively frozen independent deals and substantially stronger common decision competence, clock and hidden-information qualification, along with original exact 60 authentication of missing final ranks 23/25/28.

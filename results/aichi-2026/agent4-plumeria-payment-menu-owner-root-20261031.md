# Agent 4: owner-only Plumeria payment frontier (31 October 2026)

**Objective:** expose the real opportunity cost of Plumeria BUS at the same actor-owned root, before the common policy selects a move.

The printed Supporter discards two owner-hand cards before removing an Energy from an opposing Pokémon. The previous [27-effect native study](agent25-native-plumeria-hand-discard-census-20261030.md) found 54 actual owner payment cards, including 6 Crushing Hammer, 1 Enhanced Hammer, 5 Guzma and 7 Lusamine. One rank-6 source root spent both Hammer types to discard one Basic Psychic Energy. Neither native acceptance nor a binary "Plumeria playable" flag evaluates the cost of that choice.

**New artifact:** [collectOwnerPlumeriaPaymentMenu](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent4-plumeria-payment-menu.cjs) receives only the detached acting owner's hand, publicly visible Supporter restrictions and publicly attached opposing Energy. It separately enumerates all distinct physical Plumeria copies, each unordered pair of **two other physical owner-hand cards**, and each opposing attached Energy printing plus its Active/Bench and attached-Energy ordinal. Duplicate Energy copies and physical payment indices remain distinguishable. It excludes first-player turn-one ordinary Supporters, spent or locked Supporter quotas, and unaffordable hand payments. It reads no opponent hidden identities and does not mutate state. Each proposal is labeled **native dispatch unimplemented, native playability unverified, resource costs unscored**.

**Pure evidence:** [nine Node 22.16.0 tests](../../tests/agent4-plumeria-payment-menu.test.cjs) passed locally. A synthetic hand of Plumeria + Crushing Hammer + Enhanced Hammer + Guzma has **3 distinct payment pairs**. An opposing board with two Speed Lightning attached to Active Iron Thorns and one Double Dragon on Benched Regidrago has **3 visible physical Energy targets**, yielding **9 raw payment-by-target combinations**. This synthetic comparison is a choice-space illustration, not a natural source game or win-rate sample. Tests also cover duplicate Plumeria copies, missing costs, restrictions, opponent hidden-state invariance, immutability and a bounded 60-card input stress case.

**Important boundary:** this menu models **Energy-denial uses** of Plumeria. Playing Plumeria solely as a hand-discard outlet when no opposing Energy is attached is outside scope and is not proved illegal. Plumeria's native prompts and tactical payments have not been tested by this new module.

**Integration request:** the shared [same-root collector](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent41-same-root-proposal-choice.cjs) can consume this as a grouped read-only option, without selecting it as an action until a native dispatcher exists. On one authenticated rank-6 source root holding Plumeria and a Hammer, compare the pre-action owner-only menu with actual native two-card payment and Energy-choice prompts, then test an adverse competing action and a real opponent continuation. Preserve all negatives.

**Decision status:** no policy change or new CI workflow; 29/32 original Aichi opponent 60s available (ranks 23, 25, 28 missing); zero competitive-qualified games; no validated fixed 60 or demonstrated 99.5%.

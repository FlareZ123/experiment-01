## Why this structural challenger matters

The author's described Archie's plan puts Baxcalibur into play directly from the discard pile via Archie's Ace in the Hole, then uses Baxcalibur's extra Basic Water attachment to fund a Kyogre **Aqua Storm**. A controlled Pidgeot V **Vanishing Wings** sequence returns up to five attached Basic Water cards to the deck while the other cards are removed, leaving five Water cards to be discarded from the top by Kyogre. The printed Kyogre attack then deals **250 damage to each of two opposing Benched Pokémon**, contingent on its own Water-Water-Colorless payment. **Telescopic Sight** can add **30 damage** to a Benched Pokémon V or Pokémon-GX, making **280 to each eligible target**. Regidrago VSTAR has 280 HP and remains Pokémon V. This yields a conditional two-Regidrago-VSTAR, four-Prize tactic when both are legally on the opponent's Bench, both attacks can resolve, damage is not prevented, and all five Water are in place. The source author does not report actually scoring that exact double VSTAR knockout in all four self-reported Regidrago match wins.

The newly visible **Klefki** Active Ability restriction and **Team Rocket's Watchtower** Colorless-Ability restriction change setup and lock exposure, including potential self-interference. They require separate board-state/paper legality testing against Shadow Rider, Meloetta and Tool-Garbodor pressure. The author self-reports four Regidrago wins within a 6–2 Aichi day, acknowledging match variability. Eight non-random observed games provide no estimate for the entire Top32 and no basis for a 99.5% claim.

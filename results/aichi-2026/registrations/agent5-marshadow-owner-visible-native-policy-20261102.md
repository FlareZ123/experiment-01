The deterministic synthetic shuffle proves zero natural random access.

Four successes constitute four **staged policy-and-mechanics demonstrations**, zero gameplay and zero matchups.

Include negative pure-policy contract checks for Item lock, alternative Stadium in hand, spent retreat and opponent source printing mismatch. Explicit source specificity and prohibition of hidden-zone reads are intentional limits.
- The actor-only policy's heuristic intentionally defers to a **held replacement Stadium** rather than automatically burning Fog and mobility resources; this is an untested strategic preference, not an optimality theorem. It is not activated in actual common bilateral driver and its relative performance is unknown.

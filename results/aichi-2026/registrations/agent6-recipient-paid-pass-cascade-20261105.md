# Pre-registered Agent6 recipient-enabled paid-retreat Pass-stage census

**Registered 5 November 2026, ~19:44 UTC before any diagnostic execution.**

## Hypothesis and target

Registered 24-arm result [Agent6 recipient-plus-retreat](../2026-11/agent6-recipient-paid-retreat-factorial-native-20261105.md) used symmetric Basic Energy recipient in all arms, but its paid-retreat treatment encountered **358** genuine native Pass roots and made no native retreat proposal. The original 340-Pass baseline stage census from Agent2 had recipient OFF, so it is a different action state distribution.

Determine which of the existing fixed paid-retreat planner's eight public eligibility gates fail in each of the **same eight historical recipient-ON middle-arm source-deal/seat groups**. No new deck choice, opponent-source trial, decision policy or hidden-information access is intended.

No new source-specific policy branch is allowed. Exact printed attack and payment remain native questions. This is a passive diagnostic, not a registration of fair randomized game outcomes.

## Acceptance and limitations

A diagnostic cohort qualifies only if the A/B policy-equivalence fingerprints match all eight cohorts, all owners' true Pass roots are counted, no unexpected exceptions or terms occur, and detailed JSON diagnostics are saved with engine and runner revisions in a manually dispatched existing CI. A zero intersection remains possible and expected. No wins become competitively qualified; no final fixed 60 is selected. This study cannot estimate win rate or prove full paper validity.

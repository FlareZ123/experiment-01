## Why this evidence matters

The initial 12-pair experiment identified two native sole-Pokémon failures caused by keeping Snorlax alone. The independent experiment shows the same failure class can recur even on new source rank7 games. A narrow policy reading **only publicly in-play attached Energy** recovered a real source Basic-playing opportunity in five of the six solo deals and avoided three earlier native terminals in which unconditional preservation failed. Two source-first cases show an especially important *dynamic* decision: the player withheld a Bench Basic while the opponent had no visible Energy, then played a Basic on its next own turn once opposing Energy was publicly established. The result is more expressive than either always Bench or never Bench.

However, any attached Energy is only a crude danger proxy. It ignores printed attack costs, damage, switching/gust, normal attachment possibility and Abilities accelerating Energy, tool lock, incoming instant knockouts with zero visible Energy, source alternative attackers and opportunity cost. In this cohort no native terminal under the other two arms by the 100-root cap is **censoring**, not survival to game end or a successful stall. Rank3 and rank7 source policy and the rank1 opponent policy remain generic bots with limited competitive competence, game clock and paper/japanese card fidelity unresolved. **0 independently qualified games**, full field **29/32** exact 60s, target fixed 99.5% unverified.

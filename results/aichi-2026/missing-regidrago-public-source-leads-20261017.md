## Source matching constraints

1. The [public standings/Limitless archetype history](https://limitlesstcg.com/decks/260/results) names ranks **23 Yuki Ueno** and **28 Jo Kato** as Regidrago. The Japanese aggregator identifies entries by **social display handles** and occasionally by *Swiss* standings. It does not establish a one-to-one link between these display handles and the standings' English romanized registered names or final placement.
2. A linked X post or article with only matchup summary, tournament photo, or unspecified visual is insufficient for a printing-aware card inventory. Resolve one particular public registered 60 with counts, identities, and relevant Japanese printings before turning `published_full_list` to yes.
3. Search results easily conflate May **2025** with May **2026** lists and repeat the same social handle in different events. Year, event and version provenance are required.
4. Some authenticated event results use bracket finishing ranks while personal posts quote preliminary Swiss positions. Do not equate either numbering system without a complete independent mapping.
5. The benchmark must continue to mark all three unknown decks as unavailable. No approach-to-99.5% or complete-field coverage claim follows from source leads alone.

**Next targeted retrieval:** locate a public official 2026 card-deck code or visual roster attached to these exact May 10 posts, preserve its original image/code and dates, transcribe all 60 cards with a second reviewer, then reconcile player identity and exact opponent rank with a tournament bracket/primary registration source. If a handle only confirms archetype, preserve it as matchup context and stop short of filling missing card slots.

## Decision-first finding

A newly observed original-source rank11 opponent game contained a stranded zero-Energy `Mimikyu GRI` Active, a held `Float Stone BKT`, and a fully paid `Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX CRE` on Bench. A narrow, default-off owner-view intervention selected **Float Stone → Retreat → Max Geist** in the genuine pinned Twinleaf Store, Knocked Out a 130-damaged `Oranguru V ASR`, and **actually took two Prize cards**. The original no-intervention baseline passed at that same root and never used this chain. The control replay after treatment returned exactly the same complete accepted action history as the first control.

This is a **native Store-verified tactical payoff**, materially stronger than an inferred nominal-KO prospect. The original ranked source still ultimately won the native game in every A/B/A champion arm; the intervention improves the candidate's Prize count in this one seed without establishing a game-win benefit. Neither candidate is competitively certified; the single-game observation must not be projected onto the Top 32.

## Additional independent implementation defect fixed

The previously present generic `chooseGenericFundedFloatStone` selector inspected `options.genericFundedFloatStoneRoot`, and the bilateral runner passed the corresponding opt-in to `withActorOnlyRootPolicy`, yet the wrapper silently omitted the option from both its audit and constructed `rootOptions`. Consequently the previous generic control/treatment pair was not a genuine enabled intervention even though its separate observation found zero helper-eligible owner roots. The patch in `simulation/twinleaf/agent34-actor-only-root-policy.cjs` now passes the flag all the way to the native root. A new **controlled fake-Store integration** test demonstrates Pass when OFF versus a `PlayCardAction` when ON and passed focused CI [42287565541](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42287565541). Do not reinterpret the prior pair as effectiveness evidence; the owner-only opportunity census remains an independent, conservative descriptive observation.

The new zero-Energy rescue uses a separate seat-and-turn scope and a separate unit test proving wrong seat, wrong turn and disabled cases fall back to Pass, and an already attached Tool leads to `RetreatAction`. This is explicitly default off; no shared globally selected deck policy has been modified.

## Qualification limitations and next experiment

- This was a **post hoc tactical test** triggered by a newly preregistered natural game, rather than a prospectively registered intervention. The underlying original seed was generated before this result, but the branch was designed after observing the missed action.
- Correctly replayed Twinleaf Store actions establish what this pinned implementation did. Paper legality of the full subsequent game and tactical opponent counterplay are unqualified; generic bots still omit strong plays and may make strategically implausible decisions.
- The opponent wins all three champion arms despite the local two-Prize gain. The other contender is capped and unchanged. A positive Prize event is useful evidence for a potential control or Prize-race policy, without being a competitive win.
- Future independent evaluation would preregister **fresh** original-source physical seed groups before running them, apply a general owner-visible zero-Energy strand policy across both real seats with explicit lock/status and tactical-option checks, preserve first-divergence and opponent-response receipts, and audit full games against every authenticated source. The selected shared Issue25 v8 remains untouched.
- Current benchmark still has only **29 authenticated original 60-card opponents of 32** and no support for any claimed 99.5% win rate.

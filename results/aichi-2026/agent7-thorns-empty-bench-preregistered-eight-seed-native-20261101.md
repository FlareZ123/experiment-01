## Hypothesis and design

Original paper Expanded rank6 Iron Thorns ex (unchanged authentic original 60) can use its printed **Volt Cyclone** despite a nonpayable post-damage Energy movement clause when its own Bench is empty: the printed attack still deals **140** damage. The baseline first-return policy Passed on some payable turn4 or turn5 source positions with a Double Colorless Energy on Active Iron Thorns, Thunder Mountain Prism Star active, and no own Bench. A narrow default-off score preference selected the independently payable original attack instead. All other cards, printed source and candidate sixties, actual player seats, source initial physical decks, native prompts and policy are matched within each original-control-versus-treatment pair.

Eight fresh original source initial shuffle seeds were registered (repeats 1500-1507, each used against both fixed champion-reference and New Moon Lockbreaker). This is a 16 paired-game cohort, eight *distinct original source initial deals*. The authentic pinned Twinleaf revision was `a9905a76a6c4103ba4b6cb9a49e86bad795c5c05`. Source first/second and candidate seats alternate by registered group, and the policy is the same both-seat selector. ## Interpretation

This is a controlled policy-counterexample to **Pass as a safe default** when an apparently restrictive after-damage movement clause lacks a destination. It also demonstrates that the exact same conservative 60-card field can yield different results from a single physically legal source attack-policy correction. It **does not** demonstrate that rank6 is competitively favored, that all opponents play competently, that 99.5% is feasible, or that these uncapped and untimed terminals are real paper tournament wins. Both source deals and opposing candidate policies are narrow; 7/16 arms per policy mode remained action-censored.

New research priority: generalize the verified partial-effect eligibility to other attack texts with no legal post-effect target, and test robustly with new **both-actual-start** registered source deals and improved opponent policy. An optimizer that only repairs candidates while retaining an artificially passing original opponent will overstate candidate success.

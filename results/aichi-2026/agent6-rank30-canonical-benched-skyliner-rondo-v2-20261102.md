The unchanged paired control had Mimikyu Filch on turn 33 and ultimately the unqualified untimed **candidate** win. The enabled policy led to the unqualified untimed **original source** win, with **source 0 Prizes remaining, candidate 6**. This is a **single retrospective original deal** and **an engine-terminal policy-competence witness**, not a general field win-rate estimate.

## Interpretation and limitation

This is a genuine **one-turn resource-choice conflict**: after K1 reveals inaccessible Thunder, the source can physically remove one Energy from opponent Latias by Plumeria instead of Passing; with different prior T1 resource expenditure it can instead deal 140 damage but must sacrifice both Plumeria. The immediate attack generates zero Prizes and therefore might be weaker than Energy denial for a control/LO deck. Discard acceptability, Supporter quota, opponent counteraction and paper clock matter. The current original Store outcome horizon is action-limited and the candidate source policy is not competitively qualified.

**Limitation:** Plumeria-T3-only arm did not pre-stage DCE, while attack arm did. Same first physical deal does not mean identical T3 owner board. The higher-quality next experiment must **hold T1 DCE stage ON across both contrasting T3 policies**, then compare actual Plumeria payment/attached opposing Energy removal with genuine G&H-Speed damage, including next owner root.

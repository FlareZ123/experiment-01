**No native winner label changed across 8 matched pairs.** Seven physical contexts reached native game-end with the original rank1 reference winning; the eighth (seed `3668291815`, source seat1) hit the 180-root action cap under **both** modes and retained native winner sentinel `-1`. Therefore these observations must be used only to diagnose the actor **action availability and choice model**, not deck relative strength or an estimated win probability.

Only **one Electro Rain** declaration occurred despite 48 source attacks. In particular, the source bot still lacks verified characteristic **Battle Compressor → Electrode-GX Extra Energy Bomb → properly funded Alolan Raichu Electro Rain multi-target attack** progression, accurate on-board Special Energy conditional units, consequential opposing move choice, and a 25-minute official Open knockout clock.

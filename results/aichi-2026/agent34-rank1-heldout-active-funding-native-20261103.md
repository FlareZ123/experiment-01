## Relation to previously inspected development seeds

The earlier [retrospective first-choice and conversion analysis](agent34-underworld-active-funding-contention-20261103.md) found that 13/16 first root divergences after enabling Underworld were normal Psychic attachment versus Underworld, with two severe attack losses. The **previously inspected 16-pair posthoc correction** recovered the severe native attack counts (0 → 7 and 1 → 8), and both censored trials turned into native source terminals, but that evidence was selected after seeing the failures.

The new held-out 32-pair experiment neither reproduced similarly catastrophic cases nor showed a new native terminal increase. It did demonstrate an independently observed exact same-turn paid Active Psychic → AttackAction transition under the originally specified guard. The correct status is **narrow action conversion confirmed, broad outcome improvement unproven**.

## Decision

Keep this guard **default OFF** in any nominated cross-field playing-policy release until it has been tested jointly against rank-5 and rank-11 source opportunities, other protected native action policies, and negative controls. Its narrow conditions make it suitable for additional policy integration study. The 99.5% fixed-deck target remains unverified, the actual field still has 29/32 authenticated 60s, and no complete Aichi opponent is certified as a competent paper-game player. Native terminal categories are deliberately never scored as competitive wins.

Further investigation: compare conservative manual Active funding with mobility alternatives, and measure whether a better **owner-visible Benched Psychic recipient selection** prevents overconcentration of Underworld Energy without sacrificing useful draw. Treat recipient and root priority as separate factors with independent preregistered tests.

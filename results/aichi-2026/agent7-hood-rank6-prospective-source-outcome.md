## Evidence limitations and priority

- Only **6 of 32** engine games reached a terminal condition, and **26/32** stopped at the 140-action cap. The opponent was silent for its attacks in six of the sixteen seeds in each arm. The source may fail to use available Faba, Megaton Blower, Guzma, Tool Jammer positioning, or otherwise competent lock/attack responses. An engine winner is **not** a counted competitive win. The same physical seed is a useful pairing convention; differently constructed source-deck shuffles and pilot choices are not guaranteed to share all exogenous events or make a causal win-rate estimator.
- The post-registration version `withEarlyShadowRiderHood(...,{lockKind:'initialization'})` intentionally *prioritizes a Benched target* to keep Tool Jammer's Active-only Tool suppression from undermining its own Hood. The previous default `garbotoxin` behavior and public source-target ability test still pass. The Door wrapper's `lockKind:'initialization'` witnesses an opposing **Active** Iron Thorns at real effect time, rather than a tooled Garbodor on Bench or Active.
- Japanese printing-aware legality, complete engine semantics (including original Store snapshot/alias safety), policy information fairness, opposing Tool removal, genuine opponent follow-up, attack damage and Prize accounting, entire real matches under official 25/35-minute clocks, both-player competitive policy competence, and 32-rank exact lists remain unqualified.
- Do **not** compare the raw 4/16 rank-6 protected-Door exposure against 1/16 rank-11 Garbotoxin exposure as a causal matchup advantage. They are differently seeded, differently policed source archetypes, with different conditional windows and action caps. Do **not** derive a Hood-over-champion game-win estimate from this packet. The observed attack-declaration and terminal counts are diagnostics of incomplete bots.

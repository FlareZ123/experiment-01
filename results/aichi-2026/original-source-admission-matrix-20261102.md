## Qualification contract and provenance

The row fields `sourceLegalChain`, `sourceOpponentContinuation`, `ownerInformationSafety`, `paperMatchClock` and `competitivelyQualified` are deliberately **unqualified / false**. `nativeExposure` means presence in a reported experimental cohort or registration and is **not** a state transition to paper certification. A source-specific claim must be supplemented by real owner-visible selected root, accepted Store dispatch, prompt, payment, resolution, characteristic pressure and next opposing turn. Record the disposition of every relevant rule/effect separately.

**Physical-deal comparator:** For each *trial pair* publish `EXACT_SOURCE_DEAL_MATCH`, `SOURCE_DEAL_DIFFERENT` or `UNVERIFIED` based on the actual full native source initial setup fingerprint. Equal RNG seeds alone do not imply a shared source physical deal. This status is deliberately per comparison, not an inherent property of a deck row. Keep mismatched, failed, censored and incomplete attempts in the full experiment denominator.

**Reproducing each manifest SHA-256:** For each `full-decklists.json` original `decks[]` entry, transform its `cards` into a list of `[category,name,count,printing.set,printing.number,printing.language]`, lexicographically sort those array entries by their `JSON.stringify` representation, then compute SHA-256 over the UTF-8 bytes of the outer `JSON.stringify` array. Counts must total exactly 60. This new content fingerprint identifies the normalized *transcribed* printed list. It is distinct from Git blob SHA and from the native Store's physical source-deal fingerprint; it does not independently authenticate Japanese legality, original print matching or engine support.

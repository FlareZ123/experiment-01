## Major newly exposed missing policy channel: Underworld Door is never declared

Both prospective native policy arms complete 16 original-source games with multiple original **Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX** physical plays, yet show **zero** UseAbilityAction declarations by either actor, in every game. The [pinned native headless root ledger](../../simulation/twinleaf/headless.cjs) explicitly records each accepted UseAbilityAction and its named Ability; therefore the absence is a recorded action-policy absence, not an omitted field. The [current canonical actor-only root-action constructor](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent34-actor-only-root-policy.cjs) constructs UseAbilityAction only for Genesect Fusion draw, Farewell Letter, and Perfection intent types; it has no Underworld Door intent/action branch in this compiled policy.

The upstream [pinned Shadow Rider VMAX printing](https://github.com/twinleafgg/twinleafgg/blob/a9905a76a6c4103ba4b6cb9a49e86bad795c5c05/ptcg-server/src/sets/09-sword-and-shield/set-chilling-reign/shadow-rider-calyrex-vmax.ts) explicitly implements **Underworld Door** through a per-Pokémon once-per-turn marker, eligible Psychic Energy in hand, an AttachEnergyPrompt to a Benched Psychic Pokémon, real Energy transfer and drawing two cards. Therefore native Underworld Door capability **exists upstream**, but this default actor-only native decision route never attempts it in the observed rank1 games.

This omission directly undermines competitiveness inference for the actual Shadow Rider champion. A recipient-only modification creates 41 authentic Bench Energy attachments without exploiting the champion's defining repeatable draw/acceleration mechanic. Do not infer that the rank1 source deck naturally makes poor Energy choices, or that the candidate beats a competently piloted Shadow Rider champion, from these incomplete actor policy endpoints.

The public observer does not determine how many underlying source board states had a simultaneously usable VMAX, unspent marker, eligible Psychic Energy, legal Psychic Bench and no effective lock. That is the immediate **falsifiable next measurement**, followed by a default-off, actor-owned Underworld Door root and complete prompt choice/physical delta verification. Tool- or Ability-lock interactions, prompt target filters and existing native marker behavior must be checked rather than presumed.

## Additional read-only native source opportunity replay, 2 November

After identifying no declared Underworld Door actions, Agent31 added an owner-only structural precondition census on *all* accepted root actions. It checks an actual in-play Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX, its real unspent per-Pokémon **UNDERWORLD_DOOR_MARKER** in the owning player state, a Basic Psychic Energy in that owner's real hand, and at least one actually Benched Pokémon with the printed Psychic type. It never reads opposing concealed zones, calls a reducer, initiates an Ability, or changes a decision. A true conjunction is only a **potential**; effects such as Garbotoxin, Path to the Peak or Initialization, as well as dynamic typing and current card locks, remain untested.

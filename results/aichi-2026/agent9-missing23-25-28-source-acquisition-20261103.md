## New rank-25 eyewitness evidence and scope

The [first-person kume report](https://note.com/kume_pokeca/n/n6cfd126f5774) dated **14 May 2026** names the played deck an *Alolan Raichu one-shot*, says its player entered Top32 from **25th qualifying-round position**, and gives eight Swiss matchup results, five Regidrago encounters (four wins, one loss), followed by a playoff loss to Mega Absol/Garbodor. This helps identify its strategic anti-Regidrago intent and demonstrates a real Top32 source-author avenue. The article's public portion discusses comparing Klefki, Wobbuffet, Garbodor and other countereffects as *possible choices*; such discussion **does not establish any of those as cards in the final tournament 60**. The actual submitted 60 appears after the inaccessible paid section and cannot be inferred from its matchup record, article illustrations of *other* decks, or another Raichu player's published list.

**Important crosswalk limit:** The article states *Swiss 25th*, while the official Limitless event table labels Kazuki Kumegawa *overall 25th*. Even though the named archetype, Top32 finish, and Pokekameshi alias support this match, direct Japanese-name-to-alias verification was not obtained. Maintain the possibility of two players with similar rank/archetype, and do not elevate the author to an authenticated source of this exact rank until player identity is corroborated independently. The article is a *candidate lead*.

## Critical interpretation

**Printing specificity matters.** The field includes:
- `Marshadow` from Limitless display `UNB 81` at many ranks; the official ban names the `Let Loose` Marshadow from SM3+ and related printings.
- `Unown` shown as `AOR 30` in several decks; official bans specifically identify two SM8 Unown Abilities, `DAMAGE` and `HAND`.
- `Duskull` shown as `PRE 35` for rank 31; official ban names a specific SM11a Duskull.

These field occurrences **must not be rejected solely because their English names coincide with an explicitly banned Japanese card name**. Confirm attack and Ability identity before concluding legal status, especially where Limitless displays an English proxy printing.

The official Japanese rules further make `Professor Juniper`, `Professor Sycamore`, and `Professor's Research` mutually exclusive across a deck, and similarly `Lysandre` and `Boss's Orders`; text, character art, and spelling may vary across languages. Event-list import alone does not enforce all special rules, ACE SPEC restrictions, release timelines, or the rules on older reprints.

The repository's `resources/cards/en` is an English snapshot. Card missing from that collection is **not evidence of Japanese illegality**. The [card-coverage audit](card-coverage.json) quantifies which Aichi opponent card rows remain unresolved against the English snapshot. The official Japanese rules and original printed card texts take precedence.

## Evidence boundary

This file is a source-backed legality baseline, not a completed automated legality certification for all 29 lists or any candidate deck. A rigorous verifier needs canonical Japanese product/printing IDs (not only translated Limitless display IDs), reprint equivalence where allowed, event-day card release cutoff, exact Pokémon abilities and all restrictions in force that day.

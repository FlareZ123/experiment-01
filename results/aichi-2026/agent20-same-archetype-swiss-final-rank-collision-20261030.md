# Same-archetype rank collision: why Swiss-13 Regidrago is not final-rank-13 Regidrago

**Agent 20 | 30 October 2026 | public provenance reconciliation; zero competitive-game evidence.**

## Newly discriminating counterexample

The original [Pokekameshi 2026 May Aichi Expanded Top32 roundup, page 2](https://pokekameshi.com/cl2026aichidec-extra/2/) reproduces a 10 May first-person report by **キリン / @kirin_pokeca066**, playing **ガルーラドラゴ (Kangaskhan Regidrago)**, finishing the eight-round Swiss at **13th**, and then **losing the first Top32 elimination match** to a Snorlax/Wall Stall opponent. The first-person account lists seven Swiss wins, an Iron Thorns loss, then its first elimination loss. This is a *Top32 first-round exit*, not a Top16 finish.

Separately, the [same site's Top16 page](https://pokekameshi.com/cl2026aichidec-extra/) gives a **different** Regidrago player: **キリカ(ササキヒロム) / @kirikapokeca** states **12th Swiss qualifier** and **Top16 after winning the first knockout then losing the second**. The [Limitless final results](https://limitlesstcg.com/tournaments/566) name **Hiromu Sasaki at final rank 13**, also **Regidrago**.

This gives an unusually strong falsification of a tempting source join:

| Attribute | Swiss seed 13, キリン | Final place 13, Hiromu Sasaki / キリカ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tournament | Aichi 2026 Open League, 10 May | Same event |
| Archetype | Regidrago | Regidrago |
| Number 13 | **Swiss qualifying place** | **Final tournament placing** |
| First knockout | **Lost**; Top32 | **Won**; Top16 |
| Swiss claim | 13th | **12th** |
| Identity evidence | @kirin_pokeca066 | @kirikapokeca expressly names ササキヒロム |

**Result:** matching tournament, archetype, *and* ordinal 13 still picks the wrong player and wrong physical 60. The contradictory elimination outcomes and independently identified account distinguish them. This improves the [earlier rank-16 different-archetype counterexample](agent19-rank28-replica-60-photo-transcription-20261030.md): **matching archetype is insufficient as an identity bridge**.

## Reusable provenance decision rule

Before attaching any nickname-posted source list to a final placement:

1. Separate **Swiss seed**, **knockout round reached**, and **final ranking** into three typed fields.
2. If the two asserted knockout outcomes conflict, **reject the join**, even when number and archetype coincide.
3. If outcomes agree but participant identity is unattested, keep a **candidate-source** status, and state which identity link is missing.
4. Promote only after a first-party identity bridge or an independently attributable exact registered list.

This is provenance methodology and a genuine negative case. It recovers **zero new verified 60s**, changes **no decklist**, and supplies no game-win evidence. At the time of research, the public field remains **29/32 exact lists** (missing final ranks **23, 25, 28**), **zero competitively qualified games**, and no demonstrated fixed deck with a 99.5% win rate.

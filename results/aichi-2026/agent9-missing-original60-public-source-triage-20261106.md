The free portion gives strategic motivations, matchup logs, and a control/disruption-oriented framework. The section beginning *デッキ構築解説* is behind a paid boundary. No original complete card-by-card 60 is available in the free text.
- [Pokekameshi's contemporaneous Top32 summary page 2](https://pokekameshi.com/cl2026aichidec-extra/2/) associates **kume** with a Top32 Electric Rain/Alolan Raichu one-shot finish and links that report. The nickname suggests a lead for the Limitless player name **Kazuki Kumegawa**, but a direct identity match is **not established** by the cited public pages. The report's 25th *Swiss* placement must not be confused with the official final 25th placement without further proof.
- To admit rank 25, obtain the author's original deck code, contemporaneous registration image, or reliable exact 60 transcribed with counts, then independently tie source identity to Limitless final rank. A similar rank4/17 Raichu list is a distinct contender, not a valid replacement.

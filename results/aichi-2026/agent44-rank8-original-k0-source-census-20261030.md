## Research question

The original source rank8 Absol/Garbodor 60 permits an Item allocation where Secret Box searches for singleton Boost Shake, and its Tool category can supply a Float Stone or Muscle Band. Exact eight-card hand + six-Prize combinatorics yielded [12.5143% conditional **resource-coexistence opportunity**](../statistics/agent44-rank8-secretbox-firstturn-connectivity-20261030.md), with several overlapping alternatives. Does a **conservative hand-only / face-up-Board** classifier actually encounter a usable K0 entry state on unmodified original-source native deals, once an opening Basic has already been placed, first-turn Bench commitment and immediate physical discard payment are considered?

## Why 0/48 cannot replace the exact resource calculation

The mathematical 12.5143% condition examines the **initial seven-card sample plus one draw before placing opening Basic Pokémon and without any strategic discard-capability requirement**. The native first owner-root hand records **after setup**, when original Basic Pokémon have left the hand for Active/Bench, potentially using up the available discard material. The native classifier also treats held Trubbish/searcher as protected and reserves extra cards for Box and a later search payment; its available Bench and public lock constraints are more demanding. The no-window result is therefore not contradictory evidence of a 12.5143% actual lock chance; the probabilities quantify **different events**.

Unmodeled further constraints include uncertain K0 in-deck Shake, Garbodor, Tool and Trubbish presence, opposing first-turn restrictions, evolution-target choice, actual native play acceptance, intended line dominance over other uses of Box, and opponent reply. Even when strict K0 is true, an eventual lock need not succeed. Also, a true line may be missed by this conservative K0 classifier through other searchers, in-play Active peculiarities, or better card sequencing.

### Methodology update after this cohort

Following the 208 run's original preregistration, the pure K0 classifier was expanded in a later revision to **separately flag conditional post-Box discard subsidies**: Searcher discard costs that might be paid by Supporter/Stadium cards fetched from Box. The original strict K0 fields remain unchanged. The new conditional flag was **not present in the 208-run checkout**, so its 21 strict observations are directly comparable to the pilot's existing strict flags; it must not be retroactively counted as measured on those 208 rows. A short repeat on the *same original 48 indices* may test this extra analytic label, but it is a **post hoc classifier diagnostic** and cannot be passed off as new independent deals.

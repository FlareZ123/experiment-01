# Fixed-candidate decision brief across multiple Aichi hard archetypes

**Agent 37, 19 October 2026. Evidence class: cross-experiment synthesis of published inventories, exact resource calculations and frozen diagnostic engine replays. No new gameplay result, candidate ranking or competitively qualified match.**

## Decision the current evidence supports

**Maintain four distinct 60-card research positions without promoting a new single best competitive deck.** The published champion [rank-1 Shadow Rider list](benchmark-shadow-rider.md) is the human-usable *reference* and still the nominated source benchmark, but it is an exact mirror of one field opponent. The [Hood Shield](candidate-hood-shadow-rider.md), [Temple](candidate-temple-shadow-rider.md) and [New Moon Lockbreaker](../als/new-moon-lockbreak-60-candidate.md) 60s are mechanically distinct experiments with highly asymmetric unresolved matchup weaknesses. Every currently available simulator game remains **unqualified for competitive-strength inference**. A single fixed 60 at **99.5%** is neither nominated on demonstrated performance nor certified.

This comparison targets the primary deliverable directly: assess whether any immutable 60 has evidence of generality across hard opponents, and expose why a result in one matchup cannot justify deleting necessary answers to another. Each named fact below traces to prior human/source inventory, exact mathematical model or a compiled engine witness. Pinned-engine wins are kept separate from independently qualified competitive games.

## Why current game completions cannot order these four 60s

1. **Source opponent competence changes outcomes.** An early rank-6 panel recorded **zero Supporter actions** across all six default games, versus **78** total Supporter actions in a composite-policy rerun with unchanged registered 60s and starting-seed schedule. The engine's candidate-relative winners changed from five of six to three of six. Four of six corrected games had the losing side's deck empty. This is a powerful controlled *policy-dependence* diagnosis, not a 5/6 versus 3/6 win-rate experiment. [Rank-6 policy comparison](rank6-policy-contrast.md).
   **Rank-6 positive and resource-denial controls:** a later [source-owned autonomous hand-policy](rank6-autonomous-handline-source.md) did select real Guzma & Hala, pay two real discards, search DCE and complete an actual **140-damage Volt Cyclone** before observing the champion's next-turn Acerola/VMAX recovery decisions. That deliberately narrow trigger's exact opening opportunity is only **0.23373%** under its stated static no-bonus model; all four preselected extra seeds missed it. A different broad [source resource census](rank6-broad-discard-dci-tier-sensitivity.md) reaches **33.54814%** theoretical opening-resource opportunity by permitting 38 nominal discard copies, but the last eight include useful singleton/draw/gust resources and require actual cost-sensitive policy validation. This demonstrates both that a real signature line can execute, and why the generic bot's omissions and an access-only probability are different kinds of evidence.
2. **Archetype-specific signature actions may be omitted.** In six rank-2 Vileplume games, the default bot sometimes attached Technical Machine: Evolution and then passed; a real state-preserving pinned-Store probe accepted the missed printed Tool attack with an eligible target. The fixed campaign cannot be a qualified anti-control comparison until both opponent's Bunnelby evolution ALS and the challengers' replies have appropriate policy/rule coverage. [Rank-2/10 audit](agent35-crossrank-policy-diagnostic.md).
3. **Opening, position and Energy mistakes precede deck strength.** An authentic rank-10 diagnostic gave both champion and Temple first-acting candidates zero attacks against seven Regidrago attacks, with Energy on support Pokémon and stranded unpowered Active VMAX. Hood did attack in the same numerical-seed slot, but deck composition changes mean the source hands/initially drawn cards were different. It cannot be interpreted as a causal 60-card superiority claim. [Rank-10 actual first-turn boards](agent39-rank2-rank10-policy-diagnostic.md).
4. **A lock counter may defeat a counter.** New Moon's Ability-based Perfection may be suppressed by a public equipped Benched Garbodor, even with printed Gengar copying otherwise accessible. Shadow Rider's protection Tool can be removed by an opponent Tool-removal action or positioned away from the Ability source. Before comparing candidates, the adversarial opponent should be allowed its relevant printed source-60 answer. [Equipped Garbo live block](../twinleaf/rank11-turn23-garbodor-tool-target-contrast.md), [rank-8 Hood negative exposure](rank8-hood-lock-attack-qualification.md).
5. **Game and official tournament match are different outcomes.** The official Aichi Open uses a 25-minute Swiss game clock and unresolved Swiss turns can result in both players losing; Top-32 through semifinals follow a timed single-elimination resolution. Current long untimed source-run terminals, especially deck-out, have no calibrated human time. [Official clock scope](official-time-controls-qualification-gap.md).

## Actionable next discriminating comparison

A useful promotion test freezes all four registered candidates (or predeclares a smaller competing set), chooses *the same provenance-audited opponent 60s and decision policies* across **two distinct failure families** (e.g., source rank-6 Iron Thorns and rank-11 equipped-Garbodor), and prospectively allocates real first/second starts. A source-owner policy must execute the signature attack/lock or record honest failed/no-opportunity states; the candidate must actually execute the putative counter and subsequent attack; the opposing player must get a verified next decision. Record root actions, exact printed damage effects, Prize evolution and terminal cause; include missing/unfinished games and seed/seat proportions. Hold qualification to paper/legal and clock standards independently, and reserve separate held-out confirmatory samples.

Existing reusable source response packets sharply reduce cost. Reuse [rank-11 Tool target](../twinleaf/rank11-turn23-garbodor-tool-target-contrast.md) and [rank-11 Quick Ball decision reversal](rank11-turn49-quick-ball-compiled-result.md) as **mechanical regression controls**, not confirmatory data. The precommitted [separate eight-pair rank-11 Tool-maintenance diagnostic](rank11-garbodor-tool-maintenance-registration.md) checks *policy exposure* under new seeds and both physical seats, and reports all null opportunities. It does not meet this cross-rank comparative gate alone.

## Decision checkpoint

**No supported promotion of Hood, Temple or New Moon over rank-1 reference.** Retain source lists, tests and divergent candidates as separately versioned assets. Demand a reusable prospectively observed attack → opponent response → candidate reply packet against at least two genuinely distinct hard matchups before choosing the single 60 for wider validation. The current full-field denominator has **three unresolved exact registered opponent lists** at ranks 23, 25 and 28, as documented in [missing-source leads](missing-rank-source-leads.md). All competitive-qualified game counts remain zero; no defensible 99.5% estimate exists.

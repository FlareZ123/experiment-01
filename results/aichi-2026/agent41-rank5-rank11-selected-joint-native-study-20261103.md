# Agent 41: joint original-rank5/rank11 native competence screen (3 November 2026)

**Decision:** The first frozen symmetric 92-option policy composition failed the intended **original-opponent offensive competence** screen. Across **eight genuinely executed native attempts**, original rank-5 Mew/Genesect made **zero AttackAction declarations in 4/4**, and original rank-11 Mega Absol/Garbodor made only **three very late Terminal Period declarations in one of four attempts**, two settled before the 260-root cap. There were **no rank-11 Claw of Darkness declarations**. These are exploratory policy limitations, **not evidence that either tested candidate dominates either original deck**.

## Precise policy shortcomings

The composition was deliberately **symmetric**, with the same owner-observation root and prompt handlers on the two physical seats, plus shared paper prompt and payment corrections. Mew's natural evolution, copied Techno Blast choice, DCE mobility and Genesect Fusion Strike draw proposals were enabled. So were native rank-11 source Boost Shake scripts, VIP-before-attack, staged Mega Absol funding, preferred Active-Claw recipient and owner charged promotion. Despite the presence of these named modules, rank-5 source root traces contained **no original source attacks**, and in each row the native ChooseAttack copied-donor module recorded **zero accepted donor choices**. The rank5 seat A physical setup initially played a Double Colorless Energy then Passed; the seat B physical setup Passed. These source hands exposed cards such as Quick Ball, but this run had `actorOnlySingleSearch:false` and `actorHandDiscard:false`. Whether the held Quick Ball was genuinely payable from two strategically disposable owner-hand cards is still unproven. Those two disabled, owner-bounded components are plausible next controlled interventions, but the root traces alone do not establish a cause.

For rank11, both source-seat-B registered attempts used actual original-source Forest Seal Stone→Boost Shake and its native Garbodor-target prompt to completion on global turn 2. The two source-seat-A attempts Passed their first turn. Only the source-seat-B New Moon attempt subsequently used attacks, all Terminal Period on turns 54, 56 and 58. It took **zero additional Prizes** with the two settled attacks; the third was not fully settled at the root cap. The affected source's Active eventually held Mega Absol ex and two Darkness Energy, one short of the printed three-unit Claw. This observed shortfall is not evidence that a competent player with a different Energy attachment policy cannot use Claw.

The many native fallback prompts remain a **specific information-set competence veto**, even where the detached owner root proposals were accepted. On rank5, observed generic fallback counts were substantial and the new policy lacks a general-purpose opening setup search root. Native game length far exceeds paper round-clock equivalence.

## Interpretation and next falsification

**Reject the candidate-ranking use of this composite configuration.** The failure is about source-policy validity. A useful next controlled comparison should select in advance a new symmetric owner-only *single search* and *hand discard* profile, compare against unchanged v1 on the **same physical deals**, retain the native root/prompt payment evidence, and use fresh independent seeds to distinguish a favorable replay from a general correction. Test actual original Mew VMAX Active, payable DCE, genuine Genesect donor menu and an attack settlement as separate necessary gates. For rank11, independently check whether the source can promote and power Mega Absol to three Darkness before reaching the 260-action cap, with Active-versus-Bench funding contention and real opponent responses.

**Qualification:** The 32-field target still has 29 authentic original 60s, ranks 23/25/28 missing. This experiment produced **zero** games eligible for a paper Expanded competitive win percentage. No 99.5% claim and no promotion of a fixed best deck.

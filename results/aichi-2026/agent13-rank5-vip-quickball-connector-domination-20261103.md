# Agent13: more Quick Ball access causes a 5-to-0 source-attack collapse on a matched original rank-5 deal (3 November 2026)

**Decision:** Enabling the existing symmetric Quick Ball actor root alongside the already positive first-turn Battle VIP Pass rescued a *different* source rank5 deal (1→4 copied attacks versus Shadow Rider reference) while catastrophically **eliminating five native copied attacks** on the exact same other deal (5→0 versus New Moon Lockbreaker), with the original rank5 paper 60, source physical seed, original candidate printed 60, candidate physical seat, pinned Twinleaf, and same-root policy otherwise fixed. This is a concrete **connector domination / discard-acceptability** warning for the common policy: do not merge the present Quick Ball root as unconditional global opt-in on the strength of more accepted Items. The full search/discard-prompt handlers were actually used, but their activation did **not** rescue the New Moon failure.

No field game-win percentage was measured. This is a deterministic source native-policy development contrast with mixed consequences; alternate card use and opponent responses differ after the intervention.

## Concrete connector and discard evidence

- **Source-first, Champion reference A:** Quick Ball on global turn **1** discarded a duplicate **Double Colorless Energy SUM** (two originally in hand, one after selection), and then searched **Dedenne-GX UNB** in both root-on modes. That DCE is the deck's only Energy family for Mew VMAX's two-Colorless `Cross Fusion Strike` cost. Treating “duplicate” as “cheap discard” is strategically unsound when the deck has only four total DCE and needs future attacks. Two more Quick Balls were played on later turns; the owner hand-discard pure policy later chose duplicate Trainers' Mail and Float Stone, while raw fallback chose singleton Karen at one prompt. This is exactly where a state-aware DCI/AMR assessment matters.
- **Source-second, Champion reference B:** One source Quick Ball root on global turn **8** searched **Dedenne-GX UNB**. Native fallback selected singleton **Field Blower GRI** as discard; owner actor handler instead selected one of two **Special Red Card** copies. The restored source made **four** native copied Cross Fusion Strike declarations versus one under VIP-only. This establishes a possible beneficial connector path in that particular native state, although it occurs late and the alternative prompt handlers do not change attack count.
- **Source-second, New Moon B:** Under full actor prompts, source Quick Ball at global turn **14** discarded one of two **Trainers' Mail ROS** and searched **Dedenne-GX UNB**. A second Quick Ball at turn **20** discarded one of two **Ultra Ball MEG** and searched **Genesect V FST**. Under native fallback, the two payments instead discarded singleton Field Blower and singleton Lost Vacuum. Both variants resulted in **zero** source Cross Fusion Strike declarations before the root cap, compared with five in the identical VIP-only physical deal. Discarding these alternative search/utility pieces is a plausible contributor, but causal allocation among changed draw order, missed DCE, Bench contention and candidate responses requires a further matched root-branch audit. Do **not** assert the two discarded cards alone caused the collapse.
- **Source-first, New Moon A:** Quick Ball first discarded one of two DCE to seek Dedenne-GX. Later Quick Ball discarded a duplicate Trainers' Mail under the actor hand handler (singleton Karen under fallback). The source never used Cross Fusion Strike by 260 roots in any mode.

The owner search policy currently selects the **first lexicographically valid Basic**; in every source's first Quick Ball attempt it chose **Dedenne-GX**, independent of whether an Active Mew V lacked an Evolution or an Energy. These are actual native accepted search-to-hand receipts, and they expose a deficient strategic valuation. Further root acceptance counts can increase while the main attack line gets less achievable due to channel contention and poorly chosen discards.

## Next research priority

Before enabling Quick Ball universally in a frozen candidate/source policy, implement a genuine **owner-visibility and DCI-aware preference** for cost and result: preserve DCE while an unpowered Mew VMAX requires it, avoid spending a search connector that is strategically superior to Quick Ball, and search the appropriate Basic that advances an unfinished attacker line. Tests should be genuine native Store payer/recipient checks with fixed same physical original-source seeds, **both seats** and negative payment controls. The existing root requires a duplicate other printing, but that guard is demonstrably insufficient for preserving strategically scarce Energy.

To distinguish source actor decision changes from candidate responses, follow the *first different native source root* across modes and annotate whose side caused the divergence, the authenticated owner hand payment options, paid card destination and recipient Bench or hand.

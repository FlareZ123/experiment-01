**Agent 8 | 3 November 2026 | Retrospective read-only archive reclassification, no new native games**

## Decision question

The selected v3 bilateral release contained one activated optional funded Shadow Rider VMAX Float Stone mobility policy but selected that intent zero times. At what level of the action-opportunity funnel did the intervention disappear? In particular, did the candidate owner ever hold **Float Stone BKT** at an authentic native Pass decision where the native Trainer enumerator offered it?

The existing [selected v3 release](2026-11/agent4-selected-v3-paper-funded-prospective-48-native-20261103.md) reported 48 attempts (16 registered physical contexts, three A/B/A policy arms), 14 intact matched contexts, two matched-prefix rank6 native Ability aborts, no selected Float Stone, and zero competitively qualified games. This is a **new read-only analysis of the same historical archive**. All 48 original outcomes remain unchanged.

## Consequences for the activation gate

This establishes a **nonzero, physically observed held-Tool + native-offer + Pass frontier** in the selected v3 archive, despite zero selected funded-mobility intents. It does **not** establish that the optional root should have selected a Tool. The [actual source implementation](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent31-shadow-rider-funded-bench-floatstone.cjs) additionally requires, among other conditions, an Active Shadow Rider VMAX carrying just one or two Basic Psychic Energy, a separate Benched Shadow Rider VMAX already funded for Max Geist, a free Tool slot, a usable retreat and the absence of Tool-suppression effects. The frozen native owner-Trainer frontier contains held names and chosen roots, but it does **not** preserve all of that contemporaneous public board geometry for these ten decisions. Accordingly, the specific first failed gate remains **unknown** without a new controlled, live read-only public-board attestation on those original physical cases.

A targeted next canary should replay the same two named rank1 development cases only as a **retrospective mechanics diagnosis**, record the public board and every independent funded-mobility gate on each authentic Pass, then locate the earliest actual failed check or ordered-handler preemption. Preserve physical deal fingerprints and original actor decision hashes to verify noninterference. If a genuine simultaneous full paper-valid action exists, a separately registered prospective both-seat A/B/A activation experiment with settlement and opposing reply is then justified. No such proof is in this archive.

The 10 native-offered windows are repeated measurements from only two historical games. They are neither ten legal moves nor ten independent opportunities to win. No win-rate promotion, deck selection, or change in the 29/32 original-source coverage is supported.

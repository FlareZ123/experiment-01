## Why the conservative payoff conjunction did not arise

The original genuine actor-Pass recorder `nativeAllOwnerPassFrontier` captured **744** owner Pass roots across the 32 baseline games. A post-execution descriptive census of those **root receipts** found:

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |

| 11 | 193 | 94 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
| 30 | 126 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| **Total** | **744** | **193** | **31** | **1** | **0** |

The 744 Pass receipts and their subsets are **highly correlated within games and turns**. They are descriptive root counts, not 744 independent event trials. They are also a *necessary-condition diagnostic*, not an exhaustive proof of the same owner-view eligibility at every non-Pass action, though the complete native root history and zero treatment selections provide the broader observed outcome.

The single `held Stone + zero-Energy Active + opponent Active already damaged` source root came from **`11:1:A:new-moon-lockbreaker-v1`**, native root **152**, game turn **30**, owner source seat1. The source's Active was `Squawkabilly ex PAL` with no Energy; its three Benched Pokémon, `Garbodor BKP`, `Mega Absol ex M1L`, and `Oranguru V ASR`, also had no Energy. Opponent Active `Tapu Lele-GX GRI` carried 130 damage. A held `Float Stone BKT` could not convert this owner board into an immediately funded attack; there was no powered recipient. This illustrates why merely counting a held Tool or a zero-Energy Active seriously overestimates actual mobility payoff.

## Interpretation, limitations and decision

The prior post hoc positive root [rank11 `11:3:B:champion-reference`, seed 1101197735](agent14-native-zero-energy-strand-paid-ko-20261106.md) had the **necessary resources simultaneously**: Mimikyu with no Energy, Float Stone held, VMAX with 3 Psychic Energy on Bench, and 130 damage already on a 210-HP Oranguru V. In that case Twinleaf accepted Tool, free Retreat, Max Geist and two actual Prizes before the opponent countered with Guzma and eventually won. This fresh 16-source-group experiment, drawn independently of the example, encountered **no corresponding actionable conjunction**. The source opponent still requires a strong decision policy and real-world legal card coverage, and the sample covers four original ranks only.

The practical conclusion is to keep zero-Energy Strand→Float Stone as a **rare state-conditional tactical line** and a validated selector capability, without elevating it to a global deck win-rate optimizer or declaring the absence of benefit universally. A genuine optimization step should evaluate the **cost of occupying the Tool on the first rescued Pokémon and the vulnerability to subsequent Guzma gusts**, and should measure other mobility opportunities beyond this narrow one-step immediate-KO condition.

**Shared selected Issue25 v8 developmental policy remains unchanged. No fixed-60 champion is selected, and no 99.5% game win rate has been demonstrated.**

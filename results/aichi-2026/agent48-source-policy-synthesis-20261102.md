## Rank-5 Mew/Genesect is a complete causal example

The registered source has 4 Mew V, 3 Mew VMAX, 4 Genesect V, 4 Double Colorless Energy, one Wally and **no other Energy**. Under ordinary unmodified payments, **Mew VMAX's Cross Fusion Strike is its only payable printed attack**, requiring an evolved Mew VMAX, DCE and a legal Fusion Strike Bench donor. It can choose Genesect V's 210-damage Techno Blast using the copy mechanic. Agent25's original four rank-5 attempts yielded **zero source attacks**, all **source-first**, which establishes neither a missed legal attack opportunity nor policy competence.

Independent source-function tests identified three layers:

1. **Early access:** normal Mew V evolution selection exists but requires the explicit `actorNaturalEvolutionRoot` option. This option was absent in the original frozen v2 profile. An existing [eight-arm same-seed, same-original-runner replay design](../../simulation/evaluation/agent48-rank5-mew-original-evolution-counterfactual.cjs) preserves all Store outcomes and only reports a causal delta with exact source physical-deal parity. **Native replay was not run.**
2. **Energy recipient:** the shared Special Energy selector scores immediate printed attack deficits. In a detached Genesect Active, Crobat V/Mew V Bench state, placing Crobat before Mew causes the scarce DCE to attach to Crobat, whose Darkness attack cannot be funded from rank 5's source inventory. Reversing Bench order selects Mew. A future evolved Mew VMAX can use that DCE; local printed-attack deficit scoring misses that long-horizon value.
3. **Attack donor:** the shared copied-attack selector previously took the first eligible donor even when that was Mew V's zero-damage Energy Mix ahead of Genesect's Techno Blast. A [committed default-off policy option](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent13-actor-only-choose-attack.cjs), `preferMewGenesectTechnoBlast:true`, now ranks the exact Genesect source only when an owner's Active is Mew VMAX FST and the offered native prompt contains an unblocked Genesect V Techno Blast. It cannot read concealed opponent information. The [three-case regression](../../tests/agent48-mew-copy-actor-optin.test.cjs) first demonstrated the donor choice only in a mocked harness. This is now superseded for the narrow *integrated native attack* proposition by [Agent13's pinned v3.1 native comparison](agent13-rank5-v31-corrected-root-native-16arm-20261102.md): eight genuine copied Cross Fusion Strike declarations and effects settled across three of eight treatment attempts, versus zero attack declarations in eight frozen controls, with matching within-candidate original source deal fingerprints. Both players used the selected symmetric treatment profile. All engine terminals remain competitively unqualified and do not independently establish each individual source-policy option's causal effect. See the [earlier admission receipt](agent48-mew-donor-v3-admission-receipt-20261102.md) and [pinned-source code audit](agent48-mew-vendor-copy-path-audit-20261102.md).

The singleton Wally gives a different **going-second** first-own-turn evolution exception. All four historical source-first arms were incapable of exercising that specific first-turn route. In a no-extra-search/draw shuffled-hand model, the required Wally/Mew/Genesect/DCE opening plus a Mew VMAX left in deck occurs in just **0.7476884% of valid seven-card Basic openings**; the exact finite count is in the [Wally opportunity analysis](agent48-mew-wally-opening-opportunity-20261102.md). That is an opening *configuration* frequency, with real search, draw, locks, Prize recovery and decisions omitted.

## Rank-9 Absol/Electrode: a two-seat native source-policy reversal with a competence veto

Agent5 has now executed Agent10's existing owner-visible known-dead Mega Absol ex attack selector in [four authentic printed-60 original rank-9 native trials](agent5-rank9-known-dead-same-closure-native-20261102.md), and independently re-executed the matched default-off control on the same audited research-code closure and the **same actual source/full physical opening deals**. Treatment CI [41399309154](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41399309154), artifact 13120134600, and control CI [41400104410](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41400104410), artifact 13120433511, both succeeded. The observed `sourceClosureSha256` is identical across the two codeheads (`ff1fa1ea851160b4da95676ea2cd081e8078118c7b54f9aed4c78378b930b012`; the closure auditor has disclosed dynamic-import limitations). Three complete accepted Store root-ledger SHA256s are identical; the fourth changes from seven fruitless source Terminal Period declarations and a 260-root cap to five source Claw of Darkness declarations and a **248-root native source terminal** with six Prize cards actually taken.

The changed game is a powerful **opponent-competence warning**, because New Moon's Klefki made **19 consecutive 10-damage Joust attacks** against a 190-HP Electrode-GX, while the original source actor chose **21 PassTurnAction roots** between turns6 and46. Candidate's nineteenth Joust finally KO'd the Electrode on turn47, promoting the already-powered Mega Absol, whose Claw then took Prizes on turns48/50/52/56. The original source failed to put *any Energy* into the discard pile before that turn47 KO; consequently Electrode-GX's discard-dependent Extra Energy Bomb had no demonstrated legal early activation in this game. The candidate's five Bench slots were **all occupied from turn23 through turn47** by Unown, Dusclops, Trevenant & Dusknoir-GX, Lunatone and Duskull, each carrying **zero Energy**; Klefki held two Basic Psychic attachments and Mewtwo & Mew-GX was absent from the board throughout those nineteen attacks. This is a directly witnessed Bench-space/recipient bottleneck without proof of what the candidate held in its hidden hand. This is an actionable attack/tempo policy problem on *both* seats, with late real Store settlements that cannot be interpreted as tournament-realistic performance. The native outcome is a developmental tactical witness, not a representative Aichi match win.

A lightweight [reciprocal-stall census](../../simulation/evaluation/agent5-reciprocal-stall-census.cjs) now flags these serial attack/pass cycles while requiring settled Prize evidence and retaining missing-settlement uncertainty. [Its regressions](../../tests/agent5-reciprocal-stall-census.test.cjs) passed in manual [global field validation CI 41402455151](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41402455151). ## Fixed-candidate tactical counterclaim worth testing

Three exact original Iron Thorns ex 60s (ranks 6, 24, 29) also use only Special Energy. [Temple of Sinnoh](agent48-temple-iron-thorns-original-three-gate-20261102.md), while active, makes those Special Energy cards provide Colorless only; Iron Thorns ex's Volt Cyclone requires Lightning plus two Colorless. Hence the printed Energy inventory cannot fund its attack under that Stadium without other type/cost changes. This **does not disable Initialization**, and opposing Stadium replacement can end the denial. Champion-reference has eleven Basic Psychic Energy and can avoid this particular self-energy penalty, but the provisional New Moon candidate has three Telepathic Psychic Special Energy, three Basic Psychic and two Path to the Peak, so Temple creates material self-cost and Stadium contention. A conditional counter-line is a candidate test, not a proven better fixed 60.

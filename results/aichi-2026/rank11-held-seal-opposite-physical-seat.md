## Complementarity and limits

For the earlier 128-deal **source-in-physical-A** authentic probe at a longer 110-action horizon, [compiled run 39164058353](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/39164058353) recorded 61 source-first, 67 second, and five opportunities/five completions, **all source-first**. Its first 32 deals were then reproduced in [48-action run 39188056487](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/39188056487) with identical selected actions and full source-own-turn ledgers for both eligible deals. The source-B fixture shows the additional source-second and Muscle Band lines, without implying a quantitative seat effect: the same source seed list in different physical seats need not encode identical opening-card deals or RNG consumption.

These denominators count opportunity *detected by the currently narrow owner-hand policy*, after generic earlier play and any legitimate search/draw. They are **not universal setup-probability estimates**, since many alternative lines are unmodeled. The source-positive rows themselves were chosen retrospectively for explanation. The engine uses weak automatic bots, a short action horizon, a candidate English-mechanics proxy and unverified complete paper timing/rules. There are still **29/32 exact opponent lists, zero competitively qualified games, and no demonstrated fixed-deck 99.5% field strength**. A source-opportunity rate must not be converted into matchup or candidate-win evidence.

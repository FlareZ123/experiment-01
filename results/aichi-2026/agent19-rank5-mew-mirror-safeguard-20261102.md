# Rank-5 Mew mirror Max Miracle beats rank-3/7 PAL Mimikyu Safeguard

**Agent19, 2 November 2026. Printed-text counterexample and detached owner-choice proposal. Zero qualified games, no win-rate estimate.**

Original Aichi rank 5 (Yamato Nakano) contains **4 Mew V, 3 Mew VMAX FST114, 4 Genesect V FST185, 4 Double Colorless Energy SUM136**. Original ranks 3 and 7 contain **one and three Mimikyu PAL97** respectively. Source: `results/aichi-2026/full-decklists.json`, with printing text in `resources/cards/en/swsh8.json`, `sv2.json`, `sm1.json`.

A Mew VMAX is a Fusion Strike Pokémon. When **Active Mew VMAX** has one **DCE**, it pays the [C][C] cost of *Cross Fusion Strike*. With a **second evolved Mew VMAX on its Bench**, it can copy that Pokémon's **Max Miracle**, dealing **130 damage while ignoring effects on the opposing Active**. This bypasses PAL Mimikyu's Safeguard (damage prevention from Pokémon ex/V), Knock-Out threshold **70 HP**. The copied attack's [P][P] printed cost is ignored under the provided Advanced Rulebook's C-18; B-07 governs ignoring defensive effects. In the same state, copied Genesect Techno Blast's 210 attack damage is prevented by Safeguard.

**Key contention:** two separate already-evolved Mew VMAX, an attached DCE, legal donor availability, the native selection and suitable turn timing are all required. The original 60 includes the necessary card printings, but this says nothing about natural establishment frequency. Under uniform six Prize cards, the chance two or three of the three VMAX are Prized is `(3*C(57,4)+C(57,3))/C(60,6) = 2.42548217417%`. Its complement only measures the survival of two VMAX cards outside Prizes, not a board or win chance.

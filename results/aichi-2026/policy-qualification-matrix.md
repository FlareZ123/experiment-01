## Global confounds across all nine

- **Hidden-information leak:** pinned `SimpleTacticsAi` reads real unseen own-deck order when scoring hypothetical card draws. Independent [actual action test](../twinleaf/hidden-order-policy-leak.md) and [draw-score audit](../twinleaf/hidden-information-policy-audit.md) show information-set violations. Independent speculative RNG streams do not repair this.
- **Shared stochastic stream:** legacy headless match decisions consumed real-game pseudorandom samples during hypothetical evaluation. [Opt-in independent streams](../twinleaf/seed-stream-isolation.md) passed full compiled smoke CI. Label and stratify any new outcomes by `randomnessProtocol`.
- **Trainer/Supporter contention:** the preexisting bot mutated `supporterTurn` during action proposal. [Opt-in reversibly corrected policy](../twinleaf/supporter-decision-policy-gate.md) must be enabled and competence assessed separately.
- **Engine-card and legality scope:** a legal action resolving or a match finishing does not prove correct Expanded mechanics for every relevant source card. Test exact card text and critical interacting threats; distinguish source-printing substitutions and unsupported card errors.
- **Pale Moon-GX retreat clearing:** Compiled source-ranked rank-10 [actual-game fork](rank10-pale-moon-source-rule-discrepancy.md) showed raw Twinleaf retaining a delayed Defending-Pokémon Knock Out after an ordinary Active-to-Bench retreat, contrary to the supplied rulebook A-03. Agent 36's opt-in correction removed the pending effect and changed one terminal Prize distribution under an unchanged source seed. Raw uncorrected source games reaching this effect cannot be used as paper-rule-verified matchup outcomes.
- **Source-graph mutation and bounded detachment:** Agent 3's [compiled rank10 four-arm source comparison](rank10-attack-pass-horizon-source-policy.md) ([CI 39001518181](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/39001518181)) identified raw speculative writes to a Regidrago V hand Card's own fields and a Benched Regidrago V `movedToActiveThisTurn` flag during genuine Koraidon turn-five choices. With an experimental clone installed *only during each decision*, all five recorded source decisions in each of four compared candidate/policy arms produced unchanged root Store own-data graphs while original Pass and corrected Retribution Strike actions remained distinct. This is source-window clone noninterference evidence, **not** global source-policy competence, hidden-information fairness or a qualified game.
- **Bench-entry Ability lock timing:** pinned Twinleaf can incorrectly resolve Wonder Tag, Climactic Gate, Dedechange and Dark Asset while an opposing in-play-only Ability lock should suppress the entering Pokémon. The raw-vs-guarded Store action regressions and independently opt-in, versioned corrections are documented for [Tapu Lele-GX](../twinleaf/lele-entry-ability-lock-timing.md), [Radiant Eternatus](../twinleaf/cologne-radiant-gate-store-action.md) and [Dedenne-GX / Crobat V](../twinleaf/dedenne-crobat-entry-lock.md). The last two appear in 19 of the 29 complete source lists. Raw lock-affected trials are unsuitable for matchup inference; the corrected game-policy evidence remains unqualified.
- **Whole-field uncertainty:** ranks 23, 25 and 28 lack published full lists. The 32-placement denominator and coverage flags are retained in [field.csv](field.csv) and [the evidence protocol](evidence-protocol.md).

## Acceptance protocol

A rank becomes policy-qualified only after recorded, reproducible evidence for: (1) its intended line as executed by the evaluated bot, (2) one plausible and consequential opposing response also executed, (3) legality and rules tests of the actual cards, (4) realistic imperfect-information decisions, (5) versioned deck, engine, policy and RNG provenance, and (6) replicated scenarios across meaningfully varied seeds and board starts. The current matrix flags deficiencies, not a claim that no test of any individual component exists.

Even passing these gates produces an *experimental evaluation environment*. Human-level match-win inference would still require demonstrating policy quality against appropriate skilled opponents and an independent confirmatory sample as defined in [statistical evidence](evidence-protocol.md). As of this writing all nine policy readiness cells remain **No**.

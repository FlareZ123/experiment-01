## Statistical interpretation and scope

The opening-location [exact five-source combinatorial census](agent6-five-source-tagcall-k0-access-20261101.md) was computed separately from these seeds, with no policy or opponent interventions. The twenty new physical deals are far too small to establish the true rate of Tag Call availability, and their actual first-turn owner hands differ in length because starting Basics can be put on the Bench and opponent mulligan compensation can add cards. This cannot be naively calibrated as a sample of eight-card unseen physical positions without accounting for those setup rules.

The fact that **both** Tag/no-held-G&H eligible deals led to a successful G&H search is a *conditional native mechanics witness*. It is not a 100% success estimate over all opening states, because G&H can be completely Prized or a separate lock could forbid the Item. The source action happens before the opponent’s first ordinary reply, and these existing bot policies do not certify paper Expanded win strength. We do not infer that playing this particular first Item is the best choice or that the later G&H Supporter becomes affordable.

This is nevertheless a meaningful bridge: original field ranks 2 and 15 contain very different Evolution and control plans from rank6, and the same owner-only firstturn proposal, native Store root dispatch, genuine owner deck search, exact card prompt selection, and resulting owner hand mutation succeeded on both.

# Rank 25: first-person Alolan Raichu matchup evidence, exact 60 still unavailable

**29 October 2026, source verification only.** The original paper Expanded Aichi 2026 Open League field still has **29/32 exact published 60-card decklists**, with ranks **23, 25 and 28** not recovered. This result does **not** reconstruct or change the unavailable rank-25 list.

## Identifying the source

The [CL Aichi Open League May 10, 2026 Limitless results](https://www.limitlesstcg.com/tournaments/566/decklists) identify the **25th** finisher as **Kazuki Kumegawa**, archetype **Raichu Electrode Combo**. The Japanese event-oriented [Pokekameshi Top 32 roundup page 2](https://pokekameshi.com/cl2026aichidec-extra/2/) has an **Alolan Raichu one-shot** entry attributed to player handle **kume**, linking their account and match report.

A **first-person report by kume**, posted May 14, 2026: [`CL愛知2026エクストラ ベスト32 アロライワンショット`](https://note.com/kume_pokeca/n/n6cfd126f5774), explicitly says Swiss **25th** at **7–1**, losing the first Top 32 elimination match to Mega Absol/Garbodor. This is strong evidence that rank 25 is a second Alolan Raichu one-shot representative, not a recoverable exact print/count list.

## Strict evidence limitation

The report's **`デッキ構築解説` (deck construction explanation)** appears *after* a paid article boundary in the currently publicly readable version. No original 60-card list, original card-code export, complete printed IDs or verified counts were found in the freely readable text. Therefore rank-25 remains a **missing exact source 60**, even though archetype identity and first-person opponent history are now corroborated. It would be invalid to copy another Alolan Raichu finisher's list and label it the rank-25 registration.

**Practical use:** record the source strategy as a qualitative adversarial test when evaluating the *other* published Raichu/Electrode lists, keep rank25 excluded from exact-deck matchup simulator claims until the official sixty becomes accessible, and do not promote player-reported Swiss match results to a controlled win-rate estimate.

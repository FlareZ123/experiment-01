These are reference computations, not tests of bot skill or game win rate; the structural opportunity condition differs from the actual actor policy and neither source line opportunity nor owner resources were recorded for every arm.

The actual starter was source-first on thirteen of the sixteen independent seeds (twenty-six of thirty-two correlated arms). This is an exploratory allocation imbalance, **not proof** of a broken first-player rule or RNG. Seat placement should not be substituted for observed first-turn strata.

The 0/6 source-first absence is unsurprising against the restricted 9.666% direct-location reference and does not itself identify a policy error.

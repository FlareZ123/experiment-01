# Rank-11 Basic Energy supply invalidates Garbodor nominal attack proximity

**Agent 43, 29 October 2026. Source-derived fact plus pure constructed counterexample.** The [original exact rank-11 Absol/Garbodor 60](full-decklists.json) contains **8 Darkness Energy MEE, zero Psychic Energy, zero Special Energy**. Its Garbodor BKP 57 attack *Offensive Bomb* costs Psychic plus three Colorless; Oranguru V ASR 133 has a move named *Psychic* whose attack cost is three Colorless. These are printed costs from the provided card corpus. Under ordinary Basic Energy attachment alone, Garbodor's attack is impossible to fund from this registered deck's Energy supply, regardless of how many Darkness cards it gains.

Historical original Store owner-first rank11 continuation after native T7 Marnie showed a **T13 Active Garbodor with three Darkness, Benched Oranguru V with one Darkness and held Guzma**. In a synthetic owner-only state with those public Energy placements, the unmodified `chooseHeldGuzmaMobility` algorithm counts the Garbodor printed attack as **one theoretical attachment away**, compared with Oranguru's **two**. It returns null, despite the required Psychic being absent from the player's exact registered 60. No claim is made that an eventual Guzma would be strategically best: Garbotoxin Ability lock, Supporter costs, actual gust target and turns to attach remain consequential.

The new research-only [supply-aware analyzer](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent43-registered-basic-supply-guzma.cjs) inspects the player's own registered 60, filters Basic-Energy-unfundable printed attacks from its own detached public board, then reuses the same original Guzma mobility selector. On the test state it excludes `Offensive Bomb` for a missing Psychic source and proposes Oranguru. It preserves existing Supporter and Bench vetoes and fails closed on incomplete registered lists, unknown or Special Energy, and unrecognized attached Energy. It **does not account for alternate Energy-type modification, game-state-dependent cost changes, opponent actions or the tactical value of keeping Garbotoxin Active**.

[Six deterministic tests](../../tests/agent43-registered-basic-supply-guzma.test.cjs) passed in [manual pinned CI 40962321356](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/40962321356), including exact manifest 60-count, a one-Psychic counterfactual, unavailable physical resources and an opponent-hidden-hand getter that throws. The overall CI run **failed due to the separate native Guzma recipient integration test**, so this is pure-model evidence only. There is no game win-rate evidence from this work. The field remains **29/32 exact lists, zero qualified competitive games**.

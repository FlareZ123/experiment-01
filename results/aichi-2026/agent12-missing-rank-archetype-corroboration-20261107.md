## Rank 23: Yuki Ueno, Regidrago

The official [CL Aichi Open League results](https://limitlesstcg.com/tournaments/566) list Yuki Ueno in 23rd place. The official [Regidrago tournament history](https://limitlesstcg.com/decks/260/results) independently lists Yuki Ueno at 23rd in the 10 May 2026 event. This supports an event-level Regidrago archetype attribution.

The [Limitless player profile](https://limitlesstcg.com/players/6227) exposes a 2024 CL Sapporo result but no Aichi 2026 list. The [official Aichi decklists page](https://limitlesstcg.com/tournaments/566/decklists) does not contain Yuki Ueno. No public exact 60 was located.

## Rank 25: Kazuki Kumegawa, Raichu Electrode Combo / Eleki Rain

The official [CL Aichi Open League results](https://limitlesstcg.com/tournaments/566) list Kazuki Kumegawa in 25th place. The official [Raichu Electrode Combo tournament history](https://limitlesstcg.com/decks/282/results) lists Kazuki Kumegawa at 25th for the same event.

The public [PokeKameshi Top 32 recap](https://pokekameshi.com/cl2026aichidec-extra/2/) labels the 25th-place deck as Eleki Rain and associates it with `kume`. The public excerpt of [kume's note article](https://note.com/kume_pokeca/n/n6cfd126f5774) states 25th-place qualification and describes the deck as an Alolan Raichu one-shot strategy. The detailed article content is paid; no paid content was accessed.

There is a strong public identity bridge between the kume account and Kazuki Kumegawa. The kume article's public author bio states that kume was the CL Yokohama 2023 runner-up with Lost Box Charizard. The public [CL Yokohama 2023 recap](https://pokekameshi.com/cl2023yokohama/) names `kume@シロシ団のジャッジ` as runner-up and reproduces a post from `@kume_pokeca`. Limitless independently lists [Kazuki Kumegawa second at Champions League Yokohama](https://limitlesstcg.com/tournaments/342), held on 17 September 2022, and the official [Yokohama decklists](https://limitlesstcg.com/tournaments/342/decklists) identify his second-place deck as Lost Box Charizard. The Japanese event was branded CL Yokohama 2023 despite its 2022 calendar date. The matching unique finish and archetype make the account/player mapping strongly corroborated.

This resolves the likely identity bridge and archetype. The exact Aichi 60 remains unavailable. Do not substitute the paid article's unseen list or another Alolan Raichu list.

## Rank 28: Jo Kato, Regidrago

The official [CL Aichi Open League results](https://limitlesstcg.com/tournaments/566) list Jo Kato in 28th place. The official [Regidrago tournament history](https://limitlesstcg.com/decks/260/results) independently lists Jo Kato at 28th in the same event.

The public [PokeKameshi Top 32 recap](https://pokekameshi.com/cl2026aichidec-extra/2/) labels the 28th-place player as Replica and reproduces a 10 May post from `@nnaacc5_uec` stating 28th place and Regidrago. The public AtCoder profile for that handle uses display name `rep_` and lists the University of Electro-Communications. No source found in this search directly connects that handle to the official Limitless name Jo Kato. Preserve the uncertainty. The official Limitless row is sufficient for event-level archetype attribution.

The official Aichi [decklists page](https://limitlesstcg.com/tournaments/566/decklists) contains no Jo Kato list. No public exact 60 was located.

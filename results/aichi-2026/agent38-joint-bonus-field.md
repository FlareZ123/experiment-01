# Agent 38: joint Shadow Rider route access with opponent mulligan bonuses

**16 October 2026.** Evidence class: exact source-based finite-population resource modeling, independently exhaustive mini-deck tests and exact two-player mulligan mixture. No Pokémon match-play outcomes or win rates are asserted.

## Question and scope

The [joint two-route audit](agent42-joint-vmax-access.md) counts access to at least one of (a) a first-turn Radiant Eternatus two-VMAX Gate route or (b) an ordinary turn-two Shadow Rider V to VMAX evolution using basic draws. It includes the real overlap between routes. That initial audit assumes **zero opponent mulligan bonus cards**.

In the same fixed-deck model, how does the probability change after accounting for the optional draws generated by opponents with different numbers of Basic Pokémon? The top-32 Aichi field includes three published Iron Thorns lists containing only four Basics, which gives opponents frequent extra-draw opportunities.

The exact [new enumerator](../../scripts/agent38-joint-bonus.cjs) preserves all two-route assumptions and accepts exactly K optional bonus draws after six Prizes are set. Then it counts one ordinary first-turn draw, and one second-turn draw solely for natural VMAX-in-hand readiness. It assumes every offered bonus is accepted up to **K=45**, preserving both normal draws. All K>=45 are pooled into that final event.

## Critical Iron Thorns applicability constraint

The first row has an important gameplay qualifier. The three four-Basic opposing lists at **ranks 6, 24 and 29** contain four Iron Thorns ex and no other Pokémon. Their starting Active must be Iron Thorns ex. Its printed **Initialization** suppresses Radiant Eternatus's Rule-Box Ability while the lock is active. This is established in the source-backed [Iron Thorns Gate lock study](../als/iron-thorns-suppresses-gate.md).

Thus the 66.34% / 69.01% / 62.06% **unrestricted-resource union** figures in the four-Basic row are unsuitable as functional setup probabilities under an unsuppressed Active Initialization. If Gate is fully suppressed and no lock-escape line changes that state before Gate, the available branch of this deliberately restricted two-route model is **natural evolution alone**:

| Candidate | Restricted natural T2 opportunity against four-Basic opponent | Gate-only contribution excluded by Initialization |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Champion | 23.206899% | 43.136534 percentage points |
| Hood v1 | 28.733176% | 40.277842 percentage points |
| Temple v1 | 22.983898% | 39.080409 percentage points |

The excluded contribution is P(Gate and no natural route) under the unsuppressed hypothetical resource model. It is not a measured Gate-attempt rate. Effects that remove or suppress Initialization or change the Active board state can alter the applicable rule, while other resource channels may succeed without Gate. A gameplay-qualified matchup probability requires modeling those lines.

## Exact derivation

For each fixed eight-category source inventory, enumerate the seven-card opener by hypergeometric category count and exclude a no-Basic mulligan. Integrate a set of K bonus cards plus the first-turn ordinary card as a single size-(K+1) sample. By random-permutation exchangeability, sampling that block before the six unknown Prizes yields the same joint zone distribution as the physical order, where Prizes are placed first.

Then sum over the two Prize categories that can affect the resource event: Radiant Eternatus, with count 0 or 1, and Shadow Rider VMAX, with count 0 through available copies. Other Prized cards are counted by the complementary binomial coefficient. This mathematical reduction avoids enumerating every irrelevant Prize identity. The exact second-turn draw is marginalized from the remaining drawable deck. Integer multiplicities and successful outcome counts use BigInt; numerical conversion happens only at final probabilities.

For an opposing deck with Basic-count mulligan probability pA and candidate with pB, the accepted opening has bonus-offer distribution:

- P(K=0) = (1-pA)/(1-pA*pB).
- For K>=1, P(K=k) = (1-pA)(1-pB)*pA^k/(1-pA*pB).
- The probability of at least 45 offers is (1-pB)*pA^45/(1-pA*pB).

The weighted probability is the sum of each conditional joint event times its bonus probability, with the tail mass applied to K=45. The implementation uses the existing [mulligan distribution library](../../scripts/aichi_bonus_draws.cjs), preserving consistency across research.

## Limits and decision relevance

The category model assumes all available search Items can be used, without a discard-budget policy, lock or Supporter contest. Natural evolution assumes a Shadow Rider V can be put into play when found and survive through turn two. Gate assumes an eligible starting Active Basic and two VMAX remaining in deck; forced-Active Wobbuffet suppresses Gate under the stated no-switch rule. Opponents affect this specific calculation **only through their Basic count and associated optional mulligans**; it does not model opponents' actions, counterplay, attacks or stadium disruption.

The calculated opportunities can be unavailable in a real turn due to unmodeled play constraints; other draw/search effects can create setup routes omitted here. A higher early resource opportunity may still be strategically inferior after considering the resulting board, attack access and deck slots.

This provides a source-specific setup benchmark to compare future search and decision policies. Credible game-win assessment against the full Top 32 still requires validated gameplay and the three unavailable opponent decklists.

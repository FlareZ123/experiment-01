## Separate owner-only policy bridge and failure provenance

Both-seat regression `tests/agent49-owner-treasure-native-prompt-bridge.test.cjs` previously failed, revealing that the second owner deck prompt arrived while the bridge retained `plan.step='cost'`. Diagnostic [CI 41377396511](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41377396511) showed the physical VSTAR was native-valid yet inaccessible to the incorrect step selector. The adapter's acceptance plan was missing the `ownerId` field subsequently looked up in the paid-discard callback; the callback therefore returned before updating its step. This was patched in commit `49c6d5b97b4d2369039c5cf81c89261a9c886dd7`. **The full, independent pinned CI rerun [41377988597](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41377988597) succeeded.** Both native owner-only payment/search bridge tests completed successfully, as did both direct Store tests, the full print census, and the existing independent BUS regression. The previously failing bridge was an **adapter state-custody bug**, not a native Mysterious Treasure card-implementation failure.

Trace events now distinguish mismatched native prompt, missing eligible owner-only candidate and planned-discard callback acknowledgement. The native actor and Store prototype wrappers are restored after the scoped synchronous run. Policy remains explicitly opt-in under `mysteriousTreasureRoot`.

## Purpose and prior registration

The [preregistered five-rank 40-attempt source experiment](agent10-prospective-five-rank-funding-mobility-40-native-20261103.md) found two surprising rank-11 native winner reversals when a bundled actor policy enabled **both** exact-printing Mega Absol staged funding and symmetric funded paid Retreat. The source's first changed action was a real owner Darkness Energy attachment to **Benched Mega Absol** instead of **Active Squawkabilly ex**. Was the native change from the funding selector, mobility selector, or their interaction?

We retained **only the two already observed changed original source physical cases** (rank11 source seat0, source first, candidate seat1, parent registered seed `4096421492`) and froze their 60s/initial deals. This is a **post-observation diagnostic**, not a fresh or held-out efficacy cohort. [Registration](registrations/agent10-rank11-source-funding-mobility-factorial-20261103.json) defines the four policies for each fixed candidate:

1. Control: funding OFF; flexible paid Retreat OFF.
2. Funding: funding ON; paid Retreat OFF.
3. Mobility: funding OFF; paid Retreat ON.
4. Combined: both ON.

All eight native games were attempted, all **eight original two-player physical starting deals and printed source/candidate 60s matched the corresponding control**, and the actual starting seat stayed identical.

## Interpretation and qualification veto

The strong local native result is **useful original opponent competence**, especially for the champion reference's Darkness-weak TAG TEAM targets. The source rank11 and candidate initial 60s were unchanged; this changes **source actor policy**, so the observed `source wins` are **adversarial losses for the candidate**. The sample is one retrospectively identified original source seed with two different candidate 60s, and no direct match clock.

Do not generalize a 100% source success claim from 2 cases. Staging a 3-Energy attacker at global turn15 and first attacking at global turn33–37 could be competitively far too late; the candidate has many meaningful chances to respond, and the current policy does not model expert defense or official timing. Source attack settlement proves native prompt completion and a physical Prize transition, while full-game paper legality remains unaudited. The source deck also includes lock functionality not necessarily used in this selected route.

**Competitively qualified original-opponent games: zero. No paper-certified candidate win rate or fixed best 60.**

### Conditional tactical consequence, precisely bounded

On the observed board, **if no other retreat modifiers apply** and Skyliner were enabled, Latias ex would have zero normal Retreat Cost, with two possible Bench promotions. With the native-blocked Skyliner under source Active Iron Thorns ex, the printed two-Energy cost remains unless another effect changes it. Because Latias has zero attached Energy before the candidate turn2 first action, it cannot pay that printed normal Retreat Cost **at that recorded boundary**. This conditional mobility comparison is more informative than counting a generic native Ability suppression flag. A Trainer-switch effect, later attachments, or an alternate attacking plan can still provide other options.

This is **evidence for a potential immediately relevant suppressed movement channel**, *not* proof of an attempted or optimal retreat, a denied legal action reported by the actual policy, a complete factual active-vs-benched native-cost fork, or game-winning pressure. We have no actor-hand policy choice audit that establishes whether the candidate wanted to retreat. If another native rule or effect changes the retreat computation, the counterfactual must be recomputed and tested.

The two candidate inventories need separate *actual applicable pressure* descriptions even under an aligned source physical deal.

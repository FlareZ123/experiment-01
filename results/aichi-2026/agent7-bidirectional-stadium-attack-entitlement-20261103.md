# Agent 7: signed attack entitlement from replacing an existing Stadium

**2026-11-03.** An owner-visible printed Energy requirement feature now explicitly scores two opposing effects of Stadium replacement. It distinguishes a legal action from a genuinely improved funded attack.

## Two independent authentic native game witnesses

- **Positive, original rank6 Iron Thorns ex vs original rank9**: under own `Wondrous Labyrinth Prism Star TEU`, `Volt Cyclone` printed [Lightning, Colorless, Colorless] cost becomes [L,C,C,C], while source Active holds 2 Speed Lightning + 1 Capture Energy (3 physical energy units). Replacing the own Stadium with physically held `Thunder Mountain Prism Star LOT` changes attack cost to [C,C]. Real native Store accepted that replacement and both subsequent T10/T12 Volt Cyclone attacks, taking 2 native Prizes (6→5→4); opponent used Rescue Carrier and Unown between attacks. Exact same 60s/seed, matching root-action prefix of 61, repeated controls equal. [First-divergence report](agent7-original6v9-selected-stadium-first-divergence-20261103.md), [native CI 41707597245](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41707597245).
- **Negative, independent Agent20 original rank6 source**: replacing its own `Thunder Mountain Prism Star LOT` with `Path to the Peak CRE` or `Dimension Valley PHF` forfeited the cost discount. With two Speed Lightning on T17, the native control retaining Thunder attacked, but each actual replacement treatment passed on T17, requiring an attached third Capture Energy before attacking on T19. Native winners ultimately unchanged. [Agent20 original source report](2026-11/agent20-frozen-rank6-selected-stadium-aba-negative-20261103.md), [native CI 41664388496](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41664388496).

An **adversarial caveat** to the first positive native reply: Agent5 independently confirmed that the rank9 opponent held `Escape Rope BST` before its T11 Pass. That Item may displace the powered Thorns; a separate real source [opponent counter preregistration](registrations/agent7-rank6-turn11-escape-rope-opponent-counter-20261103.json) and test are underway. The two-Prize historical native result is therefore a *mechanistic witness*, without a claim of robustness to a skilled opponent.

## Reusable conservative owner-only calculation

[The new signed feature](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent7-stadium-entitlement-delta.cjs) reads only frozen `actorObservation` owner physical hand and public printed Active attacks, attached Energy, Stadium custody, quota and public Item/Stadium locks. [Existing typed Energy matcher](../../simulation/twinleaf/actor-energy-payability.cjs) and [printed Stadium cost parser](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent7-funded-stadium-attack-planner.cjs) compute before/after `payable`, with `gains-entitlement`, `loses-entitlement`, `unchanged` or `unresolved` for each attack. Known cost modifiers: Wondrous Labyrinth (+C for non-Fairy), Thunder Mountain (-L for Lightning), Dimension Valley (-C for Psychic). Known direct attack-cost neutral: Path to the Peak, Sky Field, PokéStop, Silent Lab. Unknown other Stadiums abstain.

The new [signed tests](../../tests/agent7-stadium-entitlement-delta.test.cjs) cover the rank6 positive, both Agent20 negative scenarios, Path→Sky neutral, unknown text abstention, printed-name/quota veto and opponent hidden-info isolation. The manual [CI 41713454688](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41713454688) reports the full **22-unit-test Stadium owner gate PASS**. Earlier CI 41712773278 had failed an outdated pre-extension expectation that Silent Lab was unknown; commit `78f2f1b` corrected that test to recognize its direct attack-cost-neutral effect and still require unsupported Stadium abstention.

**Limitations:** Exact native Store attack legality, Ability locks, prize trade, Bench capacity, future damage, Prism Star irreversibility, and the opponent's best response exceed the printed cost calculation. This is an action-comparison feature, not a blanket Stadium switch rule or source action execution. Source matches are retrospective and native-bot/action-capped; 0 official paper-competitive games have been established. Full field 29/32 authenticated original lists, ranks23/25/28 missing, no fixed 60 winner or 99.5% demonstrated.

## Why these are necessary-condition failures, not scored losses

The exact [rank26 published list](full-decklists.json) contains **one DCE**, **two basic Lightning Energy** and **one Tapu Koko Prism Star**. Crisis Punch costs **three Colorless**. Tapu Koko's Dance accelerates **one Lightning to each of two distinct Benched Pokémon**, giving at most one of the source Lightning to Meloetta, which must attack in the Active. With the one normal hand Energy attachment permitted by the [Advanced Player Rulebook](../../resources/advanced-players-rulebook.md), the identified first-turn line requires **manual DCE onto Meloetta plus one Bench-attached Lightning** before moving into the Active. The fixed original source 60 contains no Energy Switch, other attached-Energy relocation effect or replacement DCE. See the [positional/Tool necessary-condition report](rank26-bench-before-dance-positional-gate.md).

The three traces defeat **that exact one-turn line** through a wrong Energy type/target decision. Other independent combo requirements may also be missing: Cologne to overcome Iron Thorns ex's Initialization, five controlled self-KOs through Pump/Propagation, valid Risky Ruins events, TM Tool allocation, Koko timing, Bench space, and the attack's one-opposing-Prize predicate. Therefore a firstturn line veto does **not** prove a game loss, and the three selected physical deals do **not** measure the probability that an improved opponent policy succeeds.

## Discriminating action-policy repair

The current `withRank26DceReservation` wrapper addresses a different trigger: **Active Meloetta plus a proposed manual basic Lightning**. It does not protect a DCE in hand when **Meloetta is initially absent** or when the generic bot chooses to attach that unique DCE to another Pokémon. The 48-case integrated trial omitted this reservation wrapper entirely, so all three cases remained exposed.

A narrowly falsifiable repair is an **owner-visible firstturn DCE target guard**: when the engine proposes ordinary DCE attachment to a Pokémon other than Meloetta, prefer a legal Meloetta destination if already in play; otherwise defer the Energy tactic in favor of other legal actions, preserving DCE for a later Meloetta search. The guard must respect actual owner hand and board only, use the native legality validator and restore its tactical hooks after each match. It must never inspect Prizes or force a Meloetta search. Before promoting such a repair as stronger source opposition, replay **these three fixed development deals** and require actual post-attachment target/energy state; subsequently register disjoint fresh seeds and report *every* source-first and source-second case. A second independent effect-to-opponent-response gate remains mandatory.

**Verified boundary:** **3/3** held-DCE first-player observed first-action cases have the specific bad resource-allocation root sequence under *both frozen policies*, within **20/48** authentic source-first/all-deal denominators. This is a policy defect/capability gap in a diagnostic source bot, **not** three losses or a competitive win percentage. Global research still has 29/32 exact opposing 60s and zero competitively qualified games.

**Agent 33, 1 November 2026.** Source: rank-25 finisher kume's publicly readable first-person [CL Aichi 2026 Alolan Raichu one-shot report](https://note.com/kume_pokeca/n/n6cfd126f5774), 14 May 2026. This documents matchup intentions, **not** the original 60-card inventory: the detailed construction/list section is paid and unavailable here.

The author reports **7-1 Swiss**, including five Regidrago games (four wins, one loss), one Lightning Toolbox win, one Roaring Moon win and one ADP Dragonite win. The first Top32 elimination was a loss to Mega Absol/Garbodor. These are self-reported historical results and cannot serve as simulator game-win observations.

Important **initiative asymmetry** from the freely available source:
- **Source first:** suppress early opponent Latias ex and Tapu Lele-GX Abilities, reducing opponent turn-one Budew Item lock; prepare an answer to Item lock if it nevertheless lands.
- **Source second:** set up through opponent Path to the Peak, Sudowoodo Bench restriction and Wobbuffet Active; avoid or answer Regidrago VSTAR's immediate next attack.

The author considered Klefki, Wobbuffet, and Boost Shake/Garbodor for Ability denial; Marshadow against Path; Wobbuffet/Klefki/Empoleon V against Sudowoodo; Escape Rope/Canceling Cologne against Active Wobbuffet or Klefki; and Energy denial, Horror House-GX, disruptive attacks, or gust with a KO for the Regidrago VSTAR threat. The source rejects Garbodor as burdensome and argues for an approach able to incorporate Klefki. **This is a menu of discussed counterplay, not proof that all listed cards appear in the exact rank-25 original 60.**

The May10 [@kume_pokeca](https://x.com/kume_pokeca) public quotation likewise says Top32 on **アロライワンショット**. This matches the unique Limitless **rank25 Raichu/Electrode Combo** identity and event. The alias/name association is **strongly corroborated by equal placement and archetype**, though the account does not print the player's full formal name in the accessible body.
- The readily indexed article becomes **subscriber-only exactly at “デッキ構築解説”** (Deck Construction Explanation) and its preceding public portion contains extensive **metagame/plan reasoning** but does not reveal a complete registered 60. No reproducible full inventory or official deck code was found for the specific rank25 account, and nothing has been imported as a validated list.
- Distinguish [2026 Top4 Alolan Raichu source deck](https://pokekameshi.com/cl2026aichidec-extra/) code `LNnnHg-baOFyZ-Qgg69n` (nickname でんでん) and another Top32 player 湯豆腐 (Swiss10) from rank25 kume. ## Implications for the single-fixed-60 benchmark

- The 29 known sixties cannot stand in for the full exact field. For a single fixed deck, unknown opposing exact paper choices can change crucial matchups, even within the same Regidrago or Electric Rain archetype.
- The two independent alias/standing clues help **target primary list retrieval** (official deck registration, authenticated photograph with complete count, or original player-confirmed 60). They do **not** increase the 29/32 complete original-list count.
- A public post saying “Top32” or presenting a photo of card piles must be compared against the specific placement, player and date before adoption. Preserve provenance separately from guessed expansions. A paywalled original deck-analysis article does not authorize inventing counts.

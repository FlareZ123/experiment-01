## Official tournament structure

The official event page separates the Master League into three stages:

- Day 1: up to eight Swiss rounds; players with at least six wins advance.
- Day 2, Main 1: a separate Swiss stage of up to seven rounds, carrying forward the Day-1 win points. The final Main-1 standings determine the top 32.
- Day 2, Main 2: the 32 qualifiers play a single-elimination tournament.

The Limitless page's numbered 1–32 table is the final Top32 placement table. Its rank 23 is Yuki Ueno, rank 25 is Kazuki Kumegawa, and rank 28 is Jo Kato. The same page's Decklists tab has no published list for those three ranks.

## Why the photo does not establish final rank 28

The roundup quotes the account as having qualified **28th** and then lost in the first knockout round. Under the official event structure, the qualification ordinal belongs to the Main-1 Swiss standings, while the Limitless 1–32 table reports final Main-2 placement. The first-round loss places the player among the 17–32 bracket finishers, but the post does not identify which final ordinal they received.

Consequently, the equality of the number "28" in the photo caption and the final standings is not a valid identity join. The photo transcription may be a useful **provisional qualifier-deck candidate**, but it cannot be admitted as Jo Kato's final-rank-28 original 60 without a direct player-identity bridge or an authoritative bracket/match record that maps the qualifier to the final placement.

The other gallery examples in which a player's reported qualifier ordinal happens to match a final Limitless ordinal are corroborative observations only. They do not override the official two-stage-plus-bracket structure, and the already documented qualifier-16 / final-16 counterexample demonstrates why ordinal equality cannot be assumed.

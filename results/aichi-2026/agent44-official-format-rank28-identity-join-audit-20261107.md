The Limitless event page lists 32 source rows numbered 1–32, but its visible table does not define whether those numbers are preliminary Swiss standings, final bracket placements, or another source-page ordering. Do not describe the table as final bracket placement without an authoritative mapping. The official event structure itself shows why the distinction matters: preliminary Swiss order and subsequent single-elimination results are separate stages. Source pages: https://limitlesstcg.com/tournaments/566 and https://www.pokemon-card.com/info/005445.html.

The photo's “qualifier 28th” is therefore a **plausible lead** for source row #28, but it does not prove that @nnaacc5_uec is the Limitless-listed Jo Kato. The account-to-player/registration join remains unproven; a direct player identity bridge or authoritative registration/deck-check record is required. The photo transcription may be retained as a **provisional qualifier-stage deck candidate**, with its own provenance and confidence. It must not be attached to Jo Kato's source row #28 or treated as the exact registered 60 until the identity and registration joins are established.

## The decisive invalidating opponent weakness

The Catcher treatment played the actual printed Item at Store action 146 and resolved a genuine opponent ChoosePokemonPrompt at 147. The resulting **T32 public board** has the source's zero-Energy **Squawkabilly ex Active** while the source's **three-Darkness Mega Absol ex is Benched**. The unchanged control still had the powered Mega Absol Active. Both variants' initial physical setup and first source Claw were identical.

At **T33**, the source actor Passed. Its post-Pass hand observer explicitly reports **one held Float Stone and one held Guzma**. The source Passed again on T35, T37, T39, and onward through T63; by T55 it held three Float Stones and one Guzma. No relevant Tool or Supporter mobility play occurred despite a fully powered Benched Mega Absol. This is a severe **source opponent policy competence failure** and explains the stalled action-cap outcome. The fall from five Claws to one is a real native effect but cannot be treated as reliable tactical suppression of a competent player. Float Stone has a potential Tool-plus-retreat response; Guzma may provide another escape under proper permissions.

The Lillie intervention used the genuine Supporter root at T32 and native shuffle/draw prompts. Gengar & Mimikyu-GX was subsequently Benched, but candidate Active Unown remained energyless and Passed. The source continued to attack and ended with all Prizes. A single available Supporter draw action did not repair the attack deficit.

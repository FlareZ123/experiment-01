# Rank26 Meloetta against Iron Thorns: original-source policy and full-line evidence

**Agent 4, 19 October 2026.** This is a synthesis of compiled, bounded, print-mapped rank26 source experiments. The main research question is whether a source-owned autonomous rank26 opponent can execute its signature firstturn line against a fixed original-source rank6 Iron Thorns ex 60. Source rank26 is a benchmark adversary; an artificial source failure makes candidate wins look stronger than warranted.

## Source line requires coordinated constraints

The registered rank26 source 60 contains a single `Meloetta ex`, `Double Colorless Energy`, `Tapu Koko Prism Star`, 2 `Lightning Energy`, 2 `Exeggcute`, `Risky Ruins`, `Damage Pump`, `Canceling Cologne`, 2 `Technical Machine: Crisis Punch`, 4 `Quick Ball`, 4 `Trainers' Mail`, 2 `Hisuian Heavy Ball`, `Dowsing Machine` ACE SPEC and other enabling Trainers. The exact registered source is preserved in [full-decklists.json](full-decklists.json). Relevant difficulty is **simultaneous**: rank6's Active Iron Thorns Initialization suppresses relevant Rule Box Abilities until legally bypassed, Tapu Koko acceleration targets Benched Pokémon, Active/Bench positioning and Tool slot determine actual Crisis Punch availability, and source-controlled 2+2+1 self-KOs require real Prize, damage-counter and Propagation transitions. A search path that finds Meloetta can still be dominated by Energy, retreat, lock or Tool opportunity cost. Evaluate actual official printed attack and opponent reply before a performance claim.

## Core conclusion

Local search, discard, recovery and Tool-veto decisions can be made mechanically legal without producing the attack the original deck is built around. In one actual firstturn prefix, improving a Basic search was genuinely effective at getting Meloetta onto the Bench. Preserving Dowsing Machine upstream merely allowed the generic bot to consume it downstream on a circular Iono recovery; an additional targeted hold then kept the resource but still did not establish the attack. These observed facts reinforce the **connector domination**, **realistic availability**, and **state-dependent discardability** cautions in [human concepts](../../resources/human_concepts.md), while remaining insufficient to validate any general heuristic of the same name.

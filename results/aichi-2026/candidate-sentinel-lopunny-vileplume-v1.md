## Decisive lines, action costs and counters

**Early lock:** Going second, `Tag Call → Guzma & Hala` can select Grand Tree and, **only if two other hand cards can actually be discarded**, Technical Machine: Evolution plus Jet Energy. Bunnelby PRC121 has Ω Barrage, enabling two Evolution Tool attacks for staged Bench evolution if its attack cost is paid. Stage, turn, Bench and two-attack legality require real native and paper checks. Grand Tree cannot evolve a Basic on either player's first turn or the turn it entered play. When Vileplume AOR3 is established, Irritating Pollen prevents **both players** playing Items, including this deck's later Tag Call. Stealthy Hood on Vileplume may protect against opposing Garbotoxin; Tool Jammer/Jamming Tower and Tool-slot contention can undo the line. Pidgeot ex Quick Search is an alternative longer-game access channel but can itself be suppressed by Iron Thorns.

**Pressure:** Evolve Buneary into Mega Lopunny ex and move it from Bench to Active via an actually legal retreat, Jet Energy switch or Guzma. Gale Thrust costs one Colorless, doing 230 if it entered Active from Bench **that same turn**, otherwise 60. This raw 230 reaches unmodified 230-HP Iron Thorns ex but falls 50 short of 280-HP Mega Absol ex and a 280-HP Regidrago VSTAR. Two Colorless enables 160-damage Spiky Hopper ignoring effects on the opposing Active. Mega Lopunny is worth **three Prizes when KO'd**. One Stadium Temple of Sinnoh can suppress opposing Double Dragon Energy but also removes our Jet switching effect; Grass Energy still supplies a Colorless, and Artazon can replace Stadiums before a Jet. Regidrago, Iron Thorns, Garbodor and stadium responses must be modeled rather than assumed solved.

**Control pivot:** An established Active Stoutland BCR122 uses Sentinel to lock opposing Supporters while Benched Vileplume locks Items. This consumes the Active position and forfeits a simultaneous Mega Lopunny attack. Two Bunnelby preserve the Burrow/Rototiller recovery and double TM evolution option. Bench space, the single Herdier, low Energy count, a lost Stoutland to Prizes and opponent gust create obvious failures. The third Guzma, three Jet and removed recursion cards impose real Supporter/Energy contention.

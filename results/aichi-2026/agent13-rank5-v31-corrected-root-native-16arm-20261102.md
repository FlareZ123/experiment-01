## Classification and an output-status defect

The new release wrapper's `sourceWitness(row)` currently sets `status=row.status??'UNVERIFIED'`. Completed unqualified native rows store their actual `finished`, `endReason` and `actionCount` inside `rawNativeRow`, without a top-level `status`. The wrapper therefore prints **`UNVERIFIED` for all sixteen condensed evidence statuses**. This is a **reporting deficiency**, and it must never be misread as 16 engine errors or as 16 settled games.

The original archived `rawNativeRow` values establish the actual disposition: **frozen control 2 engine terminals, 6 action caps, 0 engine errors; integrated treatment 4 engine terminals, 4 action caps, 0 engine errors**. More than a few rounds of continued simulated turns happened, so the original early root assertion was truly bypassed. The source-code fix below the release should classify `finished===true && endReason==='engine-game-end'` as a developmental terminal and `finished===false && endReason==='action-limit'` as an action cap, and preserve other native failures separately. Keep the original archived bytes unchanged for provenance.

## What this establishes and what remains open

- **Observed native change:** consistent owner-visible strategy crossed evolution, Special Energy payment readiness, native donor choice, Cross Fusion Strike declaration, settled attack results, Prize changes and at least some opposing continuation in the controlled treatment. The same code profile baseline produced zero original source attack declarations. The intervention is symmetric, so some response and timing differences also originate from candidate policy changes. Causal attribution to either of the two individual toggles is unresolved.
- **Limitations:** the native `sourceAttackSettlement` rows explicitly mark `paperRuleAuditPassed:false`, `fullPolicyInformationSetAuditPassed:false`, `tournamentClockAuditPassed:false` and `qualifiedForCompetitiveInference:false`. The shared bot can delegate unresolved prompts; candidate opponent strategy remains weak in several records. A native source terminal against such a bot carries no claim of a tournament win. Late attacks and action caps cannot substitute for official paper time adjudication.
- **Next necessary research:** unmask the exact **legality and damage effect** of each paid Cross Fusion Strike with independent paper-rule tests; inspect source opportunity failures in five zero-attack treatment arms, including actual held DCE, evolved Mew, legal active positions and generational lock; observe opponent strategic choices and use prospective new independent seeds with paper clock. Do not optimize candidate deck slots from these engine-terminal counts.

**Benchmark remains:** **29/32** authenticated original opposing sixties, **0** competitively qualified games, **no** legal fixed deck demonstrated to win 99.5% against all 32 original opponents. Three missing original sixties remain ranks **23, 25 and 28**.

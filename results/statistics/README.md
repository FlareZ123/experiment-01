# Statistical methodology for the Aichi field

The target is one frozen 60-card deck evaluated against all 32 Aichi Open League placements, with actual first/second starting positions treated separately. All documents here are **methodological**; the repository has no competitively qualified win-rate sample.

- [Field evidence and per-cell exact confidence intervals](field-evidence.md): simultaneous 64-cell lower bounds, zero-loss sample requirements and the 29/32 missing-opponent ceiling.
- [Correlation-robust complete-round e-process](correlation-robust-field-rounds.md): allows arbitrary dependence among the 64 rank/start outcomes within each complete round under a conditional-round-mean null, retaining the original strict provenance checks. Synthetic adversarial CI demonstrates why seed uniqueness alone cannot justify the sharper independent-game e-process. No competitive games are certified.
- [Anytime-valid equal-seat aggregate e-process](anytime-field-aggregate.md): a predeclared test permitting repeated checkpoints after complete 64-cell rounds, mathematically crossing at 640/640 perfect games under its stated independent-trial assumptions. Its hardened input gate now rejects reused game IDs/seeds, unqualified engine diagnostics and cross-round deck/policy/version drift. These are integrity checks, not independent certification of game skill or randomness.
- [Physical Prize rescue capacity: marginal, legal-opening conditional and joint](agent27-prize-joint-versus-marginal.md): exact source comparison, supplementary calculator and an independent exhaustive-deal test ([manual CI](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/38876259687)). Static initial-Prize configurations only.
- [Field-wide 32-placement statistical evidence protocol](../aichi-2026/evidence-protocol.md): full-ranked-place manifest, uncertainty and provenance.
- [Source-sealed actual-start negative test](sealed-start-veto-protocol.md): connects outcome-blind chronological seed accrual to a frozen candidate physical slot and initial-deal-bound game evidence, with adversarial tests and compiled manual CI 39242475756. No competitive games certified.
- [Hard-matchup familywise veto](hard-matchup-veto.md): exact necessary 84% rank-average / 68% rank-seat floors for a 99.5% equal-weight target, precommitted zero-win batches, and a fail-closed structural input checker. Conditional negative evidence only, with no qualifying matches to date.
- [Archetype-group necessary win floors](group-loss-budget.md): the 32-rank 99.5% target permits only 0.16 expected rank-equivalent nonwins. Regidrago's 11 placements collectively require at least 98.545% average winning, Absol Garbodor's four require at least 96%, and smaller groups have corresponding necessary minima. Mathematical constraints, no played data.

**Always qualify policy and game-rule fidelity before interpreting any statistical certificate as competitive strength.**

# Statistical methodology for the Aichi field

The target is one frozen 60-card deck evaluated against all 32 Aichi Open League placements. The repository contains two distinct field estimands: **64 cells** (rank × candidate actual first/second) in the original anytime/robust methods, and **128 cells** (rank × candidate physical seat × actual first/second) in the current seat-balanced nomination standard. Do not interchange their certificates; the 64-cell methods need an audited round-level fair-orientation contract or independent full-environment seat-invariance proof before they can support the 128-cell claim. All documents here are **methodological**; the repository has no competitively qualified win-rate sample.

- [Field evidence and per-cell exact confidence intervals](field-evidence.md): simultaneous 64-cell lower bounds, zero-loss sample requirements and the 29/32 missing-opponent ceiling.
- [64-cell physical-seat estimand gap](../methodology/2026-11/agent42-64cell-physical-seat-estimand-gap-20261114.md): the existing 64-cell anytime/robust validators require candidate slot metadata but do not balance or randomize physical candidate seat; absent an audited seat-assignment contract, they do not certify the current 128-stratum seat-balanced field mean. Methodological caveat under Agent37/Agent12 review; no game or deck result.
- [Correlation-robust complete-round e-process](correlation-robust-field-rounds.md): allows arbitrary dependence among the 64 rank × actual-start outcomes within each complete round under a conditional-round-mean null. **It does not balance/randomize candidate physical seat and is not interchangeable with the current 128-stratum seat-balanced field mean** unless an audited round-level fair-orientation contract or independent full-environment physical-seat-invariance proof is supplied; see the [physical-seat estimand gap](../methodology/2026-11/agent42-64cell-physical-seat-estimand-gap-20261114.md). With the current mixture weights and lambda grid, the all-perfect crossing is at 603 rounds (38,592 games); this is a much larger but dependence-robust fallback than the fixed 1,280-game iid design. The pure evaluator accepts only caller-supplied complete rounds and cannot detect an omitted aborted round: any accepted campaign also needs a precommitted round roster/abort ledger, host-side coin receipt, and per-game policy-state reset. Synthetic adversarial CI demonstrates why seed uniqueness alone cannot justify the sharper independent-game e-process. No competitive games are certified.
- [Anytime-valid 64-cell rank × actual-start e-process](anytime-field-aggregate.md): a predeclared test permitting repeated checkpoints after complete 64-cell rounds under its stated independent-trial assumptions. Like the correlation-robust variant, its current validator does not balance/randomize candidate physical seat, so it is a distinct 64-cell estimand and does not by itself satisfy the current 128-stratum nomination gate. Its hardened input gate rejects reused game IDs/seeds, unqualified engine diagnostics and cross-round deck/policy/version drift. These are integrity checks, not independent certification of game skill or randomness.
- Exact fixed-sample aggregate field-mean budgets: [one nonwin](agent5-exact-field-mean-one-loss-bound-20261113.md) uses 8 games per 128-cell stratum (1,024 total); [two nonwins](agent12-exact-two-loss-field-mean-bound-20261114.md) uses 10 per stratum (1,280 total). Both are single aggregate claims, not per-opponent guarantees, and require independent fixed-probability games.
- [Per-matchup 99.5% confidence evidence budget](99-5-field-winrate-evidence-budget-20261114.md): a simultaneous one-sided 95% lower bound of 99.5% for every one of 32 opponents requires 1,290 zero-loss games per matchup (41,280 total); 1–3 losses require 1,744 / 2,135 / 2,494 games for that matchup. This is a **stronger per-matchup claim**, not the aggregate field-mean objective; do not conflate it with the 640-game all-win or 1,280-game two-nonwin 128-stratum aggregate designs.

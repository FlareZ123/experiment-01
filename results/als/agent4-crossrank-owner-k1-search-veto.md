## Mechanism

The [pure CommonJS preflight](../../simulation/twinleaf/actor-k1-search-plan-veto.cjs) accepts a frozen detached actorObservation at an authentic owner root, an optional authenticated [K1 owner-deck-search snapshot](agent4-owner-k1-prize-knowledge.md), and a declared immediate plan carrying explicit **required printed hand cards**, **required exact owner-deck card copies**, **free Bench slots**, and an action kind. The only action kinds considered are Supporter, Item, Stadium, Tool, Energy and other. It audits public one-per-turn resource expenditure and locks for the relevant kind.

Output is either **blocked** with explicit falsifying reason(s), or **not-disproven**. The latter deliberately is not labeled legal, executable, valuable or competitive. The actual native Store remains the action, payment and target-legality authority. K1 does not become an actor input until an owner-authenticated unresolved ChooseCardsPrompt references their actual deck, and the memory belongs to the same physical seat. K0 cannot rule out a search target based on a hidden physical Prize placement. A fresh K1 can detect any required target multiplicity absent from the deck whether or not its absence is proven to be a Prize event. If deck membership has gone stale, it refrains from claiming a target is absent, except when the **whole registered supply** remains certified Prized and Prize composition has not changed.

The companion compareOwnerSearchPlans retains the complete blocked/unblocked ledger, with a **caller-declared fixed candidate preference order**. The first nonblocked alternative may be used as a deterministic development intent. It has not been connected to the common active opponent/candidate action policy and does not score a rare tactical card merely from graph accessibility.

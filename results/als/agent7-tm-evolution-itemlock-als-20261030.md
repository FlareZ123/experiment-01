# TM: Evolution attack under Item lock: conditional Stage-2 Turn-2 route

**Agent 7 | 30 October 2026 | rules/printing-derived ALS, native Store untested.**

A key route overlooked by naive Rare Candy-only analysis is **Technical Machine: Evolution PAR178**, a Pokémon Tool whose **Evolution attack costs one Colorless** and can evolve up to two of the *attacking player's Benched Pokémon* by searching the deck for their next-stage cards. This can act on Pokémon just placed in play because it is an attack-produced evolution effect, which the supplied Advanced Player's Rulebook section **C-12** permits unless card text specifies otherwise. Normal first-turn/evolution restrictions still apply to manual evolutions; the special route advances the Stage 1 on the first turn going second. A Pokémon Tool is distinct from an Item in the supplied Rulebook B-02; early Item lock alone need not prevent the Tool from being played.

A source-faithful possible ALS for the [frozen Lillie-reset 60](agent7-tinkaton-lillie-reset-v1.json):

1. **First turn going second:** establish a non-Tinkatink Active Pokémon that can carry TM and pay one Colorless, plus a **Tinkatink on Bench** and ideally a **Dunsparce on Bench**. The existing **Guzma & Hala** (if independently accessible and playable) can fetch **TM: Evolution**, a Special Energy such as Double Colorless and a Stadium, with a two-card hand discard. TM is a Tool; only one Tool may be on each Pokémon at a time.
2. Attach the TM to that **Active carrier** and pay its attack cost, then use Evolution to place **Tinkatuff** on the Benched Tinkatink and **Dudunsparce** on the Benched Dunsparce when both next-stage copies are genuinely available. The Tool is discarded at turn end by its own text. A full Bench, missing search targets, Tool lock, attack prohibition or an inability to pay the attack invalidates the path.
3. **Second turn:** ordinarily evolve the already-in-play Tinkatuff to **Tinkaton ex**, attach Double Colorless/Double Turbo to Tinkaton if available, and find a genuinely legal **Active switching/retreat route** (Float Stone, Guzma if the opponent has a Bench and Supporter quota available, or another card effect). Once that has been done, use **Lillie's Determination** as the turn's Supporter if six own Prizes remain, drawing eight. If Dudunsparce is already present, Run Away Draw can add three further cards. Then Big Hammer has a nominal 240/330 line with Double Colorless and eight/eleven hand cards (Turbo is 20 less). The T2 Supporter cannot simultaneously be Guzma/Arven.

**Crucial contention:** v1 has only **one TM: Evolution, two Tinkatuff, one Float Stone, two Guzma, two Lillie's, six Double Energy** and no guaranteed initial carrier that can retreat for free. Its Active mobility is an unresolved bottleneck. Energy spent on the T1 TM carrier may be stranded; T2 still needs new Energy on Tinkaton, and the hand may need two different Special Energy cards. This is an ALS feasibility hypothesis, not a demonstrated opener. Against combined Supporter+Item lock, Guzma & Hala and Lillie may fail even though TM is not an Item. Against Pokémon that prevent Tool play or attacks, the TM route may fail earlier.

The proposed falsification: original pinned Store both-seat **first-turn going-second** Tool attachment, a paid TM attack resolving two actual evolved Benched recipients, following owner turn Stage-2 evolution, physical Active movement, six-Prize Lillie shuffle/draw, Double Energy cost and attack, with owner K0/known Prize constraints and source-first lock counterplay. Do not count a theoretical connector path or a Stage-1 search alone as success.

# New Moon Dusknoir + Stealthy Hood v4: non-Item Garbotoxin bypass versus lost Cologne redundancy

**Agent22 · 28 November 2026. Evidence class:** exact uploaded Expanded legal printings, exact original Aichi inventories, printed rules, analogous official Japanese Slaking/Hood Q&A, contemporary judge/community Garbotoxin/Hood discussion, and combinatorial opening-Prize calculations. **Not a native tested Garbotoxin-vs-Hood ruling, source-dealt game, full competitive policy or win-rate claim.** Frozen [v4 60](new-moon-dusknoir-hood-v4-60.json) is a separate challenger; it does not replace retained New Moon v1/v2 or the active Hood Shield nomination.

## New fixed 60 and zero-cost-Ability counter class

This variant is **v3 `-1 Canceling Cologne ASR 136 (2→1) +1 Stealthy Hood UNB 186 (0→1)`**. All other v3 prints remain unchanged, including **1 Dusknoir SFA 20**, 2 Duskull, 2 Dusclops, 2 Powerglass, 2 Field Blower, 1 Forest Seal Stone, 2 Mewtwo & Mew-GX, 2 Ace Trainer and 6 Psychic-providing Energy cards. Exactly **20 Pokémon / 34 Trainers / 6 Energy = 60**, 36 distinct print IDs. Stealthy Hood UNB 186 and Dusknoir SFA20 are marked paper Expanded legal by the bundled English corpus.

**Printed mechanism (conditional on a correct Hood/Garbotoxin ruling):**

- The authentic **Absol Garbodor ranks 8, 11, 16, 22** run **2/2/3/2 Garbodor** plus **4/3/4/4 Float Stone**; an effective **Garbotoxin** normally suppresses **Mewtwo & Mew-GX Perfection** as well as **Stage2 Dusknoir Cursed Blast**.
- **Stealthy Hood attached to own Dusknoir** is a Pokémon Tool, and its text prevents effects of the opponent's Abilities on the attached Pokémon. The same effect class is known to protect Pokémon from some globally stated opponent Ability denials (e.g. Japanese official [Slaking/Hood Q&A](https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/faq/search.php?page=2&regulation=all&sc_group_id=533)); [judge/community discussion](https://www.pokebeach.com/forums/threads/secret-hood-ability-denial-denial.149912/) and [Reddit discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/pkmntcg/comments/dbzot2) explicitly describe Hood protecting a holder from Garbotoxin. **Direct native or official Garbotoxin-specific confirmation is still required before treating this branch as certified.**
- Garbotoxin does not disable Pokémon Tools themselves. If Hood successfully protects **Benched Stage2 Dusknoir**, its **Cursed Blast 130 counters** can be used while a Tool-bearing opposing Garbodor remains Benched. No Item is needed to remove the source's Float Stone first.
- Even with **Mewtwo's Perfection disabled**, its **printed Miraculous Duo-GX [P][P][C] 200** is still an attack (unless another attack restriction applies), and **200+130=330** KOs **280-HP Mega Absol ex** for **three opposing Prizes**, while Dusknoir self-KO concedes one. The extra GX Energy for the all-board heal is optional, but the base three-Energy attack cost is not. This line must use the one-per-game GX allowance.
- Hood must protect **Dusknoir**, not merely Mewtwo; a Hooded Mewtwo may regain Perfection but would leave an unprotected Dusknoir Cursed Blast suppressed. **One Tool per Pokémon** means the same Dusknoir cannot simultaneously wear Hood and Powerglass; that is acceptable for Cursed Blast (zero Energy), but removes the Powerglass-assisted Shadow Bind evolution/attack bridge **on that exact holder**. In Vileplume matchups without Garbotoxin, v4 may choose Powerglass instead, retaining the original Stage2 Shadow Bind150 answer.

The key distinction is **Tool presence versus Tool effect**. *Tool Jammer* or *Jamming Tower* does not remove Float Stone, so a Garbodor with Float Stone still satisfies Garbotoxin's “has a Pokémon Tool attached” clause even if Float Stone's own Retreat-cost effect is suppressed. An actual Tool-discard/Lost-Zone effect or a shield preventing Garbotoxin's effect on the target is necessary. This is why a Hood-on-Dusknoir route is different from v2 Tool Jammer.

## Second substantive Hood holder: existing Clefairy ex versus rank-15 BUS lock

The new Hood is not exclusively an anti-Garbotoxin tech. **v4 still contains its original singleton Lillie's Clefairy ex JTG56**, printed Basic, whose **Full Moon Rondo [P][C]** deals **20 +20 for each combined Benched Pokémon**. The official Japanese BUS Vileplume / Hood FAQ directly confirms that a **Basic Pokémon wearing its own Stealthy Hood may attack through BUS Vileplume's Disgusting Pollen** ([official Q&A](https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/faq/search.php?freeword=%E9%9A%A0%E5%AF%86%E3%83%95%E3%83%BC%E3%83%89&page=2&regulation=all)); older Tools may still be attached through AOR Item lock.

In an authentic rank15 joint-lock state with **own Active Clefairy + Hood + one already-attached Energy card that provides [C]** (e.g. one Basic Psychic), three own Benched Pokémon, source Active BUS Vileplume at 140HP and source Benched AOR Vileplume, attaching **Telepathic Psychic Energy** from hand to Psychic Clefairy supplies the second **[P]** attack Energy while adding two Basic Psychic Pokémon to own Bench (assuming empty slots, searchable Basic Psychic in deck, and unused normal attachment): own five Bench + source one Bench = **six combined**, Full Moon Rondo 20+20×6=**140**, KO BUS without any Dusknoir evolution, Ability, Item, Supporter, or own self-KO. This exact conditional route was identified in the v2 research as a *hypothetical alteration*; **v4 is a now-frozen 60 that physically contains the Hood needed to attempt it**. The source can prevent the six-Bench threshold by keeping a sparse Bench or by attacking Clefairy. The card supplying [C] must have been attached earlier or accelerated by a separate legitimate effect; the same-turn Telepathic attachment by itself provides only one Energy and cannot satisfy both attack symbols.

The **singleton Clefairy + singleton Hood both unprized** event, conditional on Basic-valid initial seven, is **80.914341626%**; not a turn-access or win probability. Crucial constraint: one Hood can protect **either** Dusknoir against Garbotoxin **or** Clefairy against BUS on that board, not both; attached Tools cannot be moved or replaced freely. The Tool slot choice is **matchup- and current-state-specific**, and should be made before exposing the hand to discard effects. Thus the v4 change adds **two distinct opponent-lock action classes** while costing the second early Cologne, not just one narrow Garbotoxin response.

## Source replies and tradeoff

All four Garb source decks also use **Dark Patch**, **Mega Absol ex** and speed/draw/supporter disruption; rank8 and22 each have **one Field Blower** capable of discarding our Hood, while rank11 and16 list none. Rank11 and16 still have **Guzma** and large-damage Mega Absol attacks that can gust and KO a 160HP Dusknoir directly. Rank8/16/22 can use Budew Item lock to prevent our preexisting two Field Blower responses, although the **Hood itself is a Tool and remains playable through Item lock** under the Advanced Player's Rulebook B-02 (older pre-Sword/Shield Tool printings retain Tool classification). Rank11 can still use other disruption; don't infer safety from absence of Field Blower.

The price of adding Hood is removing the **second Canceling Cologne**, which was valued against **Iron Thorns ex rank6/24/29**, Active Klefki, Flutter Mane, and BUS Vileplume when Items remain playable. It is not a free improvement. The fixed 60 still contains only **one Hood**, and each Garb rank may deny our Duskull/Dusclops evolution before it exists. That same singleton Hood also becomes strategically *UDP* when held for a later turn, potentially worsening Secret Box/Ultra Ball discard realism and hand quality.

## Proposed controlled comparator

**Rank11 and rank16** are the cleanest falsification forks: both have exact Mega Absol/Garbotoxin/Float Stone source inventory, neither lists Field Blower, and both can gust a fragile evolution before it reaches Stage2. Compare **unchanged v3 (2 Cologne, 0 Hood)** with **unchanged v4 (1 Cologne, 1 Hood)** on identical authentic deals, both actual first-player strata and competent source decisions. Record whether the source's Garbotoxin truly suppresses Hooded versus un-Hooded Dusknoir in the pinned engine, whether actual paper ruling corroborates it, and whether **Cursed Blast→Miraculous Duo-GX** can be paid and used on the same candidate turn. Opponent Item lock and exactly which Tool occupies each holder must be in the trace.

**Counterbalance:** include Iron Thorns rank6 or24 where cutting Cologne can lose early Ability-lock bypass; include rank15 where Hood on the Dusknoir differs from Powerglass as a Stage2-attacker Tool. Keep all games unqualified until actor-visible decision-policy competence and real source response pass the project's acceptance gate.

**Disposition:** v4 is a **mechanistically distinct, legal, unqualified 60-card challenger**, not a promoted best deck. It is an attempted *solution* to a four-rank Garbotoxin obstruction using one Tool slot, with an explicit quantified opportunity cost and adversarial owner gate.

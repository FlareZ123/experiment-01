# New Moon Cursed Blast enables a non-GX, two-Energy Iron Thorns finisher

**Agent22 · 28 November 2026. Evidence class:** exact uploaded card texts, published Aichi source rank6/24/29 60s, damage arithmetic and conditional state legality. No opponent-aware native game, competent-policy evidence or win rate. All fixed New Moon Dusknoir variants **v3–v7** already contain the named cards; **no extra deck slot or printing substitution is needed**.

## Key move order: Dusknoir + Clefairy, not Mewtwo GX

Against a source **Active Iron Thorns ex TWM77 (230HP)** with **Initialization** operating, **no applicable protection against opponent Ability effects**, and any source Bench occupancy:

1. Have **Benched, legally evolved Dusknoir SFA20** with unused **Cursed Blast**, and **own Active Lillie's Clefairy ex JTG56** with a legally paid **Full Moon Rondo [P][C]** and a plan to have **at least four combined Benched Pokémon after Dusknoir's self-KO** (candidate + source). The Dusknoir itself ceases to occupy a Bench space and contributes no Rondo damage during the subsequent attack: without replenishment, at least **five combined Bench occupants before the self-KO** are necessary. Ensure opponent has **at least two Prize cards remaining**, since the next action KOs own Dusknoir.
2. Use **Cursed Blast** to put **13 damage counters (130)** on the source Active Iron Thorns. **Own Dusknoir self-KOs**, awarding opponent **one Prize**. Dusknoir is a non-Rule-Box Stage2: opposing **Initialization** suppresses Rule-Box Pokémon Abilities, **not** Dusknoir Cursed Blast.
3. **Replenish Bench as needed after the self-KO**: play a Basic from hand into its freed slot, or attach a held Telepathic Psychic Energy from hand to the Active Psychic Clefairy as a legal unused Energy attachment; the TPE effect can search up to two Basic Psychic Pokémon from the actual deck to fill available Bench slots. A previously attached Basic Psychic on Clefairy plus this TPE also supplies its printed two-Energy [P][C] attack cost. Recheck that **at least four combined Bench occupants remain at attack time** and that New Moon/Wondrous cost status is live. 
4. Use Clefairy's **printed Full Moon Rondo**. Its damage at four combined Benched Pokémon **after the Dusknoir self-KO and any replenishment** is **20+20×4=100**; Thorns has now taken **130+100=230 damage**, an exact KO for **two opposing Prizes**. No Perfection, GX attack, Stealthy Hood, Item, Supporter or Basic-Pokémon Ability is needed for this same-turn sequence. Opposing **Tool Jammer** attached to Active Iron Thorns disables own Active Pokémon Tools but does **not** prevent printed Full Moon Rondo or non-RuleBox Dusknoir Ability.

This is **a two-Energy finisher**, unlike Mewtwo's printed Miraculous Duo-GX, Latias ex Eon Blade, Galarian Articuno V Psyray or Lunatone Moon Press, each of which needs three Energy to attack. Importantly, it **still works after the original New Moon deck used Horror House-GX** on an earlier turn, so it does not incorrectly spend a second GX attack. Candidate Clefairy ex offers a two-Prize rather than a three-Prize TAG TEAM target, though 190HP makes it vulnerable to a subsequent source Volt Cyclone140 plus other damage/modifiers.

## Bench and Stadium envelope

**Count the Bench after Cursed Blast knocks out Dusknoir**. Four combined Bench positions at the moment of Rondo are attainable with **own four + source zero**, **own three + source one**, or any other combined total ≥4. If Dusknoir was Benched, the action **reduces own Bench occupancy by one**; a pre-Blast total of four becomes three without an intervening new Basic and yields only **Rondo 80 + Blast 130 = 210**, not a knockout of Iron Thorns230. Source ranks6/24/29 are Iron Thorns ex-only with four Thorns ex copies each; a source that stays at **one Active with no Bench** supplies no Bench occupancy, so candidate must have **four own Benched after Dusknoir's self-KO and any replenishment**, not merely four before the self-KO. If only one source Pokémon is in play, KOing the Active Thorns may also terminate the game by eliminating all source Pokémon, but a competent source can Bench a replacement and source positioning must be evaluated.

**Critical source correction:** exact ranks **6, 24, 29 all run one Wondrous Labyrinth Prism Star**. This Stadium adds **one Colorless** to the attacks of non-Fairy Pokémon; Lillie's Clefairy ex is **Psychic-type**, not Fairy-type, and therefore **[P][C] becomes [P][C][C]** when its attack-cost effect applies. The clean two-Energy combo above must **replace Wondrous first**, pay the third Energy, or have a specifically validated protection effect. New Moon **Lunatone+Solrock** protects the player's Pokémon from applicable Stadium effects; whether that protects Clefairy's attack-cost increase in a given native state must be tested with the actual paper ruling and Stadium/Ability activation order rather than guessed.

The **v5 Guzma & Hala CEC193** variant adds a cross-role answer: while Supporters are legal, use G&H to **search Sky Field or Path to the Peak**, play that Stadium to remove Wondrous **before attacking**, and, if two strategically discardable hand cards exist, also search **Telepathic Psychic Energy** to supply the second attack Energy and search Basic Psychic Pokémon to help fill Bench slots. TPE's search effect is a **Special Energy card effect**, so source Initialization does not directly suppress it; the original source Thorns lists do **not** run Temple of Sinnoh, although Enhanced Hammer and Faba can disrupt Special Energy between turns. As always, the card must be actually in deck, Supporter slot free and no other attack-cost effect active. Wondrous's text protects it from being discarded by a played Item/Supporter **effect**, but **playing a new Stadium from hand** normally replaces Wondrous; G&H searches the replacement Stadium and does not directly discard Wondrous itself.

All three source Thorns ranks also carry one **Tool Jammer** and **Megaton Blower** each; source ranks 6 and 29 each carry **one Spiky Energy**, rank24 none. A Spiky Energy on Active Thorns puts **20 counters on Clefairy when Rondo damages Thorns**, but that happens **after** the Rondo damage and cannot prevent the 230 target KO on a clean Clefairy. Other reactive or damage-prevention effects must be handled natively; the original source may still follow with Volt Cyclone, energy denial, Faba or gust. Source rank6/24/29 deckbuilding is highly Energy-denial-oriented, so establishing even two attack Energy under pressure is a nontrivial availability challenge.

## Official Stadium ruling closes the two-Energy path even with Wondrous active

Japanese Pokémon TCG official [Wondrous Labyrinth FAQ search](https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/faq/search.php?freeword=%E3%83%AF%E3%83%B3%E3%83%80%E3%83%BC%E3%83%A9%E3%83%93%E3%83%AA%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B9) lists an exact **Lunatone OBF92 New Moon versus Wondrous Labyrinth** test: with New Moon functioning and **only three Basic Psychic Energy** on Active Lunatone, can it use its normally **[P][C][C] Moon Press** despite the Stadium's extra Colorless tax? **Official answer: yes, it can.** This establishes that the New Moon protection prevents Wondrous's attack-cost increase for a protected Pokémon; the printed New Moon text applies this Stadium-effect protection to **all of your Pokémon**, not just Lunatone. Thus, under the same live Ability protection, **your Psychic-type Clefairy ex** can use Full Moon Rondo at its *printed* **[P][C] two-Energy cost** even while source **Wondrous Labyrinth remains in play**. This is a direct category-level consequence of the official Lunatone ruling and New Moon's global wording, not a native Twinleaf validation of the particular Clefairy attack.

**This is an explicit improvement over the initial conservative analysis.** Do **not** automatically require a replacement Stadium or a third Energy in all three source Iron Thorns matchups merely because Wondrous is active. Check **Solrock CES62 + Lunatone OBF92 both in play, Lunatone New Moon unsuppressed**: source Iron Thorns **Initialization** affects only Rule-Box Pokémon's Abilities and does not itself shut down either of these non-Rule-Box Basic Pokémon. The source's **Tool Jammer** similarly does not disable this non-Tool Ability. If New Moon is **absent, shut off, or either Pokémon KO'd**, Wondrous taxes the non-Fairy Clefairy, and **Guzma & Hala v5 can fetch a replacement Stadium** (or the candidate must fund three Energy). Source Wondrous's Prism Star protection prevents a played Item/Supporter effect from directly discarding it, but does not prevent another Stadium being played as a replacement.

**Essential self-KO occupancy correction:** at the moment Full Moon Rondo attacks, its Bench count must **exclude the Cursed Blast Dusknoir**. For example, start with candidate **Benched Solrock + Lunatone + Dusknoir (3)** and source **one Benched Thorns (1)**. The pre-Blast combined total of four falls to **three after Dusknoir self-KOs**, giving Rondo80 and only 210 total—not a KO. The **winning same-turn ordering** is: use Dusknoir Cursed Blast130 first (freeing its Bench slot), then **attach a still-held Telepathic Psychic Energy from hand to own Active Psychic Clefairy** (assuming it has one prior [P]-providing Energy, the normal attachment remains unused, and TPE effect is live); search **at least one eligible Basic Psychic Pokémon from the actual deck** and Bench it in that now-open slot. With own Solrock+Lunatone+new Basic (3) plus source one Bench, the *post-replenishment* total returns to **four**, Rondo100 and combined 230 KO. If source keeps **zero Bench**, own pre-Blast Solrock+Lunatone+Dusknoir (3) falls to two; TPE must Bench **two** additional Basic Psychic (or one TPE Basic plus another Basic played from hand) to raise own post-KO Bench to four. Solrock and Lunatone themselves protect against Wondrous while they both stay in play. They can be the two searchable Basic Psychic Pokémon if not already deployed, but the **final** post-Blast Bench count—not the count before Blast—must still be at least four. Existing restrictions on normal attachment, Bench occupancy, prior Energy, TPE search eligibility, source disruption, K0/Prized information and Dusknoir evolution still apply.

**Strategic consequence:** the 59-card core itself contains the needed Tool-Jammer-proof, GX-independent answer; the v5 G&H Stadium tutor is a **fallback when New Moon is missing or disabled**, not always a required cost. This narrows the claim of incremental v5 superiority in Iron Thorns and raises the priority of actual Solrock/Lunatone preservation and energy positioning.

# Rank-26 Meloetta chooses the held Cologne without a forced Store action

**Agent 4, 18 October 2026.** Evidence class: source-dealt, printed-60 compiled Twinleaf policy intervention. This establishes one actual bot decision and its Store dispatch. It remains unqualified for game-strength inference.

## Original source-dealt state

The preserved [authenticated first-turn source seed](../aichi-2026/rank26-thorns-authenticated-firstturn-source.md) is `49957955`, with registered rank-26 Meloetta in physical B/1 playing first against rank-6 Iron Thorns in physical A/0. Exact source 60s are mapped by `planAichi(26)`, retaining the disclosed benign print mappings and registered copy counts. The previous experiment [forceHeldCologne](../../simulation/twinleaf/meloetta-rank26-source-cologne-action.cjs) dispatched a printed Cologne directly from hand at the source root, showing Meloetta's Live Debut permission becomes available while Iron Thorns Initialization is suppressed.

The new [automatic policy](../../simulation/twinleaf/rank26-cologne-opener.cjs) makes that decision in the ordinary `SimpleTacticsAi.decodeNextAction` boundary. It requires rank-26 to be the actor on its actual first turn, Meloetta ex to be its Active, Canceling Cologne visibly in its own hand, and the opposing Active to be either Iron Thorns ex or Klefki. It selects a conventional `PlayCardAction` from the **current hand index**, targeting the board. It never reads either deck or hidden Prize cards to choose the action. Other turns and other moves fall through to the original generic bot. The hook always restores the original decoding method after the game.

The opt-in [shared research profile](../../simulation/twinleaf/policy-profiles.cjs) is `rank26-autonomous-cologne-v1`. The [common campaign runner](../../simulation/evaluation/aichi-campaign-runner.cjs) passes the verified opposing rank-26 **source seat** to that profile for both A and B candidate positions, and preserves `rank26ColognePolicy` outcome telemetry. This only applies when selected explicitly; the original policy remains the default. The manual [Aichi source-locked campaign workflow](../../.github/workflows/aichi-source-locked-campaign.yml) exposes the profile as a supported choice.

## Independent compiled validation

[Manual GitHub Actions run 39091532358](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/39091532358), pinned engine `a9905a76a6c4103ba4b6cb9a49e86bad795c5c05`, passed:

- Three pure [eligibility tests](../../tests/rank26-autonomous-cologne.test.cjs), including explicit thrown errors on K0 deck/Prize access, both physical campaign seats and disallowing irrelevant match states.
- A compiled [authentic source-dealt regression](../../simulation/twinleaf/rank26-cologne-autonomous-source-regression.cjs) that runs `runWithResearchProfile` with the original rank-26 and rank-6 mapped 60s, physical seat B, seed 49957955, live first-turn draw log, and an action budget. Its assertions require at least one bot-selected Cologne, the printed `Canceling Cologne ASR` from that hand, opposing Active Iron Thorns ex, and actual root headless dispatch logged in `cardsPlayedBySeat[1]['Canceling Cologne']`. That ledger uses normalized card names, while the policy selection trace preserves the full printing.
- An uploaded `rank26-autonomous-cologne-unqualified` action report generated by the same source-seeded runner.

The initial manual CI attempt [39091352463](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/39091352463) failed because its **test incorrectly indexed** the aggregate headless card ledger by `'Canceling Cologne ASR'`. The headless ledger actually indexes played cards by `card.name`, without printing. The original bot-selected decision assertion succeeded before that mistaken ledger assertion. Fixing the regression's ledger name (without changing the policy) produced the successful second run, and preserved the opportunity to catch dispatch failures through the engine's actual normalized play count.

### Identical-seed control arm, run 39091883991

A further [manual paired-policy run 39091883991](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/39091883991) successfully executed the [same rank-26 source seed under both research policy profiles](../../simulation/twinleaf/rank26-cologne-autonomous-counterfactual.cjs). Both arms use the same full registered 60s and original 49957955 setup seed, with the printed source first-turn slot confirmed by the engine draw log. The generic baseline's first source-player action trace starts with **Lightning Energy**, Hisuian Heavy Ball, then Unown. It **passes on action ordinal 17 without playing its held Canceling Cologne**. The autonomous policy instead plays **Canceling Cologne as its first source-player action (ordinal 12)**, then Lightning Energy, Hisuian Heavy Ball and Unown, and passes on ordinal 18. The headless `cardsPlayedBySeat` ledger confirms real Item dispatch. Each comparison branch is a separate execution; first-turn action divergence may alter later RNG arbitration, so the paired trace is decisive about the initial decision only.

The experiment provides a concrete **baseline-policy omission** in an authentic source deal. It does not prove that consuming the Cologne on that specific turn is game-optimal, and the remaining generic bot still fails to realize the staged five-self-KO Crisis Punch combination. The JSON report is retained as CI artifact `rank26-cologne-autonomous-paired-unqualified`, rather than being promoted to a game result.

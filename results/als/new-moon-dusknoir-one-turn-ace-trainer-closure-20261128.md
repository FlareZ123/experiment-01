## One main-phase and attack, no opponent reply between hits

Relevant unmodified New Moon v3 pieces:

- Stage2 **Dusknoir SFA20** in play, live **Cursed Blast** (once/turn, put **13 damage counters** on an opponent's Pokémon anywhere in play; then self-KO).
- One of **two Mewtwo & Mew-GX UNM71** Active, with `[P][P][P]` attached and live Perfection, and **Trevenant & Dusknoir-GX SMP SM217** on Bench or in discard as a printed attack donor; alternately the TAG TEAM itself may be Active and attack directly with the necessary three Psychic.
- **Two Ace Trainer ROS69** in fixed 60, a Supporter legal only when candidate has **more Prize cards left than opponent**.
- Opposing Active Regidrago VSTAR SIT136 or Mega Absol ex ME1 86 at **280HP** without effective Ability-effect protection, applicable attack damage prevention, HP modifiers, or knockouts from earlier phases. Opposing Ability lock must not suppress Cursed Blast or Perfection.

Candidate turn with both components ready:

1. Before attacking, use live Dusknoir **Cursed Blast** on the **280-HP target**; place **13 damage counters = 130**. **Dusknoir KOs itself; source takes one Prize**, unless the game has ended from the self-KO (e.g., source's final Prize). If source now has fewer Prize cards left, Ace Trainer's prerequisite may become satisfied.
2. If Ace Trainer remains legal and has not been used, play it as the turn's Supporter. It shuffles both hands into their decks; candidate draws **six**, source draws **three**.
3. Attack with **Night Watch 150** from an eligible, paid, unsuppressed Active attacker. The target now has **280 total damage** and is KO'd for **two Prizes** if Regidrago VSTAR, or **three Prizes** if Mega Absol ex. Night Watch also chooses **two random** cards from the source's post-Ace three-card hand and shuffles them away; absent intervening changes, source ends the attack with **one card in hand**.

Unlike a prior-turn Night Watch followed by Dusknoir next turn, **there is no source turn between Cursed Blast and the 150-damage attack**. This prevents ordinary opponent heal/retreat/Field Blower responses *between* the hits, though effects already in place and reactive events still matter. No GX attack is spent on Night Watch, preserving the Miraculous Duo-GX option.

**Main positional distinction:** Dusknoir should ordinarily be on the Bench so its self-KO does not require emergency Active replacement before the planned attack; the paid Mewtwo or Trev/Dusknoir attacker must be available. With only one Dusknoir, Cursed Blast sacrifices that Stage2 immediately, so a v3 Shadow Bind attack cannot occur *after* that Ability. These are alternative branches, not cumulative bonuses.

## Same-turn 330HP three-Prize branch

If the opponent's Active is **Mega Lopunny ex 330HP** (rank15) or **Mega Kangaskhan ex 300HP** (rank18), the candidate can instead:

- Dusknoir Cursed Blast **130** first (and own one-Prize self-KO);
- optionally Ace Trainer if its Prize condition is now satisfied;
- Mewtwo & Mew-GX's printed **Miraculous Duo-GX [P][P][C] 200**, reaching **330** and KOing that 3-Prize target.

The GX attack **consumes GX allowance**, and it **does not** reduce the source's Ace Trainer hand from three to one (unlike Night Watch). The source's Mega may have a Tool such as Stealthy Hood or a defensive effect that prevents Cursed Blast; rank15 also has AOR/BUS lock and can prohibit the Basic Mewtwo attack while BUS is Active. Source rank18 may attack before the candidate assembles the attack. The 200+130 math is not a free turn.

## A third matchup class: printed GX attack under Iron Thorns Initialization

The authenticated **rank 6, 24 and 29** original 60s each contain **four Iron Thorns ex TWM 77 (230 HP)**, with no Stealthy Hood and no Garbodor. Their Active **Initialization** removes Abilities from Pokémon with a Rule Box, except Future Pokémon. It therefore disables **Mewtwo & Mew-GX's Perfection** but **does not disable Dusknoir SFA20's Cursed Blast** (Dusknoir is Stage2 without a Rule Box), nor does it prevent the Active Mewtwo's **printed** attack.

Under the exact stated funding/state prerequisites, candidate can use the **same-turn** sequence: Bench Dusknoir `Cursed Blast 130` on Active Iron Thorns230 (giving the opponent one Prize), then **Mewtwo & Mew-GX's printed Miraculous Duo-GX [P][P][C] 200**, KOing that Iron Thorns for **two opposing Prizes**, without accessing Perfection or any GX/EX donor. Source Iron Thorns can respond with Enhanced Hammer (ranks6 and24 two; rank29 one) to deny Telepathic Psychic Special Energy, Guzma to pick off Mewtwo/Duskull and its own Volt Cyclone140. This same-turn two-piece line is **not** proof of viability: one Dusknoir Stage2 must actually be established, three Energy found on Mewtwo, the GX attack unspent, and both actors' Prize clock respected. Candidate Mewtwo TAG TEAM concedes three Prizes if subsequently KO'd, so the nominal two-for-one exchange can reverse rapidly.

Under source Ability lock this makes a conceptual difference: **do not treat "Perfection suppressed" as equivalent to "Mewtwo has no usable attack"**. A future candidate policy should distinguish printed Miraculous Duo-GX and copied Night Watch at an Active Iron Thorns root, and score the Cursed Blast + printed-GX plan subject to its one-Prize self-KO. No current policy has been admitted to execute this counterline.

## Matchup ceiling cautions

**Garbodor invalidates the Ability leg while Garbotoxin is functioning.** Exact Mega Absol ranks **8, 11, 16, 22** have 2/2/3/2 Garbodor plus 4/3/4/4 Float Stone respectively: nine of ten Mega Absol copies occur in these four decks. The remaining Mega Absol (rank9) is in Electrode tempo and has different threats. The five published Mega Absol source ranks do **not** represent five automatically playable Dusknoir two-piece lines.

**Regidrago copies Covert Flight / other Dragon protection and can play Item-lock Budew.** Covert Flight prevents damage from Basic attackers on a later turn, but a paid Stage2 Shadow Bind can attack where Basic Mewtwo cannot; that is a separate, slower line with its own source response. A live opponent Wobbuffet / Path / Silent Lab or other lock can also alter the intended Perfection and Stage2 availability; evaluate actual current board and Ability interactions, not names alone.

**Late Prize hazard:** if the source has only **one Prize remaining**, choosing Cursed Blast will Knock Out your Stage2 and can end the game **before the planned attack**. If the source has two Prizes left, the self-KO reduces them to one; taking the target's Prizes may still win but the opponent's next-turn outs are different. Do not use a naive local damage priority that fails to check terminal state after each KO.

**Actor decision to implement when permitted:** evaluate the entire sequence `Dusknoir Cursed Blast -> optional Ace Trainer -> funded Night Watch / Miraculous Duo` as a **single contingent plan**, comparing the opponent's immediately terminal Prize clock and the alternative of retaining Dusknoir for Shadow Bind/late Blast. A policy that rates Cursed Blast only as isolated 130 counters can throw away the winning finisher; one that auto-blasts without a funded attack can donate a Prize. This is precisely a realistic-action/connector-contention problem rather than a raw card-count problem.

## Why the second Vileplume changes the matchup

The published rank-15 source has 2 Oddish UNB 5, 2 Gloom UNB 7, 2 Vileplume **AOR 3**, and 1 Vileplume **BUS 6**, plus 2 Stealthy Hood UNB 186. These counts are [machine-audited from the original printing inventory](../aichi-2026/full-decklists.json) and [exact English card records](../../scripts/aichi-printing-mechanics.cjs).

- **AOR Vileplume, Irritating Pollen**: if active on the board with its Ability operating, neither player may play Item cards from hand. It can maintain this lock on the Bench.
- **BUS Vileplume, Disgusting Pollen**: while BUS Vileplume is Active and its Ability operates, the opposing Basic Pokémon cannot attack. It must occupy the Active Spot to project this restriction.
- **Stealthy Hood**: attached to a Vileplume, it prevents effects of the opposing player's Abilities on that Pokémon. Against a Cursed Blast counter line, this can make the relevant Ability placement ineffective, depending on the actual target and other lock effects.

**Conditional joint board:** AOR Vileplume on the Bench plus BUS Vileplume Active removes the usual Item-based answers to BUS's attack restriction. These two Stage 2 Pokémon compete for only two Oddish/Gloom chains, Bench/Active places, early evolution turns and search resources. Their ability to coexist in a real Aichi game remains unmeasured. A successful single AOR line or BUS line can still change the timing of counterplay before any joint board exists.

## What the proposed New Moon candidate can actually do

Its 60 has two **Field Blower**, two **Canceling Cologne**, one **Escape Rope** and one **Counter Catcher**. All six are Items, and therefore fail as responses after an active AOR Item lock is established (unless that lock is first disabled). The candidate has no named Guzma/Boss/Serena Supporter; its documented Supporters are Lillie's Determination and Ace Trainer.

The candidate primarily attacks through Basic Pokémon including Mewtwo & Mew-GX and Gengar & Mimikyu-GX. The BUS Active Ability would prevent Basic attackers from announcing attacks while applicable. **The frozen candidate does retain exactly two Stage 1 Dusclops SFA 19**, whose *Will-O-Wisp* attack deals 50 damage for two Psychic Energy. A clean 140-HP BUS Vileplume would require **three unmodified 50-damage attacks** to Knock Out, ignoring damage modifiers, special conditions, opponent plays and alternate targets. Dusclops also has Cursed Blast (five damage counters, then its own Knock Out), creating a possible counter line with a steep Prize cost and sensitivity to Stealthy Hood.

The joint board is therefore a **serious conditional lock-threat and tempo test**. This is not a proven total lock: Dusclops's printed Stage 1 attack remains an avenue. Whether it can be established, powered, promoted and sustained while rank15 attacks and controls resources is the decisive practical question.

## Official Japanese ruling: Stealthy Hood defeats Disgusting Pollen

**Paper-rule verification, 18 October 2026.** The [Pokémon Card Game official Japanese FAQ](https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/faq/search.php?freeword=%E9%9A%A0%E5%AF%86%E3%83%95%E3%83%BC%E3%83%89&page=2&regulation=all) asks, with the opponent's Active Vileplume applying *にがにがかふん* (Disgusting Pollen), whether one's Basic Pokémon with *隠密フード* (Stealthy Hood) attached may use attacks. Its explicit answer is **「はい、できます。」, “Yes, it can.”** The same ruling also appears under the [Vileplume-specific official FAQ search](https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/faq/search.php?freeword=%E3%83%A9%E3%83%95%E3%83%AC%E3%82%B7%E3%82%A2&regulation_faq_main_item1=all). The [official Japanese card text for this Vileplume](https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/33866) identifies the precise 140-HP Active-only attack prohibition. This confirms the bypass as **a paper rules fact**, stronger than our pinned-engine source inference. It does not establish access, deck legality for an altered 60, attack cost completion or a win.

This ruling makes the target-facing Tool choice important. Hood must protect **the attacking Basic Pokémon**. Simply attaching it to one's own Vileplume would not by itself shield the opponent's attacker. If the opposing AOR Vileplume Item lock is established, Tools are still a distinct card type (Advanced Player's Rulebook §B-02), so a *held Hood* remains playable, absent a separate Tool lock. The proposed New Moon 60 carries **zero copies**, and one Tool maximum per Pokémon causes direct contention with Powerglass and Forest Seal Stone. **A real competitive test requires a newly frozen candidate before results can be compared**.

## Additional conditional counter: a Tool-protected Basic attacker

**Printed-card inference with source-code corroboration; staged engine execution remains pending.** Stealthy Hood UNB 186 is a **Pokémon Tool**, so an operating AOR Item lock does not by itself bar a player from attaching it. Its printed effect protects the attached Pokémon against effects of the opponent's Abilities. BUS Vileplume's Disgusting Pollen Ability applies an attack restriction to the opposing Basic Pokémon. The pinned engine's [BUS Vileplume reducer](https://github.com/twinleafgg/twinleafgg/blob/a9905a76a6c4103ba4b6cb9a49e86bad795c5c05/ptcg-server/src/sets/08-sun-and-moon/set-burning-shadows/vileplume.ts) creates an `EffectOfAbilityEffect` targeted at the Basic attacker; the [Stealthy Hood reducer](https://github.com/twinleafgg/twinleafgg/blob/a9905a76a6c4103ba4b6cb9a49e86bad795c5c05/ptcg-server/src/sets/08-sun-and-moon/set-unbroken-bonds/stealthy-hood.ts) invokes `PREVENT_EFFECTS_OF_OPPONENTS_ABILITIES`, which clears the protected target for this class of effect. Thus Hood protecting the attacking Basic is a plausible bypass even with Item lock established.

This is a **hypothetical altered candidate**. The frozen New Moon Lockbreaker v1 contains **zero Stealthy Hood**. It runs two Powerglass and a Forest Seal Stone, so the single-Tool-per-Pokémon rule makes Hood compete with important attack setup and VSTAR search functions. An already attached Powerglass cannot simply be replaced without a separate removal effect, itself potentially barred by Item lock. The candidate's two Dusclops retain a separate Stage 1 attack escape. The staged Store probe now explicitly attempts to **attach Hood from hand under AOR lock then announce the Basic GX attack**; its success is not yet verified by a completed compiled run. The value of any Hood substitution requires source-dealt setup, card-access opportunity, and real opponent turns.

## Core two-Pokémon conversion

New Moon already has **2 Mewtwo & Mew-GX UNM 71** and **1 Trevenant & Dusknoir-GX SMP SM217**. New v3 adds **1 Dusknoir SFA20** through its existing 2 Duskull / 2 Dusclops chain.

- **Night Watch [P][P][P]** on Trevenant & Dusknoir-GX: 150 attack damage, can also be copied by Perfection while that Basic Ability operates.
- **Miraculous Duo-GX [P][P][C]** on Mewtwo & Mew-GX itself: 200 attack damage, consumes the player's once-per-game GX attack. The extra attached Energy for its optional heal is *not* a requirement to deal 200.
- **Dusknoir Cursed Blast**: put 13 damage counters (130 HP) on one opposing Pokémon, anywhere in play, then KO own Dusknoir; this sacrifices **one of our Prizes to the opponent**. It is an Ability, not damage from an attack.
- **Dusclops Cursed Blast**: 5 counters (50 HP) with its own 1-Prize self-KO; distinct from evolved Dusknoir and requires a *second live Dusclops* if both abilities are used in one turn.

A prior **unreduced attack hit** plus later Cursed Blast can close three-Prize Mega targets, potentially even when the injured Mega has retreated to Bench. The following target HP and counts are exact uploaded English prints joined to the authenticated source rank inventories:

## Exact opponent countermechanism matters more than raw field counts

- **Ranks 8, 11, 16 and 22** are the four **Absol Garbodor** rank variants and run **2, 2, 3 and 2 Garbodor** plus **4, 3, 4 and 4 Float Stone** respectively. A live **Garbotoxin** Ability lock shuts down *both* Perfection on Basic Mewtwo and Dusknoir's Cursed Blast on the Bench, independent of the favorable 280 threshold. These four ranks contain **9 of 10** authenticated Mega Absol copies; **rank 9** (one Absol, Electrode variant) lacks Garbodor. Garbotoxin requires the qualifying Tool attachment and live source; it is not automatically active just because Garbodor appears in the list. Candidate prelock Field Blower or other genuine removal/bypass must be available to restore an Ability line; Item-locked scenarios may block them.
- **Rank 15** carries **two Stealthy Hood**, **two AOR Vileplume** and **one BUS Vileplume**. A Hood-equipped **damaged Mega Lopunny** can be immune to opponent Ability damage-counter placement even if BUS itself was previously the Active attacker; the source can allocate Hood intelligently. Under AOR Item lock, own Field Blower/Cologne cannot be freely played. Stage2 Shadow Bind on BUS is a distinct no-Ability attacking route but does not itself KO Mega Lopunny.
- **Ranks 14, 20 and 32** contain Mega Dragonite370. The 380 package requires **both Duskull physical copies and both Dusclops copies** to be accessible, a legal evolving Dusknoir plus a separate Dusclops, correct Energy/attack setup and two own self-KOs. Rank32 also contains one Hood; ranks14 and20 have Megaton Blower and source Regidrago disruption. A copied Mega Dragonite Ryuno Glide attack or other source reply can KO the candidate before any multi-turn line materializes.
- **Rank 18** contains Mega Kangaskhan300 in a Regidrago deck with Budew; the GX opener must precede the Blast follow-up with a viable target and preserve the attacking Mewtwo, which itself concedes **three Prizes** if KO'd. An aggressive 3-for-1 evaluation that ignores that attacker liability is invalid.
- **Rank 9** has an unprotected-by-Garbotoxin Mega Absol, but its Electrode-GX / other attackers may force a faster Prize exchange than the Dusknoir evolution clock. Source response remains untested.

These qualifications make the attack arithmetic **a reason to run adversarial decision forks, not an assertion that 10 source ranks become favorable**.

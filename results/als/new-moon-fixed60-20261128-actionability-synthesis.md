## Four payoff classes that change the relevant decision

**1. Aichi rank15 BUS+AOR Vileplume:** actual source **AOR Bench Item lock + BUS Active Basic attack lock + Stealthy Hood**, with occasional source Path/SilentLab. v3's *funded Stage2* Shadow Bind150 KOs BUS140 without a selfKO but may be too slow; rank15's Jet Energy into ready Benched Mega Lopunny ex GaleThrust230 KOs 90HP Dusclops before it evolves. v4 Hood on Active Clefairy plus TPE can Rondo140 at **combined six Bench**, but needs **one prior Energy**, unless v9 Dimension Valley live while **own New Moon off**, in which case one TPE suffices. v5 G&H helps fetch Hood/TPE through Item lock; v6 Boss gusts AOR while preserving paid own Active, v7 Guzma gusts AOR and moves a paid Benched attacker Active. **The best arm depends on the real Active/Bench/Tool/Energy frontier, not abstract reachability.**

**2. Regidrago VSTAR280 at nine ranks + Mega Dragonite ex370 at ranks14/20/32:** existing Clefairy **Fairy Zone** grants opposing Dragon Pokémon Psychic ×2 when live. Paid Basic Mewtwo NightWatch150×2=300, Clefairy Rondo140×2=280 at combined six Bench, or Stage2 Dusk Shadow Bind150×2=300 can one-shot VSTAR, **without donating Dusk CursedBlast selfKO**. Seven Regidrago ranks carry Noivern ex CovertFlight donor, whose *active* Basic-attacker damage prevention beats Basic Mewtwo/Clefairy but **not Stage2 Dusknoir**. Existing Latias EonBlade200×2=400 nonGX directly KOs Mega Dragonite370—prior two-Dusk selfKO 380 is dominated when FairyZone works. Ranks12/20/21/27 source Path, ranks13/32 source SilentLab, and rank32 target Hood challenge FairyZone. v8 adds redundant Clefairy; v9 two-Psychic Stage2/Latias option needs **Valley active without own New Moon**.

**3. Iron Thorns ex230 ranks6/24/29 and rank2 Stoutland140:** their lock does not prohibit Clefairy's **printed attack**. Against Thorns Initialization, Stage2 Dusk CursedBlast130 → own one-Prize selfKO **removes one of own Bench count** → refill with a Basic/TPE → paid Clefairy Rondo100 at **post-KO combined Bench≥4** → 230 KO with **no GX**; Tool Jammer on opposing Thorns blocks own Hoodie effects, not printed Rondo. Own working Solrock+Lunatone New Moon protects Clefairy from source Wondrous Labyrinth's additional Colorless attack tax (official Japanese FAQ). Under rank2 Stoutland Sentinel + AOR Item lock, **paid Clefairy Rondo140 at combined Bench≥6** KOs Active Stoutland without any Item/Supporter/GX/Dusk selfKO, and unlocks Supporters; the v5/v6/v7 flex Supporters cannot be played while Sentinel is active.

**4. Absol Garbotoxin ranks8/11/16/22 and Snorlax Block ranks3/7:** the four Garb decks have Tool-bearing Garbodor, whose Garbotoxin suppresses both Mewtwo Perfection and Stage2 Dusk CursedBlast; v4/v5/v8 Hood on Dusk *may* protect Stage2 Ability (direct official named/natively executed verdict pending), and printed Mewtwo MiraculousGX200+130 reaches Mega Absol280 for three opposing Prizes **only if GX allowance still available** (original HorrorHouseGX often spent). v7 Guzma can simultaneously gust a different source target and switch own Active out of Snorlax Block; Boss only gusts Block away and must retreat/switch separately. Opposing Garb sources also run 2 Temple of Sinnoh, denying Psychic from attached TPE; v5 G&H may obtain a replacement Stadium but consumes Supporter+discard2.

## One implementable whole-turn policy rather than more independent effects

The research-only [composed action arbiter](new-moon-whole-turn-action-priority.cjs) with [30 passing synthetic precedence cases](../../tests/2026-11/agent22-newmoon-whole-turn-priority.test.cjs) now composes **seven complementary actor-visible guards** under a **single actor-state fingerprint**:

1. **Already payable no-selfKO FairyZone direct Psychic attack** (including Stage2 through CovertFlight). A win by taking the last Prize or KOing the last Pokémon is preferred; a nonterminal KO no longer displaces a separately certified terminal line.
2. **Ability-only terminal Dusk CursedBlast130:** close the last Prize *or* KO the opponent's **only remaining Pokémon** (actual empty Bench, exact Active target), while own Active survives and the Dusk self-KO cannot concede the opponent's final Prize. Neither is just a generic damage turn.
3. **New counter-gust terminal**: from a tied 3–3 Prize state, a Benched Dusk CursedBlast130 hits the opponent's 330-HP **Benched Mega Lopunny ex**. The Dusk self-KO changes Prizes to own 3 versus opponent 2, **newly enabling Counter Catcher**, which gusts that 200-HP remaining Mega into the Active; a paid Mewtwo & Mew-GX Miraculous Duo-GX 200 then takes three Prizes. This is Item-lock-dependent and requires full effect/turn revalidation. [Exact actor-style continuation](../../simulation/evaluation/2026-11/agent22-newmoon-countercatcher-actor-sequence.cjs) implements both owner prompts and checks actual action-frontier tokens, Prize settlement, unchanged target and paid GX attack; [129 synthetic cases](../../tests/2026-11/agent22-newmoon-countercatcher-actor-sequence.test.cjs) passed after replacing the invented direct Night Watch action with the pinned Perfection Ability and owner ChooseAttackPrompt. The independent [21-case branch gate](new-moon-countercatcher-after-selfko-finisher.cjs) is a conditional proposal, not a native game.
4. **Already payable Dusk CursedBlast130 + exact funded attack** (NightWatch or printed Miraculous): decline if the source has one Prize left or an attack alone already KOs.
5. **CursedBlast + post-selfKO Bench-refill Rondo**, either Dragon280 with FairyZone ×2 at combined Bench≥3 or Thorns230 at combined Bench≥4. The *post-selfKO* Bench count and real native TPE search must be assessed.
6. **Guzma & Hala triple-search** if no tactical finisher is already certified, Supporter is available, two cards are disposable, and Stadium/Tool/Energy axes are live. If Valley is wanted, fail closed on own NewMoon and avoid TPE searches that complete Solrock+Lunatone.

**New material fixed-v3 execution improvement outside the seven abstract guards:** the [combined original-v3 actor worker v2](../../simulation/evaluation/2026-11/agent22-newmoon-v3-countercatcher-worker.cjs) now gives the *no-selfKO* [direct Counter Catcher gust finisher](../strategy/2026-11/agent22-newmoon-direct-countercatcher-gust-20261128.md) **higher priority** than the tied-Prize Dusk self-KO line. From own3/opponent2 Prizes, a damaged Benched Mega Absol280 with 80 counters of damage can be gusted and finished by an already-paid printed MiraculousGX200 to take our last three Prizes **without needing Dusknoir at all**. From own2/opponent1, a damaged Regidrago VSTAR280 with at least 130 prior damage can be gusted, then finished by an actual Perfection Ability → owner ChooseAttackPrompt for eligible Night Watch150; 80 prior damage can suffice for paid printed MiraculousGX200 when GX remains available. Dragonite370 can be gusted at full HP if a live, unsuppressed FairyZone produces Psychic ×2; opponent Tool effects, Item lock and damage uncertainty still matter. Across the 29 authenticated source 60s, 139 named target cards occur across 28 of the 29 published ranks; this is **not** 15 observed matchups or target-state frequency. [82 direct-gust actor checks](../../tests/2026-11/agent22-newmoon-direct-countercatcher-gust.test.cjs) and [28 combined-worker checks](../../tests/2026-11/agent22-newmoon-v3-countercatcher-worker.test.cjs) have run; the [no-import standalone](../../simulation/evaluation/2026-11/agent22-newmoon-v3-countercatcher-worker-standalone.cjs) uses exact original v3 60 hash. The native frontier has no `damageAfterResistance`; the printed-base result is an explicitly **conditional**, narrowly screened projection rather than an engine-confirmed KO. **Zero native games and zero qualified matchup wins** remain.

**Priority semantics:** the composed seven-branch arbiter first selects an actor-certified *terminal win*, preferring a paid non-selfKO terminal when competing wins exist; only among nonterminals does it use the original direct no-selfKO → Blast+attack → post-Blast Rondo → setup ordering. Unknown higher-priority live branches block lower candidates instead of being presumed impossible. Existing observations remain assumptions until a native turn proves each transition.

Each branch is **only a first-action proposal**, not a performed native action. The composer fails closed rather than burying an uncertified higher-priority branch, and requires the native worker to **reobserve all state after each effect, KO, Bench placement, paid attack and search prompt**. It never reads hidden deck/Prize contents from the final 60 and never asserts source-player decisions.


### Gladion Stage2 rescue is clock-sensitive, not a +9pp win probability

Agent1's separate v3g Gladion variant improves a physical Prized-Stage2 resource gate by +9.081806579 percentage points, but an [exact natural-draw calculation](agent22-gladion-natural-turn2-frontier-20261128.md) finds the unassisted **by-own-turn-2 Gladion-visible** subset only **1.474951885% of legal openers** (16.240732% of the physical +9.08pp rescue surface). This is *not* a cap on actual search/draw access or evidence of an attack or win. Rank15's Stoutland Sentinel can deny the Supporter, and establishing a funded Stage2 still consumes evolution turns. Track native Gladion→legal funded ShadowBind before opposing pressure, then the cost of deleting the second Ace; otherwise this alternate is not a reason to revise the live nomination.

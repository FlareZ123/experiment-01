## Four payoff classes that change the relevant decision

**1. Aichi rank15 BUS+AOR Vileplume:** actual source **AOR Bench Item lock + BUS Active Basic attack lock + Stealthy Hood**, with occasional source Path/SilentLab. v3's *funded Stage2* Shadow Bind150 KOs BUS140 without a selfKO but may be too slow; rank15's Jet Energy into ready Benched Mega Lopunny ex GaleThrust230 KOs 90HP Dusclops before it evolves. v4 Hood on Active Clefairy plus TPE can Rondo140 at **combined six Bench**, but needs **one prior Energy**, unless v9 Dimension Valley live while **own New Moon off**, in which case one TPE suffices. v5 G&H helps fetch Hood/TPE through Item lock; v6 Boss gusts AOR while preserving paid own Active, v7 Guzma gusts AOR and moves a paid Benched attacker Active. **The best arm depends on the real Active/Bench/Tool/Energy frontier, not abstract reachability.**

**2. Regidrago VSTAR280 at nine ranks + Mega Dragonite ex370 at ranks14/20/32:** existing Clefairy **Fairy Zone** grants opposing Dragon Pokémon Psychic ×2 when live. Paid Basic Mewtwo NightWatch150×2=300, Clefairy Rondo140×2=280 at combined six Bench, or Stage2 Dusk Shadow Bind150×2=300 can one-shot VSTAR, **without donating Dusk CursedBlast selfKO**. Seven Regidrago ranks carry Noivern ex CovertFlight donor, whose *active* Basic-attacker damage prevention beats Basic Mewtwo/Clefairy but **not Stage2 Dusknoir**. Existing Latias EonBlade200×2=400 nonGX directly KOs Mega Dragonite370—prior two-Dusk selfKO 380 is dominated when FairyZone works. Ranks12/20/21/27 source Path, ranks13/32 source SilentLab, and rank32 target Hood challenge FairyZone. v8 adds redundant Clefairy; v9 two-Psychic Stage2/Latias option needs **Valley active without own New Moon**.

**3. Iron Thorns ex230 ranks6/24/29 and rank2 Stoutland140:** their lock does not prohibit Clefairy's **printed attack**. Against Thorns Initialization, Stage2 Dusk CursedBlast130 → own one-Prize selfKO **removes one of own Bench count** → refill with a Basic/TPE → paid Clefairy Rondo100 at **post-KO combined Bench≥4** → 230 KO with **no GX**; Tool Jammer on opposing Thorns blocks own Hoodie effects, not printed Rondo. Own working Solrock+Lunatone New Moon protects Clefairy from source Wondrous Labyrinth's additional Colorless attack tax (official Japanese FAQ). Under rank2 Stoutland Sentinel + AOR Item lock, **paid Clefairy Rondo140 at combined Bench≥6** KOs Active Stoutland without any Item/Supporter/GX/Dusk selfKO, and unlocks Supporters; the v5/v6/v7 flex Supporters cannot be played while Sentinel is active.

**4. Absol Garbotoxin ranks8/11/16/22 and Snorlax Block ranks3/7:** the four Garb decks have Tool-bearing Garbodor, whose Garbotoxin suppresses both Mewtwo Perfection and Stage2 Dusk CursedBlast; v4/v5/v8 Hood on Dusk *may* protect Stage2 Ability (direct official named/natively executed verdict pending), and printed Mewtwo MiraculousGX200+130 reaches Mega Absol280 for three opposing Prizes **only if GX allowance still available** (original HorrorHouseGX often spent). v7 Guzma can simultaneously gust a different source target and switch own Active out of Snorlax Block; Boss only gusts Block away and must retreat/switch separately. Opposing Garb sources also run 2 Temple of Sinnoh, denying Psychic from attached TPE; v5 G&H may obtain a replacement Stadium but consumes Supporter+discard2.

## One implementable whole-turn policy rather than more independent effects

The research-only [composed action arbiter](new-moon-whole-turn-action-priority.cjs) with [18 passing synthetic precedence cases](../../tests/2026-11/agent22-newmoon-whole-turn-priority.test.cjs) now composes **five already separate actor-visible guards** under a **single actor-state fingerprint**:

1. **Already payable no-selfKO FairyZone direct Psychic attack** (including Stage2 through CovertFlight): use it instead of sacrificing Dusknoir.
2. **Already payable Dusk CursedBlast130 + exact funded attack** (NightWatch or printed Miraculous): decline if the source has only one Prize left or an attack alone already KOs.
3. **CursedBlast + post-selfKO Bench-refill Rondo**, either Dragon280 with FairyZone ×2 at combined Bench≥3 or Thorns230 at combined Bench≥4. The *post-selfKO* Bench count and real native TPE search must be assessed.
4. **Guzma & Hala triple-search** if no tactical finisher is already certified, Supporter can be played, two cards are truly disposable, and the intended Stadium/Tool/Energy roles are still relevant. If Valley is wanted, fail closed on own NewMoon and avoid TPE searches that complete Solrock+Lunatone.

Each branch is **only a first-action proposal**, not a performed native action. The composer fails closed rather than burying an uncertified higher-priority branch, and requires the native worker to **reobserve all state after each effect, KO, Bench placement, paid attack and search prompt**. It never reads hidden deck/Prize contents from the final 60 and never asserts source-player decisions.

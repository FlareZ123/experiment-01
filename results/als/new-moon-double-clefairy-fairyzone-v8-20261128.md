# New Moon v8: a second Clefairy ex is a redundant nine-rank Fairy Zone attacker, not an extra connector

**Agent22 · 28 November 2026.** A new **fixed legal paper Expanded 60** [Dusknoir / Stealthy Hood / double Clefairy v8](new-moon-dusknoir-hood-double-clefairy-v8-60.json) directly responds to the [Fairy Zone 2× rank-wide breakpoint discovery](new-moon-fairy-zone-regidrago-covertflight-dragonite-20261128.md). This is a separate unqualified candidate, not an inferred best list or win rate.

## Precisely one structural change

Starting from established **New Moon Hood/Dusknoir v4**, **-1 Rescue Carrier EVS154 (2→1), +1 Lillie's Clefairy ex JTG56 (1→2)**. All other **59** printed copies stay identical, including 2 Duskull, 2 Dusclops, 1 Dusknoir, 2 Powerglass, 1 Stealthy Hood, 1 Canceling Cologne, 2 Ace Trainer, Solrock CES62, Lunatone OBF92, 3 Basic Psychic and 3 Telepathic Psychic Energy. Totals: **21 Pokémon / 33 Trainers / 6 Energy = 60, 36 distinct exact IDs**, with 18 Basics versus 17 in v4.

The deck is legal under the provided English paper Expanded corpus; neither the two-Clefairy count nor Basic ex rules violate copy limits. One extra two-Prize ex may become a worse opening Active and creates Bench/hand discard pressure, and the second Rescue Carrier's recovery function is lost.

## Actual target-class difference, not simply an extra 2-Prize Bench card

Fairy Zone's text changes opponent Dragon Weakness to **Psychic ×2** whenever Clefairy is in play and its Rule-Box Ability operates. Two Clefairy do **not** multiply ×2 into ×4 or make the effect stack: the value of the second is **surviving a Prized first copy, faster Psychic Basic search, retaining Fairy Zone after a first Clefairy is KO'd, and gaining another printed two-Energy Full Moon Rondo attacker**.

- **Regidrago VSTAR 280** — nine known ranks: own Stage2 Dusknoir **Shadow Bind150×2=300** despite source Regidrago's copied Noivern Covert Flight Basic-only attack prevention (seven source ranks run Noivern), no CursedBlast self-KO. When Covert Flight is absent, one live Clefairy plus **Mewtwo copied NightWatch150×2=300**, **Active Clefairy Rondo≥140×2=280 at six combined Bench**, or **Latias EonBlade200×2=400** also KOs. Dusk active and Clefairy on Bench must both be established/funded; the source is free to KO Clefairy first.
- **Mega Dragonite ex 370** — source ranks14,20,32: existing **Latias ex Eon Blade200×2=400** one-shot for **three opposing Prizes without spending GX or selfKOs** while FairyZone is live. A source copied CovertFlight does not protect Mega Dragonite merely because Regidrago could use it: the protection is on the actual attacker that used it, and Mega Dragonite's own 330 attack is different. Source may instead have Dragonite Active while Regidrago remains Benched, and the Tera/Tool/Path defense picture still matters. Rank32 can protect the chosen Dragon with one Stealthy Hood. The one-card v8 copy improvement is to Fairy Zone's survival/access, **not** a new 400-damage attack in the deck.
- **Iron Thorns ex 230** ranks6/24/29: source Initialization suppresses the Rule-Box Fairy Zone, but **does not stop the printed Clefairy Full Moon Rondo [P][C]**. A second copy increases potential two-Energy attacks through Tool Jammer, especially via original Stage2 Dusknoir CursedBlast130 + Rondo100 at **four combined Bench after the self-KO**, with TPE able to refill the freed spot. Original 1 Clefairy already had this line; redundancy does not solve source Crushing/Enhanced Hammer, Plumeria or other Energy denial.
- **Rank2 Stoutland HP140** under AOR Item + Sentinel Supporter locks: printed Rondo140 at six combined Bench can KO Stoutland without either type of locked card, no Stage2/Dusk needed. Two Clefairy improve recovery and alternative Active positioning, but the deck still needs the attack's two Energy and sufficient actual Bench.
- **Rank15 BUS140 + AOR dual lock**: printed Basic Clefairy **needs the singleton Stealthy Hood attached to itself** to bypass BUS Disgusting Pollen; the extra Clefairy does **not** replace that Hood. v5 Guzma&Hala is a competing arm to tutor Hood non-Item under AOR lock, v6/v7 Boss/Guzma can gust AOR and position attackers. Those three Supporter arms share 59 cards with v8 by replacing v4's same Rescue Carrier slot; compare a **searcher versus two kinds of gust versus second payoff Pokémon**, not just raw opening speeds.

## Exact initial physical availability improvement (not action probability)

A finite-population calculation conditions on a **Basic-valid opening seven**, then deals **six Prizes uniformly from the other 53**. v4 has **17 Basics, 1 Clefairy**; v8 has **18 Basics, 2 Clefairy**:

| Physical gate, no retrieval or in-game draws | v4 one Clefairy | v8 two Clefairy | Difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| At least one Clefairy **outside initial Prizes** | **90.120232254%** | **99.170647392%** | **+9.0504 pp** |
| At least one Clefairy, ≥1 of 2 Duskull, ≥1 of 2 Dusclops and singleton Dusknoir **all outside Prizes** | **79.460990955%** | **87.627018128%** | **+8.1660 pp** |
| At least one Clefairy **in raw first eight physical cards** (seven valid opening plus first normal draw; no other searches/mulligan bonuses) | **14.375346199%** | **26.685229411%** | **+12.3099 pp** |

These rates are **not** the frequency of a legal FairyZone-assisted attack. New Moon already has two **Hisuian Heavy Ball** to recover a Prized Basic, and **Fog Crystal** and **Telepathic Psychic Energy** can search remaining Basics; this may make the second copy less necessary than its raw ~9-point Prize protection suggests. But an AOR Item lock can disable Hisuian Heavy Ball at the exact needed moment, whereas **TPE remains an Energy card effect** that can search an unprized second Clefairy from deck. The original single-Clefairy dependency matters across 9/29 known Regidrago ranks and three 370HP Mega Dragonite side packages; source role multiplicity is not source game probability.

## Choice against other fixed 60 arms

v8 is not demonstrably better than **v5 G&H**, **v6 Boss**, **v7 Guzma**, or retained **v3**. All are different replacements of one or two slots, and each addresses a different real failure mode. The **proposed decisive matched choice** at this point is **v5 triple Hood/TPE/Stadium tutor vs v8 duplicate Clefairy** on authentic rank14 Noivern+Mega Dragonite, rank20 Path+Noivern+Mega, rank32 SilentLab+Hood+Noivern+Mega, and rank15 Vileplume AOR/BUS/Hood. For Regidrago, require opponent policy to see the possibility of **Covert Flight** and target the one-Clefairy bottleneck before attacking; for rank15 require item-locked Hood access. Use actor-visible search/attachment/evolution costs and source physical Prize configurations, not synthetic guaranteed access. Freeze each 60 unchanged against every source and do not change root nominated best until a competitive policy actually performs.

**Status:** 60-card construction and combinatorial opening gates demonstrated; source-dealt full games and qualified matchups **zero**.

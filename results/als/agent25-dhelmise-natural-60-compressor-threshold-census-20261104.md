## Per-arm native HNS observations

The four-donor threshold is measured by the existing native attack reducer HNS audit `perCheckCounts[].fourDonorThreshold`. The numerator is the number of actual attack-resolution checks meeting at least four currently effective discarded HNS Pokémon. These are **repeated checks within a match**, not independent games or a conversion rate. The original baseline had **zero** such threshold-positive checks on every non-exception source arm.

| Original source / candidate seat | Baseline HNS checks, four-donor successes | Owner Compressor HNS checks, four-donor successes | Native outcome of both policies |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Rank 6 / seat 0 | 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 | Untimed engine terminal, 80 roots, no qualification |
| Rank 6 / seat 1 | 6 / 0 | 6 / 0 | 120-root cap |
| Rank 10 / seat 0 | 3 / 0 | **3 / 3** | 120-root cap |
| Rank 10 / seat 1 | unavailable | unavailable | Native settled physical 60-card custody exception |
| Rank 11 / seat 0 | 30 / 0 | **15 / 15** | 120-root cap |
| Rank 11 / seat 1 | 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 | 120-root cap |

Across five nonexception cases, native attack hook checks were **39 baseline**, **24 with donor Compressor policy**. Of those, **zero** baseline checks and **18 policy checks** reached the four-donor threshold. The policy's two positive cases were exact verified `4/4` raw/effective eligible donor count on every positive check. Its rank 10 seat 0 had three successive four-donor-positive checks, and rank 11 seat 0 had 15. All other observed checks remained below threshold. The defensive Ability-correction hook reports zero suppressed donors at these positive check times.

The tradeoff is visible even in these short development samples: the Compressor-policy rank 11 seat0 has fewer attack checks (15 vs 30), and no completed competitive game. The count of threshold-positive attacks is valuable **mechanical evidence** that the candidate-only deck-discard policy can supply the required four HNS Pokémon in actual seeded source-sixty Store games, but the additional 140 damage opportunity has not been shown to improve outcomes against a competent policy or paper tournament clock.

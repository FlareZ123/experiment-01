**Agent22 · 28 November 2026. Evidence:** exact uploaded English legal card printings, original authenticated Aichi 2026 60s, rulebook Weakness/damage calculation and defensive-effect text. **No source-dealt game, qualified win rate, or promoted best deck.** This corrects the prior emphasis on adding Dusknoir self-KO damage solely to reach 280/370HP: those numbers ignored a powerful **already-present** matchup-dependent Ability.

## Exact on-board multiplier

**Lillie's Clefairy ex JTG56 (Psychic Basic, 190HP; one in fixed New Moon v1–v7)** has **Fairy Zone**: opposing **Dragon Pokémon in play now have Psychic Weakness ×2**. A Pokémon with no printed Weakness, such as **Regidrago VSTAR SIT136 (Dragon, 280HP)**, acquires Psychic ×2 while this Ability is functioning. **Mega Dragonite ex ME2.5 152 (Dragon, 370HP, three opposing Prizes)** does too. Clefairy need **not** be Active: Fairy Zone is a passive in-play Ability operating from Bench unless suppressed. It affects the type of *opposing Pokémon*, not a nonexistent Dragon typing on our attackers.

## Why newly added Stage2 Dusknoir is still strategically distinct

**Seven** of the nine authenticated Regidrago VSTAR source ranks **12, 13, 14, 20, 21, 27, 32** have **one Noivern ex PAL153** as a possible **Apex Dragon copied-attack payload**. Its **Covert Flight** reads: during the opponent's next turn, prevent all damage done to this Pokémon by attacks from **Basic Pokémon**. When Regidrago uses Apex Dragon to copy that attack, the resulting effect protects the actual **Regidrago VSTAR** against Basic attackers through the next turn. Thus Fairy Zone's 2× Weakness alone **does not defeat Covert Flight** for our Basic Clefairy, Basic Latias, Basic Mewtwo, or Basic TAG TEAM Trevenant&Dusknoir. They can legally attack but **attack damage becomes zero** under a live Basic-only prevention effect, unless another actual bypass applies.

**Stage2 Dusknoir is not Basic.** Its **Shadow Bind150×2=300** against Regidrago VSTAR280 resolves *through the Basic-only attack damage prevention* if live Fairy Zone is providing Psychic Weakness and no other protection applies. This is a **single, no-self-KO, three-Energy Stage2 attack**; notably it avoids the 130-damage-counter self-KO Prize cost **and** bypasses a live Covert Flight effect that is a direct response to the Basic-attacker Night Watch/Clefairy plan. The original unchanged New Moon v1 has two Dusclops Stage1 with printed Will-O-Wisp50×2=100 against Dragon; **v3–v7's single Stage2 is a genuine new one-attack knockout class** when that source protection is active, not merely a different way to deal 130 later.

Seven source lists including Noivern prove only **availability in the 60**. Noivern may be Prized, in hand, not discarded, or source may choose a different attack for a better board outcome. A source that actually used Covert Flight must be modeled with its turn-specific effect active; the copied attack is not automatically always in effect.

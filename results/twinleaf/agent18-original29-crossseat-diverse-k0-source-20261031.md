Rank1-versus-rank1 is a mirror with two unique seeded orientations, counted separately. The **58** RNG seeds are preregistered SHA256 first big-endian unsigned 32-bit values over:
`agent18-original29-crossseat-sha256-seed-v1|rank=<rank>|orientation=<0|1>`
and validated unique before running. Native pinned engine `a9905a76a6c4103ba4b6cb9a49e86bad795c5c05`, strict prompts, isolated native RNG, post-super alias repair, Supporter correction and 48 native actions per game.

Each of the 58 physical game arms runs once **without** observer and once **with** the original `observeSourceK0` passive observer. The observer never plays an independent alternative.

## Interpretation and next release decision

The previous single-seed [original-29 study](agent18-original29-k0-same-root-source-exposure-20261031.md) had **29** repeated Basic Energy offers entirely on the rank1 reference owner, but only **two** distinct recorded reference opener projections. The **current independent seed+seat schedule** resolves that sampling repetition in the narrower recorded projection: 116 observed roots have 116 distinct owner-local projections across roles. This validates **coverage of measured opening-state configurations**, not complete actor histories, native legality of proposed alternatives or probabilities of game outcomes.

This survey was precommitted and started before the [31 Oct 03:14 Critic release intervention](../../communications/broadcast/20261031T0314Z_critic_opportunity_denominator_and_witness_gate.md). That intervention correctly prioritizes an **actual changed native selection and settled opponent reply** over more offer counts. Further original-source K0 proposal-only campaigns are lower priority. The reusable exact source rank24 Plumeria+Flare and rank29 Plumeria+Fan roots give strong potential choice-witness candidates in the existing **canonical bilateral policy**, where any source/candidate change must retain original physical deal, owner visibility, target acceptance, prompts and opponent continuation.

**Do not count 58 original native baseline games as 58 competitive match trials.** The simulations are 48-action capped original SimpleTacticsAi development controls, not a demonstrated competent adversarial game policy. No changed challenger choices were played. The benchmark still has 29/32 source lists, zero competitive-qualified games and no demonstrated 99.5% win rate.

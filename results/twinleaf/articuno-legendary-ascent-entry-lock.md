## Source and defect hypothesis

The exact Aichi source rank **3 Snorlax Stall** includes **1 Articuno-GX CES 31**. Its Legendary Ascent Ability offers a switch of the just-Benched Articuno-GX with the player's Active Pokémon, followed by optional Water Energy movement. The pinned source file is `ptcg-server/src/sets/08-sun-and-moon/set-celestial-storm/articuno-gx.ts`, and the original reducer calls `IS_ABILITY_BLOCKED` on the `PlayPokemonEffect` for the incoming card.

As with the already reproduced Eldegoss Happy Match issue, that check may occur while the Basic remains in hand. Opposing Iron Thorns ex Initialization, Active Klefki Mischievous Lock and Path to the Peak operate on **in-play** eligible Pokémon; they must suppress the on-Bench Ability at its target destination. By contrast, Silent Lab can suppress the Basic in hand directly. Garbodor BKP with a Tool is another control. The real Store-action experiment **confirmed the divergence**: the raw reducer illegally offers the switch under the in-play-only locks, while the research-owned guard suppresses it.

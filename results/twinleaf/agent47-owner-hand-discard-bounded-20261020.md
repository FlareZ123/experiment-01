## Narrow original-Store decision contract

The adapter operates only when the *live original* Store has an unresolved `ChooseCardsPrompt` addressed to its actual actor, with exact message `CHOOSE_CARD_TO_DISCARD`, a source list containing only original physical cards from `owner.hand` (including temporary subsets that omit the Item itself), exact mandatory minimum/maximum 1 or 2, `allowCancel=false`, and at most 18 eligible physical cards. A fixed valid physical printing-multiset menu is constructed using the **original prompt validator**; the pure selector receives only the detached own-hand observation and legal printing menus. It selects exactly the required minimum. Resolution finds distinct original physical card instances; Store.dispatch remains the final native authority. Other prompts, foreign/hidden zones, voluntary discards and larger choices stay delegated to the generic policy and unqualified.

The default heuristic conserves distinct printings in hand where possible. It is not optimized play, does not value Supporter turns, upcoming opponents, board-dependent Energy, UDP, Regidrago payload, or matchups, and cannot itself be used to claim any stronger deck. An absent registered printing, a hidden opponent hand, or a purely speculative cloned Store is never offered to the pure selector.

# Binary attack-cost satisfaction misses Electro Rain's incremental knockout payoff

**Agent 10 | 29 October 2026 | source rank4/17, actual shared owner actor root.** Demonstrates a bounded attack-conditional state and installs a **disabled-by-default** source-specific policy experiment. No game win rate or full-match competence claim.

## Diagnosis

Both original Aichi rank4 and rank17 decklists use two Alolan Raichu UNM57 and four each Reversal PAL192, Counter CIN100, Unit Energy LPM UPR138. The printed **Electro Rain** attack costs one Lightning and lets the player discard Lightning Energy for 30 damage per provided unit, choosing the same opposing Pokémon repeatedly. Official Japanese Pokémon Q&A [Counter Energy versus Electro Rain](https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/faq/search.php?freeword=%E3%82%A2%E3%83%AD%E3%83%BC%E3%83%A9%E3%83%A9%E3%82%A4%E3%83%81%E3%83%A5%E3%82%A6&regulation_faq_main_item1=all) confirms an eligible one-card Counter supplies two selections. Existing [pinned native staged reproductions](raichu-electro-rain-damage-semantics.md) already demonstrate Reversal triple, Counter double, and Unit single when source conditions hold.

The shared root policy ordinarily selects an attack once its cost is already payable. With `publicContextSpecialEnergy=true` and a printed Alolan Raichu with one Unit LPM attached, it can choose 30-raw-damage Electro Rain **before** checking another source-held Special Energy. On an opponent with 50 remaining printed HP, one held, eligible Reversal could be attached with the normal Energy allowance and boost the same Electro Rain to four Lightning units, **120 raw damage**, crossing the printed KO threshold. There is one straightforward resource/turn choice where access measured solely by attack cost gives the wrong action ranking.

## Exact owner-visible demonstration

- The [pure owner/target test](../../tests/agent10-raichu-damage-breakpoint-before-attack.test.cjs) freezes one Reversal held, Unit already attached, Prize counts 6 remaining for owner and 4 for opponent, public target at 50 remaining HP, global turn 4, an unspent Energy attachment and no public lock. Baseline `chooseActorRootIntent(view,catalog,{publicContextSpecialEnergy:true})` returns `{kind:'attack',attackName:'Electro Rain'}`. With the new opt-in it returns a typed owner-hand `attach-special-energy` targeting the same Active Raichu. Once that card is actually attached and the quota is spent, the next root returns Electro Rain as normal.
- [Bounded alternative selection](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent10-raichu-damage-breakpoint-before-attack.cjs) examines exact original printed Alolan Raichu, owner-held source three Special printings, public Prize counts, verified Rule-Box profile, Temple, owner attack/attachment status, already attached Lightning units, and opponent's public remaining HP. It triggers only when one additional physical Energy crosses at least one public raw-damage KO threshold on a presently payable Electro Rain. It rejects unknown attached Energy, no extra target, used attachment quota and known attack restrictions. It consumes neither opponent concealed cards nor deck or Prizes identity.
- The addition in [shared actor root](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent34-actor-only-root-policy.cjs) is `raichuKoBreakpointBeforeAttack` (environment opt-in `AGENT10_RAICHU_KO_BREAKPOINT_DEV=true`, default false) and requires `publicContextSpecialEnergy=true`. Its provenance version includes the source-specific module version. Existing non-opt-in root semantics remain unchanged.
- The six pure tests also include no-breakpoint deliberate resource hold, Temple shutdown, Prize equalization (Counter/Reversal disabled), Unit LPM eligibility at tie, exact source printing veto, actor-only hidden-card independence and unknown Energy fail-close behavior. [Existing manual field evidence gate run 40957878612](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/40957878612) **passed** after adding the opt-in tests.

## Native Store qualification

One [pinned native paired Stage1 scenario](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent10-raichu-breakpoint-native-action.cjs) is committed and included in the [existing manual-only Electro Rain workflow](../../.github/workflows/aichi-raichu-electro-rain.yml). It preserves a directly staged original Alolan Raichu with initial Unit LPM, four-vs-six publicly known Prizes, a manually constructed opponent Benched VMAX at 60 remaining HP, and held Reversal. The control chooses an already payable 30-damage attack. The treatment obtains the alternative owner intent, dispatches actual Store `PlayCardAction` for Reversal, verifies hand consumption and physically attached Energy, and runs native Electro Rain with the existing research-owned damage classification hook. It should distinguish unchanged 30 damage/no KO from 120 damage/three-Prize KO. A completed natural evolution or a real opponent reply is outside this fixture.

**Failure and retry transparency:** Initial [native run 40958426048](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/40958426048) compiled upstream and passed all prior Raichu stages but failed our added assertion on the **control's second root**: the test accidentally turned the treatment opt-in ON for the control after reading the unchanged hand, correctly producing an extra Reversal intent. This is an experimental **fixture flag error**, not a demonstrated native Store rejection. It was corrected in commit `4a2d49e0477834ee963b2ceb87a5cea0456d06f8` to preserve each arm's flag and a rerun was dispatched. Native success is unverified until this replay completes.

## Limitations and next gate

The proposed choice compares **raw printed damage** and face-up remaining HP. It cannot fully evaluate damage reduction, special protective effects, cumulative counter strategies, alternative attackers/Supporters or the value of holding an Energy. Such state-dependent features require normal gameplay policy. Early copy-level mechanical success only shows feasibility from an injected board. To improve fieldwide research, the next step is to observe whether an unchanged original rank4/17 source naturally assembles the turn, offers both choices to the actor, dispatches the promised physical attachment, resolves a real attack and receives an opponent reply. Compare against no-attachment and unavailable resource controls. Retain failures in the denominator. Currently 29/32 exact field originals, zero competitively qualified games.

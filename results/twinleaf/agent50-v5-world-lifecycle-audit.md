**Construction-order qualification found after dispatch:** pinned `SimpleTacticsAi` constructs its per-instance tactic array in its constructor, while `withStoneStarAlchemy` prepends a tactic constructor to the global definitions only during the wrapper execution. In v5 the new bot was constructed *before* the world-specific wrapper was entered. Thus v5 rigorously isolates Field Blower closure, but does not guarantee the fully equivalent Star Alchemy tactic list. v4 had the opposite limitation: correct tactic construction inside the wrapper, but one closure reused across worlds. The roots may be unaffected by the absent extra tactic, yet absence cannot be assumed. v5 is therefore **suggestive, not a full per-world wrapper-faithful result**. Do not promote its sensitivity to qualified actual-bot information-set behavior.

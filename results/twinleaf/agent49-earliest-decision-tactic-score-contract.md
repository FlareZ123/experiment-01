### Concrete policy-version confound identified

Between [historic successful CI 39027244574](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/39027244574) on 17 October and this run, Agent20 changed [`dde-attach-tactic.cjs`](../../simulation/twinleaf/dde-attach-tactic.cjs) in commit [`52dee3e7254aa6401cd900f25ed5f8f0fbb13cc3`](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/commit/52dee3e7254aa6401cd900f25ed5f8f0fbb13cc3) (17 October 11:55 UTC). The old tactic saved `wanted=player.hand.cards[handIndex]` from **live source State**, then checked its identity against a card in **speculatively cloned** `sim.store.state` after simulated attachment: `location.cards.includes(wanted)`, plus `row.card===wanted`. On stock Simulator, Card identities are source-shared, so that worked. On the source-detached Simulator, cloned Cards are distinct identities, so the old tactic rejected an otherwise valid attachment. Agent20's v4 patch instead takes `selectedEnergy=simulatedPlayer.hand.cards[handIndex]` **inside the clone before simulated dispatch**, comparing clone-local identity throughout. Agent8 separately archived the exact old v3 implementation at [`dde-attach-tactic-v3-archive.cjs`](../../simulation/twinleaf/dde-attach-tactic-v3-archive.cjs), and added an explicitly opt-in `doubleDragonArchiveV3` runner flag.

**Inference:** The source-code diff supplies a strong, concrete explanation for why a full clone-isolation change could have caused the historic DDE skip and Apex fallback. The current compiled v4 agreement strongly supports this, while **causal retrospective reproduction remains pending**. Use a predeclared v3/v4 × stock/detached four-arm factorial on the same current head. This avoids confounding current code drift with the original clone change and will confirm or falsify that proposed mechanism.

### Public physical attack consequences

In all four first changed states, the source's Active Iron Thorns ex had one Double Colorless Energy, its Thunder Mountain Prism Star was in play, and its own Bench was empty. Volt Cyclone did **140** printed damage, and its instruction to move an Energy to a Benched Pokémon could not apply with no own Bench. The supplied rulebook allows an attack's applicable portions to resolve even when another portion cannot be completed. The original native settled outcomes were:

- **1401/B champion:** opposing Active **Dialga-GX** took 140 damage from zero; opponent kept two Bench Pokémon. Follow-up opponent first root on turn 5 attached Psychic Energy. Source eventually won at 94 events.
- **1401/B NewMoon:** opposing **Galarian Articuno V**, already holding 140 damage, was Knocked Out. Two Prize cards were taken by source, opponent promoted **Mewtwo & Mew-GX**, first opposing turn-5 owner root passed; source eventually won at 80.
- **1404/A champion:** opposing **Latias ex**, already holding 140 damage, was Knocked Out. Two Prize cards taken by source; opponent promoted **Tapu Lele-GX**, first opposing turn-5 owner root passed; source eventually won at 68.
- **1404/A NewMoon:** source Volt Cyclone immediately Knocked Out the opponent's final **Duskull**, with zero Bench. Source won at 40 events without another opponent root.

In the selected rollouts, the recorded same-root chooser made **17 total replacements** of first-return Pass with an offered attack, across the four changed games. These changes are dependent consequences of the changed game states, and only the **first native divergence** per pair has clean exact prefix/physical causal matching.

## Strength of the finding, limits, and engineering consequence

The narrow public final-Pokémon exception was too rare to activate on the earlier fresh eight-seed experiment ([negative report](agent7-ranked6-public-terminal-preregistered-null-20261031.md)). This broader active-attack criterion exposes a **systematic rank6 opponent-policy defect**, not merely one cherry-picked terminal turn: the original actor often passed at an authentic fully powered Iron Thorns ex t4 with no Bench while a 140-damage attack had no feasible Energy-movement penalty. Correcting this first action substantially altered the engine's results on **2/8** fresh source physical setups, across both candidate inventories.

That suggests a narrower, potentially more defensible next policy: only when a visible Iron Thorns ex with available Volt Cyclone and **no own Bench** would otherwise Pass, select the payable attack. This keeps the exact effect-cost rationale and avoids the broader assumption that every positive-damage attack is wise in every archetype. Test this narrowing on independent physically frozen seeds before any proposed production adoption.

**Do not use a source-engine win fraction as a competitive win rate.** These are partial decision policies on an incomplete Twinleaf paper Expanded implementation, with `PHYSICAL_INTEGRITY_UNVERIFIED` in all 16 source rows and weak opponent replies. The field still has **29 exact published source lists of 32, missing ranks 23/25/28, zero competitively qualified games and no evidence for 99.5%**.

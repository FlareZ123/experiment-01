## Owner-visible state at the exact first fork: independent compiled replay

[Manual CI 39054696250](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/39054696250) **passed** and reproduced rank2 seat B, seed 20261023, root action68, turn5, with an observer around the actual `SimpleTacticsAi.decodeNextAction` call. The two arms' **entire captured owner-visible and public-state snapshots are byte-for-byte equal**, while v1 chooses Guzma & Hala and v2 chooses Bellelba & Brycen-Man. Artifact `rank2-turn5-owner-visible-fork` ID **12109187347**, ZIP SHA-256 `844777105739a75ff1e0baf6a4cd4c04fe456222a910eb75a71ece41279198e1`, observer [code](../../simulation/twinleaf/rank2-turn5-source-fork-observer.cjs), compiled [test](../../tests/rank2-turn5-source-fork-observer.test.cjs). This is stronger evidence of a real **same-visible-state root decision difference** than the previous filtered meaningful-action-prefix comparison. The observation remains insufficient to certify equality of hypothetical evaluator states, hidden deck order, policy RNG, reducer history or every legal alternative.

The rank2 decision-maker's actual three-card hand immediately before playing a Supporter was `Stoutland BCR`, `Bellelba & Brycen-Man CEC`, `Guzma & Hala CEC`. It had an Active Bunnelby with **zero attached Energy and no Tool**, and a **five-Pokémon Bench**: Pidgey (Capture Energy, Stealthy Hood, 10 damage), Budew, Oddish, Bunnelby and another Oddish. The opponent had an Active Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX with Forest Seal Stone and one Psychic Energy, plus Latias ex, Shadow Rider Calyrex V and Mimikyu on Bench. Rank2 had 41 cards in deck and all six Prizes still face down. The owner-visible discard held Cassius and one Tag Call. Before this decision, both arms recorded identical completed owner-deck search selections at action ordinals 9, 42 and 47, so this first fork occurs **after an actual owner-deck search (K1)**, unlike a hypothetical pre-search K0 decision.

Guzma & Hala's optional mode-one discard-two effect, if selected *in this three-card hand*, would require sacrificing **both Stoutland and Bellelba & Brycen-Man**. Each is a singleton in the registered rank2 source 60. This exposes a concrete, strategically expensive discard opportunity and Supporter-use contention; it may influence the source-aware bot's evaluation of playing G&H at all. Mode zero, a Stadium-only search, remains another legal option. The preceding observation **does not establish which available Supporter line a skilled player ought to choose**, nor the root cause of the choice difference. A matched-seed component ablation isolating the new mode-one prompt resolver is the next falsification step.

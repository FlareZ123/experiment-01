## Interpretation, blockers, and next empirical gate

The exact-source K0 privacy invariant and passive Store-replay noninterference provide meaningful confidence in the **measurement boundary** at these roots. The common same-root collector is still partial. It must handle real card-specific Tool restrictions, Trainer/Supporter contention, temporal public history and later-root follow-through, and must compete actual native-accepted actions under one two-player owner-safe policy. One first-root or single seed cannot estimate matchup win rates. Recovered original source decklists cover only 29 of the 32 benchmark opponents.

The strongest next step is to add genuine typed native Tool dispatch behind a strictly gated two-seat policy experiment, with native Store acceptance, Tool recipient constraints, later first-turn continuation and opposing reply, after doing the necessary card-specific rule checks. Rank2 and rank10 already offer concrete source-positive controls. Full all-opponent matchup evidence remains pending. Do not infer utility from raw access or assume repeated champion-side exposures are statistically independent.

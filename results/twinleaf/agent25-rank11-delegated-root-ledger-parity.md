### Evidence boundary and next discriminating task

The presence of 14 actual opponent actions demonstrates continuation chronology across two observed copies. Their **strategic soundness and causality** remain unexamined. The final opponent in-play and Prize transitions confirm the **pinned Store's public effect** on its chosen line, not independent validation of the entire printed Poltergeist damage formula or every intermediate reducer. Source declarations, the terminal transition and the root ledger all originate within the **same engine process**, so matching them does not prove portable independent replay.

Meaningful next work is (1) replaying the complete original State/action/prompt transitions in a fresh process with independent effect checks, (2) assessing the original and counterfactual legal opponent decisions, and (3) preregistering both-starting-order source seeds for a frozen viable candidate after policy and card-rule qualification.

## Specific policy blind spot

The existing New Moon owner policy `simulation/twinleaf/new-moon-compressor-payload-policy.cjs` only considered Battle Compressor while **Gengar & Mimikyu-GX was absent from both own Bench and discard**. After that donor was already established, the action became categorically unavailable even when **Trevenant & Dusknoir-GX**, the **Night Watch** donor, was still missing, Mewtwo & Mew-GX was Active and an unused Battle Compressor was actually held. This is a concrete limitation of this tactic's proposal gate, not proof that the baseline bot failed a natural matchup. The original `selectPayloads` already preferentially selects available Gengar and Trevenant from a legal owner-deck prompt.

**Opt-in fix:** `allowTrevenantOnly:true` lets the tactic consider a still-missing Trevenant donor as an independent reason for playing the held Compressor. The decision screens only owner public/hand zones, Item-lock state, pending prompts, remaining deck-card count and the explicit turn window. It does not inspect the true identities of face-down own deck or Prizes, and never presumes Trevenant is still in deck. If Trevenant is **held in hand**, **on Bench** or **already in discard**, this second-donor-specific trigger stops. The native item play and owner-deck search must still confirm a legal target. Opportunity cost, discarded Item consumption and potential better tactical actions are not modeled.

The historical default `allowTrevenantOnly:false` is unchanged. The independent headless request switch is `newMoonAttackPayloadCompressorSecondDonorSeat:0|1`; when explicitly requested, it installs the same tactic in that actor's seat with a new named policy suffix `+NewMoonCompressorPayload-SecondDonor-v3@seatN`. The flag is absent from all previous registered arms. No frozen policy or archived 60 was silently modified.

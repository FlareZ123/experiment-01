## Measurement problem

The existing postgame root-action-label ledger omits the physical recipient of an Energy hand attachment. An accepted `ResolvePromptAction` under `AttachEnergyPrompt` can likewise move Energy between real Active/Bench slots without being identified by the original root labels. This is decisive for any supposed A/B noninterference or first-divergent-action analysis. Earlier real [Agent 8 and Agent 50 source-side counterexample](agent8-rank11-source-physical-target-native-counterexample-20261022.md) found a rank-11 Energy destination difference at authenticated game root 49 while the first changed legacy root label appeared only at 144.

## Why this matters to the actual objective

Its historical policy, seed cohort and research code differ from this fresh panel. The present all-Techno-Blast cohort shows why an optimization campaign must **count actual source-owned opportunities** before evaluating payoff or claiming a corrected feature improves win probability.

The current selected-policy question is whether and when a strong common policy chooses Energy Mix on genuine original-source boards, with true paper target legality, real source Energy, printed shuffle, subsequent draw effects and an opponent reply. An appropriate next run should prespecify all source-dealt arms and expose both positive and zero-opportunity cases, with immutable source/candidate inventories, pre-decision physical fingerprints and a same-attack root ledger audit. One could then compare paper-corrected and raw variants while separately reporting the baseline's paper-invalid mechanics. This is a **developmental contrast**, not a recommendation to deploy raw invalid play.

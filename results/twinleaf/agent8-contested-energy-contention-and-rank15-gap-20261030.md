## Native-decision consistency error and correction

`simulation/twinleaf/agent34-actor-only-root-policy.cjs` has an opt-in `contestedEnergyRoot` branch **before** its ordinary Basic Energy placement. `specialFundsImmediateAttack` can then return an `attach-special-energy` intent if a held Special immediately unlocks a printed damaging attack and no held Basic unlocks any printed damaging attack. The old `agent16-owner-priority-contention.cjs` described every `attach-special-energy` as **late order 16**, so an early selected Special hid its real competing normal Basic order-7 action in the diagnostic. This matters whenever a two-player policy compares an Energy attachment and its forgone same-turn alternative.

Revised `agent16-owner-priority-contention.cjs` version `agent16-owner-priority-contention-census-v6-contested-special-implicit-active-equivalence`:

- Re-derives the early Special proposal from the **same detached actor root** with `contestedEnergyRoot` and `specialEnergyRoot` both enabled; exact same physical printing and target are required. If matched, order is 6, and a separate `displaced-basic-energy` contender reconstructs the ordinary Basic choice, including the optional Energy recipient or Basic Energy frontier. Nothing is dispatched by this diagnostic.
- Normalizes an omitted Basic/Special recipient to native root dispatch's **Active/0** default. Without this, the exact same DCE selected explicitly by the early path and implicitly by the later numeric Special probe was recorded as an additional competing Energy action.
- Retains all printed-card, state, target and future-effect caveats; native legality remains `unverified`. A choice has not proved that it should occur.

Evidence: `tests/agent16-owner-priority-contention.test.cjs` now checks real selector composition with an Iron Thorns-like Lightning + DCE held owner view, displaced Basic evidence, flag/printing negatives, hidden-zone invariance and explicit-vs-implicit physical target equivalence. Exact native bilateral counterfactual has **not** been exercised for this diagnostic update.

## New original source full-60 exposure: rank 15, Mega Lopunny ex

Rank 15 **Ryotaro Nozawa, Lopunny Vileplume**, in the authenticated printed `results/aichi-2026/full-decklists.json`, includes two **Grass Energy MEE**, one **Double Colorless Energy SUM**, and two **Mega Lopunny ex PFL**. Printed card database `cards/en/me2.json` for `me2-84` verifies **Gale Thrust** costs `[Colorless]`, dealing `60+` and 170 more after moving from Bench to Active that turn; **Spiky Hopper** costs `[Colorless, Colorless]`, dealing 160 and ignoring effects on opponent Active in its damage calculation.

In a **synthetic, stationary** Mega Lopunny owner root with empty attached Energy, one held Grass, one held DCE and no other restraints, Grass funds the 60 base Gale Thrust, while DCE funds both Gale Thrust and the 160 base Spiky Hopper. The existing conservative `specialFundsImmediateAttack` returns `null` because **some** Basic-funded damaging attack exists, so the actor root selects Grass. This can be the wrong short-horizon attack-damage choice in stationary positions, though **Gale Thrust can exceed Spiky Hopper after a Bench-to-Active move** and other effects/targets/resource needs may reverse it. The owner view does not yet attest all these conditions. The example identifies a missing tactical *damage axis*, not a card-legality error, an automatic replacement policy, or a measured tournament misplay.

`tests/agent8-rank15-mixed-energy-decision-gap.test.cjs` ties this synthetic probe to the exact rank15 source sixty and records the current selector limitation. It is wired into the existing manually dispatched `agent7-canonical-actor-bilateral.yml`; no new workflow was added.

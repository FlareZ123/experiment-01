# Source-independent funded Bench Float Stone rescue: three original source families

**Agent 14 | 4 November 2026 | owner-only opt-in policy development.** This study advances the shared fixed-60 paper Expanded objective by improving the *opponent decision policy* for potential escape from Active traps. It does not nominate or optimize a final deck and has zero competitively qualified games.

## Hypothesis and separation from existing work

The old generic [Agent1 emergency Float Stone root](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent1-owner-float-stone-mobility.cjs) is restricted to an Active with **zero attached Energy**. [Agent31's independently successful rank-1 specialized native rescue](../aichi-2026/agent31-shadow-rider-funded-bench-floatstone-20261103.md) established that an Active VMAX with one Psychic Energy can still pivot to a six-Psychic funded Bench VMAX. Other original opponents such as rank 11 Absol/Garbodor and rank 30 Lillie's Clefairy contain different funded-attacker sequences. A per-printing scripted rescue cannot safely stand in for a generally competent opponent.

**Research change:** [source-independent owner helper](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent14-generic-funded-floatstone-frontier.cjs) proposes a *two-root* held Float Stone attachment then free Retreat when a partially funded, currently nonattacking Active has a Benched attacker with at least 80 nominal projected printed damage and an independently checked payable visible attack cost. It preserves an already payable useful Active attack, occupied Tool slot, exhausted retreat, Tool play lock, public Item/Tool restrictions, Paralyzed/Asleep status, active Tool Jammer on the opponent, Jamming Tower, and known public lock effects. All data come from owner hand or public board, no opponent concealed cards, registered opponent rank, future seed or Prize card identities.

Native-neutral outputs reuse the pre-existing `attach-emergency-floatstone` and `retreat-emergency-floatstone` intents; existing genuine Store handlers are responsible for physical payment, target and attack. This is an opportunity heuristic, not a claim of correct optimal choice against all Supporter, attached Energy, Prize, Bench, Stadium or opponent tactics.

Version **v2** uses the existing public attached-Energy cost prover `publicSpecialAttackPayable` and reuses Agent17's printed additive **all-own-Basic-Energy damage formula parser**. The latter permits the rank-1 Max Geist 10+30 per Basic Psychic Energy public case without specializing on its card name. Other recognized targets include fixed 200 printed rank11 Mega Absol Claw of Darkness and rank30 Clefairy's board-dependent Full Moon Rondo. Unsupported variable damage and any ambiguously attached Special Energy in the Basic-only additive branch are excluded, as are detailed KO and Weakness guarantees. This version does not replace the Agent31 specialized module or claim any measured game performance change.

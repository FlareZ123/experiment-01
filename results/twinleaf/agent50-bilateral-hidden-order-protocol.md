# Agent 50: authentic bilateral development-root hidden-order sensitivity

**Registration: 19 October 2026, before executing this new audit.**
**Status: implementation and synthetic regressions committed; no compiled four-arm result claimed here.**

## Decision this is meant to inform

Agent 41 owns the separately preregistered, prospective rank-6 Iron Thorns / rank-11 Absol-Garbodor common-candidate-policy evaluation. The unchanged champion-reference 60 and New Moon Lockbreaker v1 60 are the experimental inventories. The existing common v0 decision wrapper applies the same resource tactics in each candidate, but its generic Twinleaf bot still has access to private physical deck order inside speculative scoring. Agent 50's earlier rank-10 source-confirmed probes demonstrated conditional action-choice differences under an unseen deck permutation; those observations do not establish that a difference occurs at the decisive rank-6/rank-11 root.

A failed information boundary could invalidate a nominally matched deck-inventory comparison even when the policies are textually identical. The immediate falsifiable question is narrower: **Does the actual shared bilateral development runner's candidate root choice change when an independent freshly reconstructed policy is evaluated on two states with the same visible board/hand and deck multiset but reversed remaining physical deck order?**

## Fixed experimental unit and target

Use Agent 41's already-inspected historical development inputs from v1 registration (never relabel them as fresh v2 pairs):

| Original stream | Opponent | Candidate physical seat | Single target root |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| \`6:0:A\` | Exact rank 6 Iron Thorns | A, seat 0 | Candidate global turn 3, after possible source turn-2 attack |
| \`11:2:B\` | Exact rank 11 Absol/Garbodor | B, seat 1 | Candidate global turn 2, after possible source turn-1 lock |

Each has two fixed candidate inventory arms. **Four arms, two executions per arm:** one unmodified genuine game and one identical genuine game with a read-only-on-live-state detached diagnostic at the first candidate root matching the target turn and seat. The development seeds, printed/engine 60s, source line policies, 260-action cap, pinned engine and common resource-policy wrapper all come from the repository's existing Agent 41 runner. Record source-pressure occurrence separately: the candidate decision is an unconditional turn-root target and may exist without that pressure.

The probe reuses \`withRootHiddenOrderProbe\` and \`compareHiddenDeckOrders\`. Two independently detached whole-State copies are evaluated with freshly created tactic bots under equal independent pseudorandom streams; only the acting candidate's remaining deck physical order is reversed. Source State graph remains unchanged, checked in the established observer. The real bot subsequently selects its original live action normally.

**Fail-closed control:** compare actual source physical deal digest, first-player draw verification, root Store action ledger digest, first owner hand, actions/turn count, terminal reason, winner, prizes, declared attacks and settled attack count between the unmodified and instrumented games. A probe-world difference cannot be reported as actual decision sensitivity if live gameplay was perturbed or the root baseline fresh-bot action differs from the actual live bot action. Reconstructing the bot may lose legitimate internal history; this is tracked explicitly.

The recorded classes are: \`hidden-order-action-sensitivity-observed\`, \`hidden-order-score-sensitivity-only\`, \`no-sensitivity-on-tested-root\`, \`no-matching-candidate-root\`, \`fresh-bot-baseline-or-live-state-mismatch\`, \`probe-changed-or-unverified-live-playback\`, \`ambiguous-multiple-root-probes\`, or explicit control/probe errors. No failed or censored arm is silently removed.

## What the inference excludes

1. Real game players may legitimately know parts of their deck and Prize cards after searching. The historic root probes do **not** certify K0/K1 status or an absence of known top-prefix order. A permutation that violates a legitimate observation is an *exploratory information-boundary challenge*, not demonstrated cheating by itself.
2. Fresh detached clones may evaluate a different internal bot history. \`baselineActionMatchedActual\` is necessary for interpretation; one matched action does not prove policy-history equivalence.
3. A diagnostic sensitivity of a hypothetical action score does not imply a changed live Store action, a successful printed attack, a tournament clock outcome or a true win-rate difference.
4. The unchanged default bot also evaluates unseen opponent hand and Prize identities. This test addresses only the acting owner's deck-order dimension, does not make the common policy information-set-safe, and does not repair Twinleaf's game semantics or 25-minute paper match clock.
5. Four previously selected developmental roots cannot estimate inventory causal effects, 32-opponent coverage, or the 99.5% target. These rows must never be inserted into a prospective v2 denominator.

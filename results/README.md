# Aichi Open League Japanese Extra: fixed-60 decision board

**Decision status, 7 November 2026:** **No final fixed 60-card deck is selected; zero competitively qualified games; no demonstrated 99.5% win rate.** The Aichi Open League 2026 Open division is officially Japanese Extra (BW onward), with 60 cards, six Prizes, and Japanese-language cards only. The objective is one unchanged, legal, playable deck under that event profile against the complete Top 32; individual card and historical-printing legality remain unverified. See the [official format and event-rules source map](aichi-2026/agent12-official-format-rules-and-mulligan-20261107.md).

The report does not certify an official win rate, general paper-rule equivalence or policy quality. Do not re-open the already-closed narrow attack-settlement evidence question.

**What still gates prospective competitive evidence:**
1. **Actual hard-pressure and response, with applicability separated:** authenticate genuinely functioning source-first pressure independently of whether a particular candidate presents a vulnerable Power. Record candidate-specific affected-target, exempt-target, no-target, and unverified cases, followed by owner-legal responses and real source continuations where possible. Retain failures and naturally unaffected candidates in the unconditional comparison. The current rank-6 *both-candidates-positive-suppression* test is a demanding **mechanical stress diagnostic**, not a universal candidate-comparison prerequisite. See [Agent 16's executed counterexample](aichi-2026/agent16-rank6-applicability-vs-exposure-gate.md) and the [20 October Critic intervention](../communications/broadcast/20261020T0458Z_critic_lock_applicability_and_delivery_discipline.md).
2. **Common decision competence and information boundary:** develop an actor-observation-safe, opportunity-cost-aware setup sequencer applied under one explicitly frozen common candidate policy. The [owner K0/K1 stale-plan finding](aichi-2026/rank24-native-k1-owner-search-policy-failure.md), [two native K1 failure classes](../results/als/agent30-k1-owned-search-replanning-synthesis.md), [rank-26 held-Item pass census](aichi-2026/rank26-held-item-pass-census.md), and [K0 discard frontier](twinleaf/agent47-k0-discard-value-frontier.md) are regression cases and candidate ingredients, **not separate policy-success claims**. Verify opponent-hidden-state parity along the **full integrated decision path**, including delegated attacks.
3. **Prospective source-frozen pairs:** only after the previous gates, pre-register a small fresh, both-actual-start, failure-inclusive two-opponent comparison. Feed the **same** archive to the [strict bilateral witness gate](aichi-2026/agent33-v2-bilateral-evidence-gate.md) and [unconditional paired census](aichi-2026/agent2-v2-unconditional-paired-census.md). Retain engine caps, rejects, source-deal fingerprints, policy versions, opponent reactions, print identities, simulator limitations and tournament-clock caveats.
4. **Full-field transfer:** after credible rank-6/rank-11 evidence, include the [five unrepresented published archetypes and intra-archetype variation](aichi-2026/agent21-field-diversity-coverage.md), and continue provenance research for ranks 23, 25 and 28. Do not invent unavailable lists.

The owner of a **read-only attack audit** is distinct from the owner who integrates and validates the **complete two-player common decision policy**. A task announcement alone is not an integrated, passing gate.

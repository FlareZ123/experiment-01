## Why the qualification gate remains closed

**Opponent decision policy.** The [preregistered fresh 32-attempt rank-11 A/B](aichi-2026/agent50-preregistered-rank11-symmetric-known-dead-20261101.md) held original source and candidate decks and physical deals constant while improving one symmetric attack selector. Original-source engine wins changed **5 to 10**, candidate wins **7 to 4**, and action caps **4 to 2**. This demonstrates the fragility of interpreting native winners under weak opponent decisions. A [native rank-11 read-only root audit](aichi-2026/agent43-rank11-held-guzma-supporter-root-bottleneck-20261101.md) observed **zero accepted Supporters** despite 322 repeated owner-root observations holding Guzma; those observations are correlated and do not certify a legal gust opportunity. The [rank-6 K1 continuity counterfactual](aichi-2026/agent6-cross-turn-owner-k1-native-speed-recovery-20261101.md) separately demonstrated that retaining earlier legitimate owner deck-search information can alter a settled source attack. Other control archetypes require their own real decision policies, including [recurring mill and Peonia Prize selection](aichi-2026/agent39-five-recursive-control-mill-source-matrix-20261101.md).

**Paper action and effect fidelity.** Real Store tests have accepted a [zero-payment original Ultra Ball search](aichi-2026/agent4-original-rank14-ultraball-free-search-native-20261101.md), while the strict generic validator can reject a [paper-valid Roxie/Koffing payment](aichi-2026/agent4-original-crossrank-strict-ultraball-roxie-native-20261101.md). The [shared rank-31 prompt portfolio](aichi-2026/agent46-rank31-roxie-unified-payment-paper-portfolio-native-20261101.md) has passed bounded prepared-board regressions, with earlier root-legality coverage still missing. [Runtime direct-lookup versus enumeration](aichi-2026/agent50-original-source-dynamic-attack-census-20261101.md) warns against treating catalog identity as executable semantics. **The earlier rank-19 Bellowing Thunder zero-payment failure was retracted**: its staged fixture omitted attached Energy from the native aggregate card list; an independent, complete-zone two-seat native control confirmed a positive payment and damage pathway ([fixture correction](aichi-2026/agent50-rank19-fixture-correction.md)). This bounded correction does not certify general source-policy or game-level paper fidelity. The frozen baseline has [nine candidate Night Watch concealed-hand prompt exposures](aichi-2026/agent17-v2-fixed-deck-engine-winner-contrast-20261101.md) in its only two New Moon rank-11 engine-terminal wins, without an independent printed-randomness settlement witness. All these outcomes remain developmental.

**Representative coverage and game conditions.** The [frozen 32-arm development comparison](aichi-2026/agent41-frozen-bilateral-v2-32-original-source-energy-20261101.md) observed 19 engine terminals and 13 action caps, with reproducible physical deals and a meaningful source Energy-policy correction. Its evaluated opponents were ranks 6 and 11. The [nine-rank expansion registration](aichi-2026/registrations/agent41-nine-ready-ranks-18seeds-20261101.json) covers only **five of the 14** distinct archetypes represented among authenticated original lists; [nine archetypes still need working original-source drivers](../communications/broadcast/20261101T1803Z_critic_sample_coverage_and_nightwatch_decision_veto.md). The separate [nine-original-archetype 36-arm native readiness screen](aichi-2026/agent25-nine-archetypes-native-original-36arm-20261101.md) returned **17 engine terminals, 13 caps, six native errors and zero competitively qualified games**. Characteristic source lines remain unobserved in several ranks, and **three of 15** comparable cross-candidate same-seed groups had different original-source physical deals. The [2 November Critic source-60 admission and pairing rule](../communications/broadcast/20261102T0228Z_critic_original60_driver_admission_and_pairing_gate.md) requires source-list-level competence and exact physical-deal witnesses before claiming same-deal contrasts. The official **25-minute paper match clock** and end-of-time adjudication are absent from untimed native engine winners. Missing opponent 60s remain a separate full-field blocker.

## One executable release path

The [canonical bilateral development runner](../simulation/evaluation/agent41-bilateral-v2-development.cjs), its [common actor policy](../simulation/evaluation/agent41-bilateral-common-policy.cjs), and [source-controlled frozen v2 profile](../simulation/evaluation/agent41-frozen-bilateral-development-v2.json) are the shared integration targets. Existing manually dispatched CI should be reused.

1. **Certify actual decisions, in both seats.** For each encountered chain, retain owner-visible proposed root, original Store-accepted root, all selected prompts, physical payment and effect, KO/Prize changes and meaningful opponent continuation. Record **root, prompt, payment, effect and randomness** paper dispositions separately as verified, invalid, unverified or not encountered. An unverified phase vetoes whole-game paper qualification. Use the [latest Critic action-chain release contract](../communications/broadcast/20261101T2242Z_critic_end_to_end_action_fidelity_gate.md).
2. **Release one frozen two-player policy.** Measure legally reachable setup, Supporter, attack, mobility and control actions against what the same owner could actually know. Preserve owner-only across-turn search knowledge where legitimately acquired, with event provenance and no cross-player leakage. Retain the first natural-action and source-continuation witnesses, including when they overturn favored engine outcomes.
3. **Run a separate, preregistered deck-decision experiment.** Expand to the nine absent authenticated archetypes, then all 29 exact original lists and ultimately the entire 32. Preserve both actual starting seats, physical-deal IDs, candidate/reference 60 hashes, policy+vendor+hook hashes, all attempted games, caps, exceptions, source-policy competence and candidate-minus-reference contrasts. Do not turn repeated within-game roots into independent samples.
4. **Qualify the real game and report honestly.** A game-level paper-rule audit, owner-information safety, meaningful opponent decisions and tournament clock model must all pass before promoting a native terminal. Report every matchup and uncertainty. Treat a 99.5% figure strictly as an aspiration until independently supported.

**Publishing rule:** Keep this entry point centered on the fixed-deck decision and the current blocking predicates. Put detailed card tests, dated experimental narratives and historical observations in their own reports or the [preserved previous full index](research-index-20261101-before-critic-decision-reindex.md). New isolated hooks or standalone CI workflows need a named, tested integration path into the frozen decision profile. Distinctive positive and negative experimental evidence should be retained.

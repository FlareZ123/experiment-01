**Decision status, 6 November 2026:** **No selected final 60, zero competitively qualified games, and no demonstrated 99.5% game win rate.** The v8 bilateral common policy is now the selected developmental baseline for continued research only; competitive admission remains FAIL. The Aichi Open League 2026 Top 32 benchmark has **29 authenticated 60-card name/count inventories**; **ranks 23, 25 and 28 are missing**. All 29 authenticated opponents remain unqualified as competitive automated players. The goal is one unchanged, legal, playable paper Expanded deck against all 32 originals.

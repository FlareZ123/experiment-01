## Next decision-changing deliverable

Extend the **first pinned, genuinely selected two-seat common-policy native canary** toward complete source-paper and decision-chain certification through the [canonical bilateral runner](../simulation/evaluation/agent41-bilateral-v2-development.cjs), with its [common policy module](../simulation/evaluation/agent41-bilateral-common-policy.cjs) and explicit selected profile. The [frozen v2 manifest](../simulation/evaluation/agent41-frozen-bilateral-development-v2.json) is a historical developmental control, not a certified release.

The new prospective experiment must record exact source and candidate 60 identities, vendor and hook versions, immutable selected code/profile, actual start seats, original-source physical-deal fingerprints, every attempted arm, errors, caps and engine terminals. Admit a source's characteristic play only after **native-selected root, all prompts, payment, effect, randomness, KO/Prize outcomes and an opponent continuation** are witnessed against its original printing. Separately audit owner-only information, paper legality, opponent decision competence and the **25-minute paper clock**. Missing, rejected or unverified phases veto whole-game qualification. The [end-to-end action-chain gate](../communications/broadcast/20261101T2242Z_critic_end_to_end_action_fidelity_gate.md) and [v3 integration handoff](../communications/broadcast/20261102T0338Z_critic_release_ownership_handoff_no_stale_mailbox.md) specify detailed acceptance criteria.

Only after an integrated source policy is stable should unselected games support a **candidate-minus-reference** decision across all recovered original sixties, then any recovered ranks 23/25/28. Preserve both seats and matchup uncertainty. Never count an engine terminal as a tournament game win without the documented qualifications.

## Why more field panels are not yet the decision bottleneck

Native policy choices already reversed some winner outcomes on matched original source deals. For example, redirecting the same source DCE cards onto a real Mew attacker generated 11 settled source attacks versus zero in another policy arm, flipping one native terminal. [Prospective source-policy report](methodology/2026-11/agent8-mew-source-dce-attack-prize-conversion-v6-native-20261109.md). The Hood comparison's 14 unresolved source-matched groups and three unmatched deals prohibit treating its 6:3 completed reversals as a result for the whole field. [Sharp paired comparison](methodology/2026-11/agent21-hood-full29-sharp-censor-decision-20261109.md). **A further prospectively frozen original Mew opening with genuinely accepted source Trainers' Mail reversed a native source loss to a win against both unchanged Hood v1 and champion, one correlated source opening across two contenders.** [Source policy competence evidence](methodology/2026-11/agent38-rank5-source-mail-transfer-hood-champion-20261109.md).

**New 10 November owner-Energy counterexample:** an unmodified Hood Shield sixty changed a native *loss into a win* when the last held Psychic manually funded its Active against original rank7 Stall, while the same blanket priority changed a native *win into a loss* against original rank1 Shadow Rider mirror. Neither public-board mirror veto nor a public turn25 threshold improved observed native winners in fresh independent 32-pair panels. This is concrete evidence against promoting static root preferences without evaluating the excluded Underworld Door/Active attack alternative. [Cross-opponent native report](methodology/2026-11/agent26-three-source-hood-priority-counterexample-20261110.md) · [action-resource frontier synthesis](strategy/2026-11/agent26-energy-root-arbitration-from-opposing-native-counterexamples.md). **No qualified paper game or fixed-60 promotion follows.**

**Decision rule:** new large all-29 panels should follow a verified, sufficiently competent shared two-player policy and an explicit censor/paired-deal analysis. Before that, prioritize a traceable integrated end-to-end run and the specific original-source failures it exposes. Keep previously collected negative results and full raw tapes.

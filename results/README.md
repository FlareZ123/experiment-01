## Decision still required: what does “against the Aichi Top 32” mean?

The Aichi event used Japanese Extra. The project objective requires an Expanded candidate, but does not unambiguously specify whether every unchanged opponent list must itself be international-Expanded-legal. The owner must choose between:

1. **Strict Expanded vs Expanded:** only unchanged opponent lists admissible under international Expanded count as legal opponents. Five authenticated lists are incompatible unchanged; three exact lists are missing.
2. **Candidate-only Expanded vs unchanged Japanese Extra opponents:** a custom historical cross-format challenge. This preserves the original Aichi inventories, including Japanese-only promotional identities, but is not an Expanded-vs-Expanded metagame result or sanctioned Expanded tournament.

Do not silently normalize, substitute, exclude, or relabel the five incompatible lists. Preserve both estimands until the owner decides. ### What existing comparisons establish

Historical native comparisons are exploratory diagnostics, not competitive qualification. The all-29 cross-format panel included five now-known Expanded-incompatible lists and omitted the three unavailable ranks. In the post-hoc 24-rank identity-screened split, 45 matched pairs (34 terminal in both arms) produced 27–27 native wins with sharp difference bounds **[-7, +11]**. Across 55 matched source-deal groups, only 41 completed in both arms; completed reversals favored Hood 6–3, but the exact censored win-count difference remains **[-9, +13]**. Ordering is unresolved.

## Next decision-changing deliverable

Preserve the **52-attempt frozen symmetric selected joint-v2 release** as the newest released integration control, including its negative rank-11 Claw trajectory and the rank-6 `UNKNOWN_POWER` censored pair. The historical 92-option joint-v1 release and earlier rank-5 positive transport tests remain useful, with their distinct real source deals and policy versions explicitly separated. A broad native replay is premature while core sources still fail to execute characteristic early paid attacks and a consequential opponent response. The [Critic selected-profile transport direction](../communications/broadcast/20261103T0440Z_critic_transport_gate_closed_release_steward_and_metric_correction.md) and [new v2 release evidence](aichi-2026/agent23-selected-joint-v2-symmetric-vip-52-native-20261103.md) identify the live decision gate.

The next release should be a **small, preregistered, genuinely source-competent original-source test with clearly defined comparison estimands**. For a **same-fixed-60 policy intervention**, require actual first-source-deal and starter parity across control and selected modes. For a **comparison of distinct fixed 60s**, retain each deck's naturally realized candidate mulligans and original-source compensation draws in the unconditional game population. Those candidate-mediated setup changes can legitimately differ even under the same seed. Treat exact cross-candidate source-deal parity as an explicitly **stratified tactical diagnostic**; conditioning the entire candidate win-rate denominator on that parity could bias the strength estimate. The [original 52-run rank6/5 source forensic audit](aichi-2026/agent48-frozen-selected-v2-original-archive-posthoc-20261103.md) and [exact Basic-count causal-methodology note](statistics/agent48-deck-choice-mulligan-source-pair-causality-20261103.md) demonstrate the difference. Recover genuine first-turn root/payment/attack witnesses without trusting seed equality as physical identity. Preserve every negative, error and action-cap arm; avoid selecting only favorable seed streams. Independently verify each source's native-selected root, target or copy prompt, complete payment, printed effect, Prize consequences and meaningful opposing continuation, with source and candidate action decisions tested for paper legality and owner-only information. [The native end marker audit](../results/methodology/agent48-native-terminal-marker-20261103.md) and [native receipt integrity audit](../results/methodology/agent48-native-root-receipt-conservation-20261103.md) provide narrower accounting checks, without certifying whole games.

Separately validate the actual **25-minute paper clock**, opponent decision competence, and missing original rank 23/25/28 inventories before any field-wide win-rate claim. Only independently verified original 60-card matchups played under a single unchanged contender can support a candidate-minus-reference decision. Do not promote engine terminal counts into tournament wins.

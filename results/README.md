## Decision-relevant evidence and current blockers

- **New, separate source-attack evidence (5 November): v10 achieved bounded native payoff and response, while the selected shared v4 remains attack-free.** A [compiled original-rank-6 v10 control/treatment](methodology/agent36-v10-rank6-settled-volt-reply-20261105.md) authenticated two physically paid and settled Volt Cyclone attacks dealing **140 damage each**, with mandatory Double Colorless Energy transferred to the source Bench, **zero Prizes**, and an actual same-game opponent Psychic attachment followed by Pass. These are targeted Pass-replacement interventions under a **separate retrospective policy**, with six other censored/error arms preserved. The opponent's immediate action types were identical in each matched no-attack control. The [independent archive source-return audit](methodology/agent36-v10-source-return-after-dce-tempo-20261105.md) found no repeated source attack within the 48-root cap and documents subsequent Pass or censored Energy play. V10 demonstrates narrow developmental P3/P4 physical events; it does **not** establish adversarial opponent competence, a beneficial strategic choice, a full game, or qualification of steward-selected v4. The [new Critic integration directive](../communications/broadcast/2026-11/20261105T213417Z_critic_v10_payoff_vs_selected_policy_and_adversarial_value.md) asks for a prospectively frozen common-policy continuation with real counterplay and source next-turn evaluation.

- **An accountable, steward-selected two-seat developmental v4 now exists and has actually run, although its competitive acceptance gates still fail.** Agent36's [selected v4 executing entry](../simulation/evaluation/agent36-issue25-steward-selected-v4.cjs) combines both-seat Special Energy/root policy, physical target observation, source-stamped printed-attack prompts and the owner Capture Energy deck-search continuation. The [compiled, archived original rank6/rank11 eight-arm run](methodology/agent36-issue25-steward-v4-native-20261105.md) has **six 48-root censored attempts and two engine errors**, with all registered arms retained. One source-owned Capture Energy attachment physically moved Iron Thorns ex from the owner's deck to their Bench, but no settled source attack plus meaningful opponent reply followed before the cap; no competitive game finished. The [controlled v5 Prize/promotion prompt treatment](methodology/agent36-issue25-steward-v5-prize-promotion-20261105.md) used identical initial deals for all eight same-contender arms and rescued one of the two aborts, yielding **seven 48-root caps and one error**, still zero qualified games. **V4 is the named selected developmental baseline; v5 is separately tested, not a final selected competitive policy.** Historical v0/v1/v3 and selected-joint-v2 archives remain developmental evidence and cannot be retrospectively upgraded. The latest [Critic post-v4 intervention](../communications/broadcast/2026-11/20261105T160359Z_critic_v4_v5_release_gate_and_index_reconciliation.md) shifts the immediate goal to a source-owned, paper-paid attack or control payoff with a genuine opponent response, including failures and caps. Broader owner-information safety, complete paper payment/targets, exact physical-source comparability, 25-minute clock and full-field policy competence remain independent vetoes.
- **Actual integrated releases have not qualified games.** The [frozen selected joint-v2 52-attempt original-source experiment](aichi-2026/agent23-selected-joint-v2-symmetric-vip-52-native-20261103.md) is reproducible and retains errors/caps; its original preregistered gate failed.

# Bench Compression / Hardening Policy v1

**Agent 1 · 27 November 2026**

New Moon should not only decide what to Bench. Before passing the turn, it should also ask whether an existing Bench liability can be removed or hardened without giving up a required continuation.

## Unown Farewell Letter is a real defensive action

Unown AOR 30 says: while on the Bench, once during the turn it may discard itself and all attached cards; this does **not** count as a Knock Out; then draw a card.

When Unown is no longer required, Farewell Letter can:

- remove a 60-HP one-Prize Electro Rain target without giving the opponent a Prize;
- reduce total Bench by one, lowering opposing Full Moon Rondo by 20;
- reduce the number of candidate Pokémon that future Roxie/Koffing places counters onto;
- eliminate a future Snorlax Block gust target;
- draw one replacement card;
- free a Bench slot.

Under Horror House, Farewell Letter is still an in-play Ability rather than a hand play, so it remains a candidate action unless another Ability suppressor is active.

Do **not** cash out automatically. Farewell Letter discards all cards attached to Unown. Preserve it if the body/attachment is required for the selected continuation or if its draw/Bench role outweighs exposure.

## Ability-scope exceptions

Farewell Letter is a Basic Pokémon Ability and is disabled by:

- Active Klefki Mischievous Lock;
- Silent Lab;
- Active opposing Empoleon V Emperor's Eyes (Unown has no Rule Box);
- broad Garbotoxin-style Ability suppression.

It remains live under:

- Path to the Peak by itself (Unown has no Rule Box);
- Iron Thorns Initialization (same reason);
- Horror House hand lock, absent another suppressor.

Do not plan a cash-out from a state where the Ability is currently removed.

## Duskull -> Dusclops as spread hardening

Candidate Duskull has 60 HP. Candidate Dusclops has 90 HP and is Stage 1.

Against Electro Rain without Sight applying to those one-Prize targets:

- Duskull requires 2 packets of 30 to KO;
- Dusclops requires 3 packets.

That single packet can cross the source's exact budget boundary.

### Exact 14-packet witness

With public Telescopic Sight and a 14-packet Raichu budget:

- Benched Gengar & Mimikyu-GX: 7 packets / 3 Prizes;
- Benched Tapu Lele-GX: 5 packets / 2 Prizes;
- Benched Duskull: 2 packets / 1 Prize.

Total = 14 packets for six Prizes.

If Duskull legally evolves to Dusclops before the attack:

7 + 5 + 3 = 15 packets.

The exact same three-target six-Prize line no longer fits the 14-packet budget.

Evolution also unlocks Cursed Blast and a 50-damage Stage-1 attack, improving the body against Basic-only Ability/attack restrictions and Snorlax trap states.

## End-of-turn compression pass

Before passing into an opponent with a known Bench-punishment axis:

1. identify every nonessential Unown that can legally Farewell Letter;
2. recompute BEV after each possible cash-out combination;
3. identify Duskull that can legally evolve now and recompute spread/trap exposure as Dusclops;
4. preserve mandatory attackers, Solrock/Lunatone pair, Cursed-Blast prerequisites and other selected continuation pieces;
5. only then pass the turn.

## Family effects

### Raichu / Electrode
Farewell removes a one-Prize packet target; Dusclops evolution raises packet cost by one.

### Rank 30 Clefairy
Each Farewell Letter reduces opposing Rondo by 20 for the next attack if total Bench otherwise unchanged.

### Rank 31 Dusknoir / Koffing
Each removed candidate body reduces each future Koffing trigger's total counter production by one counter; with two Koffing discarded by Roxie, one removed body avoids two movable counters.

### Snorlax Stall
Cash out unneeded Unown before it can be gusted Active. Once Unown is Active, Farewell Letter cannot be used because it requires the Bench.

## Acceptance additions

- BENCH-UNOWN-CASHOUT: nonessential Benched Unown, Farewell Letter live, opponent has established Bench-punishment axis. Must compare cash-out before passing.
- BENCH-UNOWN-SUPPRESSED: same desire but Basic Ability suppression active. Must not use Farewell Letter illegally.
- RAI-DUSKULL-HARDEN: 14-packet witness with Gengar+Lele+Duskull. If legal evolution to Dusclops exists, recognize the six-Prize packet sum changes from 14 to 15.
- STALL-UNOWN-BEFORE-GUST: unneeded Unown still Benched and opponent has Block+gust capacity. Value Farewell before the trap occurs.

No deck change or competitive result follows.

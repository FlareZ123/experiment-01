# A resource-arbitration frontier for Psychic engine actions

**Agent26, 10 November 2026. Purpose:** identify a concrete reason the population's fixed-sixty actor policies and card choice proxies are reversing actual native game outcomes, and a safe way to improve the policy without overfitting a source rank.

## Demonstrated counterexamples

The shared policy currently can prioritize either playing a last held Basic Psychic onto the Active Shadow Rider VMAX or activating Underworld Door on a different VMAX to attach that Psychic to a Benched Psychic and draw. Both have a genuine payoff. In a real owner-root decision, the **same held physical Energy** cannot pay both actions. Treating the actions as independent positive features would be a bug.

1. **Original rank 7 Snorlax Stall:** a prospective 16 matched physical source-deal pair run [42725425474](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42725425474) changed a real source native win into Hood native win when Active manual funding was chosen; one matched example was registered seed 3104969830. Across 16 pairs, accepted manual candidate Active Psychic increased 27→44 and settled candidate attacks 191→212, but action caps 8→11 and sharp net winner bound **[-7,+12]** prevent claiming unconditional superiority. [Full evidence](../../methodology/2026-11/agent26-rank7-fresh16-active-psychic-paired-20261110.md).
2. **Original rank 1 Shadow Rider mirror:** an independent prospective fresh 24-group three-source run [42727670892](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42727670892) demonstrates the opposite. In registered pair 1:1:A, seed 763024993, at real game turn 15 / root ordinal77 the blanket policy spent the contested last Psychic on the Active instead of Underworld Door. That game **changed from a native candidate win with five settled candidate attacks to an original-source win with zero settled candidate attacks**; both started with the identical physically matched original/candidate decks. The same intervention did help one native rank11 Absol Garbodor case by converting a censored control into a candidate win. Aggregate three-rank paired native win difference bounds **[-3,+2]**. [Full evidence](../../methodology/2026-11/agent26-three-source-hood-priority-counterexample-20261110.md).
3. **Original rank 5 Mew/Genesect Perfection:** Agent36 reported a distinct native energy-priority conflict: an Active Mewtwo & Mew-GX had one Basic Psychic, with another physical Psychic in hand. An unsafe root attached that second Psychic to a Benched Gengar and native source won; preserving it for the Active enabled a native **UseAbilityAction Perfection** sequence and six actual Prize removals to a candidate win on a **single exposed seed 50703**, with all opponent attacks and prompt interactions. The independently preregistered eight new openings never reached that correction, so it remains a specific witnessed line, not population win-rate evidence. [Agent36 physical proof](../../methodology/2026-11/agent36-gengar-v3-50703-physical-repair-20261109.md); [native postgame prize counter](../../../simulation/evaluation/2026-11/agent36-perfection-prize-receipts.cjs).

**Generalizable finding:** a policy can legitimately improve attack setup in one opponent state and lose an actually winnable game by consuming the same physical Energy in another. Simple graph connectivity, attack-access counts and arbitrary static action priorities cannot represent the exclusion of the strongest alternate line. This is the state-dependent DCI/AMR and connector-domination problem identified in `resources/human_concepts.md`, now observed on real accepted native actions.

## Practical policy-design solution

Represent each decision by the **actual competing owner-root frontier**, evaluated under the same owner-visible state:

| Root | Required real resources | Potential opportunity | Excluded competing use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Manual Basic Psychic to Active | Unspent normal hand attachment, target validity, one physical held Psychic | Makes Active attack payable sooner, including through future manual turns; can enable taking Prizes under stall | Uses held Psychic that could have powered Underworld Door now |
| Underworld Door | Unused real Ability, qualifying Bench recipient, physical held Psychic, effective Ability availability despite locks | One extra Benched Psychic Energy plus actual draw; can create further hand attachments or new searchable attack lines | Consumes the current held Psychic and Ability timing, with possible loss of Active progress |
| Other Energy recipient | Normal attachment or printed accelerant, legal target, material held Energy | Funds a copied/tech attack, a future retreat, or prize-lock line | Can strand a currently payable attack on the Active; another Energy path may not be drawn |
| Attack/Prize conversion | Correct actual physical Energy, attack legality, target, turn, Active status | Settles real attack damage or copied attack effect, Prizes, opponent response | All setup and Supporter/Bench options that would have been consumed differently |

Proposed **action-ranking invariant**: never compare these roots by the existence of a reachable action alone. For each mutually exclusive root, record the same physical held Energy, owner hand, current Active and Bench, special-energy payment semantics, Ability/Item/Supporter restrictions, actual immediately payable attacks and Prize targets, and the complete action sequence it excludes. Prefer branches with demonstrated game advancement (settled attacks, physical Prize movement, viable defenses) over mere energy movement. Maintain the relevant future draw and opponent response probabilities as uncertainty until sampled. The short-turn action evaluations should account for a **future Psychic drawn by Underworld Door** allowing a manual attachment afterwards.

For copied attacks, count physically settled outcomes: `UseAbilityAction Perfection` can cause Prize removals and is **not an AttackAction**, as Agent36 demonstrated. Rank13 Regidrago copied Apex Dragon attacks likewise require genuine attack and Prize modalities rather than string name recognition. Include lock interactions that remove a root and flag whether the action's implementation is paper-legal before entering any objective.

Use paired, physical-deal-matched **native Store** experiments to distinguish different actions and reactions. Report bounded native outcomes with every action/clock cap unresolved. [Agent26 runner](../../../simulation/evaluation/2026-11/agent26-hood-three-source-fresh24.cjs) and [sharp paired bounds](../../methodology/2026-11/agent26-three-source-hood-priority-counterexample-20261110.md) provide a reusable minimal design. The two tested shortcut repairs below are insufficient:

- Veto early Active funding when opponent currently visibly has CRE Shadow Rider V/VMAX: changed three real original-rank1 roots in an independently registered 32 paired sample but did **not** improve any native winner; current-board visibility does not ensure historical archetype information persists. [Full result](../../methodology/2026-11/agent26-public-mirror-funding-veto-fresh32-native-20261110.md).
- Defer Active funding until public global turn25: changed five roots in a **different** independently preregistered 32-pair sample; native winners unchanged and settled candidate attacks **274→268**. [Full result](../../methodology/2026-11/agent26-late-turn-funding-32-pair-negative-20261110.md).

Both hooks remain default-off. Combining unproven hard gates would overfit these samples and could suppress a decisive early or late exception.

## Decision for the single fixed 60

Continue treating Hood Shield v1, champion Shadow Rider, and other authentic fixed-sixty challengers as **unselected** without actual qualified paper games. A competent actor for the selected sixty must solve this owner-root contention problem; otherwise the published win count is a property of the bot's Energy scheduling, not reliable deck strength. For the next integrated admission case, record actual chosen and unchosen root resources, physical payment and owner prompt identity, copy-attack Prize effects, and one original opponent response. This would be a concrete upgrade from yet another weak-policy aggregate field panel.

This note asserts a **proven native decision phenomenon** and a falsifiable design methodology, **not** an optimized action utility function, a demonstrated global policy improvement, paper certification or the impossible-seeming 99.5% competitive target.

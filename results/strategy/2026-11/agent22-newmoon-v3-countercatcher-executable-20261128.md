## Critical pinned-engine correction — Perfection is not a Bench Attack action

The initial synthetic 2-Prize thought experiment used a fictional direct `attack/Night Watch` action with a Bench source. **That interface is false** for the pinned Twinleaf `a9905a76…` engine. The pinned Mewtwo implementation uses `WAS_POWER_USED` for `Perfection` and `COPY_ATTACK_VIA_ABILITY`; this raises a **ChooseAttackPrompt** with copied Pokémon-GX/EX attacks from Bench or discard and blocked unaffordable attacks. The actual native action enumerator exposes only directly printed attacks, Bench attacks with explicit `useOnBench`, and active Tools as attack actions. **Do not reintroduce a direct Bench-sourced Night Watch AttackAction or count the old synthetic witness as native executable.**

The [revised staged actor selector](../../../simulation/evaluation/2026-11/agent22-newmoon-countercatcher-actor-sequence.cjs) has seven root/prompt steps (including a separate final Perfection prompt on the 2-Prize route):

1. At a native owner root, confirm tied matching Prize counts, an **actual offered Benched Dusknoir Cursed Blast Ability token**, Counter Catcher physically in own hand, a source-supported enemy Benched 2-/3-Prize target, surviving Mewtwo Active, and a funded printed Miraculous action **or** actually offered active Mewtwo Perfection Ability with unsuppressed power, own GX donor in Bench/discard and three physical Psychic-providing Energy. Temple of Sinnoh disables the special-Energy contribution to this preflight.
2. Owner `ChoosePokemonPrompt` must attribute `Cursed Blast` to Dusknoir and offer exactly the intended opponent Benched card, using its current Bench index and full printing.
3. Reobserve Dusk self-KO and exactly one opponent Prize taken. Demand a **newly offered, actually legal** Counter Catcher Item from the correct hand index; don't guess from the Prize comparison alone.
4. Owner `ChoosePokemonPrompt` must attribute Counter Catcher to the printed Item and select the same damaged target currently on Bench.
5. On the fresh root with target now Active, recheck Prize totals, unchanged surviving Active Mewtwo, damage, target, Tools and source. For Miraculous, choose its *actual* paid printed AttackAction only when current target nominal/attested damage is sufficient.
6. For Regidrago, choose the actual **Perfection UseAbilityAction** (not a Night Watch attack action).
7. On the resulting owner `ChooseAttackPrompt`, choose exactly the allowed, visible, eligible `Trevenant & Dusknoir-GX — Night Watch` attack from Bench/discard; the native engine controls energy blocking, attack resolution, and the following host-owned Night Watch random hand-disruption prompt.

After any bad source, stale turn, incorrect target or seat, altered Prize clock, absent native action, inability to choose paid Night Watch, disabled Perfection, unknown donor identity, insufficient Energy, or unexpectedly changing tools, the partial policy **abstains** instead of inventing an action or falling through to an unrelated parent prompt.

### Real action-frontier metadata limitation

The pinned `agent36-native-action-frontier.cjs` provides `attackCostPayable` and exact `attackSource` but **not** `damageAfterResistance`, `targetHp` or `hasUnmodeledModifier`. A policy requiring those imaginary fields never executes in native play. The updated overlay has two distinct damage branches:

- If an authenticated native adapter *does* attach exact numeric damage evidence, it honors this and refuses insufficient or prevented damage.
- Otherwise it calculates a **conditional printed-base projection** only from actor-observed Mewtwo's printed Miraculous 200 attack, recognized exact opposing printing, no Tools or Stadium, no opponent Psychic Resistance, and live Fairy Zone where relevant. It always rechecks after gust, marks the action `A22_MIRACULOUS_GX_PRINTED_BASE_CONDITIONAL_TERMINAL` and **does not claim native damage certainty**. Old attack-created effects or an unmodeled protective Ability can still defeat the projected KO. This is an action-selection heuristic with explicit limitations, not a native outcome.

The pinned Dusknoir implementation also unconditionally self-KOs even if damage counters are prevented, whereas its printed text conditions self-KO on placing counters. That engine gap reinforces the need for a real, no-protection game receipt rather than counting source simulations indiscriminately.

## Implication and next bounded test

The exact observed bottleneck advanced from Tag Call search access to **real G&H Supporter use**, and now to **optional two-card discard cost and additional Special Energy/Tool search**. Relative to the earlier v7 policy, two actual source Passes changed into genuine Supporter actions. This validates a particular native ALS prefix and demonstrates the relevance of state-dependent discard costs. It leaves unanswered whether paying the cost would be legal in these two naturally dealt hands, worth sacrificing the selected resources, compatible with the correct Supporter allocation, and sufficient to fund a native attack.

A separately preregistered deliberately relaxed-discard v9 could test **physical payable-card feasibility** while reporting the exact protected resource sacrificed and retaining the original v7/v8 public-root comparisons. That must be labeled a structural feasibility branch, not an optimal policy. If two-card physical payment cannot be confirmed, retain the native failure as a useful negative. A full paper-legal, opponent-responsive 32-rank fixed-deck performance claim remains unavailable.

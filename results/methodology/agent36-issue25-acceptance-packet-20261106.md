## v8 selection rationale

V8 integrates the v7 owner-only Volt prompt continuation into a broader source-independent two-seat policy. Its four-rank sample and 19 settled source attacks provide substantially more policy-execution evidence than v4's six capped arms and two errors with no settled source attack. The three source Prize transitions are useful payoff evidence. The 19 same-epoch opponent roots show that the run progressed beyond isolated source actions.

The selection remains provisional for development. Five engine errors and three root caps remain. Most opposing responses are weak. No game reached a native terminal. V8 is not a complete paper-legal policy and does not establish a competitive win rate. The cross-candidate rank11 physical-deal mismatch also prohibits pairing those candidate rows solely by seed.

## v9 failure interpretation (corrected 6 November)

The historical v9 run returned all 24 requested phase rows. The registered B flag was passed into the trial options, but the `runDevelopment` `scopedRoot` activation predicate did **not** include `!actorTwoUnitPaidRetreatRoot`. With paid retreat as the only requested root option, the adapter supplied `{}` instead of installing `withActorOnlyRootPolicy`. The treatment did not execute. Thus the correct causal classification is **NO TREATMENT APPLIED / A = B = A-REPEAT**, as verified by [Agent20's corrected independent audit](agent20-v9-paid-retreat-aba-audit-20261106.md) and the [Critic release correction](../../communications/broadcast/2026-11/20261106T162100Z_critic_v9_wrapper_activation_and_experiment_contract.md).

All eight groups retained identical original physical deals and complete accepted root tapes across the three phases. Three groups capped at 128 roots with top-level ledgers. Five groups aborted at distinct roots under the same policy-decoder quarantine exception SHA256 `2dc3a55f9b6d7dbc60aaa8883b43982e213957c44179b1baf52cb1139effe11f`; their partial accepted ledgers **are preserved** under `nativeAbortEvidence.rootActionLedger` and also replay identically. The reported aggregate `controlsRepeatedExactly=false` omitted those preserved abort tapes. It is an error-aware reporting defect, not evidence of differing A/A' actions.

The historical first-divergence check also accepted `null` vacuously, without a positive B Store retreat or ordinal/seat/custody join. A future 240-root successor must preflight actual live wrapper activation in B, prove a non-null first genuine Store divergence, preserve actor and actual root Store provenance, and separately audit error-censored versus completed ledgers. The historical common `legacy-shared-prng` remains unsuitable for a genuinely divergent B treatment. Policy and later physical-shuffle streams should be isolated with a separately registered protocol and matched A/A' controls. The known Agent42 root131 canary falls outside this historical root128 horizon and remains a distinct physical-custody/attacker-identity qualification question.

The native witness count and competitive qualification remain unchanged at zero. **This v9 run makes no negative or positive claim about paid-retreat strategy.**

### New retreat-specific release vetoes

Agent20's census identifies Latias ex SSP 76 Skyliner and Snorlax PGO 55 Block in the authenticated field. The current paid-retreat selector reads printed Retreat Cost and some Stadium/Tool/status conditions, but does not consume the public abilityDetails needed for every effective-cost decision. The selector also needs explicit negative tests for a payable Active GX attack, incomplete Active attack metadata, a held Float Stone route, and payment classes beyond two Basic Psychic.

A successor must prove the acting owner's effective retreat cost before proposing a paid retreat. If an ability or restriction cannot be evaluated from public information, it must decline the proposal. A native accepted RetreatAction alone is insufficient. Agent42 has a v2 selector prototype with Skyliner, Snorlax Block, GX, and unknown-modifier checks, but the common Agent34 root still imports v1. The v2 positive fixture omits the required Active `abilityDetails` array, and `attackBlocked` currently treats missing restriction metadata as blocked rather than unknown, which can allow a paid-retreat proposal with an incomplete Active attack inventory. Agent20's Float Stone/free-mobility supplement is still pending. Keep v2 out of the common policy until these issues are fixed and tested. The full line must prove physical payment, target promotion, settled printed attack, damage, Prize transition, immediate opponent reply, and the source's next owner turn.

### Night Watch randomness veto

Agent48's adjacent-seed audit and original-native corroboration show deterministic first-choice coupling across four adjacent seed schedules. This is evidence about the seed/stream protocol. It does not establish intrinsic sampler bias. Successor experiments must use SHA-separated seed derivation and independently reproducible policy and physical-shuffle streams. Retain the historical observations without counting them as independent random samples.

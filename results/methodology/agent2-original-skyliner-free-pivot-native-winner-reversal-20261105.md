## Interpretation, explicit limitations and next gate

This is a **real policy causality witness**: a same-algorithm both-seat positioning choice converted one genuine Pass into a Store-accepted zero-cost retreat, immediately enabled the rank1 reference's already funded attacker, and changed that single native engine result. The earlier zero-offer paid-retreat screen addressed a different position: seed3565472974 had native retreat fee three, while the seed3792360798 Skyliner position had fee zero. Agent6's separate paid-reserve 32-arm trial obtained a different real paid escape but no native winner change. Neither should be merged with this free-retreat intervention.

It is **one intervention opportunity in eight historically selected source deals**, reached as late as turn58. The `followThrough` classifier uses attack root presence and the first opponent nonprompt action; it does not independently prove damage, Prize transfers, strategic reply quality, end-of-time adjudication, or paper-correct last-promote behavior. The B final public board shows rank3 Active null while Benched Pokémon remain, consistent with the terminal attack Knock Out and immediate win on taking the last Prize; the engine's actual before/after terminal transition confirms that source of the native win. Exact independent paper legality of every earlier KO, Prize choice, board position, and terminal transition is still untested. No 25-minute tournament clock was modeled. The opponent policies are development-grade. This experiment evaluates original rank1 versus rank3, not one selected fixed contender against all original 32. Three rank inventories (23,25,28) remain missing. **Zero competitively qualified games, no selected final 60 and no evidence of 99.5% field strength.**

Recommended integration: turn the turn58 A/A-prime Pass and B Retreat+Night Watch into a regression for the selected both-seat policy's mobility and funded-attacker arbitration, with a pre-action paper-payment/lock audit, physical damage/Prize accounting, adversarial opponent response, and native terminal-cause check. The shared steward must version any successor to v4; this Agent2 hook is default-off and cannot be retrospectively credited to the selected policy.

# Agent 3: original Aichi field contains three Japanese Expanded-only Trainer print identities

**4 November 2026 | external printed-card provenance check. These are published Japanese-card database claims plus historical source 60 evidence, not a newly certified native engine implementation.**

## Scope issue for a fixed 60 against the genuine Aichi original field

The goal benchmark is the **Japanese CL Aichi 2026 Top32 paper Expanded** field. It must not be silently replaced by an international English Expanded legal-card subset merely because the bundled English card corpus cannot load a Japanese-exclusive original print.

The [complete English-corpus source-gap census](agent3-original-29-source-printed-corpus-ten-gap-20261104.md) isolates **14 original-field physical Trainer copies** in three printed identities absent from local `resources/cards/en/`: Palace Belt BWP153 (3), Palace Book SMP NAN25 (6), and Player's Ceremony SP127 (5). The per-rank source amounts are retained in the [machine provenance inventory](../aichi-2026/agent3-original29-printing-gap-20261104.json). Those are present as printed names, codes, counts and deck positions in **five** authenticated original rank sources (3,6,7,24,29).

All three have independent Japanese-print card pages on Limitless, linked below. Each page explicitly labels the card **Expanded (JP): legal**, **Expanded: not legal**, and **not released internationally**. That is evidence of a real **regional card-pool difference**, not evidence that the Japanese tournament's opponent decklists are illegal.

## Genuine engine requirements

A simulated or automated match against any of those five source ranks requires either their actual *regionally legal* original Trainer printings and well-tested effect implementations, or an explicit recorded model limitation. For fidelity, independently test all the following, rather than silently choosing more familiar international alternatives:

- The printed source's exact set, number and count remain unchanged.
- The turn-start two-card replacement timing of Palace Belt applies only while its attached Pokémon is Active, with normal Tool constraints.
- Palace Book's draw-three sequence ends the acting player's turn at the correct midturn point; any Item-lock or turn-end interactions must be decided using paper rules.
- Player's Ceremony's once-per-player-per-turn Stadium draw is optional and ends the acting player's turn when elected; Stadium replacement and lock constraints matter.
- Owner information boundaries, applicable rules updates, interaction with existing turn-end and turn-start effects, official event-era 2026 Japanese legality and match clock need independent validation before an outcome can be competitive evidence.

This source-derived requirement does **not** prove Twinleaf currently lacks a hook or that a particular opponent strategy actually chooses these effects. A repository-wide native implementation check should happen before announcing a missing engine feature. It does demonstrate the **English-card corpus cannot by itself witness those exact original printing identities**, and an international-only legality flag is not a substitute for Japanese Expanded scope.

The other **120 unmatched source copies** are seven explicit MEE Basic Energy printings, which carry known Basic Energy type descriptions; there is no evidence in this report they create a distinct novel effect. The 29 existing source 60s have fully resolved Pokémon Basic/Evolution stages. Ranks 23,25,28 remain missing complete original source lists, zero games have been competitively qualified, and no unchanged 60 has demonstrated the stated 99.5% target.

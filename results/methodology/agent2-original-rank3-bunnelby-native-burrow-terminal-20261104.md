## Statistical and methodological limitations

- This is **one replay of an already observed native terminal** selected for mechanistic investigation, not an independent prospective random win sample. It must not be counted again, included as a new matchup win, or used as a field win-rate estimator.
- The **opponent policy was unqualified**. Mimikyu remained Active and used Filch repeatedly, accelerating its own decking-out. A competent rank-1 opponent might attack differently, change Pokémon, manipulate the hand/deck, remove Bunnelby, or close the Prize game faster. This trace establishes the weakness of a particular executable opponent continuation rather than an optimized win expectation.
- The original opponent printing adapter is documented to make equivalence substitutions for cards as needed. The source inventory contains historical provenance issues and the field still lacks ranks23,25,28. Actual 25-minute paper clock adjudication is absent.
- The no-Tool branch does **not** demonstrate Float Stone's strategic contribution. The board had Bunnelby Active, ready to attack from Water Energy alone. The 35 FSS/Float roots in a separate pair of games are a distinct, unproven mobility opportunity.
- A further **per-dispatch deck-zone delta recorder** would be required to attribute individual deck removals to Burrow versus Filch/shuffles/draws. The current causal claim is limited to real native Burrow execution, matched initial/terminal snapshots, and the native opponent deck-out.
- Existing [Issue #25](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/issues/25) selected shared policy, source-side bilateral competence, full paper rules, and opponent ability to respond remain independent qualification gates.

**Conclusion:** The source-side gap has a better identified boundary. The original rank-3 printed 60 can naturally produce repeated native Bunnelby Burrow attacks and a terminal deckout in an actual unchanged physical match under this existing development policy. It remains **zero competitively qualified games, zero selected final 60, and no demonstrated 99.5% field win rate**.

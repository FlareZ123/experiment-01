## Question and controlled intervention

The existing general actor-only root policy in
[agent34-actor-only-root-policy.cjs](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent34-actor-only-root-policy.cjs)
takes its first eligible held Basic Energy before the public-context
Special Energy selector. The pre-existing opt-in
[Agent41 flexible immediate-attack contest](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent41-public-flexible-energy-contest.cjs)
instead chooses a held Special Energy only when it enables an immediate,
printed, positive-damage attack while every held Basic fails to do so.
Its independent pure suite covers a Regidrago V versus DDE coheld case,
Temple, Wondrous Labyrinth, prize-state restrictions and concealed-zone
invariance. This is a source-independent tactical arbitration hypothesis.

**Registered question:** on the **same eight original rank6/rank11 source
attempts** as the archived v13 bilateral public-context Energy policy,
does this existing helper change an actual native action, Energy
attachment, source attack, native terminal or error? A stronger
counterfactual requires actual physically matched initial deals, Store
payment, attacked target and subsequent opposing reply. Selection of
any attack without paper validation is insufficient.

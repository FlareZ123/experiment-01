- This is a **retrospective same-registration treatment**. The v8 registration was selected before this guard was introduced, while the A/B intervention was chosen after source behavior was observed.
- The 128-root cap censored three attempts; five ended with engine errors. All eight are non-qualified. No 25-minute Swiss/knockout time regime was executed. There is no full-game demonstrated win-rate comparison.
- Even if A/B accepted root counts match, replay equivalence of accepted action content needs an exact top-level + nested-abort root ledger join. The strengthened [v2 abort-aware comparison runner](../../simulation/evaluation/agent39-v8-paper-volt-original-eight-ab-native.cjs) is separately under native CI at the time of initial publication.

- The test's two original candidate fixed 60s are the already existing historical comparator decks. Selection remains **none** and the shared developmental v8 policy is not replaced.

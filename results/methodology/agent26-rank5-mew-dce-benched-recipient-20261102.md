# Rank-5 Mew/Genesect: actual DCE recipient defect and bounded owner-visible proposal

**Agent 26 | 2 November 2026.** Evidence-based actor-policy design, **inactive by default**. No additional native games, competitive win-rate data, deck change or paper-valid winning continuation generated.

## Genuine original 60 and the opportunity

Original Aichi Open rank **5 Mew/Genesect**, authenticated by the shared [printed 29-original roster](../aichi-2026/full-decklists.json), includes **4 Mew V FST 113, 3 Mew VMAX FST 114, 4 Genesect V FST 185 and 4 Double Colorless Energy SUM 136**. Exact supplied card text:

- **Mew VMAX Cross Fusion Strike** costs **two Colorless Energy** and copies an attack from one of your Benched Fusion Strike Pokémon.
- **Double Colorless Energy** supplies **two Colorless Energy** when its effect is active.
- Benched **Genesect V Techno Blast** is a copyable 210-damage attack, subject to ordinary attack-copy rules and available legal targets. Mew's Cross Fusion Strike attack bears the two-Colorless attachment cost, rather than Genesect's printed Metal-cost requirement.

The [Agent 4 preregistered rank-5 native DCE study](agent4-rank5-special-energy-root-prospective-20261102.md) observed **4/4 actual DCE play roots** after enabling bilateral Special Energy proposals. On two original-source seat-0 treatment arms, however, the source attached the DCE to Active **Lillie's Clefairy ex**, remaining unable to announce an attack while a potential Mew evolution attack channel was elsewhere. The source-root policy first examines the current Active's printed attacks and can miss multi-turn Bench evolution targets. That is a specific quality issue supported by actual Store action and board receipts. It is **not** evidence that the alternative attachment would force a win.

## A separate, testable proposal

The new [owner-only intent module](../../simulation/twinleaf/agent26-owner-visible-mew-dce-recipient.cjs) proposes a held **Double Colorless Energy SUM** for a visible zero-energy Mew VMAX or Mew V, including a Benched target. It prefers the already-evolved Mew VMAX and then an Active over Benched Mew of the same stage. It rejects an already-funded target, completed normal Energy attachment, public Energy locks, and public Temple of Sinnoh, which suppresses DCE's two-Colorless effect.

A tactically important guard preserves a **different Active Pokémon's immediately fundable damaging attack**: when the DCE alone turns a known printed attack from unpaid into completely paid, the module leaves the existing action chooser's opportunity intact. This avoids blindly overvaluing future evolution when a real attack can be executed now.

The helper sees the acting player's own hand, publicly exposed Pokémon and Stadiums, and their own attachment quota. It reads no opponent hidden hand, Prize card identities or deck order. The returned output is a normal native actor intent with explicit Bench/Active destination, to be interpreted and accepted by the actual Store when integrated.

[Eight pure JSDoc/CommonJS regressions](../../tests/agent26-owner-visible-mew-dce-recipient.test.cjs) passed in [manual Node22 CI 41486285372](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41486285372), including the Clefairy/Benched Mew case, Mew VMAX preference, no double attachment to an already-funded Mew, the attachment quota, Temple of Sinnoh suppression, source-owner seat symmetry, a hidden-state read veto, and conservation of a newly payable active damaging attack. This successful test step does **not** make the whole workflow green: the independently registered source-inventory integrity gate still fails.

## Unproven and potentially adverse consequences

This selector is a **proposal function only**. It has not been wired into any frozen shared policy, turned on in a newly registered native match or demonstrated to cause a legal Cross Fusion Strike. A Mew V on Bench might have all available VMAX copies Prized, might lack a future evolution window, or might be removed by the opponent before attacking. A Mew VMAX needs a relevant **Benched Fusion Strike donor** and valid attack conditions; the DCE alone does not supply Genesect V or secure an Active promotion. Other legal routes (draw, gust, retreat, strategic late-game Prize finishes) can dominate spending DCE on Mew.

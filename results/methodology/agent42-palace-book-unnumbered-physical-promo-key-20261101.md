# Agent42: original Aichi Palace Book SMP NAN25 has no printed numeric collector number

**1 November 2026. Source-identity correction from primary Japanese card page and published card image. No native game or legality certification.**

The 29-source original register includes six Palace Book copies labeled `SMP NAN25`, in exact source ranks 6 (one), 7 (three), 24 (two). This is correctly a known original Japanese promotional **Item**. It was flagged as absent in the supplied English card archive in the [complete source census](agent42-original-29-exact-card-archive-coverage-20261101.md).

The printed-card **identity** needs a finer distinction. The Japanese Pokémon official card-search page [殿堂の書, archive card 34382](https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/34382/regu/all) displays **SM-P** without any leading collector digits. The actual Japanese promotional card image in the [illustrated catalogue](https://pokeboon.com/jp/smp-palacebook-promo/) likewise marks its printed number as **SM-P**, without a numeric ordinal. [Limitless](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/SMP/NAN25?translate=en), in contrast, gives its electronic catalogue key `NAN25`, and [Prism's list](https://prismtcg.app/set/JPSMP?uiLang=en) also uses `NAN25`. This strongly establishes that **`NAN25` is a database-assigned discriminator for an unnumbered promo, rather than literal digits on the physical card**. The broader [SM-P catalogue](https://pokeboon.com/jp/category/sm-p/) includes multiple officially unnumbered promo releases.

**Physical printed-card key for verification:** original name `殿堂の書`, expansion/promo family `SM-P`, Item card, illustrator 5ban Graphics, verified official card-page id `34382`, and the exact card art as shown on the Japanese print. The source register's `NAN25` is a valuable *database-source key* and must be retained unchanged as the opponent-list registration; it cannot be treated as a literal collector number in a paper photo or one-to-one Archive ZIP lookup.

The official Japanese text says the owner draws **three** cards and ends their turn on using the Item; the [English-language Limitless display](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/SMP/NAN25?translate=en) agrees. The real order of native effects, turn-end transition, and any errata still require a rule/Store witness.

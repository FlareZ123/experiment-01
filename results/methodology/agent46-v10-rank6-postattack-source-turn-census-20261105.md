## Research question and frozen source

After the original Aichi rank6 Iron Thorns ex deck's first **genuine** Thunder Mountain ◇ + Active Double Colorless Energy → **Volt Cyclone** dealt 140 damage and moved the sole DCE to its Bench, what did the **same original source actor** actually do on its next voluntary turn? An accepted original-source reply is stronger evidence than a policy's `decode` call, but an observed Pass says nothing alone about whether a better paper-legal action existed.

Agent5/Agent36 independently established **two** real original-source native Store attacks and true same-epoch opponent responses in [v10 CI 42106359764](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42106359764), preserving the original eight registrations, physical deals, policy prefix through DCE and all negative outcomes. The separate v10 release is **not** the steward-selected v4.

I wrote a **read-only archived-result consumer** [`agent46-v10-postattack-source-tail-audit.cjs`](../../simulation/evaluation/agent46-v10-postattack-source-tail-audit.cjs), six [synthetic adversarial tests](../../tests/agent46-v10-postattack-source-tail-audit.test.cjs), and an opt-in [historical artifact-only workflow](../../.github/workflows/agent46-v10-postattack-source-tail-audit.yml). This consumer reads the already published 8-arm control/treatment JSON, checks each original SHA-256 sidecar and root-tape SHA, confirms exact physical deal/start identity and accepted prefixes, then reports postattack public native Store roots. It neither recompiles Twinleaf nor executes new games or consults player-private hands.

## Why this matters and what is next

The first source Volt Cyclone consumes DCE from Active and **moves that exact physical DCE to a Benched Iron Thorns ex**. The source's next turn therefore needs separate payment/position sequencing to attack again; a second strike cannot be inferred from the single earlier attack. The next scientifically discriminating study should freeze the actual original owner-visible turn4 root state in each target, inspect physical in-play Energy destinations and hand at that point, and compare **payable** lines such as Bench↔Active attack pivot, legitimate Energy acceleration, or taking a tactical Pass. Preserve all resource cost, lock, Supporter contention, 48-root censor, exact counterfactual physical deal and next opponent response. Do not promote a theoretical connector's existence to a paper-playable follow-up.

**Scope and qualification:** verified native Store roots and paper-agnostic temporal joins, not an authenticated owner decision opportunity or a full match. Still **29/32** opponent original name/count decklists, missing ranks23/25/28, **zero paper-qualified games**, no final selected 60 and no measured 99.5% game win rate. The record protects a useful source-play frontier without claiming a favorable competitive outcome.

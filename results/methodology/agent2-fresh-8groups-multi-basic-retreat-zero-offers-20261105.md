## Interpretation

This sharply limits the external reach of the [historically selected rank1 typed native 3-Psychic rescue](agent2-original-rank1-typed-three-psychic-retreat-native-v2-20261105.md) and its [source-independent same-selected-deal reproduction](agent2-bilateral-multi-basic-paid-retreat-native-aba-20261105.md). That rescue was physically real, but its necessary owner/board/draw-race conditions did **not** recur in eight new opening/seat groups. The root-level policy can only change games in which an offer exists. No fresh game improved; the absence cannot be used as evidence that the game mechanic is irrelevant or that existing bot choices are optimal.

The current pure selector requires exact printed 2–4 Colorless costs, matching attached known Basic Energy cards, a lower own deck count, an active without payable damaging attacks, and a fixed positive funded Bench move whose nominal damage would finish before deck-out. Its eligibility might be too strict; this audit does **not** identify which individual prerequisite failed in each of the 340 Pass roots. The next useful test is a **symmetric owner-observation rejection-reason census** on these same fixed seeds, with no gameplay intervention, so the team can identify which constraint actually dominates instead of speculating or loosening rules blindly.

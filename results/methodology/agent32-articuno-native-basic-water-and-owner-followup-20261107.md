## Source-level finding

The previous `simulation/twinleaf/agent36-owner-ability-confirm-policy.cjs` helper tested `Array.isArray(card.cardType)` and then `card.cardType.includes(CardType.WATER)`. Pinned upstream `EnergyCard` declares `provides: CardType[]` and `superType=ENERGY`; `cardType` is a Pokémon characteristic. A real Basic Water Energy therefore could not satisfy that helper. Its earlier `otherWaterCount=0` in the Articuno-GX confirm audit was **not independent evidence that no Water was attached**. The previous v8 native control/treatment happened to decline the two observed Articuno prompts, but those decisions were generated by a mis-specified type predicate.

The corrected native Basic Water predicate uses all three genuine fields: `superType=ENERGY`, `name='Water Energy'`, and `provides` containing WATER. The name condition is intentional for the **pinned engine's current implementation**, which opens the Legendary Ascent movement prompt with `{superType: ENERGY, name:'Water Energy'}`. It does not imply the paper attack/Ability text is limited to Basic Energy.

## Pinned native follow-up and custody

The prior adapter delegated an accepted Articuno `MoveEnergyPrompt` to the stock `SimpleTacticsAi`, whose pinned `MoveEnergyPromptResolver` makes whole-State copies and scores every candidate move. That stock policy is unsuitable as proof of actor-only information use.

The revised `chooseArticunoOwnedWaterTransfers` reads the actual pending `MoveEnergyPrompt`, actor's Active and Bench physical card lists, native allowed slots, blocked source/targets and blocked-card indices. It selects the permitted owner's Basic Water cards and returns `ResolvePromptAction` entries pointing to the actual actor's Bench source and Active Articuno destination. It reads no opponent hidden zones and no unopened Deck contents. Before dispatch, the existing strict `assertValidPromptAction` enforces the native prompt's geometry. The returned audit now checks that every selected Water card physically arrived on the Active Pokémon and left its Bench source following the genuine Store reducer. A failed custody proof raises an error instead of accepting a persuasive-looking selection.

Additional provenance hardening from the same intervention: exact prompt-object lineage prevents numeric ID reuse from confusing Confirm source cards; one-shot Articuno transfer associations retire after the first settled follow-up; staged physical cards with default ID `-1` cannot be conflated by a loose numeric equality. The pinned engine's exact-name `Water Energy` filter may exclude compatible Special Energy whose attached in-play effects provide Water. Treat a general paper-correct Energy movement policy as unresolved pending a distinct paper/rule-level test and a separately versioned native hook.

## Source-level finding

The previous `simulation/twinleaf/agent36-owner-ability-confirm-policy.cjs` helper tested `Array.isArray(card.cardType)` and then `card.cardType.includes(CardType.WATER)`. Pinned upstream `EnergyCard` declares `provides: CardType[]` and `superType=ENERGY`; `cardType` is a Pokémon characteristic. A real Basic Water Energy therefore could not satisfy that helper. Its earlier `otherWaterCount=0` in the Articuno-GX confirm audit was **not independent evidence that no Water was attached**. The previous v8 native control/treatment happened to decline the two observed Articuno prompts, but those decisions were generated by a mis-specified type predicate.

The corrected native Basic Water predicate uses all three genuine fields: `superType=ENERGY`, `name='Water Energy'`, and `provides` containing WATER. The name condition is intentional for the **pinned engine's current implementation**, which opens the Legendary Ascent movement prompt with `{superType: ENERGY, name:'Water Energy'}`. It does not imply the paper attack/Ability text is limited to Basic Energy.

## Opportunity costs and scientific relevance

The other nine cards would be shuffled away, including a singleton Secret Box ACE SPEC and one remaining Lillie's Determination. That is a substantial resource-contention cost. It may still be strategically valuable when all candidate Pokémon have zero attached Energy, as at root93, but no immediate playable attack or subsequent Prize follows logically from drawing eight. A genuinely stronger line might instead first spend Secret Box (paying three others), use Mysterious Treasure to discard a dead Battle VIP Pass, or preserve the current hand; all require separate action-sequence and opportunity-cost tests. An available Supporter is **not equivalent to demonstrated Pass regret**.

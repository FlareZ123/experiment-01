## Audit contract

New pure postgame module [`simulation/evaluation/agent18-issue25-source-root-custody.cjs`](../../simulation/evaluation/agent18-issue25-source-root-custody.cjs) accepts an actual development row and emits native accepted-root actions for the **source owner**, only if:

- Actual physical source seat is observed in a normal returned row, or recovered from a native-abort provenance whose `sourceDealStatus` is exactly `VERIFIED`.
- The native accepted-root ledger is present with a matching integer count and an exact SHA-256 of its serialized entries.
- Aborted rows additionally require `nativeAbortProvenance.acceptedRootLedgerStatus === 'VERIFIED'`.

Otherwise, action attribution fails closed, and a partial observer does not convert guessed seat or roots into positive evidence. Owner roots are grouped by native action names and preserve brief postgame examples of publicly played cards and declared attacks. Prompt responses, Pass, and waiting actions are separated from selected non-Pass roots. The diagnostic **does not** establish that a root was paper-legal, affordable, strategically preferred, or a complete game. Setup roots may also appear; make claims about meaningful gameplay only after inspecting their turn ordinals and paper transitions.

The six pure adversarial tests [`tests/agent18-issue25-source-root-custody.test.cjs`](../../tests/agent18-issue25-source-root-custody.test.cjs) cover ordinary returns, verified abort rescue, mismatched source seat, tampered accepted roots, bad digest, and incomplete registered denominators.

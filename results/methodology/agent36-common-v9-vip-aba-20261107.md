## Interpretation

This is a positive native action/custody witness for one specific owner-only fallback. It establishes that, in one fresh registered deal, the handler correctly replaced Pass with an accepted first-turn Battle VIP Pass play, resolved the owner's actual deck-search prompt, and placed two physical Basic Pokémon on the Bench. The A/A2 replay and physical-opening checks support causal attribution to the sole VIP flag.

It does **not** show that the action improved the eventual game result. The affected match was root-capped, the opponent's later response is not evaluated to completion, and only one of four contexts offered an eligible opportunity. The unchanged 3-terminal / 1-cap phase totals do not establish equal or improved strength.

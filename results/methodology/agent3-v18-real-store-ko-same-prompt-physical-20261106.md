## What was strengthened

The new `withKoSourcePromptPhysical` wrapper captures the event metadata when the inferred actual Store processes `KnockOutEffect` during `dispatch`, matches the later exact pending Prize prompt object, and only after the player has already chosen a Prize slot captures **references to its currently contained physical card objects**. Following the original Store's `ResolvePromptAction`, it tests that the pending prompt's `result` matches the selected result and that those exact selected objects are absent from Prize and present in the owner's hand. It emits session number and public seat/turn/phase, Prize-card counts and destination, with **no concealed face, name or printing**. It restores native Store method descriptors on return/exception. Existing v16 independent physical observers agree on every available public record tuple.

Both-seat pure tests distinguish exact hand/lost-zone settlement, no-transfer, silent native no-op, an unrelated matching-message Prize prompt, off-root speculative reducer invocation, hypothetical other Store without real player registration, playability reducer calls and instrumentation restoration after exceptions.

## Boundaries and remaining risk

- `realStoreIdentityProofComplete=false`: treating the last Store receiving AddPlayerAction as authoritative is an inference. A hypothetical Store that also calls AddPlayerAction could shadow it.
- `uniqueCheckStateOriginCertified=false`: the correlation by prize recipient/destination/count does not establish a unique call-site provenance token for `executeCheckState`. A different same-Store source event could coincide with an eligible prompt. **The chain joins actual objects within a Store, conditional on the correlated source being genuine.**
- Only prompt-mediated selected Prize cards are observed. The pinned native engine may take automatic KO Prizes without a ChoosePrizePrompt for last Prize or last Pokémon; the observer cannot certify those paths. It does not independently verify every printed payment or the accuracy of KO source effects.
- No changed opponent responses, substantial attack/control effectiveness, official paper clock, qualified native full-game or tournament matchup win rate follow from this result. The Aichi original field still has 29/32 authenticated complete name/count inventories; ranks 23,25,28 absent. No final fixed 60 selected and the 99.5% target is unsupported.

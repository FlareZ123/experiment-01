# Agent48: opportunity-denominator receipt, 31 October 2026

The pure postgame helper [`simulation/evaluation/agent48-decision-opportunity-receipt.cjs`](../../simulation/evaluation/agent48-decision-opportunity-receipt.cjs) and its [ten Node regressions](../../tests/agent48-decision-opportunity-receipt.test.cjs) address the [Critic's duplicate-root and missing-positive-witness gate](../../communications/broadcast/20261031T0314Z_critic_opportunity_denominator_and_witness_gate.md).

It keeps original source-deal fingerprints, physical trials, paired comparison IDs, actor-root counts and observed-configuration clusters separate. The only accepted inputs are postgame records with a real trial ID, acting seat, ordinal, detached actor-observation view, and per-offer separately observed flags for structural opportunity, native `canPlay`, selection, Store acceptance, effect settlement, next actor root and opposing response. Unknown evidence remains unknown. Duplicate physical roots, changing deal fingerprints, and logically inconsistent accepted/settled facts fail closed.

Critically, each physical trial retains its **earliest native-canPlay-positive exact action witness independently of example sample limits**. Each trial reports whether its general examples or positive examples were truncated. Consequently, an initial sample full of negatives cannot imply there were zero playable opportunities. Native-positive card/recipient options and root windows are counted separately. The observation digest retains public turn context and owner-hand multiplicities while disregarding hand ordering. As the [public-history gate](../../communications/broadcast/20261031T0103Z_critic_public_history_policy_sufficiency_gate.md) explains, this is only a provisional grouping of observed configurations: the detached view does not certify all relevant public-history variables.

## Actual actor-hook native receipt cap

Further source inspection found that `simulation/twinleaf/agent34-actor-only-root-policy.cjs` only archives the first **64** `nativeRootAcceptances` while maintaining unbounded accepted/rejected counters and a distinct `selectedRootDecisions` counter. The full headless `rootActionLedger` tracks **Store action ordinals**, including prompts, rather than the actor **policy decision ordinals**. These clocks must be joined at the same dispatch; an equal integer or shared turn is inadequate evidence.

The new pure `actorNativeReceiptCoverage(audit)` reports `NATIVE_ROOT_WITNESSES_TRUNCATED` with the exact number of lost dispatch receipts, `ACTOR_SELECTION_WITHOUT_DISPATCH` for unpublished native outcomes, or `COMPLETE_POLICY_CLOCK_ONLY` when the policy side is fully accounted. Even the last case sets `nativeActionOrdinalBound:false`. The function is meaningful only when the same-root actor hook has recorded `policyDecisionOrdinal` on its native receipt rows. Invalid ordinals and duplicated policy ordinals fail closed. Three additional regressions bring the **local suite to 10/10 passing**. This is still a static pure helper; Agent41 would need a live same-dispatch compact sidecar for native qualification.

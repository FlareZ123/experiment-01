## Decision

**Retain v2 as a working, opt-in, owner-safe strategy primitive; do not promote it into the strongest default deck policy on current evidence.** Future experiments should target effective Energy access, Ability use, retreat and attack initiation in the already-physically-realized early triad states, with source responses and physical Prizes measured. Preserve all bounded/capped contexts, restrict any eligibility filters to variables known before outcomes, and apply the shared `agent36-perfection-prize-receipts.cjs` postgame classifier so copied attacks are not undercounted. Exact remaining opponent source ranks **23/25/28** and any paper-clock/release qualification remain unresolved. The 99.5% field target is unsupported.

## Operational consequence

1. Keep both-player natural evolution in every shared original-source benchmark. The inherited common policy already does it, and source-only natural evolution would confound a candidate comparison.
2. Source Mew DCE targeting remains a meaningful **conditional** owner option: Agent8's separately preregistered positive native intervention [run 42577136715](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42577136715) demonstrated a rank5 original-source six-Prize native winner reversal in a different physical context. This 58-deal field cannot contradict that conditional witness because no different action was accepted here.
3. Do not award win-rate improvements for simply enabling no-op flags. Preregister event-level source DCE opportunities and ensure the root chooser actually activates before a strength comparison. Preserve rank5 source evolution, attachment target, attack-copy menu, attack settlement, Prize movement and candidate continuation under one independent paper-semantics profile.
4. Retain this **zero-engine-error, 58 fully matched** native receipt as a useful field infrastructure benchmark while keeping its 16 double-capped groups censored. Revisit the source policy's ability to fund combat against competent lock/control opponents before comparing new 60-card contenders.

**Conclusion:** No source-only Mew treatment activation or result change in the entire registered 58-pair field. This is evidence about opportunity and policy coverage; the fixed champion remains a development reference, with no final 60 selected and zero paper-competitive games.

### Actual DCE recipient improvement hidden by root-only trace

The strongest positive finding is a **real owner Energy recipient correction** that is invisible when comparing root action types/hashes.

At `5:3:A:new-moon-lockbreaker-v1`, original rank5 source seat1:
- Generic/unfunded `PlayCardAction` source global turn 20: action root ordinal `84`, actual Store accepted target action ordinal `87`. Double Colorless Energy SUM attached to **Active Dedenne-GX UNB**. Final source board had no Energy on Mew V.
- Generic/**Mew-funded** policy, same source physical hand, same native Energy card and same root action ordinal `84` / accepted target ordinal `87`: the Store instead attached **Double Colorless Energy SUM to Benched Mew V FST**, Bench index 3. Final board retained the DCE on Mew V.
- Both native target actions were `storeAccepted:true` and the independent `nativeAcceptedTargetSidecar` recorded the complete public board change. The corresponding **root action ledger SHA-256 did not change**. This proves that a root-hash-only comparator would incorrectly miss the actual different card recipient.
- The Mew-target Quick Ball axis separately changed a genuine source owner search at global turn 10 from `Genesect V FST` to `Mew V FST` in the repeat-3 seat-1 champion group; despite accepted search and extra Mew board presence, it still produced zero attacks.

The recipient correction produced no native source attack or Prize in its particular New Moon matchup because the funded Pokémon remained an unevolved Mew V at terminal. DCE provides two Colorless Energy; Mew V's printed attacks in this board still require Psychic Energy, and Cross Fusion Strike belongs to Mew VMAX. This is an actionable cross-turn **evolution / funded attack sequence** bottleneck, and it prevents a premature claim that a successful Energy attachment equaled source-gameplay completion.

## Decision

Source Quick Ball target preference was already able to restore one native attack plus Prize in a separate independent run. The new 2×2 study shows that actual DCE-to-Mew funding can be achieved with existing owner-visible code without modifying the original 60 or violating native target selection, but **the tested combination still did not increase actual native attacks or native game winners on these particular fresh seeds**. The absence of payoff is a useful falsification of simplistic “Mew available + DCE attached implies attack” assumptions. Future policy must form an **Mew V -> Mew VMAX evolution -> funded Cross Fusion Strike** sequence within realistic turns and survive the opponent's response.

Do not promote a deck challenger or claim a fixed 60 improvement using these native outcomes. Native Store's accepted action is still subject to independent paper legality, opponent-policy competence and event clock vetoes; three original source 60s remain unauthenticated. Competitive games qualified **0**, selected final fixed 60 **none**, demonstrated 99.5% **no**. Keep this 2×2 analysis as a reusable original-opponent source stress case for the shared final profile.

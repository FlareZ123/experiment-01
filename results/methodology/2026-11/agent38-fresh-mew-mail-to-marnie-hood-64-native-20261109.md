## Interpretation and immediate decision

The line is **real and reproducibly exposed on fresh physical deals**, but **rare** under this source policy. One of 32 complete paired openings presented its required held, Mail-selected, legal Supporter and otherwise-Pass window. A retrospective known-seed mechanism demonstration ([42603788939](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42603788939)) likewise paid the card with an opponent reply but had no winner effect.

Consequently, do not treat Mail-to-Marnie as a decisive general opponent upgrade or reason to alter a candidate 60 from these results. Keep default-off in general headless policy until stronger source scheduling and natural opponent coverage justify it. Its value is a proven owner-safe tactical root for future opponent competence research. **More useful next structural candidate action:** identify a legal card that can actually attack the original Mew VMAX for a high Prize return, with exact deck-cost and native action coverage. We separately froze a one-slot `Drapion V LOR 118` alternative to Hood, substituting one `Lillie's Clefairy ex JTG 56` (both legal under paper Expanded B&W onward, 60 preserved), to determine whether real native games ever Bench or attack with that counter under source Mew Mail. **Do not promote it until the actual accepted attack and broader 29-rank tradeoffs are supported.**

Independent game strengths and confidence bounds here remain **unqualified**. The naive 14 source wins per 32 native games is a bot/engine measurement with six censored cases and changing limited opponent decisions, so it is specifically **not** a paper competitive win-rate estimate and cannot validate the 99.5% target. The Aichi benchmark still lacks original printed ranks 23,25,28.

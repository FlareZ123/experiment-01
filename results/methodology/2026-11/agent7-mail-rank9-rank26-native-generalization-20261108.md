## Research implication

The 29 authenticated original Top32 lists contain `Trainers' Mail` in **10 source decks (29 copies)**, which spans multiple archetypes. The separate [Trainer coverage ranking](agent7-aichi29-trainer-coverage-policy-priorities-20261108.md) shows why cross-deck generalization of owner-only action and prompt policy matters more for the fixed-deck benchmark than a single handcrafted source state.

The native-positive result establishes a shared, legal-action and physical-prompt integration path that other selected-v8 successors can reuse. It does **not** prove the existing Mail prompt's *first eligible Trainer* choice is strategically optimal. An actual fixed-60 challenger decision still requires comparable opponent-policy competence, the full 32 opposing inventories and far more complete games.

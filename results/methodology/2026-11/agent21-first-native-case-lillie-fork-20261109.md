## Tested alternative: same deal, symmetric owner-visible Lillie-after-Pass

I reused the repo's existing `actorBilateralLillieAfterPass` policy opt-in (implemented by `simulation/twinleaf/agent12-actor-only-lillie-after-pass.cjs`). It replaces an otherwise genuine Pass proposal with a physically held, owner-visible, unspent `Lillie's Determination` under printed turn/Supporter restrictions. It applies to both actual seats, with no source-rank or candidate-specific decision rule. Both players retain identical original 60-card inventories, trial seed, native policy configuration and archived source-case registration; the *only intentional additional flag* is `actorBilateralLillieAfterPass:true`. The test remains a retrospective counterfactual, so earlier observations have been preserved without relabeling it as prospective evidence.

The executable [same-seed replay and exact-root assertions](../../../simulation/evaluation/2026-11/agent21-rank5-lillie-pass-counterfactual.cjs) ran successfully in existing **manual-only** [CI run 42630536221](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42630536221), head `bd3743157d59c2777a881a1163107e02cb97095d`; [full registered native replay artifact 13681335041](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42630536221/artifacts/13681335041). The Lillie native observer recorded **one accepted**, **zero rejected** intervention, with candidate seat1 global turn2, and native Supporter quota advanced to one.

## Decision

The first candidate root32 failure is **confirmed as an actionable bot policy omission**, and a genuine printed Supporter response was executed, including eight-card draw and two additional Basic bench plays. However, **a single Lillie-after-Pass fix did not produce an attack, meaningful control payoff, or victory**. Therefore, the candidate's original failure cannot be assigned solely to missed Lillie; broader candidate resource-to-attack/control planning and owner-item/energy sequencing remain insufficient under the tested common policy. The next improvement should target the *first post-draw candidate Pass at root38* and compare possible actual paid Item/Tool/Stadium/Energy actions against the complete resource-to-attack/control line. This is an observed native owner-root failure, distinct from speculative card power.

The replay is **one selected original source deal**, with no independent paper-rules verification of the entire game, no event-clock adjudication, no independent strategy review, and no authorized paper admission. Its collected physical source-deal/fork receipts are a development witness only. The rank5 winner remains native; 29/32 source lists known, ranks23/25/28 missing; **zero competitively paper-qualified games; 99.5% unproven**. Do not add this posthoc outcome to the original prospective field benchmark denominator.

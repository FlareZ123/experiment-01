# Original Mew Ultra Ball mandatory discard: source-only DCI payment intervention

**Agent37 | 9 November 2026 | Selected previous deals, retrospective native diagnostic | All results paper-unqualified**

## Implemented native fix

Earlier genuine native Ultra Ball Mew VMAX evolution lines spent important energy and search resources because a generic mandatory two-card discard actor rated duplicate quantities without considering the planned attack line. The new source-seat-only `chooseMewUltraBallPayment` in `simulation/twinleaf/agent37/mew-ultraball-evolution.cjs` receives the *actual* live Store owner's `ChooseCardsPrompt` after the Item is played; it considers only pairs validated by the physical native `pending.validate` and, for the source's resolving Ultra Ball, restricts the offered menu to combinations that preserve Double Colorless Energy, Forest Seal Stone, Secret Box, Mew V, Mew VMAX and Genesect V when any entirely unprotected two-card pair is legal. The existing `agent47-actor-hand-discard` chooser breaks ties among safe pairs and handles all other cases. Non-Ultra Ball prompts, wrong physical seats, unavailable safe pairs and original candidate policies remain unchanged.

This is source-only owner hand management on a mandatory native prompt. It does not assume a card can be discarded, invent an extra legal play, inspect opponent private cards or derive a deck win from theoretical access. The owner root's two-nonprotected-card availability condition is now enforced *at the actual payment choice*. All code is outside Twinleaf vendor.

The current fixed protected-set is only a deliberately narrow improvement over pure printing uniqueness, not globally optimal DCI.

## Decision

The concrete source Native Store 0->1 attack rescue supports keeping safe original Ultra Ball payment as an **opt-in targeted tactical policy**, and preserving its full native before/after receipts. The retrospective chosen deals cannot provide an unbiased effect estimate. A fully frozen independent 16-arm four-new-seed A/B was registered before running at `results/aichi-2026/registrations/agent37-rank5-mew-ultraball-payment-independent-20261109.json`; its subsequent outcome must be reported separately even if no attack advantage repeats.

No confirmed field matchup win-rate effect; zero competitively qualified paper games; no single final 60 selected.

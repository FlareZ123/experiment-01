## Practical direction

Do not automatically play Hood simply because a VMAX is currently untooled under Iron Thorns. Require a present Psychic Energy and a safe draw reserve before choosing the Tool over an immediately available attack. The paid-Hood development evidence now supports this stricter owner policy as **action sequencing discipline**.

## Substantive implication

This is a **new original-source opening**, distinct from Agent 37's previous prospective Mail winner reversal on original Mew physical seed `1291419999`. It independently reproduces the same important phenomenon: a legal held Trainers' Mail play, chosen and physically executed only on a canonical source Pass with actual owner top-four selection, can materially reverse natural native winner and Prize outcomes. Here the effect transfers to an untested **Hood Shield v1** fixed candidate while the original champion is included as a calibration contender. The new seed `495999543` (rank5 source physical slot B, `candidateSlot=A`) produces both reversals, so count **one independent source-opening positive exposure**, not two independent winners. The source Mail is played twice under treatment in each affected comparison.

The evidence invalidates any *unqualified* inference that Hood v1 or champion decisively beats the rank-5 original under a source actor that repeatedly passes with usable Mail. It does **not** prove that Mail is strategically the best possible play in all such states. Search/shuffle changes future draws, and the source can still choose poor later plays. Comparing fixed decks from this tiny developmental sample would overstate competitive evidence. No card in either candidate was changed or promoted.

**Next useful experiment:** pay off an actually selected Mail Trainer in a subsequent legal turn, rather than relying only on the search/shuffle changing future draws. A direct on-otherwise-Pass **physically Mail-selected Marnie SSH** intervention has been separately registered on an already-exposed positive original rank-5 seed as a **mechanism test, not a fresh statistical replication**. Its source-only Supporter use must be native Store-accepted, respect the paper one-Supporter-per-turn restriction, appear in the discard pile, and allow a live opponent reply. Keep it separate from this prospective 16-game dataset.

## Decision

| Unchanged 60 | Natural native wins | Native caps | Possible win count over all 16 |

| Original rank1 Shadow Rider reference | 10 | 3 | **10–13** |
| New Moon Lockbreaker v1 | 6 | 6 | **6–12** |

Consequently the Shadow Rider minus New Moon win-count difference lies in **[−2,+7]** over all 16 registered groups, without assuming cap outcomes. This is a deterministic sensitivity envelope, **not a confidence interval** or paper-game performance estimate.

One rank6 source opening is physically different across the two contender decks and both of its native games capped. Excluding that group specifically from **paired counterfactual analysis** (while retaining both attempts in the registered denominator) leaves 15 source-opening-matched groups and 9 pairs where both arms naturally terminated. From the reported per-rank caps and that known exclusion, the paired, same-source-opening win-count difference lies in **[−1,+6] among 15 groups**. This bound still includes zero.

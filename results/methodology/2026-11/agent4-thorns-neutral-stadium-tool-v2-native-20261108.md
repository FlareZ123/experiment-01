# Agent 4: an owner-public neutral-Stadium/Tool extension for Iron Thorns versus Underworld Door

**8 November 2026. Developmental policy correction, default-off. Zero competitively qualified games.** The unchanged rank-6 original source 60 and original candidate 60s are used throughout; no field win-rate inference is supported.

## Concrete problem

The existing owner-public [v1 Iron Thorns guard](../../../simulation/twinleaf/agent4-underworld-visible-thorns-veto.cjs) correctly declined an unsafe Underworld Door proposal against visibly Active Iron Thorns ex with Initialization, but abstained whenever any Stadium was in play or a Tool was attached to the source VMAX. The abstention covered both potential Ability-changing effects and cards whose printed text has no Ability interaction. That leaves an avoidable native `UNKNOWN_POWER` risk in some otherwise recognizable states.

The [v2 opt-in classifier and reversible native wrapper](../../../simulation/twinleaf/2026-11/agent4-thorns-lock-neutral-stadium-tool-v2.cjs) recognize only exact, closed **native name-and-set labels** for a small class of Ability-neutral effects: `Tropical Beach BWP`, `Temple of Sinnoh ASR`, `Dimension Valley PHF`, `Chaotic Swell CEC`, `Float Stone PLF/BKT`, and `Air Balloon SSH`. The actor-only observation uses native `card.fullName`, which excludes collector numbers. The original physical-print provenance covers BWP 28/50, ASR 155/214, PHF 93, CEC 187, PLF 99, BKT 137 and SSH 156; the label-level guard does not claim to distinguish variants within the same name/set. The Stadium effects concern draw, Special Energy, attack cost, or Stadium replacement; the Tools concern retreat cost. For these exact printings, the observed public board can still satisfy the original v1 lock classifier. The implementation constructs a disposable filtered observation and reuses v1, leaving the actual owner view, Store, underlying proposer, and printed cards unchanged. The wrapper is reversible, handles native exceptions, and reports proposal and veto counts.

**Abstentions are intentional.** `Path to the Peak` and `Silent Lab` can suppress Iron Thorns's own Ability, while `Stealthy Hood` can protect its holder from opposing Ability effects. They are not eligible for the same inference. Unknown printing labels, mixed known/unknown Stadiums, altered source Pokémon, and absent visible opposing Initialization remain conservatively unmodified. This is a rules-and-card-text-defined whitelist, not proof of compatibility with every game effect.

## Field-expanded v3: real native eligibility versus actual play exposure

The original v2 module remains frozen. The separately versioned [v3 policy](../../../simulation/twinleaf/2026-11/agent4-thorns-lock-field-neutral-v3.cjs) adds **eight Stadium families** (`Sky Field ROS`, `Parallel City BKT`, `Artazon PAL`, `PokéStop PGO`, `Shopping Center EVS`, `Risky Ruins MEG`, `Thunder Mountain Prism Star LOT`, `Wondrous Labyrinth Prism Star TEU`) and `U-Turn Board UNM`, after checking their print text. These have Bench, attack cost/damage, search, or Tool/retreat-related effects. The v3 classifier still requires the actual owner-visible Active Iron Thorns Initialization, a matching VMAX Underworld proposal, and no excluded ability-changing Stadium/Tool effects.

Presence-only audit of the authenticated 29 original 60s: the frozen v2 neutral families have **40 physical copies in 15 lists**; the v3 additions have **46 copies in 25 lists**; the union is **86 physical copies and at least one in each of the 29 lists**. This is an inventory upper bound for possible future situations, **not 29 proven applicable matchups or occurrences on the board**. In the relevant original Iron Thorns opponents, ranks 6, 24 and 29 have a `Thunder Mountain Prism Star`; ranks 6 and 29 also have `Tropical Beach`. The rank-1 Shadow Rider candidate contains two `Sky Field`, one `Dimension Valley`, and one `Float Stone`. The new Sky Field and Thunder Mountain cases thus have a concrete possible role in the relevant opponent/candidate inventories. The direct native v3 joint [Store test](../../../tests/2026-11/agent4-thorns-lock-field-neutral-v3.native.test.cjs) passes each new admitted Stadium/Tool family while holding the visible opposing Ability state fixed.

The first broader v3 native test [42535555277](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42535555277) failed because the pinned card manager could not retrieve `Grand Tree SCR` by the expected name. That label was **excluded fail-closed** from the native v3 whitelist, pending a correct engine alias or research-owned implementation; this is no claim that the physical paper Grand Tree card is illegal. Agent 18 received the exact blocking lookup.

[Native run 42535968614](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42535968614) then passed the full new v3 native test matrix (**3 test groups, each with multiple actual-card scenarios**), all **3 v3 pure tests**, all **7 frozen v2 pure tests**, and a same-checkout v2-versus-v3 comparison on **16 real native original-deck attempts**. This result provides a natural-deal regression against v2. It **does not establish an incremental game-strength benefit** from the eight new Stadium classes or the U-Turn Board. It also lacks paper-game certification and meaningful win-rate measurement. The next valuable evidence is an authentic original-60 owner decision containing one of the newly whitelisted cards **and** a proposed blocked Underworld Door, followed by a legal alternative, a consequential opponent response, and a subsequent own-turn decision. Until that event appears in the shared two-player policy, more isolated card-family tests have diminishing returns.

The Aichi field remains 29/32 authenticated name/count lists; ranks 23, 25 and 28 remain without exact original 60s. Neither the 99.5% aspirational target nor a selected final legal fixed 60 follows from this result.

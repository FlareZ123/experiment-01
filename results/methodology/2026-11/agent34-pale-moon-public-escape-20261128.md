## Last-Prize KO is not always a terminal loss

This code now explicitly distinguishes printed **Prize denial** from normal Prize award before overriding the worker's Pass. `Life Dew PLF 107` and a `Shedinja LOT 95` attached as a Tool subtract one Prize, so on a one-Prize Pokémon the queued effect can remove the Pokémon without ending a 1-Prize opponent's game. `Shedinja DRX 48`'s Empty Shell Ability, `Lillie's Poké Doll CEC`, `Snorlax Doll PAR`, and `Robo Substitute Team Flare Gear PHF` are known zero-Prize printings. The adapter declines a forced emergency response in those known situations. A VMAX, VSTAR, Pokémon V/GX/ex or EX normally awards at least two Prizes; subtracting one remains enough for a last-Prize win, so the guard allows a rescue. `Legacy Energy TWM` does not prevent a Prize from an effect-based Knock Out because its text requires KO **by attack damage**; Pale Moon's scheduled KO is not damage.

The printing-aware guard is a conservative tactical filter, not a complete effective-Prize oracle: suppression of Life Dew/Empty Shell, other lesser-known Prize modifications and printed exceptions remain possible. The pinned native Store remains the final execution authority. The branch includes source-specific positives and negatives, with a further local pure-helper total of **11/11 groups passed**. Neither that count nor this card-text reasoning is a qualified competitive win-rate result.

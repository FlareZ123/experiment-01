### Important current-head corrections

- PR #69 has already added several common root/Ability branches (including Teammates, Raihan, Acerola, Iono, Forest Seal Stone / Star Alchemy, Underworld Door, Wonder Tag, and Climactic Gate). Treat these as existing work to preserve and test, not missing features.
- At reviewed PR #69 head, the five remaining unsupported root Trainer names are **VS Seeker, Hisuian Heavy Ball, Night Stretcher, Float Stone, and Pokémon Ranger** (six physical cards). Their prompt-choice helpers do not make them playable at root.
- Wobbuffet appears in the source/lock resolver, but Lockbridge still lacks a Wobbuffet root-play decision and Temple of Sinnoh root-play decision.
- PR #69 does not read the already actor-visible `itemPlayLocked` field. The actor K1 projection also omits certified `knownPrized` / `prizeInferenceCertified`, and the V3 runner does not wire the existing K1 helpers into the live decision path.
- The deterministic setup prompt tests do not establish setup strategy: GO_FIRST is always Yes, and starting Basics are selected in native order. The GO_FIRST prompt belongs only to the coin-flip winner; the coin loser receives no such prompt. Both lists contain one Radiant Eternatus and one Tapu Lele-GX; at least one of these entry-trigger Basics appears alongside another Basic in 19.667% of valid 7-card hands, where a different Basic can start while preserving the trigger. Evaluate preserving Radiant and Tapu Lele in hand when another Basic can start and the relevant Gate/Wonder Tag line is strategically desired; do not hard-code unconditional retention. The full two-VMAX Gate line requires three empty Bench slots before playing Radiant, not two.

These are source-level capability findings, not proof that each card should be played in every game or that an omission causes a loss.

Reuse the repository's existing role-identity path rather than inventing a second profile registry:

For the approved successor only, thread the exact candidate options from the frozen role identity into worker init and require equality between the hashed identity options and the delivered options. The candidate protocol should allow only the agreed profile keys; opponent workers must retain the existing empty config. The candidate worker should accept only the two registered profile IDs and their exact schema/version, rejecting unknown or malformed profiles. Keep options public/non-sensitive and preserve the existing physical-context guard. Both profiles must share one candidate policy-code SHA.

Minimum profile-binding regressions: options-hash mismatch; profile/deck SHA mismatch; unknown profile; missing profile; extra config key; worker init altered after identity construction; correct Hood profile; correct Lockbridge profile; and unchanged empty opponent config. ## One-release acceptance criteria

A successor release should not be admitted until **all** of the following are bound to the same immutable release:

1. **Exact deck/profile identity:** both original JSON lists and their printing-aware fingerprints; one shared policy code SHA; two immutable profile/configuration digests; no deck substitution.
2. **Complete root-action matrix:** for every Trainer and Pokémon in each exact 60, and every Energy attachment line, classify root action/attachment as supported, state-conditionally supported, deliberately declined, or unsupported. Include manual Energy attachment recipient choices, legal target validation, and once-per-turn resource use. A later prompt helper does not count unless the root action is reachable.
3. **Prompt and effect receipts:** exercise the full search, multi-select, energy-payment, retreat, Stadium, Tool, Ability, attack, and recovery sequences with exact card printings, legal payment, correct count, both physical seats, and both turn-player orientations. Every selected action must bind to the native effect receipt.
4. **Actor-only K0/K1 contract:** the runner wraps the current `native.observe(state, seat)` boundary with an actor-seat-bound K1 lifecycle, captures only at a genuine owner-deck `ChooseCardsPrompt`, retains the snapshot, binds owner seat/prompt/turn provenance, and advances it across relevant Store transitions. Every relevant transition advances the epoch; deck movement stales deck membership but may retain certified fully-Prized facts if Prize composition is unchanged; Prize-composition changes clear `knownPrized` and `prizeInferenceCertified`. Only `assessRequiredDeckPrinting`-derived bounded facts may reach the worker; K0/unknown/partial/stale states remain conservative. Keep `publicEventReceipt` as a separate host-built allowlisted public-history input, never raw Store state. Add decision-invariance tests: mutate concealed opponent hand/deck/face-down Prize data and physical seed while holding the actor-visible observation and allowed public receipt fixed; the worker's decision must remain identical. Exercise ownerK1 current/valid, absent, stale-epoch, and invalidated states.
5. **Deck-specific branches:** prove Ranger/VS Seeker and Item-lock behavior for Hood; prove Wobbuffet/Temple, self-lock timing, Stadium opportunity cost, Marshadow Resetting Hole counterplay, and the non-Item Budew response for Lockbridge. Distinguish Items from Tools using authoritative semantics; include Tapu Lele Wonder Tag → Ranger and the fact that VS Seeker cannot retrieve Ranger while Items are locked. Test Active Klefki and Tool-live Garbotoxin suppressing Wobbuffet. Do not assume one profile's answer exists in the other.
6. **Strategy challenges:** freeze development and held-out state envelopes for (a) pre-deal GO_FIRST, and (b) hand-aware starting Active/Bench choices. The repository manual and pinned Twinleaf reducer place GO_FIRST before shuffle/opening hands/mulligans/Basic selection; only the coin-flip winner receives the prompt. Test the candidate as coin-flip winner in either physical seat and as coin-flip loser receiving no GO_FIRST prompt. It must be a profile/field-prior decision independent of future hand and opponent setup. Starting Active/Bench choices occur after the 7-card hand but before opponent Basics are revealed. Test optional Bench occupancy, evaluating whether to preserve Radiant Eternatus and Tapu Lele-GX hand-to-Bench triggers when another Basic can start and the line is strategically desired, and the three-empty-slot pre-Radiant Gate requirement. Include an Active-selection negative control for Dialga-GX UPR 100: it is a Basic Dragon with no Ability, its printed attacks require Metal, and both candidate lists contain only Basic Psychic Energy/no Metal or Special Energy, so do not select it as an attacker merely because it appears first in native hand order. Include the Lockbridge Wobbuffet Active lock vs Gate fork (including Tapu Lele → Arven → Forest Seal Stone → Star Alchemy → Radiant under Item lock, which still requires Wobbuffet to leave Active before Climactic Gate), Latias ex retreat support, Marshadow Resetting Hole from the Bench, and profile-specific GO_FIRST priors: 14/29 sourced lists contain Budew (provisional); with ranks 23/25/28 missing, the complete 32-rank equal-weight prevalence is bounded 14/32=43.75% to 17/32=53.125% without assumptions about the missing lists. The current V3 policy receives no opponent rank/deck identity at GO_FIRST, so this is a field-level prior only, not matchup-conditioned input. A valid opening hand contains at least one of six one-Energy attackers (four Shadow Rider V, Mimikyu GRI 58, Girafarig LOT 94) plus a Psychic Energy in 46.720% of cases (40.245% of raw hands); the Shadow Rider V + Psychic Energy subset is 34.197% of valid hands. These are access priors only, not attack rates: Mimikyu Copycat and Girafarig Get Lost have additional conditions. The policy cannot see the future hand at GO_FIRST. Also distinguish first-turn Supporter rules: the first player cannot play an ordinary Supporter on global turn 1, while the second player may play one on their first turn. Thus Tapu Lele → Arven → Forest Seal Stone is a second-player Item-lock recovery line; first-player Gate access must use already-held Tools/Abilities or legal Item search rather than Arven. Also test Underworld Door before Gate, Fairy Zone/Weakness breakpoints, Pale Moon-GX extra-Energy and retreat escape, Tool/Stadium suppression, Budew, Prize-recovery, and each deck's actual root Trainer choices. Keep `WANT_TO_DRAW_CARDS` correctly classified as the optional bonus draw after an opponent mulligan, not the candidate's own mulligan decision. No synthetic test count is a game result.
7. **Native execution and independent review:** only after a separate authorization and accepted execution gates, run the actual frozen policy (not scripted helpers) through complete native games. Require resolved prompts, no engine errors, actor-only inputs, source-bound opponent policy, complete terminal/effect receipts, and separate pre-deal candidate-policy and opponent-policy strategy reviewers. Those reviewers must be distinct from each other and from policy/bundle producers, and must have no authorship or material conflict for the relevant rank; retain the separate post-game paper/clock adjudication. Apply the existing issue #39 reviewer-role separation rather than a generic single-reviewer sign-off.
8. **No early competitive aggregate:** the current field is 29/32, 14/32 opponent modules exist but 0/14 have demonstrated full-game competence, and the natural-64 receipt validator remains unadmitted. No 99.5% or full-Top-32 claim follows until the 32 source lists and competent rank policies are admitted.

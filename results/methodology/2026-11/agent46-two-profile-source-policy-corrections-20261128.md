Agent2's independent exact-worker matrix for items 2–3 found 12 expected decision changes and 16 preserved controls, across two profiles and seats. [Independent handoff](../../../communications/agent46/2026-11/20261127T2300Z_agent2-exact-worker-28case-receipt.md).

A stale `abilitySuppressions` row *alone* is not proof. This avoids both missing an immediate win through active Garbotoxin and accepting a phantom one after a projected source leaves play. Regression uses both positive suppression sources and no-source/live-Ability controls. Agent2's independent worker matrix found 8 expected changes and 12 preserved controls. ## Direct-damage terminal certification through nondefensive Special Energy — `8612983b`, `6ddd751b7879d8f24b9523b4cfd1e81a69b8a40f`

A separately discovered benchmark-relevant failure: `cleanPublicTerminalView` formerly rejected a real direct final-Prize KO if *any* opposing Pokémon held a Special Energy other than Dragon-only Double Dragon Energy. This is too conservative when the attached Energy only changes its own Energy types, attachment-triggered setup, retreat, or the **damage it deals**; none changes direct incoming damage or prevents the opponent being Knocked Out. Critically, the attached English corpus's Mist Energy TEF text explicitly prevents **effects** of attacks and says damage is not an effect; Astral damage-counter placements remain blocked, but a printed Max Geist direct KO is unaffected.

The corrected `directTerminalSafeSpecialEnergy` has an exact name-prefix closed set: Double Colorless, Capture, Jet, Reversal, Counter, Unit Energy families, Memory, Recycle, Prism, Powerful Colorless, Mist, plus pre-existing Dragon-only Double Dragon. These identities were checked against the local English card corpus. This gate applies **only to a present direct damaging terminal attack**, not to Astral/copy effects, attack planning, unknown printings, or projected future board hypothetical mode. Spiky Energy, Legacy Energy, Stone Fighting Energy, Horror Psychic Energy, and unknown Energy remain unsafe for this narrow terminal certificate.

The unchanged original Aichi `results/aichi-2026/full-decklists.json` has **12 of 29 known ranks with at least one newly admitted harmless Special Energy identity**: ranks **2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 15, 17, 24, 26, 29**; other ten known ranks have Double Dragon Energy, which was already admitted in the Dragon direct mode. This establishes *opportunity for the specific prior policy under-admission*, not how often these Energy end up attached on a terminal turn or a change in any game win rate. The rank7 Recycle Energy returns to hand on discard; this does not change an immediate Prize-win KO. Powerful Colorless raises **outgoing** damage, not incoming damage.

Both-profile/both-seat decision tests include each named Energy, reactive-unsafe negatives, and Mist-protected Astral negatives. Exact updated source-worker run in an isolated V8 test/assert shim at branch commit `6ddd751b...`: **76/79 root test groups passed, 1,647 assertions**; the three missing tests import native prompt-projection or owner-prize modules beyond this shim. ## Source-field direct winning-attack certificate: support, lock, Stadium and Tool matrices (28 Nov)

A principal tactical failure mode emerged from extending the source-card evidence: a present, fully funded **Max Geist / Shadow Mist** that would take the last Prize(s) was deprived of its final-attack priority if *any* opponent Pokémon held an Ability, Tool, or Stadium not on a tiny initial whitelist. The card effect may be substantial in general yet irrelevant to **incoming immediate direct damage**. Tactical opportunity from a current winning attack should not be silently displaced by setup cards.

The successor now contains source-printing-scoped **closed positive matrices**, with conservative negatives and exact current-play enforcement:

- **Entry/draw/search utility Abilities:** `Dedechange`/Dedenne-GX, `Dark Asset`/Crobat V, `Squawk and Seize`/Squawkabilly ex, `Flip the Script`/Fezandipiti ex, `Happy Match`/Eldegoss V, `Quick Search`/Pidgeot ex, `Air Mail`/Pidgeotto, `Fan Call`/Fan Rotom, `Fusion Strike System`/Genesect V, `Resetting Hole`/Marshadow UNB, `Extra Energy Bomb`/Electrode-GX CES; commit `8e8d98b22dd805a13b003e2ed3680c27de58da27`. - **Lock Abilities with no direct-damage modifier:** `Initialization`/Iron Thorns ex, `Bide Barricade`/Wobbuffet, `Irritating Pollen`/Vileplume AOR, `Disgusting Pollen`/Vileplume BUS, `Sentinel`/Stoutland BCR, `Mischievous Lock`/Klefki, `Neutralizing Gas`/Galarian Weezing, `Power of Alchemy`/Alolan Muk, `Midnight Fluttering`/Flutter Mane; commits `8b3ecfd7ae0cfabd493de7e1f5e51873e9c57ebf` and accuracy correction `266ad9f6be94cb4949296b30f6f41e9eb0c2bb58`. ## Original rank-4/rank-17 Raichu Counter and Unit Energy: real Energy Drive math

**Source patch** `df4af649379a12e249d761bf6b5cd3a7cbd43c67`; physically correctly resolved Prize-win fixture `7335d2018b6834ff437301905f9781ec8218f45f`.

In original Aichi ranks **4 and 17**, the Raichu/Electrode lists carry **four Counter Energy CIN 100 and four Unit Energy LPM UPR 138** each. The candidate's real Tapu Lele-GX Energy Drive directly scales with Energy units attached to both Active Pokémon. The existing `publicEnergySupply` refused both those source Energy names as unknown, so even an immediately lethal fully paid Energy Drive could be wrongly declined. The new source-side supply projection implements:

- Counter Energy: 1 Colorless normally, but if the holder is **not Pokémon-GX or Pokémon-EX** and its player has *more* remaining Prize cards, count **2 units of every type**, respecting physical Prize lead and printed exclusions;
- Unit Energy LPM/GFW/FDF: exactly **1 unit** offering the corresponding three types (LPM = Lightning, Psychic, Metal); unknown variants remain unresolved rather than guessed;
- `publicEnergyCanPay` adds the Wondrous Labyrinth extra Colorless requirement for non-Fairy source attacks when the Stadium is live/unknown, mirroring the candidate-cost gate. The new explicit supply and payment functions are exposed through the existing non-enumerable `__test` interface only, without broadening the actual worker request API.

The exact decision fixture uses original Alolan Raichu UNM Stage1 with two attached Counter and one Unit LPM versus a candidate Tapu Lele-GX with two Psychic and **one remaining Prize**. When the source has four remaining Prizes, Counter empowers the Raichu to 5 Energy units and Energy Drive reaches 140 into 110HP, taking the final Prize. When the source has only one remaining Prize, the same attachments produce 3 units, Energy Drive reaches only 100, and the deterministic final-KO override is correctly withheld. Also verified source Electrode-GX does not empower attached Counter, unknown Unit variants remain unknown, and the source's Wondrous +Colorless attack-cost distinction holds.

**Actual current V8 worker/protocol/root suite after patch:** **83 of 86 test groups passed, 1,979 assertions**; the three unavailable groups import additional native host modules, not policy failures. Earlier eight pure setup groups also passed in shim. No Node-native game or 32-match result; no cardlist or profile changes.

## Causal interpretation and next solution

**Keep VIP-only as the viable narrow early-turn policy candidate, and isolate the exact cost of the two added funding policies.** The return of the rank-11 paid-attack terminal and the rank-9 terminal after withdrawing funding supports an actual **composition-level interaction/regression**. Since two flags were withdrawn together, no single subcomponent is yet proven responsible. Freeze the three existing archived arms; a new limited retrospective mechanistic fourth/fifth arm can enable each added funding flag separately on the same known high-value contexts. Future prospective validation needs genuinely unseen deals.

In earlier combined treatment the recipient logic had physically attached **at least five Basic Psychic Energy cards** to public Benched Mewtwo in ranks 5, 9 and 26, yet no copied attack was selected there before censoring/root cap. The unresolved tactical bottleneck is **Active mobility** of funded Benched Mewtwo and turn/Supporter/attachment contention, not merely attaching Energy. A native owner-only pivot from a nonattacking Active into an Energy-paid Mewtwo, with legal Retreat or physically held switching effect, is more directly productive than more inventory counts.

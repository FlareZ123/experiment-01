# Entire authenticated Aichi field: full-game Hood Shield 60 versus champion 60

**Agent 3, 9 November 2026. Two complete unchanged sixty-card decks, 116 fresh independent native Store games, both candidate seats, all 29 authenticated original source 60s.**

## Decision result

The modified [Hood Shield Shadow Rider v1 complete 60](../../aichi-2026/candidate-hood-shadow-rider.md) is **the most promising modified fixed 60 directly compared against the champion so far** in these experiments. It retained the same 31 native wins as the champion, with 13 versus 17 losses, but added 3 action caps/one native error. Actual physically matched, both-terminal pairs yielded **six game-winner reversals favorable to Hood Shield versus three favorable to the champion**. These are **developer-policy outcomes**, insufficient to call the modified list a confirmed improvement in competitive paper games, still less the extraordinary 99.5% target.

| Direct native outcome | Source rank1 Shadow Rider | Modified Hood Shield v1 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Pre-registered source opportunities | 58 | 58 |
| Actually completed native wins | 31 | 31 |
| Native losses | 17 | **13** |
| Native full terminals | **48** | 44 |
| Root action caps | **8** | 11 |
| Native engine errors | **2** | 3 |

Exact original source physical openings and original starter seats matched for **55/58** pairs; **41** pairs reached terminal in both arms. Three original-deal mismatches, ranks **8,16,26**, are excluded from paired winner-reversal evidence. The run and archive completed **without changing candidate inventories during observation**. The common owner decision policy made no privileged Hood-specific Arven choices, so smart Arven→Stealthy Hood action remains an untested improvement.

### Research decision

**Keep Hood Shield v1 as the top current *modified-deck hypothesis* for a better one-list target**, and maintain source rank1 Shadow Rider as the native developmental baseline until further high-quality comparative evidence. A concrete high-value next experiment is an identical-seed, source-comparable **Arven→Stealthy Hood resource decision policy** against active Iron Thorns/Garbotoxin, then testing 58 originally pre-registered source groups with the complete fixed 60 and a true opponent-capable policy. Preserve all opponent source lists, missing-rank caveats and full game decisions. Investigate the three champion-favorable reversals (Snorlax rank7, Absol Garbodor rank11, Regidrago rank12) to identify whether decreased Tapu Lele/VS Seeker access explains the losses.

## More precise actionable failure revealed by actual board positions

The archive's physical post-Store receipts show `Gengar & Mimikyu-GX TEU` and `Latias ex SSP` **both in play together** in **three of eight** B contexts, which is an improvement in co-occurrence over the first non-Genesect eight-context panel (zero of eight). But the B policy still never triggered even once:

- **8201 A:** first co-occurrence root74/global turn9. Active **Mewtwo & Mew-GX**, Gengar Benched without Energy; no Basic Psychic in hand in that snapshot.
- **8203 A:** first co-occurrence root59/global turn8. Active **Duskull**, Gengar Benched without Energy; no Basic Psychic in hand at that root.
- **8203 B:** first co-occurrence root24/global turn3. Active **Duskull**, Gengar Benched without Energy; no Basic Psychic in hand at that root.

The existing narrowly staged Gengar planner `simulation/twinleaf/2026-11/agent20-gengar-horror-house-tempo.cjs` only funds/retreats a Benched Gengar when the owner Active is exactly `Tapu Lele-GX GRI`. Later physical records show the three contexts each contain owner-turn snapshots where Gengar+Latias coexist with an **unspent-looking physical Basic Psychic in owner hand** while Gengar has no attached Energy, frequently with Active Duskull or Latias. Those are **candidate causal opportunities**, subject to the owner's actual once-per-turn Energy quota, relevant Pokémon restrictions and the relative value of other funding lines. A general mobility planner must compare those costs and native offers; it cannot simply attach to Gengar in every such state.

This is the next physical-action bottleneck for this strategy: **Gengar-specific setup policy is overfitted to one stranded Tapu Lele opening**, while real independent deals present Active Mewtwo/Duskull/Latias and different attached-Energy states. Generalize the candidate policy using owner-public funded attacks and valid retreat, retain ability of Mewtwo to attack, then preregister fresh independent paired contests. Do not retest the already failed global Path-after-Pass line.

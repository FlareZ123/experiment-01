**Agent 23, 9 November 2026.** Completed 8/8 same-original-source-deal physical native comparisons, deliberate replay of previously known original rank-6 donor opportunities, no fresh field win-rate inference.

## Why this mattered

The original rank-6 Iron Thorns ex native owner prompt had a real operational flaw: its mandatory Volt Cyclone Energy transfer could remove a crucial Lightning supply while retaining a single Colorless unit on the Active, leaving future printed [Lightning, Colorless, Colorless] attacks unaffordable. Agent45 previously demonstrated a source-only donor counterfactual with two useful selected native cases. Our independent contribution was to integrate a paper-bounded donor selection into the **shared all-turn owner prompt decoder** `simulation/twinleaf/agent4-allturn-owner-volt-attach-energy.cjs`, rather than a one-off postattack replay hook. This chooses a real attached physical Energy card through the actual actor-owned `AttachEnergyPrompt` and `pending.validate`, using public Stadium state and actual residual attack-cost calculation. The default fallback remains the generic original for unsupported printed Energy or Stadium.

## What this proves, and what it does not

The new **shared all-turn** owner priority is actually exercised in the full pinned Twinleaf engine, on both physical source seats, and can correct a meaningful tactical deficiency with genuine attack/Prize consequences. This adds native confidence to the earlier Agent45 donor hypothesis and upgrades our v3 integration beyond pure regression.

The seeds are **historically selected**, the bot policies and simulator do not qualify as strong competitive paper play, and there is no evidence that this is the best donor choice against every opposing line or that a deck reaches the project's 99.5% field target. The only demonstrated competitive advance is that the **opponent model itself** has a previously exploitable flaw corrected in two real Store trajectories.

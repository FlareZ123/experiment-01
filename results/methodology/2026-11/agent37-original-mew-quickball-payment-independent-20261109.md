## Interpretation and next work

The structural hypothesis is valid only as a conditional action-choice improvement: actual owner menu and actual native prompt allowed a dead Battle VIP Pass to replace a more playable Muscle Band discard. The **fresh prospective outcome estimate is null**; it does not justify placing this guard into the fixed common policy or comparing any fixed-deck whole-field win rate. Any additional targeted work should emphasize source **turn-level attack/Prize conversion** and adversarial replies rather than repeating low-exposure dead-card payment A/B samples. Resource saving is useful when it changes a future playable line, and preserving Muscle Band is not automatically better against every opponent.

All 16 are developmental native attempts. **Zero paper-competitively qualified games; missing original Aichi ranks 23/25/28; no selected fixed 60; no evidence of 99.5%.** Native opponent-policy and Japanese Extra card/clock qualification remain independent open gates.

**Agent20 · 12 November 2026 · frozen before native attempts.** This is a developmental experiment, not a deck promotion or competitive result.

## Question

Does the shared owner-public dynamic attack-fuel discard frontier improve the New Moon Lockbreaker v1 candidate's ability to retain and attach an Energy that completes a currently available Active printed attack, including a publicly available Mewtwo & Mew-GX Perfection donor attack, when a native mandatory discard prompt would otherwise remove every qualifying Energy?

This targets the concrete gap exposed by the earlier New Moon v2 VIP study: physically assembling Mewtwo/Gengar/Latias did not reliably produce a funded Perfection attack. The earlier eight-context v2 panel recorded three candidate native wins per arm and zero treatment winner reversals; its next bottleneck was Energy funding and attack initiation, not another VIP recipient preference. The earlier Lillie+Gengar native winner reversal remains a single posthoc source-deal result, not a matchup estimate.

## Decision rule under test

The reused Agent47 v3 selector changes the generic payment only when:

1. A currently available printed Active attack is unfunded.
2. One known Energy attachment from the owner's hand would make at least one eligible printed attack payable.
3. The generic native-valid discard would consume every copy of every such Energy option.
4. Another exact multiset in the native `ChooseCardsPrompt.validate` menu preserves at least one option.

The bounded catalog includes Basic Energy, Double Colorless Energy, and the verified `Telepath [P] Energy M3` mapping; Temple of Sinnoh reduces Special Energy to one Colorless unit. Public Mewtwo & Mew-GX Perfection donor attacks are considered only when the donor is actually visible on the owner's Bench or in public discard and the public Ability veto permits it. Unknown Special Energy and unsupported states abstain. The native Store remains authoritative for prompt acceptance, attachment, attack declaration, and resolution.

This is a narrow one-attachment frontier, not a general hand-discard optimizer and not an assertion that nominal attack payment dominates all strategic costs.

It deliberately does not credit future-turn Energy access from Powerglass (which attaches a Basic Energy from discard at end of turn), recovery lines, or other delayed acceleration. The tested decision is whether a currently held Energy completes a currently available attack with one legal manual attachment now.

## Limitations

This is eight developmental contexts against one original-rank-5 opponent. The New Moon list is a proposed fixed 60, not an Aichi Top 32 list. Twinleaf's supported mechanics and native prompt execution are not a substitute for independent paper Expanded legality, current errata, complete human play, or an event-clock audit. The experiment cannot establish a Top 32 aggregate, a field win rate, or the 99.5% target. No final fixed deck is selected by this protocol.

The separate 10 November Fog-energy experiment failed in its preflight/test step before native attempts; it supplies no Fog-policy game evidence and is not reused as this study's seed panel.

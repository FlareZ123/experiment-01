## Material observed bottleneck, with genuine root receipt

At original rank 5 group `5:3504:A:new-moon-lockbreaker-v1`, P0S1, the source in physical seat 1 attached **Double Colorless Energy to Active Genesect V on global turn 12**. Owner-only Pass observations show that DCE still on that Genesect on turns 14, 16 and repeatedly through turn 48. The source's Benched Mew V became **Mew VMAX on turn 46**, never received its own DCE, and never attacked. The v5 retreat proposal required a separate already DCE-funded Mew VMAX and accordingly found no valid opportunity.

This is a concrete opportunity-cost failure: an owner-native attachment spent the only visible DCE on an off-axis Active while the intended attacker stayed unfunded. A previously implemented [owner-visible Mew DCE recipient selector](../../../simulation/twinleaf/agent26-owner-visible-mew-dce-recipient.cjs) conservatively attaches held DCE to unfunded Mew V or Mew VMAX, preserving immediately funded damage attacks. Reusing it addresses the root Energy choice.

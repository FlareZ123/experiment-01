The one fixed 60-card New Moon Lockbreaker candidate and all eight authenticated original source 60s were unchanged.

## Decision

The earlier historical rank-11 [full-game paid Perfection win reversal](agent24-v8-paid-perfection-native-eight-arm-20261108.md) remains a genuine same-deal *positive policy counterfactual*. The new independent 16 deals reveal the **opportunity/execution bottleneck**: the existing policy rarely reaches a state in which its opt-in paid Perfection root is selected. It would be incorrect to convert the historical single positive example into an estimated 99.5%, or to treat this fresh zero-activation exposure as evidence that a resolved Perfection attack is weak.

**Next advancement must create a genuinely reachable paid attacking opportunity earlier** by arranging Mewtwo & Mew-GX, a public/accessible donor, an appropriate Active/promotion, and sufficient attached Energy with live owner-only search and attachment actions. Existing Agent24 `bilateralPerfectionBenchFunding` and Agent44 `bilateralBasicEnergyRecipientFrontier` are separately implemented candidate setups. They should be subjected to a different preregistered native causal test, including the opponent reply and subsequent owner turn, before common-policy promotion. A repeated scan for the same late Perfection prompt with the unchanged policy would add little. The field's three repeated native bot-decoder failure contexts should be separately repaired for a valid whole-field benchmark.

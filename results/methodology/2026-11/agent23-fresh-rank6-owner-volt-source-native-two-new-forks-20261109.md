## Use for later deck evaluation

Use the shared integrated owner Volt v3 as a serious adversarial-policy option on future fresh rank6 source cases, preserving the old baseline and source-only original physical-deal comparison. The important game-level outcome is whether the stronger source response creates Prizes or wins against a fixed 60 with proper paper-rule and opponent-quality admission, rather than accumulating additional attacks. A held-out larger sample, complete turn-sequencing/Bench-recipient opportunity cost and actual tournament clock are still needed. No fixed final 60-card winner and no 99.5% field performance has been established.

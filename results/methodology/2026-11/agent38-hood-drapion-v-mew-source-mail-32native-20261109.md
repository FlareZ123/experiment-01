**9 November 2026. Actual native 32-game registered candidate comparison. Does not justify promoting the Drapion variant.**

## Structural intervention

Current Hood Shield v1 60 has **two Lillie's Clefairy ex JTG 56**. A separate complete **fixed 60** was built at `results/aichi-2026/candidate-hood-drapion-mew-responder-v1.json` with exactly:

- **−1 Lillie's Clefairy ex JTG 56**;
- **+1 Drapion V LOR 118**.

Every other exact printing and count is identical. Card archive confirms Drapion V's printed `Wild Style`: each opposing Single Strike, Rapid Strike or Fusion Strike Pokémon in play reduces a Colorless attack requirement, down to zero Colorless with four such opponents; `Dynamic Tail` attacks for 190 base damage and damages one of the attacker's Pokémon by 60. Mew VMAX FST has Darkness Weakness and is a three-Prize target. Against actual rank5 Mew V/VMAX + Genesect V, this is a plausible high-impact tactical counter. It is conditional on the card being available, in the Active Spot, with Wild Style enabled; source `Path to the Peak` can disable Drapion V's Rule Box Ability. Native checks must demonstrate gameplay action rather than assert benefit from printed type.

The original fixed contender Hood v1 remains intact. This experimental variant is **not** nominated as best60.

## Concrete decision

**Do not promote this one-slot Drapion 60.** It has the desired *printed capability* and one genuine native three-attack witness, but **performed worse in the only ten interpretable paired native terminals**, and the sole direct Dynamic Tail exposure had a mismatched original-source opener. Merely having a hard counter in the candidate deck did not make that counter regularly available, positionable or useful; the meta opponent's Path to the Peak and one-slot search access remain tactical constraints. Next material candidate improvement would require a genuine access/positioning policy for Drapion plus adversarial Source Path handling, followed by a full cross-archetype evaluation proving the sacrificed Clefairy doesn't damage matchups.

The overall target remains one **unchanged** 60 against 32 original opponents. Authentic opponent printings still number 29 of 32; ranks23/25/28 missing. No competitive paper game has been qualified; 99.5% remains an aspiration, and this experiment is unqualified as a paper win-rate estimate.

# Agent45: independently signed paper-competitive admission and first authentic intake

**9 November 2026 | operational negative-control release.** This contribution adds a potential *positive* acceptance route for one frozen original-source game, with a production trust root that has **no reviewers yet**. The first real candidate game has native evidence and **fails admission** because essential paper, clock, owner-choice and independent adjudication evidence is absent. No 60-card deck is selected and zero paper-competitive games have qualified.

## Implementation shipped

- [Independent admission module](../../../simulation/evaluation/agent45-independent-competitive-admission.cjs) binds the full canonical game bundle and all its receipts to a SHA-256 digest. It reuses [the existing structural bilateral competence and official Aichi clock gate](../../../simulation/evaluation/agent45-bilateral-competence-gate.cjs). It expects a verified natural paper-compatible result or independently adjudicated stage-specific time-call result, a causal source→opponent→source episode, common two-seat policy and reviewed printed payment, targets, hidden-information scope and original physical provenance.
- The bundle needs nine immutable, hashed evidence objects: registration, each complete printed 60, both-player physical deal, full accepted-root tape, every owner payment and target transition, paper rules, uninterrupted official clock, and policy/counterplay assessment. The manifest contains actual evidence references and content digests. A manifest alone is a claim; the independent reviewers must inspect the original artifacts.
- [Production trust root](../../../simulation/evaluation/agent45-authorized-reviewers.json) starts with an empty `reviewers` array. Only an independently authorized, code-reviewed trust update may register reviewer IDs, the allowed `paper`/`clock`/`strategy` roles and their pinned Ed25519 public keys. Three separately named reviewers must sign a review containing their role, same exact bundle SHA-256, agreed paper winner, approval and an independently reviewable signed review-evidence digest. Reviewers must differ from the bundle producer and one another. A producer-supplied reviewer list is not consulted for production admission.
- [Single-file tests](../../../tests/2026-11/agent45-independent-competitive-admission.test.cjs) include the synthetic positive *signature/structural condition* and genuine negative controls for tampering, forged or unauthorized identities, unsigned or duplicated review, missing strategy coverage, incomplete manifest, caps, asymmetric actors, unpaid resources, absent owner source continuation and unsupported clock/winner decisions. Even the successful signed synthetic fixture returns `competitiveAdmission:false`; production admission remains false with the empty trust root.
- The existing [manual-only bilateral workflow](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/blob/main/.github/workflows/agent36-bilateral-competence-v9.yml) runs this SFT in its `contract_only` lane, with no Twinleaf compile or added push-trigger workflow. **[Real successful CI run 42592546138](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42592546138)** executed the new test against the actual shared competence gate on commit `e0be6a240351379361f6244c2bd24f03ec486680`.

The module returns `external-review-unavailable` when no trusted external review can be obtained. `acceptanceReady` in the fixture-only helper is a synthetic contract property; only the production helper may return `competitiveAdmission:true` once independently approved reviewer keys are correctly installed and the actual original-game bundle meets all conditions. Signatures authenticate the reviewer to the pinned trust keys; they do not prove the reviewer examined the evidence competently. Independent provenance, authorization and rulings must be evaluated before any reviewer key is approved.

## First real completed native game intake, explicitly unqualified

[The machine-readable review-intake case](agent45-first-independent-review-intake-rank5-20261109.json) is based on Agent8's **prospectively registered original rank5 Mew Genesect versus fixed New Moon Lockbreaker** four-way native A/B experiment, [source report](agent8-mew-source-dce-attack-prize-conversion-v6-native-20261109.md), [native CI 42577136715](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42577136715) and [raw ZIP artifact 13655243326](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42577136715/artifacts/13655243326). Original source rank5 is native seat 0, candidate seat 1; the `P0S1` test has the real source-funded Mew DCE policy enabled, candidate Perfection off, and unchanged printed sixties. The source naturally won on 156 accepted Store roots, taking six Prizes to the candidate's four. Source `Cross Fusion Strike` accepted AttackActions occurred at root ordinals **99, 117, 139, 153** on global turns **14, 18, 22, 26**.

The archived exact accepted-root tape SHA-256 is `111e5c2a8d8c1b4f10341283fc51011cd87620e7bc498d7c7de3471ae5d2bc56`. The full real physical-deal fingerprint is `a4c49b1f901ca273f8411dba89a42724cfc9c3b6623ef70ebe960e6f4ce68678`. Artifact ZIP SHA-256 `324fbc141c2464715b689c23db4319d8858f873fbcf8855d0aba1b3496fc6f61`, inner `agent8-source-mew-funding-v6.json` SHA-256 `84d4e157822605dfa4eef12660328fc403a1da766e36acdb00520a549d96d5f0`. The pinned engine was `a9905a76a6c4103ba4b6cb9a49e86bad795c5c05`. This provenance enables later reviewers to inspect the unchanged episode.

This genuine game is better source-pressure evidence than a mirror where the source repeatedly passes without attacking, yet it **does not meet the existing structural acceptance standard**. For example, after the source's T14 `Cross Fusion Strike` root99, the candidate used Unown and Telepathic Psychic Energy on T15 (roots106,107), source passed T16 (root111), and the next source attack was T18 root117. The opponent's plays were actually accepted, but the archive does not independently establish their legal payments, tactical causal necessity, complete owner alternatives or a certified source-response-source continuation.

The actual native record explicitly labels physical-zone integrity **unverified**, original printer/target certificate coverage selective, and Supporter quota validation unverified. The event-stage clock was never recorded to the independent official no-call/time-call standard. No independent hidden-information, complete paper-card or opponent-competence adjudication has been supplied. The producer may not infer a paper win from `nativeFinished:true`. Signed reviewer packets are absent and production reviewer registry is empty. Therefore `structuralGatePassed:false`, `competitiveAdmission:false` and `externalReviewUnavailable:true`.

## Operational acceptance handoff

**Shared runner steward (Agent36):** instrument the *next* prospectively frozen complete original-source two-player game to emit the nine digest-linked raw evidence objects; preserve the same full physical deal, both printed sixties, first-player seat, all Store roots and owner-visible payment/target options. Put actual clock coverage and event stage in the recording rather than reconstructing it after a winner is seen. The native source-funded rank5 game above supplies a good pressure and Energy-recipient counterexample for that trial.

**Independent reviewer (Agent41 or externally approved reviewers):** inspect all real payments, printing/legality and owner alternatives and adjudicate the true paper result independently. Separately assess continuous clock coverage and two-player strategic competence. Only after reviewer independence and keys are authorized should the maintainers populate the production registry, sign the exact immutable bundle and rerun the admission evaluator. A signed denial or dispute remains a denial, even when the native winner is plausible.

**Decision effect:** The project now has a code-covered cryptographic acceptance path and one concrete blocked original-source native case. The next useful milestone is obtaining **real authenticated evidence**, particularly a two-player clock-aware paper-compatible game, rather than accumulating more structurally unqualified terminal percentages. The Aichi Top32 field is still 29/32 exact original name/count sixties, with ranks 23,25,28 absent. The 99.5% target remains unproven.

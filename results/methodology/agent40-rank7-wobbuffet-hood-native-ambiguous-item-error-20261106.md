## Official Japanese paper-ruling cross-check

The official Pokémon Card Game [Wobbuffet card page](https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/31919) confirms that its Active-only Bide Barricade removes non-Psychic Pokémon Abilities. The [official Stealthy Hood card page](https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/36191/regu/all) says its attached Pokémon does not receive the effects of opposing Pokémon's Abilities. Especially relevant, [official Japanese Q&A for Slaking's Active-only Lazy Face versus Stealthy Hood](https://www.pokemon-card.com/rules/faq/search.php?page=2&regulation=all&sc_group_id=533) explicitly rules that attaching Hood to a Pokémon affected by that opposing Ability-removing effect restores the Pokémon's own Ability. This is an **analogous effect**, rather than a newly located literal Wobbuffet/Hood ruling. It supports the paper expectation that Hood shields Vileplume's Irritating Pollen against opposing Bide Barricade. The observed Nest Ball nested error still needs a genuine control; this ruling does not certify the native Store's action handling.

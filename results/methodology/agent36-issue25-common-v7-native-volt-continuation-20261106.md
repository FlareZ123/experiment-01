## Remaining release vetoes

1. **No complete games:** 6/8 root96 caps and 2 bot-decoder errors, zero genuine native terminal results.
2. **Paper-specific effects:** Prompt-native payment confirmed here; unvalidated remainder of opponent + source cards, print provenance, payment and control interactions still prevent paper qualification. The engine has independently found printed Night Watch randomness and attack effect errors elsewhere.
3. **Complete information-set proof missing:** Owner-only Volt component is guarded on own public board and prompt, but generic bot fallback/scorer remains unqualified; an independently varied opponent hidden-zone negative-control test is required over the full executing policy.
4. **Actual decisions:** Owner Pass after source attacks may sacrifice available Energy/Tools/Supporter opportunities. The next-action and root-opportunity audit must compare real same-root alternatives without consuming the Supporter quota on rejected proposals.
5. **Clock and field:** 25-minute official event clock not implemented; only original ranks6/11 covered; ranks23/25/28 original printed lists absent. No 99.5% field estimate.

**Conclusion:** v7 solves a repeated mandatory source attack prompt failure using a source-independent physical owner action and enables five demonstrably settled attacks with genuine next opponent replies. This is significant *developmental* policy progress. It does **not** select a final 60 or qualify any game.

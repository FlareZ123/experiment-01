## Exactly what this does not prove

The original source v5 was an unmodified **control**. It cannot establish by itself that Agent20's Tool settlement, Agent35's later search-prompt abort, or Agent7's paid Plumeria/Hammer treatment happened. Those native intervention claims rely on each agent's separately cited CI archives and receipts in the [prior-test registry methodology](agent18-native-source-fork-overlap-preflight-20261105.md) and their original reports. A valid original pre-root checksum proves that the **same starting physical game and action prefix** were registered, only. It does not ensure a paper-legal, tactically beneficial action, a good opponent response, a competent general policy, a paper-clock-valid terminal, or one of the missing full Aichi 32 decklists.

The source has 8 original attempts, 7 returned capped developmental and one engine aborted; the failed arm remains in the archive and index denominator. No new source 60 was authenticated and the known benchmark completeness remains 29/32. **Competitively qualified games remain zero.**

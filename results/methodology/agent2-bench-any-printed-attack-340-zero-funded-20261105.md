## Important limits and next priority

**The zero is specific to known-Basic printed Energy costs**. It does not prove no attack could be legally used following switching; attachments may be Special Energy, Ability-granted attack-cost waivers, Tools, Ability-granted attacks, copied attacks, or Tool attacks that the printed-attack classifier cannot represent. Nor does it mean no valuable non-attacking switch exists, and no native Store legal-attack probes were performed for hypothetical target promotions. The matches have an unqualified native policy, limited original source coverage, imperfect paper-engine and clock certification.

This strengthens the mechanism that the historical rank1 rescue needs not only a payable Active retreat but also a previously funded attacker. The general approach should **investigate proactively funding an appropriate Bench attacker** under owner-information-safe rules, rather than arbitrarily relaxing the damage or deck-count thresholds of the retreat planner. The zero-slot result also motivates a native Store shadow-state attack menu probe, performed without mutating original games, to detect Basic-only classifier false negatives before claiming full in-game lockout.

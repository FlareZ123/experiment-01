# Agent 3: verified 29-original Basic count census and bounded 32-rank setup-coupling yield

**4 November 2026 | mathematical setup analysis using original printed-card records; zero games played and zero competitive game results.**

## Full 29-source rank census

After the [exact bundled SMP, XYP and Prism Star glyph provenance repair](agent3-local-promo-prism-stage-completeness-20261104.md), the original roster now has all 29 available source Pokémon stages verified from bundled English card records. The [pure source census](../../scripts/agent3-original-source-basic-mulligan-census.cjs) evaluates the hypergeometric first-hand failure probability for each rank:

```text
q(rank) = C(60 - basicCount(rank), 7) / C(60, 7).
```

A fresh [manual-only CI 41773113494](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41773113494) passed full resolution and independent count/probability invariants. Its complete deterministic 29-rank result was captured as [versioned machine-readable JSON](../aichi-2026/agent3-original-29-first-seven-basic-census-20261104.json), including the original `rank`, exact local Basic count, and ideal initial-seven mulligan chance. This is a mathematical uniform-without-replacement opening model, distinct from an observed native outcome or evidence that the 32-bit xorshift stream is genuinely uniform.

Across the 29 authenticated original 60s, **Basic counts range from 4 through 16**. Under ideal uniform first-seven card sampling, the single-initial-hand mulligan chances range from **9.9223%** (16 Basics) to **60.0500%** (four Basics). Those numbers refer to random first seven, before player mulligan retries, initial coin decisions, source compensation draws, or gameplay. The four-Basic sources are original ranks **6, 24 and 29**. The paper first-hand probability is an attribute of an original source list's Basic inventory, not a rate observed in the historical games.

The original known rank1 has **14 Basics**, rank5 **11**, rank6 **4** and rank11 **11**, all checked directly against source rows and locally corroborated printed card subtypes. The known source dataset remains 29/32 because complete original paper 60s for **ranks 23, 25 and 28** are missing.

## Partial identification: all 32 ranks with three missing lists

The [previous exact geometric source-bonus model](agent3-ideal-source-bonus-mulligan-geometry-20261104.md) calculated how often two fixed candidate arms would give an opponent **different optional mulligan-compensation card counts** under a carefully specified **ideal** common-index shuffle. For each one of the 29 sources, its exact verified Basic count is now available. For the three unknown original 60s, rather than inventing their Basic counts, the [full32 pure bound](../../scripts/agent3-source-bonus-field-32-bound.cjs) enumerates **every integer 1 through 60** and takes the smallest and largest possible model compensation-difference probability. Every rank is weighted exactly `1/32`.

All candidate inventories remain exactly the historical rank1 champion Shadow Rider reference 60 and New Moon v1 proxy 60. The three cases differ **only in the source-array input position ordering of the same New Moon card copies**, which determines a common random-index coupling.

| Frozen input-order coupling scenario | Ideal average over 29 known source ranks | Possible equal-weight average across all 32 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| **Original source-row ordering**, champion/New Moon share 12 Basic indices | **9.1707%** | **8.4555% to 9.3622%** |
| Reorder New Moon's same 60 cards to maximum Basic-position overlap, 14 indices | **4.9535%** | **4.5692% to 5.0532%** |
| Reorder New Moon's same 60 cards to zero Basic-position overlap | **17.3893%** | **16.0224% to 17.7729%** |

These are **probabilities of a mismatch in hypothetical source bonus-card count**, which may lead to different source physical openings under the specified coupling. They are **not probabilities of winning games**, and do not certify actual native initial-deal frequencies. The wide difference across unchanged-card-order scenarios reinforces that experimental source pairing depends on the *registered common-RNG design*, not solely on what cards are in the compared fixed 60s.

The full32 uncertainty ranges account **only for not knowing the three remaining source Basic counts** in the ideal model. They do not account for stochastic generator quality, unknown matchup policies, interaction of first-turn draws with Basic placement, independent opponent mulligan simulation, deviations from choosing every optional bonus card, changes to input order, paper printing legality, or native game mechanics. They are deliberately not a confidence interval for competitive strength.

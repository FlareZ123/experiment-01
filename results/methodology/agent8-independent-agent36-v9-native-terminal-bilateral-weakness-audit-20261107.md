## Reproducible connector route through a specific later-used Item

The unchanged New Moon 60 contains **four Fog Crystal CRE** (see `results/als/new-moon-lockbreak-english-proxy-60.json`). On the actual 209-root ledger, candidate seat1 played Fog Crystal at **root19 turn2**, then again at **root175 turn36** and **root197 turn40**. At its first Pass root93, neither Fog Crystal appeared in the complete candidate owner's ten-card hand. Between root93 and root197, the candidate's only non-Pass/non-prompt Store roots were **Squawkabilly ex root116, Fog Crystal root175, Duskull root180, Tapu Lele-GX root188 and Fog Crystal root197**. The candidate took **zero Prizes** before the final root203. No recorded Trainer or Ability root returned an Item from discard or Prize cards.

Therefore the two later Fog Crystal copies provide strong retrospective evidence that the deck still contained **searchable Fog Crystal at root93**, assuming no unrecorded passive Item recovery. <https://asia.pokemon-card.com/sg/card-search/detail/12644/> independently verifies Secret Box's permission to search for an Item after discarding three other owner-hand cards; `Fog Crystal CRE` is an Item and its printed effect searches the deck for Psychic Energy or a Basic Psychic Pokémon ([Chilling Reign 140 printed text](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/en/cre/140)). The physical candidate hand at root93 contains two Battle VIP Passes that can no longer use their ordinary first-turn effect and a duplicate Rescue Carrier that could be considered as the third discard. Under a legal unlocked Item play, **Secret Box → Fog Crystal** is thus a grounded access route, irrespective of whether any Energy or search target remains available to the subsequent Fog Crystal.

This is a **postgame necessary-access and cost-availability observation**, with a realistic third-card opportunity cost. The absence of opponent lock effects and full paper timing need separate verification, and native acceptance of Secret Box's mandatory discard/search prompts remains untested at this exact root. The route's existence is not evidence of attacker funding, improved Prize trade, a legal full line or a preferable action. In particular, the candidate Active Unown and all four Bench Pokémon have **zero attached Energy** at root93; merely finding an Item will not immediately unlock their multi-Energy attacks.

## Boundaries and decision

The positive event means a support Basic was *available to keep in hand*. It does not verify the actual hand-to-Bench Ability was activated on a later turn, that a Bench slot remained open, that the Pokémon survived, or that preserving the card improved prizes or match outcomes. The test's `runMatch` continues only until the fail-closed owner-information quarantine catches an unsupported later action; it records **zero qualified games**. The seed-level receipt lacks a paper-certified full-deal fingerprint, external-paper legality proof, complete opposing decision opportunity, or a same-deal baseline comparator with the greedy setup algorithm. Setup-only Store acceptance is narrower than competitive player competence.

**Integration recommendation:** keep this default-off choice available as an explicitly registered A/B treatment, with actual source physical pre-treatment opening and seat parity, strict native prompt settlement and physical ownership of retained supports. The **same-start matched opening-policy A/B already exists**, as independently reverified above. The useful next treatment should carry the retained entry Pokémon through an actual in-turn hand-to-Bench Ability, printed payment/targets, measurable payoff and real opponent reply before considering any selection for the steward's policy. Record any added solo Active/empty Bench exposure: the [Bench occupancy analysis](../../simulation/evaluation/agent50-setup-bench-occupancy-tradeoff.cjs) quantifies that tradeoff independently. Agent4's later exact prompt-settlement v2-v3 evidence should be the acceptance standard for any new integrated test. No list promotion or 99.5% inference follows.

## Research question

The original champion rank1 Mimikyu counterline against the original rank10 Regidrago is a staged native success but a naturally missing setup. Test whether an owner-visible, Regidrago-conditional **search-and-preserve** policy can create an authentic Mimikyu Bench before Apex exposure, while retaining Dialga-GX and Radiant Eternatus as Dragon payloads for a possible later discard. This is an experimental tactical policy on **unchanged original full 60s** and does not imply a deck change.

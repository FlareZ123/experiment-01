# Prospective common-policy owner-Supporter contention 2×2 native test

**Agent1, 9 November 2026; preregistered before the combined-policy native run.** The same authentic fixed four rank-3 Snorlax Stall versus rank-1 Shadow Rider 60-card original-source seats and seeds are used. This cohort was previously exposed in Agent38's Mail and Agent1's single-treatment studies. Results estimate **only causal outcomes under those exact native game settings**, with no fresh-seed or field win-rate claim.

## Design

Four precommitted policy modes on each unchanged original trial:
- **B**: owner Ability confirmation, owner Mail-after-Pass, root tape; no new owner-held Supporter.
- **H**: B plus `followHeldHandiwork:true`.
- **R**: B plus `followHeldRefillSupporter:true`.
- **HR**: B plus both owner-held Supporter actions.

Run exactly original trial IDs `3:312:B:champion-reference`, `3:312:A:champion-reference`, `3:213:A:champion-reference`, `3:213:B:champion-reference`, physical seed 2404595552 or 948758444, both source-seat orientations. Pinned Twinleaf revision `a9905a76a6c4103ba4b6cb9a49e86bad795c5c05`; 260 native root cap; same two fixed source/candidate original 60s, common v8 actor-only policy; `followMailHandiwork:false` in all four modes.

In **HR**, held Handiwork takes precedence over a low-hand draw Supporter at the same root. Both use native `canPlay`; the game has one Supporter per turn. This arbitration is part of the declared treatment and may prove strategically unfavorable. The goal is to measure interaction between two real policy actions rather than assuming their independent benefits add.

Every mode retains all four trial endpoints (win/lose/tie if native terminal, cap, engine error), exact first root divergence from B, accepted held-Handiwork and accepted refill roots, Store payment/hand/deck/Supporter counts, deferred mill effects after both native WaitPrompts, opponent strategic follow-through and source subsequent turn, meaningful native attack/Prize movements where available. Both positive and negative effects must be retained.

Qualification remains zero paper-competent games without an independent stronger opponent policy, printed paper semantics and clock/physical legality acceptance. Differences in root-action counts and resource movement are developmental policy evidence only; no 99.5% full-field inference.

## Boundaries and paper-game relevance

This positive source-owned Supporter transfer is directly valuable as an incremental resource sequence. It has no claim of superior tactical choice: the current search chooses the first eligible owner Supporter, no competing connector uses were optimized, no Energy/attack followthrough or opponent tactical response is yet certified, and 48-root caps censor both changed trials. No actual source-only legal full-game win can be inferred.

**Important independent fidelity warning:** the separate [Agent8 native run 42077657557](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42077657557) passed its both-seat staged affirmative search and zero-search shuffle tests but failed a negative involving an artificially staged opposing active Iron Thorns ex. A `ConfirmPrompt` remained when none was expected. This may reflect paper-rule lock fidelity in Twinleaf or an incomplete/ambiguous test setup; the prompt origin was not yet confirmed. Do not use this development result as evidence that Wonder Tag always bypasses Initialization or that legal Ability lock has been resolved.

For a retrospective diagnostic, an explicit 96-root cap fork may reveal whether the rescued source next obtains a genuine attack and opponent response, but it must be labeled as a distinct post hoc horizon.

## A pinned, non-invasive engine source hook

The pinned Twinleaf `Store` imports the live CommonJS export `checkState` from `game/store/effect-reducers/check-effect`. An independent probe, [native CI run 42311732213](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/42311732213), demonstrated that temporarily wrapping that **export** intercepts **765 actual native `checkState` calls** on eight `onRootStoreCreated`-authenticated Store instances, without intercepting any foreign Store. Every genuine Store is also bound to the exact registered `trial.id` on the research-owned `agent41-bilateral-v2-development.cjs` callback.

The new default-off research observer `simulation/twinleaf/agent3-checkstate-generator-origin-token.cjs` allocates a fresh token to each actual `checkState(store,state)` invocation. Temporarily and reversibly wrapping its `Store.waitPrompt` and `Store.prompt` callback registrations carries **the same token** when native suspended generator continuations and pending prompt callbacks execute on later Store roots. A temporary `Store.reduceEffect` wrapper records final `KnockOutEffect.prizeCount`, its owner's seat, `DrawPrizesEffect.prizes.length`, destination and `preventDefault`; a `Store.prompt` wrapper captures exact `ChoosePrizePrompt` creation from that active context. It logs identities and effect metadata only; no Prize-card faces, card names, owner hand contents or candidate decisions are inspected.

The engine's actual methods and prior exported descriptor are restored in `finally`, including injected error cases. One counterexample regresses **two separate native `checkState` calls occurring inside the very same Store root**: a KO from the first and unrelated Prize prompt from the second have **different tokens**, and are not falsely correlated. Separate synthetic tests cover callbacks deferred across roots, foreign Store isolation, multiple check-state origins, thrown exceptions and hook cleanup. This directly addresses the residual ambiguity of same-root matching noted in v19–v23.

## What this certifies and what it cannot certify

The previous same-Store-root matching could potentially associate independent same-root KO and Prize events. Tokenized execution context now gives an exact native `checkState` call ancestry. Deferred continuations preserve the original token as intended. This is stronger *engine-source provenance* but cannot prove a specific physical card's paper legality, a strategically sound attacker decision, the complete paper Expanded rule matrix, official match-clock adjudication, or competent original opposing player. A check state can in principle process several KOs together, so a shared token alone is not a proof of one-to-one source attribution in an arbitrary game; **in these eight actual groups it is one-to-one**.

The rank-1 original source opponent declared **zero attacks** in the only native terminal. That fatal opponent-policy weakness still prevents treating the terminal as any evidence of 99.5% fixed-60 performance. Do not promote either candidate or call any historical action-cap outcome a game result.

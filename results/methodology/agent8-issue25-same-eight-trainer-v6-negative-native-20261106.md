## Interpretation and next decision

Under the frozen shared developmental v5 sample, the native Trainer fallback is **not a useful demonstrated remedy** for the source-owner Pass/attack-starvation bottleneck. Preserve its opt-in option for diagnostics; do not select it as a default common policy on this evidence. The next decision-changing test must instrument actual owner-private hand and public state at Pass roots, enumerate **card-specific paid/targeted alternatives**, and compare a prospectively selected common owner-information-safe policy through real attacks/control and meaningful opponent counterplay. Retain one fixed list per comparison, new prospectively registered action horizon, all error/censor attempts, and all printed-payment/clock vetoes.

This experiment changes no candidate-deck selection, no original rank admission, and no claim to a 99.5% field win rate. The benchmark still has **29/32 authenticated original name/count 60-card sources**, missing ranks 23,25,28; no selected final contender; **zero competitively qualified games**.

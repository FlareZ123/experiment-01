> **Correction and withdrawal of the prior inference.** The frozen v6 execution set `nativeOwnerTrainerFallback:true` but inherited `bilateralSpecialEnergyRoot:true` from the v4 selected runner. At the actual `agent41-bilateral-v2-development.cjs` → `agent7-canonical-actor-composition.cjs` boundary, the live Special Energy `scopedRoot.root` adapter took precedence over the latter module's built-in Trainer fallback constructor (`const root=adapters.root??(...)`). The scoped root did not carry `nativeOwnerTrainerFallback:true`. Therefore the native Trainer selector was absent in all reported B attempts. This is a source-code path proof at historical CI revision `c79da088d37242234420c454f630d2b497ed1738`; the archive's eight identical ledgers are consistent with it. The former conclusion that a running Trainer fallback failed to improve play is **withdrawn**. Historical raw receipts, seeds, 48-root caps and failure outcomes remain unchanged.

The preregistered [v5 control versus intended native Trainer fallback v6](agent8-issue25-trainer-v6-prereg-20261105.md) was executed without the fallback selector installed. Other already chosen v5 options, all eight original rank-6 and rank-11 source registrations, two fixed contender 60s, physical seat assignments, initial seed/deal protocol, and **48 accepted-root cap** remain unchanged. This is a retrospective comparison of frozen developmental v5 openings. It is not a held-out game-performance evaluation.

The **uninstalled intended** native fallback would consult a native bot at a root where the owner-only decision would otherwise Pass with `no-supported-owner-visible-root`. Its negative screens check generic public Trainer blocking, printed timing, and certain fixed-size owner-hand discard requirements. A proposed action may still rely on hidden-state native advice, unresolved paper payment/targets or questionable strategic preference. Native Store acceptance is insufficient to certify full paper legality.

This sample is **invalid as a Trainer-fallback effectiveness experiment**: the feature was requested but masked before native installation. Do not call it a negative treatment result or use it to select/reject the fallback. The scoped root now forwards the opt-in (Agent 8 commit `a6c06badd771345cdd58cfbe0072bf35a9b0df49`), and the canonical wrapper rejects missing/incomplete fallback root audits (commit `66a02883b8d379e5b2b3412014fb24fb5c9ba087`). Retain one fixed list per comparison, new prospectively registered action horizon, all error/censor attempts, and all printed-payment/clock vetoes.

This experiment changes no candidate-deck selection, no original rank admission, and no claim to a 99.5% field win rate. The benchmark still has **29/32 authenticated original name/count 60-card sources**, missing ranks 23,25,28; no selected final contender; **zero competitively qualified games**.

## A strict, role-symmetric causality gate

The read-only generic [attack-to-Prize reconciler](../../simulation/evaluation/agent3-accepted-prize-attack-join.cjs) takes each real, Store-accepted physical Prize-exit root from the existing v26 callback and requires **exactly one** attack in the *original source or fixed contender* true native `sourceAttackSettlement.attacks` / `candidateAttackSettlement.attacks`:

- the attack is marked genuinely `settledPromptChain===true` by the existing real-native attack observer;
- its native attack declaration and completion have unique accepted roots from the same registered trial, with an actual `AttackAction` or other correctly recorded triggering root;
- the real Prize-transfer `actionOrdinal` lies **between** that attack's declaration and settlement completion, inclusively, with matching actual actor, taking seat and global turn;
- the attack's before-versus-after remaining Prize delta for the taking player's seat equals the **exact number of physical Prize card objects** exiting that owner's Prize zone to their hand;
- the same attack cannot be reused to justify a second independent Prize event; no multi-event, unknown, unfinished or cross-sudden-death action is silently accepted as proof.

The helper and its 16 [pure positive and adversarial regression cases](../../tests/agent3-accepted-prize-attack-join.test.cjs) are reusable and fail closed on incorrect seat, turn, action, root order, Prize count, a missing settlement record, duplicate source, unearned replay and wrong destination. The fully separate [v27 actual-native test](../../tests/agent3-issue25-v27-settled-attack-to-prize-root.native.test.cjs) combines the strict joins with the already established unique check-state KO/Prize effect, native `ChoosePrizePrompt` and actual physical Prize-object custody.

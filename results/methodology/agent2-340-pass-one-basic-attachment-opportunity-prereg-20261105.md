## Interpretation and release

A positive nominal one-attachment opportunity shows a potential resource-allocation **diagnostic**, not a proved miss or beneficial policy change. Negative observations show only that this restricted one-Basic route is absent. No physical card is attached during this passive diagnostic. Any subsequent treatment must be separately predeclared, control the same physical initial deals and seats, trace actual native Store payment and settled attack, preserve failures and genuine opponent reply, and remain unqualified until independent paper rules/clock acceptance.

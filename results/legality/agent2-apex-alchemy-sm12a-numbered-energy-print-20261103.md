# Agent 2: fully dated numbered Basic Energy alternatives for the two frozen Apex 60s

**3 November 2026. Japanese May 10 Aichi paper Expanded print provenance; no games.**

The previous [v1 print crosswalk](agent2-apex-alchemy-jp-crosswalk-v1.json) suggested six Championship 2025 SV-P Basic Energy copies. Their publisher record identity was clear, but the original award day of each exact promo number was uncertain. A **v2 physical-print alternative** replaces those six illustrated energy copies with exact, publisher-numbered, pre-event Japanese products while **keeping each candidate's original registered 60 English proxy identities and counts unchanged**.

The publisher confirms that SM12a, high-class pack TAG TEAM GX Tag All Stars, **launched 4 October 2019** on its [original product page](https://www.pokemon-card.com/ex/sm12a/index.html). That page includes basic Energy cards, and the numbered records identify the two specific cards. This is before the [10 May 2026 Aichi tournament](https://www.pokemon-card.com/info/005445.html).

The original immutable candidate manifests remain [Apex v0](../aichi-2026/agent43-apex-alchemy-paper-expanded-v0.json) (digest `21846db95f88d5d5abc6ee3876b3522732092e27ca5e7485948f354d8468a769`) and [Shake4/Quick2 v1](../aichi-2026/agent2-apex-alchemy-shake4-quick2-v1.json) (digest `5cd6b0fc7781e1a5a3b29f5a23654750295af89bdc63621f235fbd0c56701fd0`). No gameplay swap occurs.

The new [34-identity full crosswalk](agent2-apex-alchemy-jp-crosswalk-v2.json) and [29-family publication chronology](agent2-apex-alchemy-jp-release-chronology-v2.json) cover **all 60 physical slots** in each fixed candidate via *exactly dated pre-event Japanese product families*, versus 54 slots with exact product dates in v1. The remaining 28 product families and 32 mapped card identities are unchanged. [Two fail-closed regression tests](../../tests/agent2-apex-sm12a-release.test.cjs) check the two specific substitutions, fixed-source integrity, the existing Japanese ban/identity necessary checks, 29 unique release dates, and exact family copy totals. [Manual-only CI 41709762457](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41709762457) passed all old and new tests.

**Limitation:** Historical product launch dates are necessary publication evidence, not complete event legality; Japanese errata, individual printing restrictions, card effect fidelity and physical acquisition remain separate. This print change cannot improve simulated gameplay metrics. There are **29/32** original source 60s, **zero qualified games**, **no final selected 60**, and **no demonstrated 99.5% field win rate**.

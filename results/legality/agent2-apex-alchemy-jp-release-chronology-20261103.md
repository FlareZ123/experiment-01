**Agent 2 | 3 November 2026 | necessary Japanese print-publication evidence**

## Why this matters

The official [Aichi 2026 event announcement](https://www.pokemon-card.com/info/005445.html) imposed a Japanese-language paper Expanded 60-card, six-Prize event on **10 May 2026**. The frozen English proxy manifests cannot themselves be entered as Japanese physical decklists. This audit follows the separately mapped [exact 34-identity Japanese printing crosswalk](agent2-apex-alchemy-jp-crosswalk-v1.json), verifying that the proposed Japanese products had appeared before that event.

**28 of 29 identified Japanese expansion/product families have exact publisher-stated original release dates. All 28 predate the Aichi 2026 event.** Those families account for **32 of the 34 identities and 54 of the 60 card slots** in each unchanged candidate. The six remaining physical slots consist of 3 Grass and 3 Fire Basic Energy cards printed for **Championship Series 2025**. Primary and independent sources associate both numbered printings with Japanese 2024–25 Championship promotional Energy awards. The first specific award day of those numbered prints is not established.

## Provenance of promotional Basic Energy

The [publisher's Grass Energy record](https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/46296) independently identifies **202/SV-P** as a Championship Series 2025 Basic Grass printing. Its [Fire Energy record](https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/46297) likewise identifies **203/SV-P**.

The official Japanese Championship Series 2025 [schedule](https://www.pokemon-card.com/info/004566.html) specifies the series ran from September 2024 through August 2025, with **Champions League Tokyo on 22–23 September 2024**. The official [Tokyo 2025 side-event prize announcement](https://www.pokemon-card.com/info/004611.html) explicitly identifies Basic Energy promotional cards randomly awarded from eight types, and a [December 2024 Osaka announcement](https://www.pokemon-card.com/info/004733.html) independently documents the same kind of prize. The [independent print-number catalog](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/SV-P) ties exact 202/SV-P and 203/SV-P print numbers to participation rewards at these Championships.

Thus there is triangulated evidence of **pre-event issuance** of the chosen two Japanese promo Basic Energy printing identities. The exact first distribution date of the two numbered printings is still pending a directly numbered official prize notice or comparable publication. Do not substitute the first Tokyo event date as a proven date for each individual printed number.

# Apex Alchemy: official Japanese printing candidates for both unchanged 60s

**Agent 2 | 3 November 2026 | physical-print provenance research | no game-win evidence**

## Decision-relevant finding

The 10 May 2026 [official Aichi Open League announcement](https://www.pokemon-card.com/info/005445.html) explicitly requires a 60-card Expanded deck with six Prize cards, and **only Japanese-language cards may be played**. The pre-existing Agent43 Apex Alchemy v0 and Agent2 v1 registered manifests use English print IDs for **all 60 physical slots**. Their English proxy lists are therefore *not event-registration manifests*. Names and counts can nevertheless be translated into candidate Japanese printings without changing either fixed deck.

The machine-readable [34-card crosswalk](agent2-apex-alchemy-jp-crosswalk-v1.json) proposes one specific publisher-recorded Japanese printing for **each of the 34 distinct English proxy identities**, accounting for all 60 slots in both fixed candidates. Every selected Japanese print is accompanied by its Japanese-language card-search detail URL, expansion and collector number. This is a source-first *printing-identity bridge*; a separate full print-by-print semantic, errata, ban, release-date and physical-paper registration audit remains required.

v0 source SHA-256: `21846db95f88d5d5abc6ee3876b3522732092e27ca5e7485948f354d8468a769`.

v1 source SHA-256: `5cd6b0fc7781e1a5a3b29f5a23654750295af89bdc63621f235fbd0c56701fd0`.

The **only changed slots** are Quick Ball 3→2 and Boost Shake 3→4 (v0→v1). Both physical categories still total 60. Japanese-name 4-copy census finds **zero obvious over-four identities** apart from the expressly exempt three Grass and three Fire Basic Energy. Both have one Prime Catcher ACE SPEC and four Double Dragon Energy. A comparison with the frozen [Japanese 10 May banned-identity list](japan-expanded-aichi-2026.json) yields **zero exact-name matches** in the mapped 60. None of those necessary checks alone proves tournament approval.

## Mechanically important verification examples

- [Alolan Muk SM1M 023/060](https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/33087) has the Japanese Power of Alchemy equivalent, which removes Basic Pokémon Abilities in play, in hands and in discard piles. In this candidate it suppresses the owner's own [Latias ex SV7a 019/064 Skyliner](https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/46248), so Latias cannot provide free retreat after Muk's Ability is working. Previous Agent2 paid-mobility results explicitly excluded that incorrectly free route.
- [Boost Shake S6a 060/069](https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/39644), whose Japanese printed name is **ぐんぐんシェイク**, ends the player's turn and can evolve even on the first turn or a just-played Pokémon. It cannot be counted as simultaneously allowing an attack that turn.
- [Regidrago VSTAR S12 077/098](https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/42169) offers the Japanese Apex Dragon/Legacy Star text. Its attack depends on actual accessible Dragon attack payloads; the existence of an attack in the discard must be simulated as a paid, realizable line.
- [Double Dragon Energy XY6-B 078/078](https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/30933) only attaches to Dragon Pokémon and provides two units of any type while attached. The rendered text can drop Dragon-type symbols, so do not infer wider attachment legality from plain text extraction.
- [Prime Catcher SV5M 062/071](https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/45279) is an ACE SPEC and allows opponent gust plus own switch. Its tactical value is not captured by hand-access incidence alone.

## Source, coverage and remaining gates

**Source status.** Individual Japanese printing pages are primary publisher records, and the event announcement is the binding primary event restriction. The existing `results/legality/japan-expanded-aichi-2026.json` preserves the 19 February 2026 Japanese historical ban snapshot. The English source printing IDs and original two registered 60s are kept unchanged in `results/aichi-2026/`.

**What has been directly checked.** Physical slot count 60/60, source-English identity coverage 34/34, a selected numbered Japanese record for every identity, v0/v1 count differences, one Prime Catcher ACE SPEC, no obvious four-of overcount by mapped Japanese name, and no exact name conflict with that frozen Japanese banned-identity list. These checks are reproducible from the linked JSON, and can be independently challenged.

**What remains UNKNOWN.** Exact latest pre-event errata and card-text equivalence for each of the 34 entries, individual numbered Championship promotional Basic Energy release days (the other 28 publisher product families have pre-event release dates), whether the specific 202/SV-P and 203/SV-P Championship promo prints satisfy all event floor criteria, and a formal full Japanese legality adjudication. No physical registration, native games, matchup wins or qualified game evidence are asserted. The 29/32 authenticated opponent roster still lacks ranks **23, 25 and 28**.

**Strategic status.** Previous paid-resource opening tests do not select v1 over v0: v1 improves narrowly funded Muk/Regidrago lines while v0 has better broader either-lock setup access. This printing crosswalk does not decide which of the two, if either, is worth nominating as the single best fixed deck. **99.5% is still an unverified aspiration.**

## Reproducible integrity test and CI

The [source-bound necessary-condition audit](../../scripts/agent2-apex-jp-print-audit.cjs) recomputes the canonical SHA-256 of **both independently registered** 60s and checks every English card name, printing, category and count against its crosswalk row. It also checks all mapped Japanese URLs have the official publisher's card-detail shape, that Japanese print identity is unchanged across v0/v1, historical banned-Trainer names and unresolved matching banned-Pokémon identities, the Japanese named-card four-of limits, the Prime Catcher ACE SPEC, 20/30/10 category totals, and exactly the registered Quick Ball versus Boost Shake swap.

[Ten source-integrity and tamper/negative regressions](../../tests/agent2-apex-jp-print-audit.test.cjs) cover genuine source drift, false English ID/number, foreign host, variant Japanese print mismatch, over-four Japanese names, historically banned Trainer names, unlisted variant changes, incorrect historical date and ambiguous Pokémon ban identity. The existing lightweight Japanese legality workflow now runs these. [Manual GitHub Actions run **41675682531**](https://github.com/FlareZ123/experiment-01/actions/runs/41675682531) **passed** on main commit `c75605cfff3ab023b348b07403d69e88b16a0aad`, alongside the pre-existing historical Japanese legality tests. This qualifies the **identity/copy/negative-screen toolchain**, not the physical Japanese prints' full paper legality or any game result.

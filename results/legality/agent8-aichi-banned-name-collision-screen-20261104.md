# Print-sensitive paper Expanded legality screen: 14 false bans under name-only matching

Agent 8, 4 Nov 2026. Static comparison of 29 original Aichi 60s with October 2026 public Expanded banned-printing list. This is a targeted warning, not complete format legality certification.

**Key finding:** 14 distinct source ranks contain a legal *different* printing of a Pokémon that shares a name with a banned effect. Marshadow UNB81 (ranks 1,10,12,14,17,18,19,20,21,27) differs from banned SLG45; Unown AOR30 (ranks 9,17,26,30,31) differs from banned LOT90/LOT91; Duskull PRE35 (rank31) differs from banned CEC83. There are 16 rank-name collisions, 31 physical source copies. No exact banned printing overlaps the source's name+printing strings. The 29 published originals still require full independently proven paper legality; 3 originals remain unpublished.

**Recommendation:** Check card effect/printing equivalence, not just name, and explicitly account for alternate illustrations and true equivalent reprints of banned effects. Build a regression for both the banned and differently printed legal versions.

# Pokémon TCG Expanded research workplace

This repository coordinates the search for **one fixed, legal, playable 60-card paper Expanded deck** against the complete Aichi Open League 2026 Top 32 field. The 99.5% win-rate objective is an ambitious research target, and any claimed performance needs actual evidence.

**Repository creator's rule:** Do not create GitHub Actions workflows triggered by every commit. Such workflows would exhaust the shared CI budget.

## Current critical path, 19 October 2026

**Status: 29 of 32 exact opposing source 60s recovered; zero independently competitive-qualified games; no verified final deck and no demonstrated 99.5% win rate.** Ranks 23, 25 and 28 remain unavailable as complete published lists. The [single-deck decision board](results/critic-review-70.md) tracks candidates and qualification vetoes; the [research index](results/README.md) links supporting results.

The priority experiment is the [canonical bilateral v2 development runner](simulation/evaluation/agent41-bilateral-v2-development.cjs), comparing the unchanged champion reference and New Moon Lockbreaker 60s against exact rank-6 Iron Thorns and rank-11 Absol/Garbodor source lists. [Agent 10's compiled integration checkpoint](results/aichi-2026/agent10-active-bilateral-v2-integration-state-20261019.md) identifies the active caretaker and exact revisions. Check remote identity leases and current communications before transferring integration ownership. Preserve **one** runner and policy comparison rather than creating a competing campaign.

The four previously inspected historical arms are an essential **regression fixture**, not win-rate samples. Both New Moon arms reached the 260-root cap with zero recorded candidate attack declarations. Genuine owner-visible inspections found an unestablished attack payload in the rank-11 fixture. The unmodified decision scorer can [change an action based only on the opponent's hidden hand](results/twinleaf/perfection-hidden-state-action-choice-reversal.md). A separate [rank-26 source census](results/aichi-2026/rank26-held-item-pass-census.md) documented held setup Items that the one-step bot did not use. Together these findings show that policy competence and information boundaries are immediate barriers to meaningful deck comparisons.

**The next deliverable is a complete-game causal checkpoint.** Repair the common setup decision layer in the existing executable, using only information available to the acting player. Demonstrate native accepted actions and closed prompts that can establish or conclusively rule out an attack line, with legal alternatives and resource costs recorded. Re-run the same four historical arms under a versioned common policy. Archive all root decisions, genuine effects, subsequent opponent actions, caps, rejects, and counterexamples. If attacks remain absent, explain the first evidenced cause and whether it is a legal resource impossibility, engine failure, or avoidable policy choice. The information boundary must be checked on the **integrated runner**, including delegated attacks.

**Go/no-go for new opponent-dealt trials:** First pass the integrated historical regression or preserve a specific, reproducible failure with an assigned repair. After that, pre-register a small **fresh, physically paired, both-actual-start** comparison with frozen source/card printing identities, full policy and engine hashes, all attempted arms, censored outcomes, and paper-clock limitations. Feed that same archive to both the [unconditional paired census](results/aichi-2026/agent2-v2-unconditional-paired-census.md) and [strict bilateral witness gate](results/aichi-2026/agent33-v2-bilateral-evidence-gate.md). Their currently passing data-only tests do not certify real games.

Spend scarce compiled CI time on this integrated blocker. Favor existing manually dispatched workflows and reuse source-dealt regression fixtures. Additional isolated card or engine tests need a named dependency on the shared complete-game decision gate, an exact falsification criterion, and a route back into its executable. Parallel independent priorities remain the recovery of exact missing rank lists and genuinely discriminating fixed-60 deck hypotheses. Subsequent field expansion must include [five presently unrepresented published archetypes](results/aichi-2026/agent21-field-diversity-coverage.md), followed by meaningful intra-archetype diversity.

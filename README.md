## Repository practice

- Preserve source evidence, failed trials, raw ledgers, matched physical deals and provenance in `results/`; preserve research-owned Twinleaf adapters in `simulation/twinleaf/`. The actual pinned upstream submodule is **`vendor/twinleaf`**, as recorded in `.gitmodules`; do not modify upstream directly.
- Coordinate release changes through `communications/` and the existing research ownership. A personal hook or local staged proof is supporting evidence until it is selected in one frozen shared two-player release.
- **Repository creator's CI rule: do not introduce workflows triggered on every commit.** Use existing manually dispatched workflows, control their compilation/runtime budgets, and avoid adding another per-card workflow merely for a green local test.
- Keep [`results/README.md`](results/README.md) short and decision-first. Update it for a changed fixed-60 contender, source coverage, actually released shared policy, or competitive qualification status; link other atomic reports from their specific research area.

**Do not publish a best-deck or win-rate conclusion from unqualified engine terminals, retrospectively selected successful deals, prompt-only mocks, or action-capped games.** Pursue original-source fidelity and a complete same-policy opponent contest before interpreting candidate-versus-reference performance.
